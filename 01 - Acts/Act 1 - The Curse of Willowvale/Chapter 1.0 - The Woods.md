### **1.1 Introduction**

The adventure begins offscreen, in the town of [[Willowvale]]. The intent is to place the characters immediately into a practical adventure rather than a roleplay-heavy session as a start. As the story begins, read the following text:

*You are a group of adventurers on the road on your way to Candlekeep Hall, a famous fortress of knowledge on the Sword Coast. You began your journey in the town of [[Willowvale]], a quaint little village between the city of Baldur's Gate and the Cloak Wood forest. [[Willowvale]] wasn’t known for much, and has very few amenities save for 1 inn that struggles to stay afloat. What few visitors they get are usually on their way to or from Baldur’s Gate.*

*About a year ago a curse befell [[Willowvale]]. It was subtle, at first. The rains that quenched the land began to slowly dwindle, bit by bit before coming to an eventual end. Without the rain watering the soil, [[Willowvale]]’s crops began to wither and die. And because of that, livestock have had little left to graze on. Cows and sheep stand in fields thin and emaciated, much like the citizens themselves. With their food sources slowly dwindling, the townsfolk are living on borrowed time. [[Feldir Whitefyre]], the local wizard, was stumped. He knew that this was no ordinary blight. When you and your companions stayed the night, he implored you to go to help them.*

*"We won't survive another year if things go on like this. Please you must go to [[Candlekeep]] Hall. Find [[Matreous Melwin]]. Find whatever you can about this curse. We'll be forever in your debt."*

If the party only has 2 party members, [[Tierbhal an Garrán]], a druid from [[Garrán]] in the [[Misty Forest]] will join their party. He is eager to learn more to learn about the plague that has befallen the land. Allow the others to roleplay their trek through the woods before continuing on with the following.

### **1.2 Saving Grace**

*As you’re walking down the Coast Way, you hear the sounds of high-pitched yelling up ahead. Tyrval asks ‘I wonder what that could be’ and looks down the road.* 

On a DC 12 intelligence check, the character determines that the growls are coming from wolves known to roam the plains of the Sword Coast.

*A young girl sitting atop a tree branch about 12 or 13 feet off of the ground. She’s wearing a colourful rainbow scarf that bristles in the wind as she throws rocks and her shoes at the three wolves growling hungrily at the base of the tree. She matter-of-factly asks for help because the longer she stays trapped up here the longer it’ll take for her to save up money.*

**Encounter**: 3 wolves and 1 alpha wolf surround a tree where [[Grace]] sits.

When the characters free [[Grace]], she climbs down from the tree and then immediately gives them a pitch. Asking them for coin so she can afford to go to the academy over in [[Elturel]]. 
1. If the characters will ask how she plans on getting there considering how far Elutrel is, she calls over Lonkey the Donkey who is an old looking Donkey missing a left eye and its right ear.
2. If they don’t ask and skip to giving her coin, she thanks them and leaves.
3. If they give her a gold piece or more, she sheds a tear and says its more than what shes made all year. 
4. She then gives the character a cutting from her sister’s scarf and says if they bring this back to her when she becomes famous she’ll give them one lesson for free and then runs off into the distance.

### **1.3 Road Work Ahead**

*Further down the Coast Way, you come into an enclosed area. To the east is a natural wall of rock. To the west, a path leads deeper into the Cloak Wood. Your party senses something strange. A wolf appears in front of you, seemingly not hostile. As you approach the wolf runs away further down the road.*

Roll a contested perception vs stealth check to see if the party is able to discover the Goblin Ambush. If not, they are accosted by 5 Goblins in a surprise attack. On a success, the party avoids the surprise round and combat begins.

**Encounter:** 3 Goblin Minions, 2 Goblin Warriors, 1 Wolf (runs away).

The party notices there is a damaged caravan, its horse dead on the ground and its passenger nowhere to be found. Further to the left is a camp, its fire still burning and its owner (later revealed to be [[Lyam Bentos]]) has been slaughtered. On his body is a note, containing the following:

	"Deliver the key to Kevdor, time is of the essence. 
	T."

Upon investigating the body, on a DC 10 investigation check, the party is able to find a pouch well hidden inside of his coat. Within is a Satchel of Holding (given to Lyam by [[Matreous Melwin]]), containing one key. Close inspection will reveal this key to be Infernal in origin.

Behind the damaged caravan is a 30ft wall of stone encased by vines. Further investigation reveals that a significant portion of the road was blocked off. Magical and physical attempts at penetrating the wall will result in the wall reconstituting. Ultimately, there’s no other way to progress than to head further west.

### **1.4 I sure hope it does**

Further west into the forest is a great tree atop a hill. To the south is a small pool of water, to the east heads deeper into the woods. The party also meets a badger sitting right in front of the pool, seemingly unbothered by their presence.

If the players decide to speak to the badger, the badger is quite angry and complains about a lack of food. The party can ask the badger where the wolf may have run off to or if it has seen any activity nearby. Decide its level of knowledge based on a DC 5 intelligence check.

Further exploration of the forest reveals there is a sleeping creature nearby to the south west. On a DC 10 perception check, the players are able to discern that something non-humanoid is further down the grove. On a roll of 18 or higher, the player is able to learn that a sleeping bear (2 if running more than 2 players) is hibernating.

A Goblin woman and two warriors are atop the hill, bickering about the lack of a report from the party they sent out to retrieve the key not too long ago. The goblin woman appears to be far stronger than the other two.

If the characters are badly injured (no rest) from the earlier encounter, skip this and head straight inside. If the characters are fine, run this encounter. For stat blocks, use the following:

**Encounter:** 1 Goblin Boss, 2 Goblin Warriors

On the body of the goblin boss is an amulet matching the symbol on the key. If this encounter was skipped, place the amulet on top of the table of the camp within the tree hideout.

Around the tree there is a massive wall of stone about 7ft high. It does not appear to be man-made, but there are footsteps pointing towards the tree. Further investigation will reveal that there is a hidden door inside.
