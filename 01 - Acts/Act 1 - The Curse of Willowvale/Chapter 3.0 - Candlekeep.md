### **3.1 Journey to Candlekeep**

The journey to Candlekeep can either be initiated either before or after [[Chapter 5.0 - Blood of the Wolf]]. 

****

### **3.2 The Joy of Extradimensional Spaces**

The entrance to Candlekeep is guarded by five Priests of Deneir.

****
### **3.3 A Changeling Among Us**

After the events of 3.2, the adventurers will want to report to [[Sylvira Savikas]]. If [[Matreous Melwin]] is present, he will be able to confirm their story. If not, Sylvira will need to be given evidence of Matreous's death. The party may be suspicious of Sylvira due to the [[The Joy of Extradimensional Spaces (Book)]] having her handwriting and alleged lip stick stain on it. In order to prove her legitimacy, Sylvira will either threaten or go along with the party's suspicions depending on their tone. Regardless, Sylvira will successfully prove her legitimacy and suggest that someone may have sabotaged the book.
