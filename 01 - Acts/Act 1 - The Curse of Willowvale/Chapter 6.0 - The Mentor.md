
Upon completion of both [[Chapter 4.0 - Veins of Ice]] and [[Chapter 5.0 - Blood of the Wolf]], [[Matreous Melwin]] or [[Rhylrae]] will contact the adventurers to meet at a specificed location.

****
### **6.1 If [[Matreous Melwin]] is alive**

Matreous will initiate travel to Willowvale upon the completion of either [[Chapter 4.0 - Veins of Ice]] and [[Chapter 5.0 - Blood of the Wolf]], after which he will arrive after the party completes the other. He will be accompanied by [[Torbar Dragonsbane]], his Half-Dragon companion from decades past. Matreous will evaluate the party's current skillset and provide kindly advice as to how they can perform better as a group. [[Torbar Dragonsbane]] will be more quietly hostile, and say that with the way the party looks right now they stand no chance against Mellara. 

As the meeting ends, [[Torbar Dragonsbane]] will push through the doors and attack the party. Matreous will not intervene. 

****
#### **6.2 If [[Matreous Melwin]] is dead**

[[Rhylrae]] will have been told by either [[Sylvira Savikas]] or [[Lorcan Ravenshade]] that the adventuring party wants to meet with him. He will arrive in [[Shade Rest]] after 3 days or upon completion of [[Chapter 4.0 - Veins of Ice]]. He will be at the [[Friendly Arms Tavern]], joined by his half-elf companion [[Leosin Erlanthar]]. Rhylrae will heavily criticise the party and refuse to give information to them without first giving him anything of substance.

He will ask the party to take a walk with him to somewhere more private as he does not think it is safe for them to discuss sensitive topics in a private place. Regardless of where the party brings him, [[Rhylrae]] will continue expressing his dissatisfaction with the state of their abilities. He will then challenge them to a duel, while [[Leosin Erlanthar]] simply stands by to watch.

****
#### **6.3 Aftermath**

Given [[Torbar Dragonsbane]] and [[Rhylrae]]'s CR (16) players will more than likely loose both battles by a large margin. [[Matreous Melwin]] and [[Rhylrae]] will offer advice to the players in more or less the same words;

"When you're facing the Herd, you cannot have any doubt in your heart. 
The Cult of the Dragon works as their Queen does, speaking in many voices but with a singular mind. Treat every wound your party members suffer as if it were carved into your own flesh.

Hesitate, and you die.
Fight alone, and you lose.
Only together do you stand a chance against her."

He steps back, lowering his stance and letting the tension bleed out in his shoulders. His face shifts, no longer hostile but still stern.

"Your Shadow Druid. Thaddak, was it? You said he disappeared into a pile of ice..." the Mentor says. "A simulacrum most likely, he will return. Though what his true plans are I cannot yet be certain. Whatever spell or curse he placed on the land still permeates through the Weave. I can sense it, as I suspect you can as well."

"I will speak plainly. Ending the blight on the land will require you to pursue Mellara to wherever she may be."

"Our agents spotted her warband up east of Baldur's Gate. We thought they would continue their pillaging of the land, but then something strange happened." [[Leosin Erlanthar]] or [[Torbar Dragonsbane]] says. "They turned around and began heading south, straight back down from where they came from."

****

"What few friends I have in Candlekeep haven't been able to give us any solid information, and neither have the druids." Rhylrae says. "The last thing Matreous sent to me were his findings on a strange reading in the Cloud Peaks, far to the south."

OR

"I never told you how I found out about the blight on Willowvale, did I?" Matreous says. "It happened by chance, you know. Magic in Candlekeep can be amplified given the right preparations. I was preparing a variation of a scrying ritual and needed somewhere to look. So I looked to the surrounding area. During that reading, I found two strong corruptions in the Weave. One in the Cloakwood, and one in the Cloud Peaks, far to the south. I thought nothing of it at the time, but when [[Feldir]] reached out to me to speak of the blight. Well... the rest is history."

****

"Whatever the [[Herd of the Dragon]] has found must be of great interest to the Cult." the Mentor says. "Especially if they were willing attempt an assassination in Candlekeep."

"But to pursue them now would be suicide. You may have crippled Mellara's army, but we need to be smart about our next move." the Mentor says. "Some of Mellaras men have been left behind to enforce their hold on this part of the Sword Coast. If we are to gather more information about her plans, they will be our best lead."

The Mentor will then present the party with a map of a keep either North of [[Shade Rest]] or South of [[Willowvale]]. This map leads to an abandoned fortress that Mellara used to occupy but a small army of Kobolds and two of her fellow Black Dragonclaws (a title the party does not know Mellara occupies within the [[Cult of the Dragon]]). 

In addition to the map, the Mentor will grant the party 500 GP, 600SP and 700 CP.

****

#### **6.4 The Keep**

The keep is running on the Cragmaw Keep Map from LMoP but instead of Goblins it will be heavily occupied by Kobolds. In the master room, there is a Kobold Tinkerer and two Black Dragonclaws [[Harandiir]] and [[Zeraxes]].
