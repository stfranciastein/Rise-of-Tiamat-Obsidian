### **8.2 The Ruins**

*As you reach the summit of the mountain, you come upon a door of Dwarven make. The symbol etched on the front, however, is out of place. A silver dragon depicted in fine detail makes up the middle portion of the door. It has been defaced, painted over with blood and acid in the crude shape of Tiamat's form. The towering stone double door before you is also adorned with two arrow slits, one on each side of the door and each about 10 feet above the ground, guarding the entrance.*

*Dim light bathes the humid and chilly interior as spiral staircases rise along this area's northern walls. Distantly you hear the faint sound of chatter and utensils clattering on dishes. This place seems to be occupied, and from the looks of things they weren't expecting visitors.*

*Blue-tinted liquid flows through geometric carvings that run along the wall's top edges, bathing the room in eerie light. Three rectangular stone tables stretch across the length of the room along with several sets of stone benches. HOBGOBLIN: We're here to make sure you don't interrupt the Chief's plans. No hard feelings.

### **8.3 The White Dragon Mask**

*You step into the elevator, and immediately you feel an oppressive cold force surround you, the energy pulsing faster and faster as you descend, almost like a heartbeat. Somehow it's much colder than the frigid winds outside the ruins. The platform continues to make its way down, the walls around you opening up more, allowing blue light to eminate from below. As you await your fate, you get the sense that this is different from the blue-tinted lava below. There's something more sinister down here, something more infernal.*

*As the elevator lowers you down to the room, you can see the source of the light now. A glowing blue and white bubble pulses with energy as three familiar figures stand before it. Mellara, Ysra and Thaddak. The bugbear stands in front of the bubble, arms raised in the air as he chants in Infernal (a dispelling ritual). Mellara and Ysra turn as the elevator finishes its descent, acknowledging your presence.*

YSRA: (Laughing) Oh Mellara look, it is as I said, the heroes have come at last. You've come just in time heroes, Thaddak here was just about to get to the good part.

MELLARA: I commend you for defeating my men. If I'd known that the cowards that hid from us in Shade Rest would turn out to be such a nuisance I would've brought the might of the Herd down on their hovels with Batuu. A mistake on my part, one that I won't make again.

YSRA: This is where your story ends, heroes.

MELLARA: If you give up now, I promise to make it quick. I'll even grant Bonham and the other vermin the same mercy.

MELLARA: Witness me, Tiamat. Witness your Champion!

After the 1d4 ritual is completed and Mellara is bloodied, Thaddak will cast Wall of Thorns in order to obscure him and the Dragon Mask from view. As a 6th level spell this will be more than likely impossible to Counterspell. In the span of 2 turns, he will cast Transport Via Plants in secret in order to escape from the party. Wall of Thorns will vanish after the fight concludes, revealing nothing but an empty, frozen circle where the White Dragon Mask was once kept.
