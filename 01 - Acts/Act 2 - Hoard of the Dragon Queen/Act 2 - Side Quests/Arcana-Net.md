### TRIGGER

Entering [[Whitkeep Hostel]] in [[Baldur's Gate]] will trigger this quest.

____
### PART I - Revolution is afoot

*As you enter the Whitkeep Hostel, you immediately notice two gnomes arguing in the corner. One, a female, wears half-plate and leathers. Her dark skin is marked with freckles and her hair is tied into a ponytail. The other is a man wearing brass metallic armour, and a blue tunic beneath. A pair of goggles adorn his face, and his white beard is trimmed quite well.*

One of the neighborhood's more outspoken critics of the Gate's government, [[Pernilla 'Prole' Cabrenock]], a chaotic neutral female rock Gnome bandit captain, has teamed up with an oddball inventor and arcanist named [[Ardryn Deagle]], a chaotic good male rock gnome artificer.

PROLE: Oi Ardryn, keep your bloody voice down will ya!

ARDRYN: I can't Prole, we're so close to finally stripping away the bonds of capital. Soon the people will be able to flourish in their own light!

If the players approach, Prole will be on guard but Ardryn will gladly let the party hear him out. He will explain that he is developing something called the "Arcana-Net" which will allow people to talk to each other or access information stored in augelite crystals.

ARDRYN: Think of the possibilities! Vast swathes of knowledge easily available at one's fingertips! It would revolutionise the way people learn!

PROLE: It's true, I've seen and used it myself. It's quite the invention. Problem is one of those Flaming Fist Fuckfaces stole Ardryn's plans. Said he was a 'threat to the safety of the Gate' or summat stewpid like that.

 A DC 10 Insight check will reveal Prole is quite heated about the confiscation. The reality is that while most people assume this is merely some strange new critique of the city's Patriars, both the Guild and the Flaming Fist would dearly love to infiltrate the revolutionaries' project and make sure it doesn't pose a threat to the status quo.

ARDRYN: Say, the lot of you look like quite capable adventuriers and heroic types. For the price of two of my prototypes, would you be willing to retrieve it for us? It would help us quite a lot! The officer that took the schematics is a Dwarven Wizard by the name of [[Gardak Brighthorn]].

____
### PART II - Wyrm's Rock

*You approach Wyrm's Rock and behold the sight of the large structure in front of you. An assortment of Flaming Fist guards of different races line the large tunnel that goes through the length of the fortress.*

There are several ways players can locate Gardak. The simpliest way is to approach one of the Flaming Fist guards and ask them. Almost all of them will be 'happy' to help with locating the soldier, and will direct the party to ascend up the steps to the Commissary. The actual office is located on the second floor of [[Wyrm's Rock]] and appears as the following:

*Stepping into this room the first thing you notice that this is a haphazardly thrown together office space. A counter that belongs to a kitchen stands on the right hand corner. A makeshift bed formed from an old couch adorns one corner while an oversized desk and mismatched chairs mark Gardak's office.*

*A dwarf standing at around 4 and a half feet tall approaches from behind the desk. His hair is blonde and cut quite close, and he has a thick beard that extends past his neck. He wears a far less intricate and lighter Flaming Fist garb and carries a quarterstaff on his back.*

Speaking to Gardak will reveal he confiscated the schematics because [[Ardryn Deagle]] refuses to sell the plans to the [[Flaming Fist]]. He will agree to let go of the plans only if Ardryn returns to working on the object for [[Baldur's Gate]] and for profit.

____

### PART III - Possible Endings
### Good Ending
If the PCs help Prole and Ardryn acquire the blueprints 5x Mirror, Steel(5gp each) and 100 Gold worth of Unrefined Ioun Stones, they promise to reward the PCs four "Arcana-net" mirrors which among other things, allow the players to communicate up to 2 miles apart from each other.

### Bad Ending
The mage that is now in charge of the fortress on Wrym's Rock, Gardak Horn [NE M Shield Dwarf |mage|], is interested in learning what they might be working on. If he finds out what they are working on he offers the PCs 2x health potions and 100gp if they can steal a working prototype of the device. He wants it for himself so that he can sell it as a premium product to the Patriarchs in the Upper City. [reputation increase w/ anyone in magic guild].

**The Flaming Fist** - If you report this to any flaming soldier that isn't at Wrym's Rock they will bring you to their unit captain. The captain seeing this as a prime opportunity for an easy promotion offers the PCs 50gp and 2x Silvered Javelin's that he confiscated from some traders. The Flaming Fist sees these as a threat to security and moves in to shut them down. Prole and Ardryn can either work for The Flaming Fist and their Patriars handlers, whom they loath, or hand over all schematics and devices to The Flaming Fist. [reputation increase w/ flaming fist and Patriars]

### Neutral Ending
**The Guild** - If the Guild finds out about his project they will provide you with all necessary supplies and pay you 150gp to ensure he finishes it. If you can find and copy the gnomes schematics on his desk at his workshop they will pay you an additional 100gp. [reputation increase w/ the guild, thiefs and such respect you]
