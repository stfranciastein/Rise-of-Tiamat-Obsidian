### TRIGGER

This quest 'begins' after players visit their second shop in [[Berdusk]]. If no shops have been visited, they will skip the encounter with the creature and instead find out that a gruesome murder has happened again.

### PART I - Berdusk 

#### The Crime Scene
*A small crowd is gathered on the banks of the River Sulduskoon, and you make your way over there to investigate. It's hard to see much of anything, but there's a stench of death in the air.

*You push past the busy crowd and into the Before you is a gruesome scene, a human man is tied to a pole with what looks like melted steel cuffs. His eyes are gouged out, his mouth hangs agape forever frozen in terror. His stomach is cut wide open and his guts spill across the floor in what looks like a dried up puddle of blood. One of the Orc guards holds his hands up to you as soon as he spots you.*

GUARD 1: Stay back travellers.

When asked about how they were found, the Guard will tell the party that he found him on patrol this morning. He informed his superiors but some of the citizens stumbled across him like this, thus the crowd. The body is that of a human man named Orville Dengir

On the following day after encountering [[The Entity (Slaughter Demon)]] and while walking through the city streets, players will feel a sense of unease wash over them as they move through the streets. On a DC 8 Perception check, the adventurers will recognise the features of a man from afar. [[Jol]], a human farmer from [[Berdusk]].

*As you're walking down the street, a man accidentally brushes your shoulder. He apologises politely and flashes you a quick acknowledging smile. You keep your eyes trained on him and a sense of familiarity washes over you. You realise you've seen him before. At the shop when he ran away from you. Looking at the back of his head, there is no mistaking this is the creature from before.*

[[Jol]] is a human male farmer living outside of the city walls. Upon confrontation, he will claim that he has never been to that part of the city, but several shopkeeps dispute this. A detect thoughts or high insight check will reveal he's extremely afraid and confused.

#### Leads
The following leads can only be gained in [[Berdusk]].

- At the scene of the crime, the body of the victim will be cut in half with its guts pulled out.
- On a DC 10 Medicine check, the player(s) can learn that the wounds look like they were made with a slashing weapon. On a 15 or higher, they will learn it was made with a dagger.
- A human woman named [[Vittoria]] will be at the scene and say she believes these are connected to the Orcs retaking [[Berdusk]] somehow, and she believes they will only stop happening if the Orcs leave the city.
- On a DC 8 Persuasion check, the minotaur blacksmith [[Flint]] will claim that a month ago a female elf commissioned an especially sharp sword from him, and paid an extra 500 gold for it to be extradited.
- In the [[Patriarch's Rest]], [[Amalicia Red-Bone]] will claim that [[Avdul]] was a regular there and often drowned his sorrows in drink. She will also let them know he reported the murder of his wife to both the [[Harpers]] and the [[First Folk]] but neither could do anything. She will also say that he saw [[Avdul]]'s wife arguing with the victim a few months ago.
- The [[Harpers]] will claim that a red-skinned Tiefling man came into [[Twilight Hall]] several months ago, begging for justice for his murdered wife [[Serena]]. The [[Harpers]] claim that the man did not identify himself, and what little evidence they could find about his murdered lover suggested she was accidentally killed during the takeover of [[Berdusk]].
- [[Grimnir Blood Hallow]] will claim there was a complaint raised by a Tiefling by the name of [[Avdul]], but they had not heard from him since.
- [[Lucien]] will claim he does not particularly enjoy the scent of this killer, but refuses to let the party know more than that. He will agree to reveal the identity of the killer by saying "I will tell you, but only if you sell me your soul for eternity." This is presented seriously, but a 25 insight check reveals it is largely a joke offer. 
  
  This is a valid way to circumvent a good bit of the investigation. If a player does accept this offer, Lucien be taken aback and laugh, saying he did not expect them to actually agree. For better or for worse, he can't currently claim their soul because of the [[Cataclysm]]'s effects on them. Amused, he will instead ask for 1 gold piece. When asked, he says he hasn't made a transaction in a while and he felt compelled to do so. Upon payment, he will let the party know that their prey is a Demon that can swap bodies.
  
- [[Avdul]] lives in the slums of [[Berdusk]] in a broken down cottage. Upon discovering his abode, the party will be subject to a horrid stench. Inside, they discover his body as it hangs from the ceiling. A DC 10 Medicine check reveals that he has been dead for several weeks, if not months. Questioning his body reveals he thinks the Orcs are responsible for his wife [[Serena]]'s death.
- They will also learn that [[Avdul]] spoke to a female elf about his wife's murders and was interested in reading her journal.

#### Serana's Journal

	*This journal details the life of a Tiefling woman named Serena. It talks about her meeting her husband Avdul and how they met while working for a mercenary company known as the Red-Bloods.* 
	
	*In it, the most pertinent thing that stands out is how much she expresses her guilt for the last raid she participated in where they killed an entire Orc settlement. She details letting an elven woman and her children live and leaving the Red-Bloods.* 
	
	*In her last note, she talks about feeling like she was being watched. First there was a female elf following her, then a gnome girl, a dwarven male then a human man.* 

Flipping through the pages, it names several people.
- Orville Dengir (human male).
- Zavia Gren (human female).
- Alissa Telvir (drow female).
- Jenna Maris (high elf female).
- Orcish (Orc male).
- Thragg (Goliath female).
- Boron Gravedigger (Dwarf male).

The party can choose to get [[Jol]] imprisoned at any point, but the day after his imprisonment another murder will occur. [[Jol]] has no recollection whatsoever of any of the events of the murder. He will explain that the only difference is that he has been feeling a lot more tired lately and doesn't hang out with his friends as much as he used to.

### PART II - Elturel

The conclusion of all of these facts are:
- [[Serena]] was involved in a raid on a fishing village on the River Chionthar many years ago. Her adventuring party included the other two victims and three others who are now in [[Elturel]]. 
- The Demon is contract-bound to look for and kill every single member of the party that helped destroy that village, though its origins are unknown. It is physically incapable and unwilling to stop doing what it was told to do.
