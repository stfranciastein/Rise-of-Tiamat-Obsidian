### TRIGGER

This quest is triggered upon inquiring about Entharl Danthelon's missing shipments or by looking at the bounty board in the [[Elfsong Tavern]] or the [[Blushing Mermaid]]. Speaking to [[Lorgan Brandis]] or [[Sister Éabha]] will also lead to both directing the party to Entharl Danthelon.

____
### PART I - Introduction

*As you enter Danthelon's Dancing Axe a blonde-haired dwarf yells out from further inside. You can see his form from where you're standing. He stands at around 4ft 8 inches tall, has a middle-part in his blonde hair and a clean-shaven face revealing his thick neck. He wears what appears to be the clothes of a higher-end merchant and is expressing his frustrations rather dramatically.*

ENTHARL: Gods-damned shipments are still gods-damned missing! Dumathoin help me I- Oh, customers! Sorry about that. Eh, what can I do for you?

When pressed over what the problem seems to be, Entharl Danthelon will discuss his grief over his missing shipments. He will impress upon the players that it will likely be solved by the [[Flaming Fist]] but it may take days.

ENTHARL: My shipments from Amn have gone missing. First it was those blasted Rockseeker Dwarves disappearing on me and now this! I thought the Coast Way was supposed to be safe now that the Goliath is gone.

If the party wants to help him, he will direct them south of [[Baldur's Gate]] and down the Coast Way. He will speculate that bandits or the remainders of the [[Herd of the Dragon]] may be responsible but has no real idea of who the culprit may be.

ENTHARL: If you do this for me you'll have a permanent discount at my store! 10%! That's higher than what I'd give my brother. Oh, and of course a sum of 2000 Gold. My caravans were last seen just north of the Cloakwood.

### PART II - Old Friends

#### Familiar Ambush
*Riding south through the Coast Way your party finds itself passing through familiar territory. Just months ago you began your adventuring journey with the party and have been practically together ever since. As your journey brings you just past Willowvale and at the edges of the Cloakwood forest, you see a familiar sight in front of you.* 

*Further down the Coast Way, you come into an enclosed area. To the east is a natural wall of rock. To the west, a path leads deeper into the Cloak Wood. Your party senses something strange, then in an instant a wolf appears in front of you, seemingly not hostile. As you approach the wolf runs away further down the road. As you follow in its footsteps, you see a familiar face who seems absorbed in their investigation.*

Depending on the events of [[Act 1 - The Curse of Willowvale]], [[Tamae Tideon]] or [[Feldir Whitefyre]] will appear here investigating a nearby tree. The first thing the party will notice is that the trees near and around this area of the Cloakwood do not conform to the season in the rest of the world. The leaves all seem to thrum with colour. Either wizards will greet the party as soon as they get close enough to speak.

WIZ: Oh, long time no see. Come over here a second, will you?

*As the Wizard beckons you closer, you see an assortment of squirrels encircle them for a second before coming to a quick stop. As they look up at you, you notice that they have more eyes than a regular squirrel. When you stare too long they hiss before disappearing into a puff of pink and purple smoke. Your eyes catch a glint and you see them reappear atop the branches of the tree.*

WIZ: You saw that too right? Something is wrong with the Cloakwood. Well, again. It's not as serious as when Thaddak blighted the land, but there's something not right here.

On a successful DC 15 Arcana or Nature check, a player will be able to recognise the weakened barrier between the Feywild and the Material Plane. If this is brought up to Tamae or the character was present for [[Chapter 1.0 - The Woods]], they will note that this weakened state is different to how it felt before a few months ago.

WIZ: I sent word to Baldur's Gate to see if they could send a healer, but I suppose my favourite adventurers will do. You feel like you're up for a hunt?

##### Feldir Whitefyre

#### Know Thy Enemy
*As you find yourself in familiar territory, you don't have much time to catch up to any old faces as the situation seems quite urgent. You walk and talk as the Wizard explains the situation to you.*

WIZ: Ever since Mellara died the land has been healing, but Thaddak's magic left its mark on the Material Plane. It took me a while to figure out, but the veil between the Feywild and the Material Plane has been weakened. I've done what I can to strengthen it, but whatever jumped through is still here and causing havoc.

*The Wizard leads you towards a hut. Inside, there is an androgynous lizard-folk with bright red scales and an impressive plume of feathers emerging from their head. Looking at them now, they appear to be wearing the clothes of a Druid.*

WIZ: Shhh... [[Pyrax]], it's just me.

*The Wizard places another warm blanket on Pyrax's head as the Druid lies back down on the bed. The door closes behind you and Pyrax's hand moves to clutch their head.*

WIZ: [[Pyrax]] and I have been trying to hunt down an Owlbear. I told them to wait for me, but they got a little too cocky. When I found them, Pyrax was already like this. They said they attacked the creature with fire while it was sleeping but it didn't seem to hurt it. I mean it did, but it was like it barely scorched a feather or two. Once it stepped into the light, the feathers fell out and new ones appeared to take their place. 

*As they speak, the Wizard stands up and picks up a jar on one of the wooden shelves in Pyrax's hut. Inside the glass container, you can see what looks to be a pristine feather that glows with radiant light.*

WIZ: [[Pyrax]] was holding this when I found them. Probably from the Owlbear, but it doesn't look like any Owlbear Plume I've ever seen. It's likely its been warped by the Fey.

Clues can either be imparted by the Wizard directly or gathered by the party by examining [[Pyrax]] the fire druid. They can learn the following information:

***Sunlight Metabolism.*** successful DC 15 Medicine check on [[Pyrax]] reveals that: their eyes are milky white as they have been blinded. Their skin is also covered in horrific sunburns (radiant damage), and if they despite holding the feather in their hand when the Wizard found them, their feather seems undamaged and pristine. If the party attempts to experiment with it, they'll learn the feather is resistant to radiant damage and it rejuvenates when exposed to sunlight.

***Constellation.*** Pyrax's notebook is in their pack and has a drawing of a constellation is on the left hand side of the last page depicting five different coloured stars; white, black, red, blue, and yellow, arranged in a cross. The largest star—the gold one—sits at the centre of the constellation. The black one is always above it (to the north), the red below it (to the south), white is on the left (to the west), and the blue to the right (east). On the opposite page on the right is the following:

	`Thaddak's work combined with the alignent of the stars has weakened the veil between planes. The Owlbear has been warped with Fey magic. I can see that the constellations are still active in the sky even now. Yorith, the big yellow one in the middle moves west to east. Nagar is the black barely visible one above to the north. Sagin is the fierce red one to its south, Wiyfa—the shimmering white one—is on the west, and Bolubos is the pulsing blue one on the right there.`

***Fey Nature.*** Given the presence of the Redcaps and the Blink squirrels, the players can easily determine that the Sunbear is a Fey and not a Monstrosity. If they do not pick up on this, the Wizard will point this out before they leave.

THADDAK: Tenacious, aren't we? Very well, I'll entertain you for now. If you've come here seeking answers for the Owlbear, I'm afraid you're going to come up short. The veil is weakened, true, but nothing you find here will help you close it.

Thaddak

*The foreboding emptiness of the garden ahead is juxtaposed with the melodious birdsong, sweet blossoms, and soft breeze that decorates the air. ##### Approaching at night
*In the distance, in front of a small, moss-covered shrine, a mound of purple feathers lies, seemingly asleep. This gently snoring mass lies in the midst of a smorgasbord of half eaten carcasses and fish heads.*
##### Approaching in the day
*In the distance, in front of a small, moss-covered shrine, a mound of purple feathers basks, maw agape, in the day’s warm light. This unmoving mass sits in the midst of a smorgasbord of half eaten carcasses and fish heads.*

The party will then be confronted by the Suneater Owlbear which has three waves in total. The first begins with a single Adult Suneater Owlbear, 2 Sunsprites and 2 Faerie-o-Fires. The second wave is a puzzle that triggers when the Sunbear's regular form reaches 0 hit points. It creates a cocoon around itself as it begins its transformation process.

*A bright golden forcefield of energy blooms out from the owlbear, its motionless form a faint silhouette floating within the sphere. The air tingles with the scent of ozone as four glowing orbs coalesce around the now-calm garden. These smaller 1-foot-diameter orbs are each a different colour: one sparkles with a bright white light, a second swirls with fiery reds, a blue one pulses rhythmically, and the last is a dark black that drinks in the light of the others. They bob slowly, levitating in the air and swaying in the garden’s calm gusts.*

**_Solution._** These orbs can be physically pushed around by the player characters. When arranged around the owlbear in the same pattern as the constellation (black, north; blue, east; red, south; white, west), the owlbear’s protective cocoon disappears and wave 2 ends.

***Consequences.*** If the players take more than 1 hour to open the forcefield (for example, if they choose to short rest), the owlbear gains the benefits of a short rest, spending Hit Dice to regain hit points, and then emerges from its cocoon. Its mythic trait does not trigger. This means the player characters must fight the wave 1 owlbear again. If the owlbear is reduced to 0 hit points for a second time in this wave 1, its mythic trait triggers and wave 3 begins immediately.

**_The Forcefield & The Owlbear._** The forcefield lasts for 1 hour, during which the owlbear takes a short rest inside it. Nothing—not creatures, physical objects, energy, or spell effects—can pass through the barrier. While in the cocoon, the owlbear is [incapacitated](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#IncapacitatedCondition), can’t take lair actions, and has a speed of **0 feet**.

**_Optional Solution: Brute Force._** If the party is struggling to figure out the puzzle, you can allow them to destroy the forcefield. The forcefield has AC 10, hit points equal to fifty times the average party level, and **immunity** to fire, poison, psychic, and radiant damage. If it reaches 0 hit points, wave 2 ends.

**_Reflective Forcefield._** The forcefield retaliates whenever it is damaged. When the forcefield is hit by an attack or is damaged by a spell, the attacker or spellcaster takes radiant damage as a crackling arc of radiant energy strikes the source of the attack. This damage is equal to the amount used on the cocoon.

#### Supernova
*With a ripping sound, the tang of ozone fills the air and the cocoon breaks open. The owlbear rears back on its hind legs, its face top-lit in terrifying shadows cast by the 5-foot-radius star that floats above its head. Its featherfronds erupt in a retina-searing burst of light that settles into twinkling crackles of radiant energy.*

The owlbear emerges in its mythic form to defend its lair and activates its Supernova trait. In addition to the creatures it had in the first wave, it will also activate 2 shambling mounds. These mounds are luminescent and emit sunlight in a 20-foot radius.

When the Owlbear is defeated, it implodes into a 5-foot-diameter black hole. Each creature must succeed on a DC 16 Strength saving throw. On a failure, a creature takes 5d10 Force damage. Upon completion, it will leave behind its hind side which has been cut in half.

### CONCLUSION

Upon finishing this quest, the players will be invited to spend the night with the Wizard back at the tavern. Inside, they will be treated to a song telling the tale of their adventures sang by a travelling bard known as [[Cacafonix]].

	The story begins at the festival  
	Where stars are all symmetrical  
	But a monster is its mimicked face  
	And anger it does bring.
	
	Its nature is to steal the sun  
	And wish the night time not to come  
	Oh the wicked beast would fire its beam  
	And burn the skin of those who’d seen.
	
	Some brave adventurers would cross its path  
	And hold their swords aloft  
	And once defeated they would find  
	The monster rose a final time  
	Defiant till its end.
	
	Our travellers overcame the night  
	And brought the sunlight back to life  
	Our friends, we owe our world to you  
	You’re heroes through and through.

After the celebrations are over, [[Pyrax]] makes a full recovery and pulls one or more of the party aside in order to pass them one of their other findings. They will tell the party that they suspect that the [[Winter King]]'s activities in the Feywild may have forced more creatures to cross the barrier. They will then say the following:

PYRAX: I didn't tell (the Wizard) this because I didn't think to add more to their worries here, but I suspect that more events like this will occur as time goes on. The Winter King's ambitions will surely lead to further disruption across the Sword Coast. Be on the lookout for strange activity in nature, friends. May [Silvannus](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Silvanus) guide your path.

Upon returning to Entharl Danthelon in [[Baldur's Gate]], he will award the party with the agreed upon gold price and thank them for their service. After two days he will have a replenished inventory and will provide players a 10% discount on all purchases.
