### TRIGGER

This quest is triggered upon inquiring about a Giant in the vicinity of [[Elturel]] or hearing about it through [[Flint]] after the completion of [[Ill met by Moonlight]].

____
### PART I -  A strange circumstance

*Your ship crosses the Chionthar and makes its way to the south side of Elturel. You inquire a few guards as to the location of Lerwickchester and are able to ride south after an hour or so of travel. When you arrive, you come upon a sleepy hamlet in the plains, shaded beneath what looks like a large and sturdy tree. As you get closer, a Gnome approaches your party.*

ROVA: Oh greetings adventurers! Are you here to deal with the Giant?

This is Elder [[Rova]], a Gnome merchant who leads the town of [[Lerwickchester]]. He proports that the reason why the local Hill Giant [[Lythe]] is terrorizing the town is because she has grown bored of their accomodations and wishes for the town to be slaughtered. He, along with the majority of the town including [[Myualxeanshtik Zenzys]], the local Dragonborn Druid and [[Kancinshtuuk Havyre]], the town Huntsman and her husband.

_____
### PART II - Dark Forces

*As your party makes its way to the camp you find yourself looking at a familiar sight. Nestled in the hills is a camp flying the banners of the Cult of the Dragon. The walls are fortified with wood taken from the nearby forests, and they've made little to no effort to conceal their forces.* 

LYTHE: Big evils, they smell bad!

*The leadership here is unclear, there are recruits that walk to and from the camp but they don't seem to be reporting to anyone in particular. You search the grounds for anything that would tell you if they have a dragon here but nothing stands out.*

![[05xjhatjuvnb1.jpg]]GRID TILES: 50x60

This camp holds 30 [[Cult of the Dragon]] members, though the majority of them are Kobolds and Goblins. The camp's leader is one of [[Thaddak Ice-Veins]]'s Dragonclaws, a Blue Tiefling named [[Ozereus]]. He largely remains in the captain's tent in the centre of the camp and will only inspect what's happening should any noise happen in the camp.

*A blue-skinned Tiefling exits the captain's tent and smiles as he sees your party. He stands at about 6 feet tall, with short blue-black hair and a goatee which adds to his fiendish appearance. On his back are two scimitars, both of which gleam with silvery light. His blue coat is lined with what looks like Hunters furs. His gloved hand is wrapped with what looks like a white mark.*

OZERIUS: Bravo, brava. The Wyrmspeaker was not wrong about you. A town in peril and here you are coming to rescue them. With the aid of a Giant no less!

*The Tiefling puts his foot down on a stool and leans forward, smiling as he flicks his tail to reach for what looks like a small cutting knife. He retrieves an apple from the inside of coat, skinning it deftly with his tail before taking a bite.*

OZERIUS: Allow me to introduce myself, Ozerius the Swift. I work for our mutual bugbear friend. He told me that you would be likely to come pay my little colleccion a visit. I've been itching to see the upcoming heroes for myself. Langdendrosa gets all of the fun.

*His golden eyes glint with mischief as he takes in his surroundings. As his gaze meets yours, he looks off into the distance, past your party and into the trees as the wind begins to pick up. His smile widens a little and he lets out a small, playful laugh. *

OZERIUS: Before we begin, I do really wish to congratulate you on getting this far on your journeys. It couldn't have been easy dealing with us for so long. We are a tenacious bunch, but that is something we have in common, yes? 

*He finishes eating his apple and tosses the core to the ground, dusting off his hands in mockery.*

OZERIUS: Unfortunately, Thaddak would be quite displeased with me if I were to partake in a little fun. But don't be disappointed. There is someone here who would love to have a dance - a mutual friend of ours - he would really like to re-introduce himself. 

*He claps, and the remaining cultists all begin to cover in fear as the ground shakes and the walls begin to creak. You feel the wind shift close to your party as a large invisible presence inches closer. You hear its heavy breathing and your eyes start to sting as an acrid aroma fills your lungs.*

OZEREUS: Ladies and Gentlemen, boys, girls, kobolds and everything in between. If I could please have your attention! Making his debut performance in the second act. The Silent Stalker, Bane of Shade Rest, King of the Cloakwood. Enter the magnificent; Venomfaaaang!!!!

At the end of the fight, players can attempt to search Ozerius's quarters. They will find one Potion of Lightning resistance as well as a Potion of superior healing. There will also be a letter addressed to him written in Sylvan. It is sealed with white wax marked with a Dragon's head.

	Ozereus
	
	Continue your work in Lerwickchester and support Langdedrosa where you can.
	After the events of Willowvale I'll need a new source of power.
	Arkhan will take care of things up North.

	Wyrmspeaker Thaddak the White

Returning to Lerwickchester will reveal that their crops have also started failing, which is why Elder [[Rova]] and the Druid [[Myualxeanshtik Zenzys]] have been adamant in ending the deal. If [[Lythe]] is alive, they can convince the villagers that they will need her help in either defending the town or relocating. Regardless of how her situation is resolved, Lythe will be able to provide the players a vial of her blood, either by force or of her own choice to remember her by.
