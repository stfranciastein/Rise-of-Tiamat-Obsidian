*The front cover of this book is worn and soiled. Opening it reveals quite the opposite, however, as each page contained within is pristine and well-preserved.*

This side quest can be triggered at any point, but likely during [[Chapter 2.0 - Tale of Two Cities]] after [[Glass]] and optionally [[Grace]] who will reunite with the party accompanied by [[Mr. Greenie]].

The three will say there's a druid that came with them from the Feywild who went up north to speak to a hag. That was 5 days ago and she hasn't come back since. Following this trail will lead them to [[Marianne]]'s hut where she is holding [[Rynae Kiln]] hostage. At this point, she will be fed up with trying to keep the party at bay and toss them into a pocket of the Feywild via the [[Grim Tales for Bratty Children]] Book.

### PART I - Little Red Riding Hood

*Either [[Ulena Kiln]] or the one who read the book will wake up in the middle of the woods as she hears a voice speaking clear as day in her ear. The voice is male, young enough sounding and speaks as if its reading a tale to a child.*

THE VOICE: Once upon a time, in a land far, far away in a place called the Reaching Woods, there was a sweet little Red-skinned Earth Genasi woman loved by everyone who met her, but especially her mother. The woman loved her so much that she made her a thick riding cloak with a hood inlaid with leaves and vines. It suited her perfectly, and from that day on, everyone called her Little Red Riding Hood.

*You look around your surroundings and find yourself in the middle of the dark forest once more. As you get up from the ground, you realise you are in fact wearing a riding hood. But as you look around you realise that your companions are gone. Your weapons and armour are gone, and on the floor right next to you is a basket. You peak inside of the wicker container, the scent of cinnamon and sugar hits your nose as you see a slice of freshly baked cake inside, and a bottle of wine.*

*As you rise up from the ground, you hear footsteps as a man approaches you from behind. He stands at about 6ft 5 inches tall, with a thick mane of brown hair brushed back with a thick beard to match. His hands are on his bow which looks old and worn. He's wearing simple hunter's clothes with a button-up shirt reveals just a little bit of chest hair. His golden eyes glisten in the light and his toothy grin is wide but friendly.*

THE MAN: Good morning, Little Red. Where are you headed off to then?

THE VOICE: As Little Red stood there confused as always with that look on her face, she pondered her choices. Does she turn tail and run like a coward, or does she go deeper into the woods to give her grandmother the food and booze she desperately needs like her mother asked her to? Choices, choices.

*The man doesn't seem to hear this voice as he puts away his bow.*

THE MAN: Off to see the Grandmother, is it? Say, where does she live again? I was supposed to deliver this to her but haven't had the chance to visit. (He shows you two dead rabbits which have been skinned, ready to cook).

[[Ulena Kiln]] or the adventurer can either tell him or keep it to herself. The man will cheerfully step away either way, not caring if Ulena is disrespectful.

*As you venture deeper into the woods, you hear the sounds of howling and rustling in the vicinity. You know this tale all too well, and as you approach the end of the path you see a little hut in the end of the trail. Beneath three oak trees surrounded by hazel bushes.*

THE VOICE: Little Red could see Grandmother's house up ahead, but she was so dumb that she forgot to bring her Grandmother flowers! Kids these days, always forgetting to be respectful to their elders. So like the forgetful little miss that she was, she went to go pick some up from nearby and hoped her Grandmother wouldn't notice.

*When you finally approach the house, you find the door to Grandmother's house already opened. Inside, you see a gory scene before you as the body of your Grandmother torn to shreds atop her bed. The man is straddling her corpse, blood foaming in his mouth as he stares back at you with a glint in his eyes as he stares you down.*

THE MAN: My my, what a delicious feast I have. Two meals and some desert.

THE VOICE: Because of Little Red's stupidity, the big bad wolf killed and ate her. That is the moral of the story.

A fight ensues between Little Red and an Enchanted Werewolf. At the end of the fight, Little Red will pass out once more as the voice sings in their ear.

THE VOICE: Now now Little Red, you're not supposed to kill the wolf. You were supposed to wait for the hunter to come rescue you. Let's try someone else.
