### TRIGGER

This quest is given by [[Flint]] at the [[Silver Sword]]. It can also be 'accepted' by the players and investigated without consulting him through looking at the bounty boards in [[Berdusk]].

____
### PART 1 - It came in the night

*You approach the village and immediately take note of their wooden walls. A pair of watchtowers stand tall next to a thick wooden gate reinforced by metal braces. Atop each watchtower stands a Goblin archer, and though both spot you neither seem to have drawn their weapons just yet.*

GOB 1: Right, who's there? Listen 'ere, we've not been touching nobody yeah? So if yiz have come from the city tell them we've been keeping to ourselves.

The Goblins will impress upon the players that they simply hunt in the woods for food. If asked, they will claim they have no connection to the [[Cult of the Dragon]] and are simply trying to live off the land. Though they will tell the adventurers that they may not have long left, given the recent attacks coming from the mountains.

GOB 2: It started about a year ago. These creatures come down from the mountain and started snatching up our livestock. We sent some hunters to try and check, but they never came back. It didn't happen often maybe once or twice a month, but lately its become more common.

GOB 1: If yiz want to help, head inside and speak to our champion, Jaflin.

*The inner walls of the Goblin camp is dreary and grim. What looked to be a once thriving settlement seems to have been drained of vitality. Their crops grow steadily just fine, but their livestock look tense and afraid as if waiting for their turn on the chopping block. On the far side of the camp, next to a path that leads up the mountain stands a large tent. Crude tribal decorations mark the other tents, but this one is far more civilised in appearance.*

*Inside the tent, the interior of the tent matches the civility of the outside, and for a moment you could almost mistake it for a merchant's tent. A bed of decent make sits on the far left, a kitchenette on the far right. In the center you see him before he spots you. A large, yellow-skinned Hobgoblin wearing half-plate with no sleeves sits by a campfire. His left sleeve is completely gone, exposing his scarred bicep. An intricate tattoo crawls from the top of his shoulder and disappears into his gauntlet. What draws your attention the most, however, is the Belt he wears over his armour. There is no mistaking it, this is the Belt that Flint was looking for.*

The players will meet [[Jaflin Stormcrown]]. This is indeed the former leader of the [[Herd of the Dragon]] but was replaced when [[Mellara, the Terror of the Sword Coast]] left him for dead and assumed his position as the Herd's leader. Since his betrayal, he has been in hiding at the Sunset Mountains to live out the rest of his days in peace. If [[Stone]] is present, he will sympathise with [[Jaflin Stormcrown]]'s position and tell the party that they must do all they can to help this village.

The players can partake in some conversation with the local Goblins. They will discover that when the [[Cult of the Dragon]] tried to recruit them, they hid [[Jaflin Stormcrown]] from them until the Cult attempted a hostile takeover. The Hobgoblin defended his fellow villagers and strung up their bodies in front of the camp as a warning the next day.

If the players agree to help, [[Jaflin Stormcrown]] will tell the players they can either leave during the day or at night. At night, the creatures are out in full force. In the daytime, the creatures are in hiding and may be harder to spot but are weaker.

### PART 2 - Temple of the Moon Maiden
#### N3. Solitary Tomb
*Standing in this room, you can see the roof has caved in. A single solitary tomb stands in the middle. Its surface is worn away and dusted, as is the plaque.*

On a DC 10 Investigation check, the party will notice something glinting inside of the tomb. Opening the tomb requires a successful DC 15 Athletics check. Upon opening it, they find a set of Moonswaddled Padded armour.

#### N4. Old Library
*Rotted and ruined books line the shelves of this library. The smell of dust and death is heavy in the air, as skeletons of several robed bodies litter the floor. Perusing through the tables, you get the idea that this was both a reading room as well as a place where scribes kept records of their day-to-day activities.*

On a DC 15 Investigation check, they will find a preserved page of a journal as well as a +1 Dagger. Reading it reveals the following text:

	`30th of ???? 1489`
	
	`Sharrans have been spotted in Berdusk, it's only a matter of time before they learn of the monestary. We cannot expect nor ask the people of the Vale to hide our location. In the event that I fall, may whoever find this make right what was done to our people.` 
	
	`Kaelor has asked us to run, but I will not leave. Tovan has agreed to run with the young ones should the Sharrans come. They will make for the other Temple in ????.`
	
	`Moon Maiden guide us.`

Another note says:

	`I don't know where Tristan found that book, but I don't like it. The thing creeps me out. I hid it behind some of Alexia's used smut novels so hopefully nobody finds it.`

In the old library, there will be a heavy book marked with what looks like arcane symbols behind a rotted smut fiction called 'The Minotaur's Prize'. The book is quite heavy and appears to have been made with old leather. A red book ribbon is nestled right in the middle of the tome which appears to be mostly well-preserved. If the characters decide to pick it up, they will gain an Insatiable Tome.

#### N5. Catacombs
*This room smells of old bones and rot. Skeletal remains of different shapes and sizes litter the floor of this cavern, clothed with moth hole-ridden monks robes. Rusted blades pierce some bodies, while others seem to have had their bones crushed by a strong bludgeoning force.*

Should the party walk into this room and fail a DC 10 Stealth check, they will be ambushed by 6 skeletons. 

##### Fallen Monk
*Pressed up against a stone table are the remains of what looks to be one of the Monks of Selune. His robes have worn off with time, and his once trusty quarterstaff decorated with arcane sigils seems to have decayed.*

On a DC 15 Investigation check, they can determine that the body is wearing a magical cloak. Regardless, they will find it is holding a cog with the symbol of a full moon.

#### N6. Mess Hall
*This great hall opens up into what appears to be a large mess hall. Two tables flank an extinguished firepit. As soon as you enter, standing before you are three monks dressed in dark robes.*

If any of the party members are Selunites, the monks will immediately taunt them. They are also accompanied by a Vampire and 2 Shadow Mastiffs. At the end of the fight, they will notice that the Monks seem to be tattooed. On a DC 10 Religion check, they can determine they are worshippers of Shar.

#### N7. Sleeping Quarters
*The hallway is dimly lit, and what little light that comes from the small window does nothing to improve your visibility. Before you there are four doors on the left, and a larger door at the end of the hallway.*

There are five Sharran monks in this hallway hiding up in the ceiling. Characters with a Passive Perception of 15 or higher will notice movement from above. If not, they will get a surprise attack on the party. 

At the end of the fight, the players can explore each room. The third one from the bottom seems to be mostly intact. In the north corner of the room there is a chest, which is actually a mimic. Killing it will reveal it has swallowed 67 GP, 67 SP and 67 CP.

#### N8. Larder
*A cold draft filters through this room. Barrels of rotten food and drink line the walls of this larder. On the far left corner rests what appears to be a cupboard with a hidden door, but the tunnel behind it has collapsed entirely.*

#### N9. Gate to the Grand Chamber
*This golden framed wooden gate is lined with silver filigree and stands tall and gleaming despite the state of the Temple. A shining cog rests on the left door, with the symbol of a new moon engraved on it. The right door has a divot that appears to be missing something.*

On a DC 10 Investigation check, players can determine that the gate can be manipulated into opening a different way but it would be extremely difficult. On a DC 25 tool check with Thieves Tools, players can successfully bypass the mechanism.

#### N10. Grand Chamber
*Gleaming tiles of different colours fill the floor of this forgotten chamber. A set of wooden pews look up towards a beautiful stained glass window depicting the Moon Maiden herself in battle with her dark sister Shar. Panes are missing, but the grandeur remains.*

*As you walk into this room, you see a figure standing in front of what looks like an altar. His shock of white hair flows down to his back, and his beautiful noble's clothes gives him a regal air. He turns as you enter and flashes you a toothy grin.*

FLORIAN: I didn't think anyone would ever come here, you know. Other than the Goblins, I suppose. (If [[Jaflin Stormcrown]] is present). It looks like you've brought me quite the delectable snack!

FLORIAN: Allow me to introduce myself, my name is Florian Montebank and I am so very pleased to meet your acquaintance. Of course, now that you've entered by lair and killed quite a lot of my guests, our relationship is quite soured.

*A young man wearing similar clothing steps up to stand next to him. He appears to be young, human and quite terrified. He holds what appears to be a goblet full of wine in his hand. As you look to Florian's left and right there are two monks standing next to him. Before you can act, two more vampiric creatures drop down from the ceiling and bare their fangs.*

FLORIAN: You understand, don't you? It's nothing personal.

At the end of the fight, Florian will turn into dust and his remains will fly out of the window. On a DC 10 Arcana or History check, players will learn that Florian will have more than likely returned to his place of rest and will reconstitute there within the hour.

On the altar is a collection of four gems, with each gem is worth 200 Gold. On a DC 15 Investigation check, they will discover that there is a false panel on the floor near the pews and will find the Moonlight Bolter.

### CONCLUSION

Upon returning to [[Flint]] he will be curious to hear the tale of how they managed to retrieve the item. He will then reward the players 750 GP for completing the quest and return the [[Belt of the Giant]] to the party in one day.
