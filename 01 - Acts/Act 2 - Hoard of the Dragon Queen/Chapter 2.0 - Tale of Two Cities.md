### **2.1 - Berdusk, the City of Pariahs

After the events of [[Chapter 1.0 - The Great Hunt]], the characters emerge from the Dragon Hatchery with little direction. If they should choose, they may be able to catch up to the runaway Cultist. Otherwise, they exit the back entrance safely.

#### Infernal Curse
*You emerge from the Dragon Hatchery and breathe in freedom. But just before you can celebrate you see white light flash across the sky in the distance, and the clouds above you begin to darken. Your heart sinks as the sound of thunder strikes in the distance. Above you, the wind is displaced violently as Lennithon's massive form flies overhead, thankfully not noticing your presence. You wait a few seconds before you hear a massive thud, signaling Lennithon's arrival in the valley you left.*

GLASS (as a message): Big dragon is here friends. I can't stay here. Meet us somewhere?

Regardless of what they send back, at this point they will find themselves completely separated from [[Glass]] and [[Grace]] who will fail to retreat with the [[Sylvira-Van]] safely. [[Lucien]] will try to intervene, but thankfully they are rescued by a Dao named '[[Mr. Greenie]]', who will whisk them away to the Feywild before [[Lucien]] can manipulate [[Glass]] into accepting help from him. If the party chooses to look for them, they will only catch a whiff of [[Lucien]]'s attempted intervention and will not suspect anyone else but him.

If [[Leosin Erlanthar]] is alive and present, he will assert that he, the prisoners and the party east towards the city of [[Berdusk]] to meet with the [[Harpers]] in [[Twilight Hall]]. From the Hatchery, it will take the party 3 to 4 days to travel to [[Berdusk]]. 

*Leosin wipes his brow and looks at the party, then back at the prisoners. He looks off into the distance and takes out what looks to be an Orb of Direction and begins stepping towards the East before stopping 5 seconds later.*

LEOSIN: The closest city is [[Elturel]], but the [[Cult of the Dragon]] will be looking for us. While I don't doubt that the [[Hellriders]] are competent, the [[Harpers]] are stationed in [[Berdusk]] at [[Twilight Hall]]. Whatever happened to you inside that cave, perhaps the High Harper could be of help. She is a powerful druid, after all.

On their first long rest, the effects of the [[Cataclysm]] will begin to show, as their hands grow itchier and itchier. At the end of their second long rest, black scales will have begun growing out of one of their hands, marking the beginning of their transformation into Abishai.

*As you try to lay down and rest for the night, a cold uneasy feeling makes you pause. As you toss and turn in your sleep, you find yourself getting itchier and itchier. You scratch and scratch all you want, but the itchiness doesn't seem to stop. When you close your eyes for long enough, that's when you see it. Visions of death, of blood and bloodied black scaled claws grasping and slashing and tearing at throats. Teeth gnashing at innocent children and drinking the life out of their fragile necks.*

*But you are not being chased. You are the danger. It is your claws, your teeth. As your dreams are haunted by these horrid images you find it unbearable and you sit up from your position and get up. When you wake up the next morning, however, you realise that the dream wasn't so farfetched after all. For now, where your hand used to be is a darkened scaley hand with claws to match.*

During the same night, on a DC 15 Perception check, the member responsible for keeping watch will hear a strange noise in the distance. On closer inspection, they will discover that this is a group of Orcs deployed from [[Berdusk]] known as the [[Free Riders]]. The leader of the Riders is an Orc named [[Grimnir Blood Hallow]], who says by the decree of [[First Folk]] has been sent to watch out for the [[Cult of the Dragon]] and suspects the party is with them and arrest them for questioning.

*You awaken to the sound of what you think are stones being disturbed in the grass and you shoot up. As your eyes re-adjust and try to make sense of the darkness around you, the sound comes back again. It's the sound of footsteps from what you can tell, and whoever or whatever it is has begun to surround your camp.*

*Before you can stand up, however, a wailing whistling sound interrupts your thoughts. An object flies past your head, inches away from a fatal blow. When you turn around to look behind you, there's a javelin the ground. You look towards where it came from and you see a dozen or so Orcs riding atop strong horses. Each Orc seems to be wearing a mix of well-crafted looking half-plate and furs to keep them warm. Their helmets cover the majority of their heads, giving just enough room to show off their toothy scowls.*

LEOSIN: What is the meaning of this? Who are you?
 
GRIMNIR: You stand before Grimnir of the Blood Hallow tribe. I am the Commander of the Free Riders, the cavalry of New [[Berdusk]].

If [[Leosin Erlanthar]] is present, he will tell the Riders that he is a member of the [[Harpers]] and wishes for an escort to [[Twilight Hall]] as soon as possible. [[Grimnir Blood Hallow]] will begrudgingly take the Half-Elf and the party back to [[Berdusk]] unspoiled but will leave their company once they get within the vicinity to return to his duties. Otherwise, [[Grimnir Blood Hallow]] will take them straight to the Pens, [[Berdusk]]'s jail.

#### Arrival in Berdusk
*At the third hour, Leosin sprints ahead of your party up a particularly steep hill. Try as you might to catch up, you cannot move as fast as the half-elf monk as he steps like the wind. When you finally do, your hands rest on your knees for a brief second as you crest the top of the hill. It's been a long and ardrous journey, and this... predicament your in has only made matters worse.*

*When you look up, you can finally see the city in front of you. In the horizon, the massive sprawling city of Berdusk is in plain sight now. The Sunset Mountains behind it in the far, far distance giving you a picturesque view of the Jewel of the Vale.*

*As you get closer you can see its massive stone walls now. The stone-faced guards on the carved grey battlements are sheltered from the sun by wooden roofs that almost look tribal in appearance. Flags and banners of what looks like different clans decorate the stone surfaces, while furs and tokens of various creatures are scattered about.*

In [[Berdusk]], the party will notice that the vast majority of people here are Orcs, Goliaths and Goblinoids. Despite its history and its name, it seems the city has been reclaimed by its previous occupants. Some of the human citizens despise their Orc neighbors and there is constant tension in the air. 

*When you follow Leosin down a wide road, you notice a Human man carrying a crate of bottles accidentally bump into a Goblin woman. The man's face immediately contorts to that of anger as the bottles shatter on the ground spilling their contents everywhere and he begins to curse and yell at the Goblin who is profusely trying to apologise.*

MAN: YOU SHOULD'VE WATCHED WHERE YOU WERE GOING CREATURE.

GOBLIN: I-I-I'm sorry ser, it won't 'appen again!

*A Goliath man clad in similar armour to the Free Riders pushes past the onlookers, trying to defuse the situation. The man screams in anger and demands retribution, calling the Goblin different names. The Goliath keeps calm the entire time, but as the Human gets more heated and heated, something shifts in the air. The onlookers scowl in disdain, and before the Human can utter another word in hatred a Goliath picks him up and impales him on a jagged fence nearby. The Orc mutters the phrase 'Berdusk will never appease your kind ever again' and watches him bleed out.

#### The Harpers of Berdusk
[[Leosin Erlanthar]] will leave the party to their own devices, asking them to meet him in [[Twilight Hall]] later that evening. When the party concludes their business, their first point of contact in [[Twilight Hall]] is another Orc named [[Arif Wolf-Blood]].

*You walk towards the Inner Chamber District and find yourself standing in front of Twilight Hall. This building stands out from the others as it seems Elven in architecture. Where the other structures in Berdusk are characterised by their sharp, pointed angles, Twilight Hall is rounded and elegant without being ostentatious.

*The outer courtyard is highlighted by two large stone posts. The columns seems to be decorated in painted artwork of an exagerrated starry night. The tops of the posts have a small metallic tube sticking out from the stone with blue candlelight burning atop them. A large white and blue banner with the symbol of a harp hangs just above the large rounded doors.*

*When you step inside the building, the inner foyer is something to behold. The floor is made up of finely polished granite, and the walls are a mixture of wood and stonework. A massive sprawling blue carpet leads your eyes towards another gorgeous set of double doors at the end of the hallway, and the three pairs of rounded stone pillars reaching up to touch the ceiling.*

*Maps of Faerun and paintings commisoned from various artists hang on the walls leading up to what you assume is the audience chamber. As you stand there admiring the view, an Orc sees your group enter and begins to walk towards you. His Orcish features are rugged, but softened to by what looks like a touch of Human ancestry. He appears to be wearing a white shirt and black pants, with a heavy Calamshite sash wrapped around his waist which is secured by a brown belt.*

There is something familiar about this Orc, and on a DC 8 History check the adventurers will notice an uncanny resemblance to someone they've met before. [[Arif Wolf-Blood]] is, of course, [[Batuu Wolf-Blood]]'s paternal twin. Unlike his sister, however, Arif has joined the [[Harpers]]. Initially he did so in hopes of finding and redeeming his sister from the path she went on, but in the wake of her death he stayed to help put a stop to the [[Cult of the Dragon]]. 

ARIF: She might be gone, but the Cult of the Dragon still remains. I will help in the fight as much as I can. The High Harper has told us this may be a long battle, but I'm ready to do what it takes.

The players will be able to call on Arif for help while they're in [[Berdusk]] and potentially recruit him.
Later, High Harper [[Jaheria]] will meet with the party after having a debrief with [[Leosin Erlanthar]]. She will inform the adventurers that their time to act on their transformation is short, for soon they will permanently lose their body and souls to whatever Devil the [[Cult of the Dragon]] has promised them to. She will offer to use her druidic magic to delay the progression of the curse which prevents it from spreading past their hands. [[Jaheria]] gives the party estimates that the party's transformation will resume in 6 months, after which she will need to recast the spell to stop its spread. She states that in order to end it for good they will need to kill or convince the source of the curse to stop it. [[Jaheria]] will then tell the players to get some rest and to meet with her in the morning. 

On their first night in Berdusk, the party will have a collective dream of Rezmir's face as she and her forces completely obliterate the caravan city of [[Scornubel]] like they did with [[Greenest]]. However, unlike with Greenest there are no defenders strong enough to stop them and the city is flattened by the Cult's Dragons. In the morning, the itching from their curse will have stopped and the scales will have disappeared, for now.

Upon meeting with [[Leosin Erlanthar]] again, he tells the party that there has been talks among the [[Harpers]] and [[Emerald Enclave]] of a [[Council of Waterdeep]] forming, though it is currently in its infancy. After informing the Harper of their dream, Leosin strongly advises them to seek out [[Onthar Frume]] in [[Elturel]] to inform him about the Council and to mobilise his forces. He suggests journeying there via a boat, but the party is free to go back on the road on horses.

### **2.2 - Murders in Berdusk

While walking around [[Berdusk]] and shopping for supplies, the party will run into a peculiar event. Upon walking into any store for the first time, they will encounter a human man talking to the shop's proprietor facing away from them. As soon as they walk in, the man will refuse to turn around and will instead walk forward without turning around. The shopkeeper will ignore this and instead greet the travellers as they enter. On investigation, he will run away. In the event that the party catches up to him, the following happens:

*As you approach the man from behind, he does not move, nor does he even twitch. For a split second you could swear he isn't even breathing. A cold, creeping dread washes over your body as you get close enough to touch him, and every fibre of your body is telling you that you should stop.*

*You try to turn the man but he doesn't want to budge. As you step forward to confront him, you feel shock and horror as you realise he has no face. Instead, all you find is a gaping hole that reveals the inside of his skull. You see his lower jaw still remains, and his throat gargles at you in a gutteral growl. As you prepare yourself for a confrontation, the man's body contorts and twists as his arms and legs elongate unnaturally. Before you could have a chance to react, he jumps to the wall and crawls off into the distance.*

The details of this mission are outlined in [[Demonic Vengeance]].

[[Flint]] the Blacksmith will have a job posting up on the board of [[Patriarch's Rest]] and the [[Flourished Flagon]] taverns about a job. When asked, he will say he's looking for someone to retrieve the Belt of Giant's Strength for him, with the promise of 750 Gold for those who can retrieve it. He will not be open for any bargains either way, and will only pay up once the belt is on him. 

He will direct the party to the Sunset Mountains. According to him, there is a Hobgoblin Champion in a village at the foot of the mountains who is currently in possession of it. It is a three day's walk to the foot of the mountains. Once the party arrives, they will begin the [[Il met by Moonlight]] side quest.

### 2.4 Trouble on the Road

Regardless of whether or not the adventurers take the River Chionthar or the Trade Way, they will be accosted by Dragon Cultists consisting of a warrior, a cleric, a ranger and a spellcaster. On their body, there will be orders from [[Langdedrosa Cyanwrath]], stating the following: 

	`"Orders from the Wyrmspeaker herself. Lock down the Wilderness near Elturgard, but don't wander too far north. Zariel's cultists are abound and the last thing we need is we raise too much noise that the Hellriders start marching out of their Holy City. Pillage any gold you can manage and head to Baldur's Gate and head for the Zhent hideout."`

If they're aboard the Tidecutter, Captain [[Viz]] Saltbeard will tell the travellers that having them on board is far too much trouble and force them to disembark just before [[Scornubel]], either forcibly pushing them into the river below or telling them to jump. As they wash up on the riverbank, thankfully, the party will run into [[Glass]] and [[Grace]] in the Reaching Woods. 

[[Grace]] is accompanied by [[Mr. Greenie]] who will tell them that [[Lucien]] tried to seduce the two into a deal but he was there to intervene. His magic however, is weakening and [[Grace]] insisted on being brought back to Faerun so you could take her somewhere to learn magic. 

If asked, [[Mr. Greenie]] will tell the party that the Feywild is unstable and could either turn minutes into days or days into minutes, and on the rare occasions days into years. He will also tell them that a druid is nearby who is dealing with a familiar entity that the party may be interested in. The druid is [[Rynae Kiln]], and the entity is [[Marianne]]. If the party chooses to engage in this side adventure, it will trigger the quest [[Grim Tales for Bratty Children]].

Upon completion of this encounter, the party can continue on to [[Elturel]]. On the road, they will run into a band of 3 travelling knights on horseback fighting a group of six Dragon Cultists. [[Reya Mantlemorn]] leads the group and will ask the travellers for help. At the end of the fight, she will dismount and greet the party.

*As the woman walks towards your party, she takes off her helmet to reveal her face. She appears to be a young human woman, possibly in her early to mid 20s. Her olive skin glistens with sweat and her hazel eyes light up with the light of the sun. Her curly auburn hair is tied into a bun, and an impressive horizontal scar decorates the bridge of her nose.*

REYA: Thanks for the help travellers, though I wish you could've arrived sooner. The name's [[Reya Mantlemorn]], Hellrider of [[Elturel]]. This here is Tilses and Ikaron.

*She points towards two red-skinned tieflings wearing the same half-plate as her. Both have the symbol of Elturel emblazoned on them, a large circle representing the sun and a smaller sun blazing with fire on the top left. Both look towards your party with their devilish eyes and give you a respectful nod as they clean up the bodies and put their men to rest.*

REYA: That caravan is pretty impressive. I can feel the magic from here. Not many people in [[Elturel]] mess with Arcane magic apart from the women up in the [[Silver Fire Academy]]. 

REYA: You must be on the way to [[Elturel]], yes? The journey should be clear from here, we've been trying to keep these Cultists out of Elturgard. Guess they thought they could mess with the Hellriders.

If the party mentions the [[Cult of Zariel]], she will find it difficult to believe that [[Zariel]] is anything but what the people of Elturel know her as; a hero and a fallen angel. [[Lioran]] can tell the party this beforehand as he is a native and would know.

At the end of this conversation, at the behest of either [[Cormyc Ravenshade]] or [[Tarbaw Nighthill]], the party can ask [[Reya Mantlemorn]] for a way into the city if one of their party members is an undead. Reya will judge the person wanting to enter the city, and if she deems them honourable enough she will give them a [[Hellrider's Pin]], enchanted to provide them immunity from the Companion's radiant damage.

If asked about why she's so casual to give this away, she'll say if the party member is lying to her the citizens of [[Elturel]] and the Order of the Companion will bring justice down upon them. She will bid the party farewell and ride off with the rest of the Hellriders, allowing the party to progress to the city.

### 2.5 Elturel, the Holy City

#### Arrival in Elturel
*You approach the Holy City of Elturel. The High Observer's keep dominates the view, situated atop a cliff that looms over the River Chionthar. Near it, there is a blazing orb that hovers over the city like a second sun. The closer you get, the less sure you are of what time of day it even looked like before you approached as the light of the Companion washes over you.*

*The white walls of the city do nothing to abate the brightness, if nothing else it simply enhances it. For every corner the Companion's light cannot see, the city's architecture helps it reach those forgotten spaces. The watchtowers are supported by surely unnecessary but dramatic flying buttresses. From here you can see large structures that reach up to the sky with windows larger than any you've ever seen making good use of the light. It's not a sterile brightness, but a comforting one. For you know while you are under the light of the Companion, evil shall not touch you here.*

*As you approach the gates of the city, a Tiefling woman walks up to your group. Her auburn hair complements her red skin well, and her glowing yellow eyes stand in stark contrast to her skin. Her armour is made of what looks like silvered plate and tanned leathers. On her chest is the symbol of Elturel, and on her hip is a well-crafted sword.*

GUARD A: Hail traveller. You are most welcome in [[Elturel]], but we must first inspect your caravan if that suits. 

The guards in [[Elturel]] are welcoming, but are very aware of the [[Cult of the Dragon]]'s presence. They will inspect the [[Sylvira-Van]] for any signs of danger. Unless the players have stored the dead bodies of their enemies here, the inspection will go smoothly. 

They do advise that [[Glass]] remained either outside the city walls at all times as many of the citizens are paranoid about Dragonkind. They are not strict about this. [[Glass]] is allowed to go inside, but must be supervised at all times. If [[Auron Whitestreak]] is alive and with the party, he will opt to stay with [[Glass]] while the party explores the city.

#### The 'Holy' Order
The [[Order of the Gauntlet]] can be visited at any time, but it is the purpose of their visit here in [[Elturel]]. They are headquartered in a collection of buildings near the [[Pair of Black Antlers]] tavern on the north side of the city. When the players arrive, the following scene occurs.

*As you make your way through the paved city streets of Elturel, you find yourself somewhat disoriented by the constant and never-changing light of the Companion. People smile as they pass you by, cheerful smiles on their face as they head off to continue their day.

*It takes you a while, but eventually you find yourself standing on the street in front of the Pair of Black Antlers. The buildings that make up the structures looks to be composed of the same white stone as the rest of the city, signifying its age. Unlike the towers of the walls the building looks conservative in comparison, though the pointed arches and intricate tilework still present a more extravagant image than what you expected of a military organisation.*

*On the front of one of the buildings you can see a pair of banners that fly beside a rather large wooden door. You can see the outline of a smaller, squarer door right before it opens. A female elf walks out from inside and notices your party standing there. Her pale, almost white blond hair is tied up in an intricate collection of braids and plaits. Her armour appears to be made up of some sort of white exoskeletal material, and her inner robes are thick and winter-ready.*

TALIS: Oh, hello there. If you're looking for Onthar Frume or... well, anyone. Apparently he's not in his office. I was hoping to talk to him about a lead I had, but it looks like I'm gonna have to come back some other time.

This is [[Alia Sanem 'Talis Kasterel']]. She is looking for [[Onthar Frume]] in order to report the impending arrival of [[Langdedrosa Cyanwrath]]'s forces. She did not go to [[Thavius Kreeg]] or the [[Order of the Companion]] nor the [[Hellriders]] as she believes this is a threat that only the [[Order of the Gauntlet]] is equipped for. When asked about her reasons for coming here, she will refuse to disclose any information to them regardless of who they may bring up or what they may say and state "I will only disclose this intelligence to Frume."

If the players offer for [[Alia Sanem 'Talis Kasterel']] to join them as they find [[Onthar Frume]] (or if they already know where he is) she will thank them for their generosity and say they will not regret this. 

*You open the smaller wooden section of the massive door and step inside. The smell of lavander hits your nose as you step into the inner hall of the Hall of the Triad. The tiled floor is quite beautiful and an assortment of earth-toned pillars and marbled surfaces greet you as you get deeper within. White busts depicting various heroes of old line the walls.*

*At the end of the hallway you see the a wooden desk with a marble surface. Stacks of papers and other office equipment are visible from here. On the other side of the desk you see a rather bored looking receptionist in the form of a female elf with blonde hair and pale skin. She's wearing a white button-up shirt and a pale blue skirt that sits high on her hips and goes down just past her knees.*

AURELIA: If you're looking for [[Onthar Frume]], like I told that lady he's not here.

The players can either look around the [[Hall of the Triad]] and speak to the 'receptionist' [[Aurelia Vos]] at the front, or if a character is a member of the [[Order of the Gauntlet]], they will know that if [[Onthar Frume]] is not in his office he will most likely be in the [[Pair of Black Antlers]] right next door where he likes to spend most of his time. When they eventually find his whereabouts they can move to the [[Pair of Black Antlers]] through the [[Hall of the Triad]]'s side door.

*You walk inside the tavern and find yourself a little dizzy as the smoke and smell of alcohol hits your nose. The inside of the taproom is dimly lit, and despite the large windows light barely penetrates the inside from all of the vapour flying around. Thick stone pillars reach up to the ceiling which is supported by low-hanging beams. Old but beautiful looking dark furniture is littered across the floor and driftglobes aid the chandeliers and windows in providing dim light. The walls all around are heavily decorated like a hunting hall. Trophies, adventurer's finds and other paraphernalia such as well-used weaponry and split-shields, monster heads and ancient maps purporting to detail castle dungeons, crypts, etc. 

*A collection of brawny looking Paladins, Fighters and Clerics are gathered in the halls, clinking their glasses together in joy. An Orc bartender stands behind the counter, polishing glasses with both hands while a spectral Mage Hand flies around providing customers their drinks. Behind him, a large pair of black antlers takes up most of the wall. From your estimate, it must be at lest 20 or so feet wide. Right next to it, there is a ribbon tied to one of the points which reads "Volo's Stamp of Authenticity".

*In the far corner of the room near the hearth is a large table. A rather large but strongly built human man throws his bald head back in laughter, revealing his long but well-groomed beard. His plate armour hugs his body tightly, and his trusty warhammer stands next to his side leaning against the wall. An assortment of Paladins surround him, with one particularly rugged looking gentleman being the most boisterous.*

[[Caldus Merevain]] and [[Onthar Frume]] are long-time drinking buddies. If the adventurers attempt to speak to either of them, Frume will simply tell them to wait just a moment before going back to his stories to the others. Further attempts to verbally get his attention will not work. If the players speak to [[Ggrog]] the Bartender, they'll learn that [[Onthar Frume]] likes to see a good fistfight and one will likely get his attention.

It doesn't matter who the players pick a fight with, [[Onthar Frume]] will take note of their loss or victory and order another round of drinks in honour of their fight. He will then finally address the adventurers and say he's been expecting them but didn't expect them to know the culture so well. [[Leosin Erlanthar]] will enter the tavern and be relieved to see that [[Onthar Frume]] is finally speaking. The human will invite them back towards the [[Hall of the Triad]] and up to his office.

*The inside of Frume's office is far less brutish. In some ways, it almost resembles a well-maintained mage's study but with far less books and more animal and monster heads. Of note is the skeletal head of what looks like a wyrmling mounted right above his personal fireplace. Leosin pulls on Onthar's curtains, blocking out the light of the Companion and the view from outside. 

*The elf takes a seat in front of Onthar's desk and sets down his staff just as Frume lets out a rather loud burp. Before Leosin can bring up the obvious, Onthar places his gauntleted hand on his chest and clears up his inebriation in a flash of divine magic.*

ONTHAR: Torm knows I love being a Paladin (he laughs). Right, to business. We have important business to discuss.

*He opens up a box and brings out a cigar and lights it.*

ONTHAR: The Dragon Cult. Those fuckers. At this point, you know almost as much about them as we do, and thanks to you, we know twice as much today as we did a tenday ago. You'll have heard about what happened to [[Scornubel]] and [[Triel]] by now, yes? High Observer [[Thavius Kreeg]] has told us to keep him informed if any more information comes up, but that's the thing. We've had none.

ONTHAR: I had hoped that you would have something to give me, but I can understand why you don't. [[Jaheria]]'s council will have to wait, our priority right now is defending [[Elturel]] and I can't be sending valuable men I don't have to attend a Council our chapter may not even be around for.

LEOSIN: I have reports from our scouts near [[Triel]] that the [[Hellriders]] were dealing with some Cultists. Perhaps our friends here can shed some light on that?

ONNTHAR: If the [[Cult of the Dragon]] is planning on targeting [[Elturel]] next, we need to put a stop to them. If the city falls, the rest of Elturgard may as well follow.

LEOSIN: Their dragons have not been spotted in the area. Perhaps they're planning on attempting the use of a small strike force instead?

If [[Alia Sanem 'Talis Kasterel']] is present, she will disclose her findings with [[Onthar Frume]]. In reaction to this information, [[Onthar Frume]] will task the characters with heading out to deal with this problem. He will either send [[Mira]] or [[Caldus Merevain]] to accompany them, depending on the choice of the adventurers. Both will agree to go, but only one can be taken. There are no consequences to this.

### 2.7 Whispers of the Brotherhood

The [[Arcane Brotherhood]] has a presence here in [[Elturel]] but [[Onthar Frume]] does not know why. The adventurers will need to uncover the purpose of their arrival in the city. During their nightly walk likely after meeting with [[Onthar Frume]], the party will be confronted by [[Thuran Yale]].

*The city streets are quiet and serene, a strange sighting in what appears to you as stark daylight. Your mind can't comprehend the idea of an everlasting day, but in Elturel this is the reality that many of its citizens must live through. As you find yourself walking down a narrow path, the sound of creaking wood catches your attention. You instinctively look up, only to find nothing there but a cat that was jumping through a rather highly-placed clothesline.*

*When your vision goes back down to street-level, however, you see an individual standing before you. His shoulder-length golden hair is brushed back, with a few throwaway strands falling down his forehead. His thick eyebrows give him a stern but eye-catching visage, and his sharp jawline frames his handsome face well.*

THURAN: A pleasure to meet your acquaintance, my name is Thuran Yale. I'm sure you've not heard of me, but I've heard so much about you.

*Thuran's hands remain at his back as the Tiefling and Goliath next to him spread out to either side of him and watch your party closely. Their garb is noticably less intricate than Thuran's, and what little you can see of their face does give much in the way of insight to their intent.*

If [[Azbara Jos]] is in good terms with the players, he will simply ask them to invite the man to meet with him and give him a letter. On a DC 15 Persuasion check, [[Thuran Yale]] can be convinced to reveal that he is part of the [[Arcane Brotherhood]]. If they are unfamiliar with the organisation, Thuran will subtly cast Detect Thoughts to learn this and he will tell them that the Brotherhood has 'rebranded' as an organisation for good.

### CONCLUSION

Upon killing [[Langdedrosa Cyanwrath]], [[Onthar Frume]] tells the party that they must head to [[Baldur's Gate]] and speak to the [[Talons of the Raven Queen]] to get them to send aid to [[Elturel]]. He will suggest two ways of getting there;

The first is to let the players and the [[Sylvira-Van]] board the Warship called the Piercing Light, captained by [[Caldus Merevain]] which will take them to [[Baldur's Gate]] within 3 days. However, once the ship has sailed they will have no chances to stop on the way there.

Alternatively, they can travel via the [[Sylvira-Van]] which would normally take them a week but they risk running into something [[Onthar Frume]] only knows as the [[Shadow Curse]]. If they take this road, they will need to find a way around it which could offset their journey by a day or two. However, if they travel via the [[Sylvira-Van]], they have a chance to visit one of the [[Emerald Enclave]]'s Circles, the [[Emerald Grove]] lead by the druid [[Halsin]]. 

Before the decide which path they want to take, [[Onthar Frume]] also strongly advises that they seek out [[Ackyn Selebon]], a former [[Order of the Gauntlet]] member who now operates an equipage business in the Outer City. According to [[Leosin Erlanthar]], the Harpers say that the [[Cult of the Dragon]] were in [[Baldur's Gate]] recently and Ackyn may have information as to what they were doing there.
