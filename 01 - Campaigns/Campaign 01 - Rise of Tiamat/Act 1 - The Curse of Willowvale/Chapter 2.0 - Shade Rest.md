### **2.1 Arrival at Shade Rest**

The party arrives in [[Shade Rest]] after clearing out the Underground hideout.

****
### **2.2 Goblin Hideout**

The adventurers will stumble upon this hideout upon accepting either [[Britha Torin]]'s request to find [[Gundren Rockseeker]] or [[Gnorma]]'s plea to find her son [[Aidan]]. [[Gnorma]] will be found on the Cliffside Path looking for her son who left [[Shade Rest]] on the night the adventurers first arrived at. She will direct the players further down the path where they will discover track marks leading off-road.

	Your party heads southwest of Shade Rest, teetering just on the edge of the Cloak Wood. Even from this distance, strange ominous noises emanate from deep within, reminding you of the danger you avoided not too long ago. The path becomes slightly rougher as you make your way towards the Coast. The ground beneath your feet is even enough, making the walk much more bearable.
	
	As you’re walking down the path, you notice track marks from what appears to be a wagon. About 10 minutes down the trail, there is a 6ft wide, 10 foot deep pit made by the Goblins. The party must succeed a DC 15 perception check to spot the hidden pit. If they do not spot the hidden pit, they must succeed on a DC 10 Dexterity saving throw or fall in, suffering 1d6 damage. No ability check is needed to scramble out.

	You descend further down the path, the view opens up to what looks like cave mouth with an underground river coming out of it. Just as you think you’ve hit a dead end, you notice that there’s light coming from within the cave’s entrance and sounds of laughter coming from within.

The rest of the adventure follows the standard Lost Mines of Phandelver Cragmaw Hideout progression with a few changes:

1. The Wolves chained to the wall will provide very vague information the Cult of the Dragon i.e. lizards love following other lizards. A DC 10 animal handling will get them to calm down. Giving them food will automatically make them succeed.

2. Goblin Den holding [[Siddar Rockseeker]] instead of Sildar Hallwinter. Siddar is now Gundren’s brother who is also a Ranger (Monster Slayer) but cannot engage in combat until he has 1 long rest to recover. There are 5 Goblins torturing him, two regular Goblin minions, two Goblin snipers and 1 Goblin Skullcleaver. He has 1 hitpoint. If the players (more than likely) kill most of the Goblins, the leader Yeemik will ask for a truce and says he just wants to oust the local leader Klarg, a bugbear.  

3. Siddar will tell the adventurers his brother is more than likely being held at Cragmaw Keep (the location of [[Chapter 5.0 - Blood of the Wolf]]) because Siddar has something important (the Ironhand Gnome's recipe for Runepowder). At the end of the dungeon, he offers the party 50 gold to escort him back to Shaderest.

4. The inattentive Goblin will still call to the others, but instead of regular Goblins the other room (Twin Pools Cave) will contain 2 Goblin minions, 1 Goblin sniper and 1 Goblin Psi-Brawler. If the characters engage in open combat, one runs to warn Klarg. 

5. Klarg’s Cave. Sacks and crates of looted provisions are piled up in the south end of this large cave. To the west, the floor slopes toward a narrow opening that descends into darkness. (the chimney). There are crates marked with different symbols. The one that sticks out is the Lionsheld Coster, a merchant company based in Amn. You assume these to be part of the supplies Gundren and Siddar were transporting. 

**Notable Loot:** 
- A portable Hole (which Siddar owns)
- 2 Healing Potions. 
- 300 Gold worth of Loose Treasures.
- Supplies for [[Shade Rest]] (contributes 1 point to the Defense).
- [[Siddar Rockseeker]] (contributes 1 point to the Defense and is a potential permanent companion upon the death of [[Batuu Wolf-Blood]]).

### **2.3 The Herd Arrives**

Upon their return to [[Shade Rest]], the characters will more than likely initiate a long rest before moving on to [[Chapter 3.0 - Candlekeep]] or will do so directly after dealing with Klarg. In either case, when the party returns to the town square, the following scene triggers when they come to the end of their time in the town where the characters will meet [[Mellara, the Terror of the Sword Coast]], [[Batuu Wolf-Blood]] and [[Thaddak Ice-Veins]].

	The townsfolk outside gather in the square, murmuring amongst themselves as the sound of a warhorn draws their attention. A half dozen unknown figures atop brutish creatures ride into town, circling around the commoners and herding them like sheep. The creatures appear to be large, monstrous canines with elongated, brutish features. Their top halves were covered in thick fur, while their lower bodies were bare and exposed to show the musculature beneath.
		
	On a DC 10 Check, the players will recognise these as Worgs, commonly ridden by Goblinoids and their ilk as part of their warbands.
	
	As one of the villagers tries to run, a Worg bites at the back of his shirt. He screams as it tosses him onto the ground and pins him down. Larissa steps up and tries to help him, but the creature growls and stops her before she gets any closer. The others panic but don’t move.
	
	A woman dismounts from the beast, standing at least 7 and a half feet tall. Her vascular body is dressed in tribal leathers and furs, her grey skin is mottled with dirt, her hair a mix of grey and white streaks tied into a thick braid that trails behind her. Her almost clear, white eyes scan the commoners as she smirks, patting the Worg on the head.
	
	“That’s enough, girl.” she says. 
	
	The Worg moves back, and as the pressure lifts from the man’s back, he scurries to his feet and goes to his wife’s side, holding her close. Elder Bonham pushes past all of the townsfolk and confronts the woman, his warhammer in hand. 
	
	“The agreement was the end of every harvest, Mellara. We have nothing to give you.”
		
	The woman laughs and circles Bonham. “We’re not here for that, Bonham.” 
	
	She reaches to her back and grabs her large, jagged greatsword. It’s about 7ft in length, the handle wrapped in leather and decorated with furs. 
	
	“We’re looking for a Wizard. Human trash. Goes by the name of Matreous.”  
	  
	“He’s not here.” Larissa says, defiantly. “So take your thugs and get-”  
	  
	Mellara raises her hand and slaps her. Larissa falls to the ground and Mellara picks up her greatsword, readying herself to swing down, but a hand reaches out to stop her.  
	  
	“Perhaps we might exercise some caution, Mellara.” a voice calls. A brown-skinned, brown-haired bugbear spekas up next to her. “We only came for information.”  
	  
	“You’re too soft, Thaddak.” Another voice says.
	
	 A half-orc woman steps forward from behind two Worg riders, not quite as tall as Mellara but just as intimidating. Her long coat is adorned with what looked like a wolf’s pelt on the collar, dragging behind her and billowing in the wind, as did her long black curls. On her side are two scimitars, and around her chest is a longbow. 
	 
	 “I think the girl deserves a lesson in humility.”  
	  
	Mellara laughs at the half-orc. “Normally I would agree with your bloodlust, Batuu.” She lowers her hand and turns back to Bonham and pats his shoulder. “But you’re right, Thaddak.” she says as she turns around and walks to the crowd. They all back up and nearly fall over on top of each other as she steps forward. 
		
	“We’re looking for another man, a Hobgoblin by the name of Kevdor.” Thaddak, the bugbear says.  
	  
	“We’ve seen no Hobgoblins pass through here.” Bonham says.  
	  
	“Is that right?” Batuu asks.  
	  
	“It’s the truth.” Bonham says. “The last Goblins to walk these streets were your men from the last harvest. When they came to collect your ‘tithe’ for you.”
		
	“You’re feeling a little braver these days, aren’t you Bonham?” Mellara asks.  
	  
	“Enough, Mellara.” Thaddak says. 
		
	“We’ll see if you’re telling the truth, little human. We’ll find Kevdor soon enough.” Batuu says, pointing her scimitar at Larissa. “My wolf will return to me within the next few days.”  
	  
	Mellara scoffs then laughs. “I guess it’s your lucky day.” She gets back onto her Worg and turns.
	
	"But if I find out you’re lying, Bonham, there’s going to be a reckoning.” Batuu says, as she also mounts her Direwolf.	  
	
	The Hobgoblins turn back to the north road and ride forward, breaking crates and pushing barrels to the side as their heavy footsteps make contact with the ground. The townsfolk scramble back towards their houses, some rushing back to the Inn as the group vanishes from sight. Melarra rides past your group along with Batuu and Thaddak.

At this point, a silent choice is presented as the adventurers deal with the aftermath of [[Mellara, the Terror of the Sword Coast]]'s appearance; either proceed with [[Chapter 3.0 - Candlekeep]] or initiate [[Chapter 2.3 - Saving Shade Rest]]. Choosing the first will lead to [[Shade Rest]]'s total destruction/partial destruction (unless its defense requirements are met as outlined in Chapter 2.3). The second choice results in the death of [[Matreous Melwin]] and the loss of the reward he planned to give the party.

****

When the players leave the town, they can move to the next chapter; [[Chapter 3.0 - Candlekeep]]. If the quests to find [[Gundren Rockseeker]] or [[Aidan]] are not accepted, the [[Herd of the Dragon]] lead by [[Mellara, the Terror of the Sword Coast]] will ride past them instead.
