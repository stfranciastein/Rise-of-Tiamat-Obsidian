### TRIGGER

This quest 'begins' after players visit their second shop in [[Berdusk]]. If no shops have been visited, they will skip the encounter with the creature and instead find out that a gruesome murder has happened again.

### PART I - Berdusk 

#### The Crime Scene
*A small crowd is gathered on the banks of the River Sulduskoon, and you make your way over there to investigate. It's hard to see much of anything, but there's a stench of death in the air.

*You push past the busy crowd and into the Before you is a gruesome scene, a human man is tied to a pole with what looks like melted steel cuffs. His eyes are gouged out, his mouth hangs agape forever frozen in terror. His stomach is cut wide open and his guts spill across the floor in what looks like a dried up puddle of blood. One of the Orc guards holds his hands up to you as soon as he spots you.*

GUARD 1: Stay back travellers.

When asked about how they were found, the Guard will tell the party that he found him on patrol this morning. He informed his superiors but some of the citizens stumbled across him like this, thus the crowd. The body is that of a human man named Orville Dengir

On the following day after encountering [[Pacted Slaughter Demon]] and while walking through the city streets, players will feel a sense of unease wash over them as they move through the streets. On a DC 8 Perception check, the adventurers will recognise the features of a man from afar. Jos, a human farmer from [[Berdusk]].

*As you're walking down the street, a man accidentally brushes your shoulder. He apologises politely and flashes you a quick acknowledging smile. You keep your eyes trained on him and a sense of familiarity washes over you. You realise you've seen him before. At the shop when he ran away from you. Looking at the back of his head, there is no mistaking this is the creature from before.*

Jos is a human male farmer living outside of the city walls. Upon confrontation, he will claim that he has never been to that part of the city, but several shopkeeps dispute this. A detect thoughts or high insight check will reveal he's extremely afraid and confused.

#### Leads
The following leads can only be gained in [[Berdusk]].

- At the scene of the crime, the body of the victim will be cut in half with its guts pulled out.
- On a DC 10 Medicine check, the player(s) can learn that the wounds look like they were made with a slashing weapon. On a 15 or higher, they will learn it was made with a dagger.
- A Gnome woman named Vittoria will be at the scene and say she believes these are connected to the Orcs retaking [[Berdusk]] somehow, and she believes they will only stop happening if the Orcs leave the city.
- On a DC 8 Persuasion check, the minotaur blacksmith Flint will claim that a month ago a female elf commissioned an especially sharp sword from him, and paid an extra 500 gold for it to be extradited.
- In the [[Patriarch's Rest]], Amalicia will claim that Avdul was a regular there and often drowned his sorrows in drink. She will also let them know he reported the murder of his wife to both the [[Harpers]] and the [[First Folk]] but neither could do anything. She will also say that he saw Avdul's wife arguing with the victim a few months ago.
- The [[Harpers]] will claim that a red-skinned Tiefling man came into [[Twilight Hall]] several months ago, begging for justice for his murdered wife Serena. The [[Harpers]] claim that the man did not identify himself, and what little evidence they could find about his murdered lover suggested she was accidentally killed during the takeover of [[Berdusk]].
- [[Grimnir Blood Hallow]] will claim there was a complaint raised by a Tiefling by the name of Avdul, but they had not heard from him since.
- [[Lucien]] will claim he does not particularly enjoy the scent of this killer, but refuses to let the party know more than that. He will agree to reveal the identity of the killer by saying "I will tell you, but only if you sell me your soul for eternity." This is presented seriously, but a 25 insight check reveals it is largely a joke offer. 
  
  This is a valid way to circumvent a good bit of the investigation. If a player does accept this offer, Lucien be taken aback and laugh, saying he did not expect them to actually agree. For better or for worse, he can't currently claim their soul because of the [[Cataclysm]]'s effects on them. Amused, he will instead ask for 1 gold piece. When asked, he says he hasn't made a transaction in a while and he felt compelled to do so. Upon payment, he will let the party know that their prey is a Demon that can swap bodies.
  
- Avdul lives in the slums of [[Berdusk]] in a broken down cottage. Upon discovering his abode, the party will be subject to a horrid stench. Inside, they discover his body as it hangs from the ceiling. A DC 10 Medicine check reveals that he has been dead for several weeks, if not months. Questioning his body reveals he thinks the Orcs are responsible for his wife Serena's death.
- They will also learn that Avdul spoke to a female elf about his wife's murders and was interested in reading her journal.

#### Serana's Journal

	*This journal details the life of a Tiefling woman named Serena. It talks about her meeting her husband Avdul and how they met while working for a mercenary company known as the Red-Bloods.* 
	
	*In it, the most pertinent thing that stands out is how much she expresses her guilt for the last raid she participated in where they killed an entire Orc settlement. She details letting an elven woman and her children live and leaving the Red-Bloods.* 
	
	*In her last note, she talks about feeling like she was being watched. First there was a female elf following her, then a gnome girl, a dwarven male then a human man.* 

Flipping through the pages, it names several people.
- Orville Dengir (human male).
- Zavia Gren (human female).
- Alissa Telvir (drow female).
- Jenna Maris (high elf female).
- Orcish (Orc male).
- Thragg (Goliath female).
- Boron Gravedigger (Dwarf male).

The party can choose to get Jos imprisoned at any point, but the day after his imprisonment another murder will occur. Jos has no recollection whatsoever of any of the events of the murder. He will explain that the only difference is that he has been feeling a lot more tired lately and doesn't hang out with his friends as much as he used to.

### PART II - Elturel

The conclusion of all of these facts are:
- Serena was involved in a raid on a fishing village on the River Chionthar many years ago. Her adventuring party included the other two victims and three others who are now in [[Elturel]]. 
- The Demon is contract-bound to look for and kill every single member of the party that helped destroy that village, though its origins are unknown. It is physically incapable and unwilling to stop doing what it was told to do.

The quest will then continue during [[Chapter 3.0 - The Bloody Gate]]'s introduction.

### PART III - Baldur's Gate

In Baldur's Gate, [[Xaria Trevisa]] will task either [[Khasar the Blooded]] or one of the adventurers with investigating a threat in [[Tumbledown]], a district of the  [[Outer City]] just past Cliffgate. The Talon under initiation will be tasked with finding a wizard known as the [[Harvester]]. 

XARIA: Outside the city walls, there is a refugee from [[Berdusk]] who is rumoured to be responsible for the reawakening of the undead in [[Tumbledown]]. Go there, be the Raven Queen's eyes. And may the darkness protect you.

#### Tumbledown

*As you approach Tumbledown, you immediately take note of the barricades set up by Cliffgate. A Flaming Fist guard sees you approach armed and armored, and simply moves out of your way. You can only assume that he's either too indifferent to prevent you from going through, or hopeful that you'll do something. As he opens the gates, you step into the misty covered road that leads to Tumbledown.*

![[Demonic Vengeance-1784591019485.webp]]
GRID TILES: 26 x 32

##### T1. Gatehouse
*This pair of watchtowers have long since collapsed. What appears to be the town sign hangs pathetically on the ground. Rusted remains of weapons and armour litter the floor.*

On a DC 10 Investigation check, players will be able to notice that there are recent tracks here.

##### T2. Old Tavern
*Rotting wooden furniture sits inside this old tavern. You can nearly hear the echoes of laughter standing close to the bar, though as you take a moment you realise there's nothing there.*

There are five ghosts waiting to attack the players in this town. Any loud noises will wake them up. Upon investigating further in, they will discover the remains of four dead bodies in the stables. On a DC 10 Medicine check, they will be able to learn that the bodies died from sundown fever.

##### T3. Squatter's House
*This run down house stands strong despite the water damage and mould. The fire is warm, suggesting it was used recently. It's likely squatters have been living here.*

Along with two rations and 50 SP and 400 CP, there is a hastily written note on the ground says the following:

	She's in the catacombs, the one from Berdusk.
	Blood for blood she keeps saying. Over and over again.
	I told her we could get her help at the Temple but she said no.
	I need to tell someone, but she won't let me leave.
	She won't let me bloody leave.
	Whoever finds this... please, save me.

#### Funeral Home

*You look to the south and can see what appears to be a lit building in the distance. As you approach it's clear that the building is empty, however, as the doors are all wide open and no noises come from within. The building appears to be a funeral home.*

![[Demonic Vengeance-1784593135837.webp|697]]
GRID TILES: 20 x 34

##### M1. Entryway
*The figure on the statue depicts Kelemvor, the judge of the dead. The candles that surround the statue seem freshly lit, and there are muddy footprints on the ground. Scattered on the wall behind the statue are letters of remembrance written to the dead.*

On a DC 10 Investigation check, characters will discover one note that sticks out; it speaks about the following.

	He didn't deserve to go out the way he did.
	If I could find a way to make them pay, I would.
	If I could've given her joy...
	I do all of this for her.

##### M2. Waiting Room
*This waiting room stands empty. The candles in this room also seem freshly lit, but upon closer inspection they seem to be magically enchanted with continuous flames.*

On the ledger is a list of recently buried individuals. Most of the patrons seem to have been transferred to Candulhallow's Funerary Arrangements in the [[Lower City]]. The author of the ledger seems to have made a note of a mother from [[Berdusk]] squatting in [[Tumbledown]] with her two young daughters. He details attempting to contact them but the mother is always gone and the daughters always run off whenever he tries to speak to them.

##### M3. Coffin Storage
*The smell of wood hits your nose. The coffins here are stacked upon one another but are empty.*

##### M4. Hallway
*Two boxes that appear to made to contain bodies lie on opposite ends of the room. One of the bodies seems to have been hacked upon by a sickle or pick, while the other remains untouched.*

On a DC 10 Investigation or Medicine Check, players will be able to determine that the body was drained of bone marrow and pieces of its skin were cut off. It is likely whoever did this performed it in a hurry as the remains are poorly worked on.

##### M5. Workshop
*This capentry worshop looks to be recently used, though its occupant is nowhere to be found. On the table sits what appears to be a morbidly instructional book containing instructions on how to construct a coffin. Another that sits open on the desk details how to dissect a body and prepare it for burial.*

There is a ring of keys on the desk that are labelled with the names of catacombs.

On a DC 10 Investigation check, they will be able to determine that it's likely someone stole one of the keys for themselves as on close inspection of the ring, it appears to have been broken and re-forged. Meaning one of these keys was likely copied.

##### M6. Outer Courtyard
*A collection of half a dozen people stand here, seemingly unaware of your presence. As you approach, you can hear the sounds of gurgling coming from their direction. When you get close enough, you realise that they are undead.*

*In the distance, you see what looks to be a young woman running towards the crypts accompanied by an older figure. As soon as she sees you she yells out "No! Please stop!" and disappears into one of the crypts. The doors slam shut, and the undead attack.*

Four ghouls and two corpse walkers attack the party. The door behind the girl becomes Arcane Locked. It can either be opened by speaking the passphrase or with a spell.

#### Necropolis
*You follow the girl's path down the tomb and find yourselves chasing her deeper and deeper beneath the earth. A few minutes pass and you can see light coming from up ahead. The cavern opens up into what looks to be a grand necropolis beneath the earth. How, or why this place is here confounds you.*

![[Cold, Red-Blooded Murder-1784656982130.webp]]
##### 1. Cobblestone Walkway
*The natural caverns you’ve traversed give way to precisely worked cobblestones, a twenty-foot-wide road leading to an enormous stone structure, studded with gemstones, that is covered in angular depictions of soldiers drilling, angels floating, and families crying. Gravestones are spaced to the north and south.*

Characters enter the necropolis from this location. Characters who choose to approach quietly and without a light source can enter and move about unmolested if they succeed at a group DC 15 Dexterity ([Stealth](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#Skills)) check. Those using a light source or failing to move quietly draw undead from [area 7](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/loe/sardonyx-necropolis#7AssortedGraves). Those who use light are able to see the stone structure to the east is red and white, confirming its identity.

##### 2. Deva of Health
*An angelic statue of a Deva holding a staff in one hand and a chalice upraised in the other watches over the graves in this area. A spongy fungus grows over the base of the statue and over many of the graves in this area. It's not quite the same fungus that Thaddak created, fortunately.*

No creatures threaten the characters here, but the “fungus” hides a danger. The soldiers buried here died from disease they brought home from battle, not from battle wounds.

**_Deva of Health._** The statue depicts Julara, a figure of health and servant of Kelemvor. Characters who clear off the fungus from the base of the statue discover a plate inscribed with the following prayer: 

	“Julara, deva of health, protect us from disease and death.” 

The blessing of Julara is potent: cleaning the statue grants characters who participate in the cleaning advantage on saves against being poisoned while in the Necropolis.

**_Spongy Fungus._** Closer inspection (and knowledge) can reveal that what appears to be fungus is really a yellow, disease-bearing moss. Druids and those proficient with [Medicine](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#Skills) automatically recognize it. Others must succeed at a DC 15 Intelligence ([Nature](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#Skills)) check to discern that the spongy stuff is really moss infected with the Weeping Pox. Physical contact with the moss is sufficient to spread it (though if this contact is in service of cleaning the statue, the character gains advantage on their checks against the disease!).

##### 3. Unmarked Mausoleum
*This gated and walled mausoleum bears no identifying markers.*

One of the general’s humbler lieutenants requested an unmarked grave, but the people couldn’t bear the thought of not honoring him; the compromise was this unmarked but magnificent mausoleum. Characters can climb the walls, unlock the gate with a successful DC 15 Dexterity (Thieves’ Tools) check, or use another means to get to the door of the mausoleum proper.

**_Riddle of the Mausoleum._** The door has no handle or keyhole but bears a riddle in archaic Celestial: 

	“If thou art better, thou dost not possess me; 
	yet they who do art the best of men. 
	
	I am never shouted, often overlooked, 
	and cannot be bragged of. Speak my name and enter.” 

The best answer to the riddle is “humility,” but “humble,” “meek,” “modest,” or some other similar trait works as well. Players who struggle with riddles can still gain access if a character makes a successful DC 15 Wisdom ([Insight](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#Skills)) check.

**_Inside._** This tomb’s interior contains a simple stone platform where a corpse in tarnished armor reposes. Gleaming in his hands is a greataxe called the [Cauteriser](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/11795894-cauteriser-inert).

##### 4. Obelisk
*A towering obelisk shaped like a saber looms over the graves here.*

One [corpse walker](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4037095-corpse-walker)* and eight [skeleton riflers](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4037155-skeleton-rifler)* lurk in the dark. They attack living creatures on sight. The obelisk bears a simple inscription in Infernal:

	“The Szarr family shall rise once again."

#### 5. Crypt Niches
This crypt will only open if the zombies in area 8 and 9 have been defeated. Upon their deaths, they will each impart an essence that fly towards the door as Alessandra's magic wanes. When you enter, you may read out the section Cold, Red-Blooded Murder.

##### 6. Statue of Lady Gilrain
*A life-size statue of an armored woman wielding a blade guards these graves.*

Lady Gilrain Szarr died leading her troops in battle, one of the only nobles to lead from the front. Her remains were never recovered, but her sword found its way back to friendly hands and is held by her statue.

**_Gilrain’s Blade._** Characters with a passive Perception of at least 13 or who succeed at a DC 13 Wisdom ([Perception](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/playing-the-game#Skills)) check, recognize that the sword itself is metal (not stone) and removable. It is a [+1 longsword](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/5245-longsword-1).

##### 7. Assorted Graves
*Scattered gravestones, precisely planned and placed, mark the resting places of the fallen. These dense crypt niches are stacked from the floor to the ceiling in two rows beside the central tomb and a smaller mausoleum.

A pair of [corpse walker](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4037095-corpse-walker)* trundle through these graves looking for other undead agents. They attack on sight.

##### 8. Sisterly Love 
*A simple wooden box, mostly rotted, sits on a stone pedestal in the center of this mausoleum. The lid is askew. A woman wearing a once-fine but now-bloodied cloak stands over the coffin, unmoving. Unlike the other undead and corpses in the necropolis, the zombie’s clothes are modern.*

The zombie is the reanimated corpse of an elven woman named Kassalia, sister to Alessandra. She is someone lucid, but it is clear she's losing her mind. The zombie will tell the players she is pact-bound to protect her master, but is unable to tell them who her master is.

KASS: She saw, we saw... mercenaries... father cut down. She didn't forget... she never forgot...

She will then engage the party in combat. Upon her defeat, the players see her spirit fly towards the sealed crypt in area 5.

##### 9. Destroyed Crypts
*This half of the mausoleum has been utterly destroyed. What were once row upon row of crypt niches have been pulverized. The corpses within are missing, but the musty dust of the crypt has settled over the copious debris.*

An [ancient corpse walker](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4037084-ancient-corpse-walker)* and a [skeleton commander](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4037154-skeleton-commander)* are here and attack on sight. At the end of the fight, players are able to loot a locket with a picture depicting a family of four.

*You open the metal locket and peer inside. You see the painted visage of a human man, an elven woman and their two children. The painting looks old and worn, and was likely an expensive purchase given what skilled hands were required to paint something this detailed at this size. On the other side, you see the names 'Konstantinos, Eleni, Kassandra and Alessandra'.*

Upon their defeat, the players will see their spirits fly towards the sealed crypt in area 5.

##### 10. Niches & Mosaic
*Densely stacked crypt niches reach to the ceiling along the western and northern walls. The eastern side of this area has been repeatedly smashed with something large and heavy, but it’s clear that once these were more crypt niches too. Beyond the niches to the east, a twenty-foot-diameter floor mosaic of a radiant, angelic figure is visible in the gloom.*

One of the dead bodies from the niches has found itself flung onto the mosaic, but otherwise most of the debris is far from it. The mosaic was under the influence of a [hallow](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2135-hallow) spell that kept out the corpse walkers and other undead, but when the necromancer opened the general’s coffin in [area 8](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/loe/sardonyx-necropolis#8GeneralsTomb), it destroyed the effect. The undead still shy from this area, however.

**_Niche Search._** The destroyed niches have been looted, but the sealed ones have not. A thorough, systematic search of these niches (which requires breaking their seals and/or smashing them open) reveals one of the soldiers was interred with an outer garment that hasn’t succumbed to time: a [cloak of elvenkind](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/4606-cloak-of-elvenkind).

### PART IV - Cold, Red-Blooded Murder

*These dense crypt niches are stacked from the floor to the ceiling in two rows beside the central tomb and a smaller mausoleum. The floor here is made of smoother, red pavers compared to the white cobblestones of the road outside.* 

*As you approach you can see dozens of papers and notes put up on the walls, connected by pieces of string and illuminated by the candlelight from below. When you're too close for her comfort, she turns around and inhales deeply before wiping her tears. In the light, it's plain as day she's less than 15 years old.*

ALESSANDRA: Stay away! Just leave me alone.

*The man sitting on the ground next to her stands up and reveals himself to you. This disfigured, undead creature closely resembles the man from the pendant. His skin, however, seems pieced together. The girl must've been putting this monster together piece by piece.*

ALESSANDRA: Father, no, they'll hurt you.

KONSTANTINOS: Unhhh.... free... help... me...

*The girl lets out a small sniffle as she puts her head down. She touches the back of the zombie and it seems to respond to her magic. A wave of necromantic energy fills the air and the zombie sits.*

ALESSANDRA: What do you want? Are you here to stop me? Do you even know what you're asking? Do you?!

Alessandra is the one who made the deal with the [[Pacted Slaughter Demon]]. 

She says that her family were forced to watch her father be slaughtered by a mercenary group known as the Red-Bloods prior to the [[Reclamation]] of [[Berdusk]]. She said that her mother tried to move on, but when they moved to [[Elturel]] she could still see Konstantinos' face in her dreams. Eventually, they moved even further west to [[Baldur's Gate]] but her mother didn't get better. She went to the [[House of Grief]] in the [[Lower City]], but even they could not stop her nightmares. 

One night, when Alessandra and her sister Kassandra were asleep, Eleni killed her sister by gutting her in the belly. Alessandra ran, and when she returned she found her mother had gouged her eyes out, guilty for what she did. Alessandra, now physically and mentally scarred by her encounter, wants to put things right again. She believes that in order to begin healing, the people who wronged her and her family need to pay.

During her time in the [[Outer City]], Alessandra has been able to steal many books and spy in on many spellcasters. She learned of a ritual that could summon a Demon, one she could make a pact with. She stole the components required to make contact with another plane, and eventually she reached Orcus. Orcus gave her the [[Pacted Slaughter Demon]], and in return she promised the Demon many souls. All she wanted were the deaths of the Red-Bloods and their families. The demon itself is mindless, and it does not care for its mission. It is only glad to be unleashed upon Toril once more.

Orcus also gave Alessandra the knowledge of necromancy. Because of this, she believes that reviving her father will fix her broken and shattered psyche. Once that's complete, she can resurrect her sister and finally her mother.

ALESSANDRA: So you see, you can't stop me. Not until they've all paid. Blood for blood. I will not stop until they're all dead!

*Alessandra cuts her wrists and a shadow appears on the wall. As her eyes roll to the back of her head, her and Konstantinos's forms retreat into the corner. Out of the shadows, four large, armoured arms emerge. Each hand carries a large blade, and many frantic eyeballs look towards you. The head of the slaughter demon follows after, and its glowing white eyes light up as it steps out from the abyss.*

### CONCLUSION

#### Dealing with the source
*At the end of the fight, Alessandra lets out a pained yelp Konstantinos collapses to the ground, weakened by the disruption to her magic. She hovers over her undead father's body, crying into his neck. She turns towards your party and yells out.*

ALESSANDRA: You can't do this! You can't take him away from me again.

*She goes back to crying on his neck and sobs loudly. Konstantinos's eyes flicker for a second, and for a moment they almost seem like they're full of life. Alessandra takes a second and holds his cheek, caressing the leathered skin and closes her own eyes. She eyes the blade she used to cut herself with and looks back at you. There's nothing but sadness in her eyes, knowing there's nowhere to go anymore.*

ALESSANDRA: It's not fair.

*She wipes her cheek once more and lays Konstantinos down on the ground.*

The deal with Orcus cannot be broken. The only way the Red-Bloods will be freed from the [[Pacted Slaughter Demon]] is if Alessandra either calls it off or she is killed. Persuading her will require a successful DC 30 Persuasion check. The DC is lowered by 5 for every instance of pain and suffering the players can present to her about the consequences of her actions.

#### The Talons
With Alessandra dealt with, the Red-Bloods are now safe. Players can return to [[Xaria Trevisa]], leading them to finish their initiation into the [[Talons of the Raven Queen]],  or lead to [[Khasar the Blooded]] being awarded for his role. 

Either way, the players are awarded with 4,000 GP, a scroll of revivify and the Conspirator's Coat. If [[Khasar the Blooded]] is with them, he will immediately take it for himself.

[[Xaria Trevisa]] will then move out to the common area and invite the rest of the party who may be waiting outside. She will then inform the players of information she learned about the Serpent.

XARIA: You are all concerned with the [[Cult of the Dragon]], yes? My men tell me that they ran into this individual before. Allegedly, they wore red clothing and carried a string instrument. If I were to guess, they are likely to make an appearance during the Feast of the Moon.
