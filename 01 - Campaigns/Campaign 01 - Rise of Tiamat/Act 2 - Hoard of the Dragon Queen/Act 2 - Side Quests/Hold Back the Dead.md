### TRIGGER

Given by Father [[Lorgan Brandis]] in the [[Temple of the Open Hand]], who will mention that one of his monks have yet to return from their visit to [[Ironspine Keep]], a fortress north of [[Baldur's Gate]] that is being rebuilt by the [[Lords' Alliance]]. If the players do not visit the [[Temple of the Open Hand]], [[Margaret Ravenshade]] or [[Xaria Trevisa]] upon the completion of [[A New Duke]] or [[Cold, Red-Blooded Murder]] will instead mention that [[Ironspine Keep]] has stopped reporting back to the Alliance.

If [[Azbara Jos]] is on friendly or neutral terms with the players, he will be mentioned as an 'inside source' who believes that the [[Red Wizards of Thay]] may be involved as [[Szass Tam]] has dispatched several forces to the Western Heartlands.

Additionally, while This quest is triggered in Chapter 3, but it can't be completed until [[Chapter 4.0 - Long and Winding Road]] at the very end of 4.1. when [[Balthazaar Baradult]] and [[Renaer Neverember]] force the party into staying there in fear of the convoy being attacked.

### PART I - Enter Halvachar

Upon arriving at [[Ironspine Keep]], [[Corlie Halvachar]] will approach the party. Halvachar is a representative of the [[Lords' Alliance]] and a member of [[House Halvachar]].

*A stout halfling wearing plate armour inlaid with filigree of lions and roses decorate approaches. A red cloak follows behind her, trailing gracefully in contrast to her strong demeanour. She gives you all a warm, affirming smile as she scans your party.*

[[Corlie Halvachar]] will tell the party that she wasn't expecting reinforcements to come so soon. [[Renaer Neverember]] will tell her that she would be waiting for quite a while as it seems her messengers haven't made it far from [[Ironspine Keep]]'s surrounds. If [[Lorgan Brandis]] was spoken to, the party will also learn that the Knights of Neverwinter never made it here either.

CORLIE: Five great cities of the Sword Coast have combiend forces to restore this fortress. [[Mithral Hall]], [[Neverwinter]], [[Waterdeep]], [[Baldur's Gate]] and [[Elturel]]. We expected it would give us better visibility into trouble on the frontier, on places in the edge of our sight but...

RENAER: Speak plain, Lady Halvachar. You are among friends.

CORLIE: Necromancers seem to have targeted [[Ironspine Keep]]. Just yesterday a legion of skeletons assailed the walls again for the fifth time in the last tenday. The masons are doing as much as they can but...

*As she says this, she looks towards the tired workers who all seem close to the brink of breaking down. The walls of Ironspine Keep may be strong, but how long until the ones keeping it up can no longer maintain it?*

BALTHAZAAR: If this is true, then the convoy cannot move past Ironspine Keep without risk of being attacked. 

RENAER: We can't hold back hundreds of undead. Let alone-

*As Balthazaar and Renaer discuss with you, the rest of the convoys stop just outside of Ironspine. The civilians all begin to filter in along with the children lead by Beyda. Alain and Cacafonix disembark from their horses and join you.*

ALAIN: Vellin and Kagain say that they spotted activity to the north and north west. They've gone up to take a closer look, should be back with us before nightfall.
