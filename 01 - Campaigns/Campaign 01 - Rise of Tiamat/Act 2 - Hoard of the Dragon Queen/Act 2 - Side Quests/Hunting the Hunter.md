### TRIGGER

This quest is given by [[Thelvin Kaervath]] in [[Berdusk]]. He will be found either in the [[Patriarch's Rest]] or the [[Flourished Flagon]] in [[Berdusk]]. If the first part of this quest is not completed, he will complete it himself and part 2 continues in [[Baldur's Gate]].

### PART 1 - The Runaway

*You make your way through the plains outside Berdusk once more, heading towards the ruins of the Sanguine Dawn Inn. According to Thelvin, the place has been abandoned ever since the Reclamation War took place. The cobbled road you travel on fades as the Vale welcomes you deeper into its soft grasslands. In the far distance, you can see the treeline of the Reaching Woods approaching closer and closer.*

*When you're close enough to get a better look, the remnants of the building. It looks tall and proud, despite the passage of time wearing away at its joints. The windows have been boarded up, and what little detail you can discern from distance doesn't make it obvious that anyone has been occupying it at all.*

*You approach as quietly as you can, making sure not to wake any potential foes from within. As you inch closer it becomes evidently clear that the place is unoccupied. There is no hearth burning, no footsteps creaking on the wooden floorboards. You are completely utterly alone.*

![[GL_ThievesGuildHideout_Raid_Night.jpeg]]

#### N1. Prison
*The doorway to what looks to be an old prison stands open. On the desk are faded papers and a logbook of every person that passed through these halls.

There is a note on the table that reads:

	`Those damned Orcs are at it again. Uppity Greenskins need to be taught a lesson. All this 'rebellion' talk. As if they could outpower the First Folk.`

#### N2. Reception
*The entryway into the Inn looks quite dusty and unused. A noticable dip on the floor starting from behind the bar suggests a commonly travelled-path for the person who used to work here.*

On a DC 10 Investigation check, players can find a stash coin with 1 Platinum.

### N3. Bedrooms
*Each of these shared rooms carry the dusty smell of... well, dust. A crunchy sock filled with Gods know what is on the foot of a bed, untouched and unwashed for months.*

A rat will be hiding in the corner of the bottom left room. If it is spoken to with Speak with Animals, it will say that a few people come in and out of this old Inn. In the room above, there is a journal which states the following:

	`That woman Serana came by earlier today. Said she couldn't stop thinking about... well, it hurts to even say. To think that they could be capable of doing such a thing?`

#### N4. Garden Room
*The plants in this room have largely been untended to, though it appears that almost all of them have survived. In the back corner of the room is a lone desk with the gardener's logbook.*

On a DC 15 Investigation check, players will notice that the logbook makes no sense. The quantities written within seem to be completely random. IT is likely that the 'Inn' was used as a front, likely by a Thieves Guild.

#### N5. Library
*This old library's walls are full of dusty shelves and a long-forgotten reading nook. What catches your attention is there is a trail along the ground of fresh footprints. Humanoid in size and shape. It appears that whoever Thelvin thinks lives here is quite the reader.*

#### N6. Bakery
*The smell of mould is heavy in the air here. Several rodents skitter about the floor of this long-forgotten bakery, and white power from a spilled bag of flour is dusted with what looks like small droppings.*

On a DC 15 Investigation check, players will discover a Container of Heat and Frost.

#### N7. Stables
*The scent of old and recently dropped manure fills your lungs. The stables are uncharacteristically well-stocked for a place allegedly 'abandoned'.*

#### N8. Stockroom
*The smell of freshly-baked food and water hits your nose. The steady sounds of an underwater stream fill your ears, and up ahead you see light. From what you can make out there's an entire inner chamber between each of these buildings.*

Near the winch that lowers the crates into the underground river, there is a golden statue of a pig. On a DC 18 Investigation check, players can discern it's worth at least 800 gold. 

There is also a journal near one of the chests. If one of the players decides to try to open it, an explosion rings out and players must make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw. On a failure, they take 8d6 Cold damage as the Glyph of Warding explodes or half as much on a success.

#### N9. Guard Tower
*This circular stone 'tower' stands at about 2 stories tall, but stepping inside there doesn't seem to be a way up, nor a floor on the second story either. A solitary bed with a journal on top is all that remains of note in this room.*

The note reads:

	`Amory said we'd only be here for as long as it takes for Anduin to come crawling. Tired of living out in the woods. Berdusk is nothing compared to Waterdeep. I just want to go home.`

#### N10. Butcher
*This room appears to be a butcher's shop, still being used to gut local hunts. An especially sharp knife glints on one of the meats, glistening in the day/moonlight.* 

If the players pick up the dagger, it is immediately perceived as magical. Identifying it will reveal it is a Dagger of First Light.

#### N11. Smithy
*This old smithy appears unused, and what remains within consists of rusted blades and shields.*

On a DC 8 Investigation check, players will find a set of Tinker's Tools and 50 Gold.

#### N12. Map Room
*This old office is lined with maps of the surrounding area. A layout of the inside of Twilight Hall sits on the table, marked with danger zones and entry/exit points. There is something written in red ink on the bottom of the map that states 'You have to be extremely stupid to try and steal from Jaheria. Burn this now and don't let the Harpers find out you're trying to rob them.'*

#### N13. Scrolls Room
*This room is full of scrolls containing maps for buildings that likely no longer exist in Berdusk. Several papers make mention of knowing Clan Chain Smasher's plans to unite the Orcs and reclaim Berdusk. It appears that a lot of this place's previous occupants have moved on to greener pastures.*

On a DC 10 Arcana check, players will find two scrolls of Magic Missile and a scroll of Revivify.

#### N14. Amory's Hideout
*This central room is well lit from above by a lit chandelier composed of candles mounted on goat horns.  A large table makes up the majority of the decor here, and a map of what appears to be the Patriarch's Rest is sprawled out on the surface of it.*

Entering this room from the North will trigger trap as the door is also marked with a Glyph of Warding. Players must make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw, taking 8d6 Cold damage on a failure and half as much on a success.

*Scattered around the room are missives and bounty notices for several humans. One of them is most notably Thelvin, allegedly wanted for crimes against Waterdeep. A dagger pierces one of his pictures on a table, with its eyes crossed out with red ink.*

*A set of letters litter the table adjacent to the main one. Some of the notes here seem to be written on freshly paper, while others look like they've gone through the test of time. Three letters stand out to you.*

The first note says:

	`Thelvin has been spotted in Triel.`
	`Scouts are saying he's on the hunt for me. Hah, me?`
	`Brute thinks he can outsmart this Ranger, well I don't think so.`
	`Humans are always so stupid, no wonder Audric got himself executed.
	'I am the hunter, he is the hunted.`

The second note says:

	`It looks like the little brat Audric had following after him is nearby too.`
	'Didn't think the brat had it in him to keep going after Mellara.'
	`According to the locals he's part of some hero group.
	'Apparently working with the Harpers on some mission for Jaheria.`
	`If he's with Thelvin, I don't care what he's doing. He's as good as dead.`
	`That and the cunt always annoyed me.`
	`I'll put him in the ground and reunite him with Audric.`

The third note says:

	`Waterdeep is so far away, but I will return one day soon.`
	`Once I bring in their heads, it'll all be over and I can retire in peace.
	`First, the Greypools. Then the lizard.`


When the players have finished reading through the notes, [[Amory Silent-Step]] will appear in the doorway to the North. If the trap has not been triggered, he will have set up an ambush with three Assassins. 

AMORY: There you are. Was wondering how long it would take for you to track me down once that cunt set you on me.

*Amory leans against the doorframe, holding his longbow in one hand and lowering his green cloak with the other. His long brown hair cascades down his temples and his back. He looks far more rugged than the last time you saw him, from what little memory you have of interacting with him. He smirks as he beholds your party, waiting for you to respond.*

#### If [[Caradil Anduin]] is present
AMORY: You know, I've always hated you, Caradil. You're a sanctimonious fuck, never one to admit he was wrong about anything. It's funny because you were never smart enough to be that sure of yourself either. You were always shoot first, ask questions later. Exactly the type of scum that brought the Renblades tumbling down. Mindless drones waiting for their orders.

AMORY: I had a huge grin on my face when I found out Audric was getting executed by Neverember, you know. Because I knew then and there that even you in your thick brain had to know you were wrong about his character. But you ran away, because obviously you did. Coward.

AMORY: You want answers? It's too late for that.

As the fight happens, [[Amory Silent-Step]] will continue to taunt Caradil round by round until he is dead. In no particular order he will say the following at the top of his turn.
1. Tell me, dog. How did it feel leaving those people behind in Shade Rest to die, eh? Another responsibility you ran away from just to save your own hide.
2. Your Gnome friend must be rolling in his grave knowing you doomed a town to die because of your greed. I tried to tell Audric, once a thief always a thief. Looks like you haven't changed.
3. How quickly do you think the Elf and the Giant will die, eh? I give them a few weeks before they drop. But what do you care, right? As long as you're safe.
4. When Audric took you in I knew that it would harken our downfall. The old man had too much of a soft spot for you. That's why he's dead.
5. Hero of the Sword Coast? How long before the going gets too tough and you 'disappear' again, dog? How much do you have left in you, weeks? days? Maybe you want to run away now.

At the end of the fight, [[Amory Silent-Step]] will continue to taunt [[Caradil Anduin]] to his dying breath. He will grit through bloodied teeth and get the last word out.

AMORY: End it, dog. I go to my Ancestors proudly. You? Heh... can't say the same about you. 

	`If you're reading this, then you bested me. When you go back to Thelvin, I hope you choke on his cock. You sniveling, brainless idiot.`

Looting his body will yield a Scimitar of Spite and a Scimitar, +1. At the end of this encounter, players will need to head back to [[Berdusk]] to inform [[Thelvin Kaervath]] of [[Amory Silent-Step]]'s death. If [[Caradil Anduin]] is present, he will already be gone with nothing but a note stating the following:

	`Caradil`
	
	`Had to stay on the move. Got on a ship heading South.`
	`I know you'll survive Amory, and thank you for the help.`
	`It's just not safe for us to be seen together, not yet.`
	`I'll find you again real soon.`
	
	`Thelvin`

He will then leave 500 Gold behind for Caradil.

### PART 2 - The Hunted

Upon arriving at the [[Sylvira-Van]], the companion that was chosen to stay behind during [[Chapter 1.0 - The Great Hunt]]'s climax will be missing. Following the trail of their disappearance will lead them to the smoking town of Triel where they find the following note:

```
"You'll see them again soon.
Sooner if you give up this hunt."
```

==This section of the quest will only occur if [[Caradil Anduin]] is present. No other scenario exists wherein this will occur. In the alternate group settings, there is no reason for [[Thelvin Kaervath]] to go after the party's friends.==
