### TRIGGER

This quest is given by [[Traxigor]] in [[Baldur's Gate]]. [[Glass]] will inform the players on their second day in the Gate that [[Sylvira Savikas]] informed him a 'friend' of hers is in the city who can help him enchant the caravan with the sending station. The quest begins upon meeting [[Traxigor]] in the [[Blushing Mermaid]].

_____
### PART I - Essays and Ramblings 

*As you settle yourselves down in the mermaid, you hear a skittering coming from the ceiling. The barkeep, a human male in his mid-40s, lets out a groan of frustration in response and takes out what looks like a long broom. He taps on the ceiling with force and after five or so taps the skittering stops. After he dusts himself off he walks back over behind the bar again.*

*A few minutes pass before you hear the tapping once more, but closer to your table. In the distance someone drops a glass and everyone glances over. In that same moment, part of the ceiling collapses and a small furry creature falls right onto the middle of your table. It appears to be an otter, wearing red robes and holding a wand in its spare hand.*

TRAXIGOR: Oooh bother. Ah! It's you all again.

Archwizard [[Traxigor]] is in [[Baldur's Gate]] on business for [[Candlekeep]] after [[Orenius Savikas]] convinced him to return to the fold under one condition; he be allowed to do field work as opposed to being sequestered in his tower in the Great Library. He has come to [[Baldur's Gate]] in pursuit of a lead on a 'misbehaving book' in [[Candlekeep]].

TRAXIGOR: The book is called Mazfroth's Mighty Digressions (he takes a moment to scan the room and clicks his fingers, taking one of the patron's drinks with a fast mage hand), named after its author Mazfroth Gethur. It's a collection of eclectic essays that often go on long tangents. Not one for those with small attention spans.

*As Traxigor speaks, he downs the drink he stole and pulls out what appears to be a small pipe from inside his coat. He lights it up with a quick firebolt before inhaling and exhaling a puff of white smoke.*

TRAXIGOR: Now, the problem. I've never read it myself, but an Avowed tried to read it and... well, it turned into a gingwatzim tried to eat him. You can see how that would be a problem for [[Candlekeep]], yes? It's not the only book to transform into an ectoplasmic monster either. The other two books were the Dark Hunger and Fallen Tethyamar.

*Traxigor lets out another puff of smoke and takes a sip from one of the nearby drinks before he shapeshifts into the form of a handsome young man that passed by earlier. As he stretches out, he orders a drink for himself at the bar and comes right back.*

TRAXIGOR: While this all sounds very interesting to you I'm pretty sure, I find it quite repugnant to talk and walk around in this form. I was eh... doing reconnaissance on Sylvira's tower just in case one of those Changelings ever returned and found out you're in need of a sending station for your caravan, yes? Do this for me and I'll help your Kobold install it. Free of charge.

If the players agree to take on this quest, he will then call on his companion on the far side of the room. A Tiefling Purple Dragon Knight named Valor.

TRAXIGOR: VALOR! Come over here, tell them what you know.

*With broad shoulders and a permanent scowl on her face, Valor appears to be an intimidating but young-looking Tiefling knight. Her skin his bright yellow, and her eyes glow red with a fierce determination in them. Or perhaps annoyance, given Traxigor's presence.*

VALOR: Shout out louder, why don't you? You said we'd be discrete.

TRAXIGOR: Yes yes tell them what you know so we can hide you away again.

Valor will disclose the following information about herself and where she purchased her books. She will not reveal her true identity as a Purple Dragon Knight of Cormyr, but characters with a passive insight of 15 or higher will note that she seems quite strong and disciplined like a soldier.
- Thirteen days ago, she arrived at [[Candlekeep]] with Mazfroth's Mighty Digressions as her entrance fee to get into the fortress.
- She purchased a book from a market stall in the Wide here in [[Baldur's Gate]]. She doesn't remembermuch about the stall because she was in a hurry to leave (it reeks of greed). 
- She remembers that the corner she purchased it at was "not the most reputable looking area" of the marketplace and that it smelled of cinnamon nearby.
- After perusing the library and right before she could leave, the Avowed informed her that her gift has attacked one of them. The wizard killed the monster before it could harm anyone else.

TRAXIGOR: Illusory books. If they were copied by gingwatzims that means that the culprit have the real books. If you can find them and give me the books I can bring them back to Candlekeep.

### PART II - Stalling for time

*As you enter the Wide you behold the magical lights, luxurious boutiques and expensive restaurants all around you. Unlike Elturel, here in the Upper City of Baldur's Gate the lavishness of it all is not reserved or held back whatsoever. What you do take note of, however, is that the elegantly dressed seems to treat those less well-off with disdain. The Upper City is decorated and pampered like the body of a nobleman. Unapologetic, exclusive decadence. 

*While walking through the Wide it becomes ever more clear to you that the Wide is not the easiest place to navigate. Not by a long shot. Let alone find a specific stall in. As you push past the crowds of nobles, commoners and fellow adventurers, a near overstimulating experience follows soon after.*

The following named stalls can be found in the Wide:
- Autumn's Breads, a street bakery with fresh pastries, breads, and cakes for 1 sp each.
- Coppers to Crowns, an "antiques" shop selling a miscellany of trinkets and oddities (roll 2d4 times on the [Trinkets table](https://5e.tools/items.html#trinket_phb) in the [Player's Handbook](https://5e.tools/book.html#phb)). Each trinket costs 2d10 cp.
- Gems for All, a shop with a variety of gemstones, all worth 50 gp or less.
- Heath's Hot Drinks, a stall offering cups of mulled wine, hot cider, and strong teas for 2 sp each. In the summer, the sign is repainted to read "Heath's Cool Drinks" and the stall serves chilled fruit juice and iced tea.
- Heap's Paint, a male tiefling named Jasper.
- Wizards of the Wide, a tent bursting with dozens of arcane foci and [spell scrolls](https://5e.tools/items.html#spell%20scroll_dmg), including scrolls of [comprehend languages](https://5e.tools/spells.html#comprehend%20languages_phb), [detect magic](https://5e.tools/spells.html#detect%20magic_phb), [feather fall](https://5e.tools/spells.html#feather%20fall_phb), [find familiar](https://5e.tools/spells.html#find%20familiar_phb), [mage armor](https://5e.tools/spells.html#mage%20armor_phb), [magic missile](https://5e.tools/spells.html#magic%20missile_phb), [shield](https://5e.tools/spells.html#shield_phb), and [unseen servant](https://5e.tools/spells.html#unseen%20servant_phb) (25 gp each).

The players can find the specific stall that Valor mentioned on a successful DC 15 Investigation check. Otherwise, they spend over an hour trying to search for it before finally coming across the stall. When they get there, they see a table full of commonplace books with some rare and expensive ones put on the back wall. The sign above the stall reads 'Amberdune Books.'

*As you approach the stall, a young man with olive skin, dark hair and spectacles on his eyes greets you. He appears to be wearing clothes that suggest an Amnian background. His loose, flowing fabrics are only tightened by a bright red sash on his waist. He's accompanied by a middle-aged woman dressed in similar clothing who seems to be staring off into the distance.*

INBAR: Greetings dear ones, my name is Inbar, how can I help you?

Although it's located in an out-of-the-way corner of the Wide, Amberdune Books always has a few customers perusing its wares. This market stall is stocked with legitimate but commonplace books (25 gp each), as well as illusory copies of rare and expensive books (250 gp each). The stall does not sell spellbooks. 

***Speaking with the Manager.*** After their introduction to the quest, players will likely ask to speak with their manager or owner. When a request similar to this is made, Inbar will cast Sending and call upon Korvala who arrives within 10 minutes of being called.

*As you wait for the manager, a woman with similar appearance to the other two walks with a strong gait towards your party. She appears to be in her mid-to-late 40s. She wears a purple dress, with a dark purple sash going across her chest and a cowl that conceals her hair. Only her face is visible to you. She raises one of her thick black eyebrows as she approaches.*

KORVALA: Is there something the matter, Inbar?

Korvala takes her duties as the owner of Amberdune Books seriously. She denies all accusations of deception with curt professionalism and threatens to call for the guards if characters are too aggressive. A successful DC 15 Insight check reveals that Korvala isn't being entirely truthful. To avoid making a scene, she invites characters who see through her lies back to the Amberdune Pack's hideout as long as they are willing to talk peacefully.

***Tailing the Jackalweres.*** The characters can also find the Amberdune hideout by following pack members after their shift. If the characters succeed on a DC 13 group Stealth check, the jackalweres don't notice that they're being followed. If they do notice the characters, they hurry to alert Korvala but don't engage unless the characters do so first.

The Amberdune jackalweres are not hostile unless provoked. They attack only as a last resort, preferring to use their Sleep Gaze to subdue foes, then flee. They are notably more reluctant to fight spellcasters and characters using silvered or magic weapons.
