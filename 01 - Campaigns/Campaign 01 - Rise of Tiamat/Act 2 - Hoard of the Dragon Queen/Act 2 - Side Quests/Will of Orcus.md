### PART I - Scouting Ahead

*As the meeting begins, Khasar shuffles through a duffle bag and brings out a rudimentary map of what appears to be a temple. Marked on the bottom is obliterated script written in what you can only guess is an ancient language. Judging by age, it looks at least to be a few hundred years old but the map itself likely a traced copy of the original.*

KHASAR: This map here was given to us by a couple from the Western Heartlands who did battle with Cultists of Orcus over three decades ago.

*Khasar spreads the map out fully and pins each end with four heavy marked stones in order to keep it open. His eyes gleam as the stones activate and projects what looks to be a floating hologram of the temple.*

KHASAR: I'll get to the point. The Second Talon sent me up north to investigate Ironspine Keep and stop the undead, though we couldn't have anticipated we'd be dealing with a Zulkir of this strength.

ALAIN: Zulkir or not, no common Necromancer can raise dead at this level. More than likely this ancient Temple still carries some residual energy from when the Cultists were operating in the area. If the Zulkir is anywhere, they're likely deep within.

KHASAR: Exactly. I propose we visit this Temple and see what we're dealing with. If [[Venomfang]] was operating here, his phylactery is likely nearby. A Dragon is one thing, but a Dracolich is another problem entirely. He can't be allowed to continue his unnatural lifespan.

The Temple of Orcus is a day's travel away from [[Ironspine Keep]]. After the meeting is concluded, [[Alain Gilfont]] will remark that he is able to assist them in this mission, but [[Khasar the Blooded]] will recommend against it, stating that there's a high chance that there may be another attack on [[Ironspine Keep]]. [[Corlie Halvachar]] will say whoever doesn't accompany them will instead pursue [[Shadowsteel Citadel]].

Prior to leaving, [[Renaer Neverember]] will remark that it's not safe for the [[Mutated Dragon]] to be close to the Zulkir and advises they're left behind there. Unless the Invisibility upgrade was purchased, [[Glass]] will also recommend against taking the caravan as it's far too conspicuous. Regardless of the amount of party members present, [[Khasar the Blooded]] will accompany the party.

Doing this quest is entirely optional, though if the Zulkir is not killed they will simply resurrect [[Venomfang]] through his phylactery and attack [[Ironspine Keep]] once more. Alternatively, [[Khasar the Blooded]] and the able-bodied members of the convoy can be sent in place of the party. This will result in everyone except [[Khasar the Blooded]] and [[Alain Gilfont]] perishing.

*The journey to the temple is short and uneventful. A crust of snow covers the ground, and the biting wind is the only foe you encounter. Clouds obscure the peak of the windswept mountain before you. Khasar moves ahead, and for a second he disappears into the snow. When you catch up to where you thought he was, he is no longer there. Instead, there is a tan-skinned elven woman standing at 6 and a half feet tall. Her golden hair and bright green eyes make her look both fierce and beautiful.*

ZALLA: Hold there, interlopers! Thou trespassest where thou dost not belong. Speak thy purpose.

*The elven woman points her longbow at the party and the arrow begins to glow bright green. All along her face are tribal markings seemingly tattooed onto her skin with off-white paint. Her armour carries the obliterated symbol of a god beyond recognition. Her stance suggests she's a ranger.*

This is [[Tazmikella 'Zalla Temik' the Copper]] in her 'humanoid' disguise. She is quick to threaten the party at any sign of veering from her line of questioning and is unafraid to injure a member of the group in order to get her point across. 

ZALLA: A wretched play unfurls below the depths of the earth. Within, a vexing plague stalks thy halls once more. These asps serves a foul creature born in darkness, whom thy holy father abhors above all else. Thou shalt enter and slay them and consecrate this temple. Tis my command. Refuse it, and thou shall feel the wrath of the heavens bear down upon thee.

If the party attempts to threaten [[Tazmikella 'Zalla Temik' the Copper]], they must succeed on a DC 24 Wisdom saving throw or become frightened of her. Party members who have raised the [[Mutated Dragon]] will feel a sense of familiarity emanate from her. On a successful DC 25 Arcana check, they will be able to feel her immense power. If Zalla is asked why she doesn't enter herself, she will remark that there is a hallow placed on the Temple which prevents 'her kind' from entering. As a reward, she promises to allow the party to keep any spoils from within and to not kill them upon exiting as she senses their 'corrupted spirits' from here.

ZALLA: Thou art abominations, walking amongst the sheep. Were I not merciful, I would have cleaved thee from navel to neck, then given thy remains unto the Treefather’s embrace. Yet my generosity hath allowed thee to draw breath a while longer. Thou shouldst be grateful for the privilege.

*After dealing with the party, Zalla's eyes will flash green and she'll disappear into the snow. Khasar comes into view a few footsteps in as he stands at the base of the mountain. Carved into its base, the old temple’s entrance yawns ten feet wide by twenty feet high. A few feet inside, a rough-hewn staircase descends into the cold bedrock. Eerie green light emanates from goat skulls embedded in the walls at regular intervals.*

KHASAR: There you all are. We're here. You've all got the numbers, so you lead the way.

This text seems to be written in Ancient Draconic, even if you understand the words parsing it is especially difficult given the dialect. However, the intention seems clear enough. This book describes the former Chosen of Mystra, Sammaster, becoming driven to fulfill a prophecy written in the time of Netheril by an ancient Archwizard named Maglas known as the 'Chronicle of Years to Come'.

In this text, Maglas describes several cryptic prophecies. It took Sammaster decades to translate the text, but eventually he managed to get most of it. The majority of the prophecies are all lost to time, but Sammaster seems to have been hyper-focused on a specific line.

"And naught will be left save shattered thrones with no rulers. But the dead dragons shall rule the world entire, and…"

It's obvious now that Sammaster believed Maglas forsaw that Faerun would eventually be subject to the rule of undead dragons. Thus, this was this spark that made him form the first Cult of the Dragon.

This tome is written in a more recent version of Draconic, thought he majority of it still reads as far older than its modern counterparts. At first it reads like a history book, but it's blatantly skewed towards the Cult of the Dragon.

The first few chapters describes the centuries of Sammaster's work with creating dracoliches before his first death at the hands of the Harpers lead by a Chosen of Lathander. It then moves to several pages of his eventual resurrection as Lich and how he took advantage of the Dracorage in order to secure his power before being finally slain once more at the hands of several Chosens.

The next few chapters then discuss the Time of Troubles just over 140 years ago and how Tiamat was forced to walk Toril temporarily as a 'mortal' being. It talks of how she was momentarily defeated by the demi-god Gilgeam before she brought herself back by 'ressambling' her avatar. It boldly proclaims there is no end to the Dragon Queen, and how nothing short of Asgorath itself could put an end to her.

The thesis of the book is clear, if defeating Sammaster himself took nothing short of multiple Chosen, then the next Chosen of Tiamat would be equally as strong, if not more for Tiamat only chooses those who can match her in tenacity. Its author proports that the sages of the Cult believe that the arrival of the next Chosen is imminent and will be a Sorcerer come from the sandy ports of Calimshan.
