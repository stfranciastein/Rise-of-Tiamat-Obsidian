### **1.1 Introduction**

An entire month has passed since the events of [[Chapter 8.0 - Herd of the Dragon]]. The reason for the party being here will depend on how the final fight with [[Mellara, the Terror of the Sword Coast]] went.

Governor [[Tarbaw Nighthill]] from the town of [[Greenest]] will be throwing a Great Hunt. The guest list includes the adventuring party from Act 1 aka the new Heroes, the old [[Heroes of Phandalin]], dozens of nobles as well as emissaries from the [[Talons of the Raven Queen]], [[Emerald Enclave]], [[Harpers]]  and [[Lords' Alliance]]. If the party from Act 1 did not survive the events of [[Chapter 8.0 - Herd of the Dragon]] then the adventurers will meet for the first time in [[Greenest]]. If there are new players at the table, or if there are new characters for any reason, it is important that you use [[Rise of Tiamat - Character Hooks]] to show ways on how this should be done.

[[Greenest]] is the biggest town along the Uldoon Trail, and serves as an important hub for travellers coming to and from the cities. As a result of its location, many merchants ply their trade in Greenest. When the adventurers arrive, the town is in the midst of preparing for the the Great Hunt. The townsfolk are beyond excited and will tell them that they look forward to this event as it brings not only commerce but all sorts of interesting characters to their otherwise peaceful town. 

The Great Hunt is a week-long hunting event that Governor [[Tarbaw Nighthill]] likes to throw at least once every four years. The original festival was done in reverence of the town's patron deity [Mielikki](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Mielikki), though most of the hunters that participate these days do so for the sport of it. The legendary ranger [[Drizzt Do'Urden]] participated in one of the first Great Hunts, boosting its popularity across Faerun.

### **1.2 The Second Day**

The second part of the Great Hunt is the beginning of the festival. There are a myriad of different activities that the party and the townsfolk can participate in:

1. An Archery Competition: Run by a Drow named Razzle.
   Razzle is about 6ft 7 inches tall. He is quite lanky but has a somewhat impressive sinewy figure. His short white hair is tied into a ponytail and his purple eyes are framed by thick white eyebrows. More importantly, he also claims to be [[Drizzt Do'Urden]]'s first cousin. On a DC 8 Insight check, it becomes painfully obvious he is lying. On a DC 5 History check, it's even more painfully obvious that the legendary ranger's cousin would have no business running an archery stand in a village like this. 
   
   During the competition, players must simply hit 3 targets in a row using a -5 bow. A DC 5 investigation check reveals it has been crudely made to rig it against the people participating. Winning the competition gets the player 150 gold and a Cloak of Billowing.
   
2. A Strength Competition: Run by a Goliath named Gribnak the Destroyer.
   Gribnak is an 8 foot tall, well-mannered gentle giant. He has a thick blonde beard, sandstone skin and is shirtless. He wears a finely-made loose-legged trousers with an intricately patterned sash. 
   
   The objective is to throw the bag as far as possible (hammer toss). Each character is given three tries to get it as far as possible. There are three tiers of prices for your final 'toss', a 5-10 gets the participant a lollipop, an 11-15 gets them a cheaply made mug with 'Gureenist' (mispelling of [[Greenest]]) and a +16 nets the adventurer a giant stuffed toy of various animals or of [[Drizzt Do'Urden]], [[Torbar Dragonsbane]] or [[Mordenkainen]]. The toy is a Talking Doll.

3. A Pie Eating Competition: Run by a stout Dwarven man named Stout.
   Stout is a round-bellied Dwarven man with short red hair, thick mutton chops and is wearing a sleeveless shirt and brown trousers.
   
   The pie eating competition can either be run with all of the adventurers or with the adventurers and 4 other npcs. The goal is to attain the highest number by the end of the 5 rounds of Constitution checks without ever rolling below a five. At the end of the competition, the winner gets an apron that allows them to summon any type of fruit pie once per long rest by uttering 'I want more pie!'

4. A Dunking Competition: Run by a human woman named Alicia and a gnome named Bryn.
   Alicia is from Baldur's Gate and speaks like someone from the lower city. She has short brown hair and wears a shirt under a corset. Bryn is a silly man wearing a finely made garment.
   
   The adventurer must pay 3 silver per try to attempt to dunk the gnome. However, what the characters don't know is that the game is rigged and they must get +22 on a strength check. A DC 15 Insight check reveals that something isn't quite right with the gnome and he's far too comfortable sitting in that chair. Confronting Alicia reveals Bryn just doesn't like getting wet so they made it close to impossible for them to lose. They will pay the adventurers with an Alchemy Jug if they let them keep going.

5. An Acrobatics/Athletics Course: Run by a Dragonborn Monk from Beregost named Xaloxan
   Xaloxan is a Silver Dragonborn and a Monk of [Bahamut](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Bahamut) working for the [[Order of the Gauntlet]]. He was once a devout follower of the Platinum Dragon but left the Order to 'retire' to a less dangerous line of work.
   
   The game is simple. Characters must pass 5 Acrobatics or Athletics skill checks in order. The DC begins at 5 and goes up by 5 each time. By the end of the course, if the adventurers succeed in running it they receive the Slippers of Spider Climbing.
   
6. A tent run by [[Marianne]] the fortune teller from [[Shade Rest]] will also be present. This time, however, she will be strictly working as a fortune-teller and not plying her trade as a Hag. Mechanically, she will foreshadow the events of each player's backstory.

7. A cup shuffling game run by a wizard named Neesha. 
   At first, this lanky human wizard seems untrustworthy, but he will reassure adventurers that they are overthinking the game. 
   
   He places a 6 sided dice on one cup next to two others and he will shuffle it with magic. There are two things he asks for, where the dice is and if the dice is currently 3 or below or 4 and above. Winning 1 prize nets the players an Orb of Time and winning both gets them an additional Orb of Direction. If they can precisely guess what side the dice landed on, they also get a Rust Bag of Tricks.
   
In addition to these minigames, the players are also able to buy special foods at the pop-up vendors in [[Greenest]]. This includes Baldur's Crepes, Waterdhavian Gelato (flavoured with literal feelings), Greenest Corndogs, Milkshakes and other sorts of festival junk foods.

At the end of the day, [[Torbar Dragonsbane]] arrives at [[Greenest]] accompanied by [[Cormyc Ravenshade]]. Cormyc will retire to his room while Torbar will greet the aventurers, having met them before in [[Chapter 6.0 - The Mentor]] and/or [[Chapter 7.0 - Deathstruck]]. He tells them that he has been waiting for this event for such a long time as he is always glad to time out of his schedule killing monsters out in the Sword Coast to watching others do the same.

He will invite the adventurers to drink with him for the night. In order to succeed a night out with the Half-Dragon, the adventurers must succeed on three Constitution saving throws, starting at 10, then 15 then 20. At the end of the night Torbar will name them a true hero in the making and continue drinking. If the adventurer who participated in the Pie eating competition joins, they will make all of their rolls with Disadvantage.

### **1.3 The Third Day**

On the third day of the festival is the actual hunt itself. The rules of the Great Hunt are simple:
1. The hunt begins at noon on the third day of the festival. 
2. No outside help. Every member of the hunting party must be declared to Huntmaster [[Delaan Winterhound]] at the beginning.
3. The hunt lasts for four days, ending on the seventh day at precisely 12pm.
4. You cannot harm your fellow competitors.
5. You can only hunt monstrosities.
6. Whichever group can bring back the most impressive kill will be given the title of Grand Hunter/Huntress, 5000 gp, 1997 sp and 13cp. This year's prize also includes the deed to a private manor in [[Greenest]] itself which [[Tarbaw Nighthill]] previously owned.

The teams will have four days to go out into the wilderness and return with a trophy taken from their target. As a part of the festival, the adventurers are given a loan of an enchanted cart and two horses for ease of transport, as well as a Bag of Colding which they must return at the end of the Hunt or pay a fee of 500 Gold. While this event is happening, many merchants from across the Sword Coast will set up shop here, effectively turning the town into a hub for the duration of the festival. Most importantly, the party is not allowed to bring the [[Sylvira-Van]] with them.

The third part of the Great Hunt occurs at the end of the five days. Each trophy is analysed by the judges; [[Tarbaw Nighthill]], [[Torbar Dragonsbane]], [[Delaan Winterhound]] and [[Aestree Silvermoon]]. This happens concurrent to the celebration at the end of the hunt, where all the participants get to relax and boast about their kills while awaiting judgement at Greenest Keep. At the end of judging, the winner is announced before desert. The next two days are reserved for further celebration.

### **1.4 The Hunt and Potential Targets**

The adventurers will be given leads on 3 possible creatures; a Chimera, a Hydra and a Medusa. All of these monsters are far enough away from one another that hunting more than one within the time constraints is impossible. Each journey will take precisely 1 and a half day to travel to, and 1 and a half day to return from, giving the players 24 hours of 'free' time to do as they please before or during the Hunt. Every single night, a small bell on their rented caravan will ring signifying the end of the day.

- [[Aestree Silvermoon]] can be sweet-talked with a DC 15 Persuasion check into giving the party the precise location of the Medusa. It is in an underground ruin, but it is the most dangerous of the three due to its petrifying gaze. One of the rival adventurers will stumble upon the Medusa's lair and will be found petrified by the time the party arrives. 
- If [[Torbar Dragonsbane]] is spoken to, he will say he's been particularly eager to see a Hydra be brought back as he believes it to be the most exciting creature out of all of them. If one of the characters is a member of the [[Talons of the Raven Queen]] or if the players drank with him, he will also tell them about [[Daelaan Winterhound]]'s pick for the hunt.
- [[Daelaan Winterhound]] will openly tell players at the beginning of the hunt that a local Chimera has moved into the area. Since its arrival, it has been a huge problem for the hunters in Greenest as it has taken residence in the nearby woods and chases away wildlife.

It ultimately does not matter which of these 3 the party decides to hunt as the other hunters are far less powerful than a group of level 5 adventurers. They will win the Great Hunt regardless of their target as these creatures are at least CR 6. The three battle maps that are attached which will be their final arenas.

Lord [[Theodore Goldmane]] can also be potentially overheard bragging about how easy the competition will be for them and the [[Lords' Alliance]]. If the players choose to investigate, they will find that [[Theodore Goldmane]] has set up a meeting with a defector from the [[Emerald Enclave]] at a small hut in the Troll Mountains. This hunter will direct him and the members of his party to the lair of an Adult Red Dragon and will help them slay it, though [[Theodore Goldmane]] is an accomplished fighter and gets the final blow.

### **1.4 The Cult Strikes Back**

At the end of the Great Hunt, all there is left to do is announce the winner. As outlined in 1.3, the adventurers will always win as they are hunting CR 6 monsters while the other hunters will have brought back Direwolves, Gnolls and other smaller creatures. The nobles and locals are impressed, and Governor [[Tarbaw Nighthill]] invites the leader of the party to make an acceptance speech. If any nobles sponsored the players, now is the time to mention them. As the party leader finishes their speech, the Governor follows with this.

As his speech goes on, players will notice on a DC 15 perception check that some of the guards are whispering to one another. In the distance one of the guards will run towards the town walls. Moments later a bright spark of light flashes in the horizon, followed by a thunderclap 10 seconds later. A moment passes, then another bright light flashes, followed by a thunderclap 9 seconds later. Then again, bright light followed by a thunderclap 8 seconds later. The adventurers will have some time to react to the coming events, but ultimately they will not be able to escape. When the lightning flashes and the sound comes a second later, it's too late. That's when the adventurers see it, [[Lennithon]], an Ancient Blue Dragon sweeping down on [[Greenest]].

### 1.7 - The Dragon Hatchery

After the players have finished their business outside in the Raider Camp, all that's left to explore is the Dragon Hatchery. They will find that while it seems to be a place of importance, the Cultists themselves know not to get near there. *As the doors open for you, the interior of the cave greets you with a foreboding noise. Somewhere deep in the dark, dank cave a frightful scream pierces your ears. The two Dragonborn close the door behind you, and when it shuts out the last of the outside light, you are left with nothing but your thoughts as it seals itself.*

*The interior of the cave looks to be about 15 feet high. The inside is dimly lit, making it difficult to accurately see too far ahead. #### 1. Cave Entrance
The entrance to the cave is broad and tall, but the ceiling quickly lowers to a height of 15 feet. 

#### 2. Concealed Passage
The passage at the end of this alcove is deeply shadowed and hidden by a cleverly cut fold in the rock. It's obvious to anyone who walks to the end of the alcove, but from elsewhere in the cavern, it can be spotted only with a successful DC 20 Wisdom (Perception) check.

#### 3. Fungus Garden
The kobolds cultivate fungus in this cavern to supplement the meat brought in by the hunters. Mixed in among the mundane fungi are violet fungi. The drop-off from area 2 is 10 feet high. The stairs are trapped.

*The entrance to the cave ends here at a 10-foot drop-off. To your right, broad steps are roughly hewn into a natural stone ramp. The cavern below is carpeted with a profusion of fungi ranging from a few inches high to nearly as tall as a human adult. Two paths lead through the fungi: one on the right and one on the left.*

Nothing distinguishes the paths to casual inspection. The path on the right is flanked by four violet fungi, while the path on the left is free of these dangerous growths. The violet fungi can be spotted among all the other mushrooms with a successful DC 15 Intelligence (Nature) check, but only from the base of the steps, not from atop the ledge. Likewise, a careful inspection of the path reveals that only the left trail sees heavy use.

The stairs are constructed so that the lowest steps collapse into a ramp that dumps a character right at the base of the violet fungi. Roll any die as each character descends the steps; on an odd roll, the character triggers the trap. A character who is actively looking for a trap on the stairs can find the trapped step with a successful DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) check. The kobolds and guards know where to avoid stepping, of course.

#### 4. Stirge Lair
Characters notice dead bats on the floor (victims of the stirges). If that causes someone to look up, describe how the ceiling is carpeted with bats with a successful DC 10 Wisdom (Perception) check. 

To avoid startling the bats, characters must proceed very quietly. This requires a successful DC 10 Dexterity (Stealth) check. If three or more characters fail the check, the bats are alarmed by the noise, drop from the ceiling, and fly through the cavern in a blinding cloud of flapping and squeaking rodents. 

The bats present no danger, but their racket drowns out other noise and makes it impossible to see more than 5 feet. It also alerts the stirges, and the opportunistic bloodsuckers attack in the confusion. The stirges gain a +2 bonus to AC in the bat storm.

#### 5. Trash Dump
The floor drops down 10 feet at each ledge. Cultists, specifically the Kobolds, seem to use this portion of the cave as a trash dump. Along with normal sorts of refuse, such as broken pottery, rotted baskets, and mouse-chewed rope, they've also thrown out items that appear useful from a distance: discarded clothes, worn boots, tattered books, cracked lamps, and so forth. Some of these items came from prisoners who died, and others were taken in treasure raids and later deemed to be unworthy of Tiamat.

A strong, foul smell hangs in the air.

#### 6. Meat Locker
*At the base of these steeply descending steps, a curtain hangs across the passage. It is made from hundreds of heavy leather strips, each about the width of a human hand. The strips are fixed to the ceiling and are long enough to drag on the floor. The curtain extends from wall to wall. The leather comes from a variety of local animals and is badly cured. The curtain is several layers thick with no gaps, so you can't see through it at all.*

*This cavern is naturally cold. It hovers a few degrees above freezing year round, regardless of the season. Much of the meat brought in by the hunters feeds the cultists and the kobolds, but the extra is stored here for eventual use when the dragon eggs hatch and the ravenous hatchlings emerge. The carcasses range from very fresh to several months old. The meat is only cold, not frozen, so the older items are slowly going bad.*

*The smell of old blood assaults your nose. The floor is covered with dried puddles of it. Four floor-to-ceiling columns are spaced across the chamber, and chains have been strung between them like clothesline. Animal carcasses hang on hooks from the chains. You see gutted and skinned antelope, deer, goats, what might be big cats, and even a few small bears. Some of this meat has been here for a while if the smell is a reliable indicator.*

#### 7. Drake Nursery
*This room appears to be where the Cultists train their drakes. A rack along the southwest wall holds implements used in training the drakes: long poles with lassos at the end, used for snaring and controlling young drakes; leashes and collars; sharp prods; mock weapons made of wood; human-sized dummies stitched out of sailcloth and stuffed with straw, with ridiculous expressions painted on their faces.*

#### 8. Cultist Barracks
*Thin mattresses of straw covered with badly cured furs form small beds that are haphazardly positioned around the chamber. Rats and small lizards scurry through the food scraps and moldy wine skins littering the floor. This seems to be where Kobolds and low-ranking recruits stay when they need to maintain the Hatchery.*

#### 9. Dragon Shrine
*This chamber has been enlarged and reshaped from its original form. The floor and three of the walls are smooth, and stalactites and stalagmites have been polished into gleaming columns. Every surface glistens with moisture, and the air is warm and humid.*

*The flat walls of the chamber are decorated with shallow abstract carvings of dragons. Dragons' tails coil into intricate patterns and knots that flow across the walls. The creature portrayed in the northwest corner stands out: a five-headed dragon, rising from an erupting volcano. Other dragons, which seem dwarfed by the five-headed monstrosity, flock to its side.

*What stands out to you the most, however, are the three human sacrifices in front of this altar to the Dragon Queen. Tears streak down their face as they are forcibly bound to the floor on their knees, hands chained to the ground making them unable to escape. In front of them stands a silver-clad human woman dressed in what appears to be the armour of a Cleric of Bahamut. You recognise her right away as Frulam Mondath she turns to face your party.*

FRULAM: Interesting, so Matreous's pets have come at last. // Interesting, so Rhylrae's pets have come at last.

*Frulam leans against her glaive and the two Cultists behind her ready their weapons*

FRULAM: I've never been one for dramatics, but.

*Frulam slices all three of the human's necks in one swing of her glaive. Their bodies slump to the ground like sacks of potatoes, their blood draining into the base of the statue. The carving of Tiamat begins to glow as Frulam prepares her weapon, ready to fight.*

During the fight, one of Frulam's men will run away and jump into the hole in Area 10. At the end of the fight, Frulam will use her final gambit and hold her glaive close to her chest with both hands. She begins to disintegrate like the other high-ranking Cultists, and mutters something incoherent to herself as she disappears from the players.

FRULAM: My Queen, let it be.

*As Frulam disappears into dust, the passage around you begins to shake. You look up at the statue of Tiamat, and for a split second you could swear it was moving. You shake your head and look again, only to confirm your fear. As the Dragon Queen rises from her seat, all five mouths open and exhale their terrible arcane blasts.*

A white light envelops the players as they die. Then, the following happens.

*The white light breaks and the room goes black. When you open your eyes you find yourself in a desolate landscape, red and brown terrain in an endless stretch all around you. You turn just in time as a viscious roar tears across the hellish skies. You find yourself unable to turn, not out of fear, but an unknown force keeps you looking ahead. The flames begin to lick at your feet and you see your vision darken once more until all you see ahead of you is a tiny white light.*

If the players do not chase this white light, they will die and turn into [[Abishai]]. If they do, they will wake up and receive the benefits of a Long Rest and find themselves back in the Dragon Hatchery. If [[Leosin Erlanthar]] has not been freed yet, he can be found in 10.A.

#### 10. The Prisoners
This area is warm and humid. As soon as the players enter they will see dozens of men and women huddled together looking beaten, bloody and starved. [[Leosin Erlanthar]] is among these individuals and will explain that [[Wyrmspeaker Rezmir]] kept him alive for questioning. 

He will tell the party that there is no time to do anything else other than run, and will direct them to a drop on the ground about 30 feet from the top. If the players want to bring the other prisoners with them, they will need to either use rope or cast featherfall as none of them will be able to survive the fall.

### CONCLUSION

Down below, in the caves, the players will come across an interesting sight. Dozens of what looks to be dragon eggs have been smashed to pieces, with the exception of one. At the far end of the cave is a pristine looking dragon's egg.

*At the end of this narrow passage, you come into a room you feel as though you were not meant to see. The ceiling is taller, the air here is as humid and warm as the area above, and there is a still and peaceful puddle of reflective water coats the floor. It is abundantly clear that this is no natural formation, but one deliberately carved into the rock, the same as the camp's location itself.* 

LEOSIN: What strange magic is this?

*Leosin's footsteps do not disturb the water, in fact, neither do yours. Arcane symbols line the walls pulsating in chromatic colours along with various depictions of Dragons in flight. What draws your attention the most, however are the displays. Atop what looks like pedestals lit by torches and candleflame are the remains of dozens of dragon's eggs smashed to pieces. There is a groove in the wall where it looks like a hammer used to be housed, but it is long gone. However, at the end of the hall your eye catches it; a singular surviving egg.*

The colour of this is determined by the players in a secret choice at the DM's discretion. They are told to pick a number between 1-5 which correspond to:
1. Black/Copper egg.
2. Green/Brass egg.
3. Red/Gold egg.
4. Blue/Bronze egg.
5. White/Silver egg.
   
*The egg is a marvelously saturated and bright (chromatic colour), but in the light you can see it reflects (metallic colour). Looking at it, there's something strange about this egg. It stands at about 3 feet tall and looks to be quite heavy.*

If they decide to keep the egg, they can determine on a DC 10 Nature or Animal Handling Check that this is no ordinary Dragon's Egg and it will hatch within the week. Should they choose to attempt to destroy the egg, they will find it magically immune and resistant to damage. Specifically, the egg has the following stats:
- AC of 10.
- HP of 30.
- Immune to Magic.
- Has a minimum damage threshold of 10.
