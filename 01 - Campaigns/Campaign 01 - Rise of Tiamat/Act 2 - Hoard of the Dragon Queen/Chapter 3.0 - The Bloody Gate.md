### 3.1 - Not-Quite-There

#### Part One: The Briefing
*Later that evening as the Piercing Light sails gently down the Chionthar, Alia approaches your party. She seems to have shed her exoskeleton armour and is wearing an expensive but practical looking set of black leathers with a reinforced grey coat. What appears to be a rich tapestry of fungal structures and webs dance along the inside of her coat, while the outside is stitched and patterned less ostentatiously.

ALIA: I know I haven't been completely forthcoming and we haven't had much chance to speak. So allow me to reintroduce myself. My name is Alia Sanem from Eryndlyn.

*Her form shifts as she deactivates a spell. In an instant, Alia's visage fades. Where she once had pale skin desaturates until she's grey. Her eyes shift from their cool blue to a warm pink, and her white-blonde hair falls to her neck ending just before th shoulders. She stretches one last time before speaking.*

ALIA: Forgive the deception, but when you're one of my kind you tend to want to hide the truth about yourself from others.

*Some of the crew members of the Piercing Light look with shock, while others turn away almost as if they were afraid. Alia lets out a defeated sigh as she turns back to you.*

ALIA: I'm glad we've all managed to remain despite Langdedrosa's plans, but I'm afraid we're not quite out of the water yet. No pun intended. 

*Leosin emerges from inside with a stern look on his face. He seems unphased by Alia's true form.*

LEOSIN: We're taking a shortcut through the Chionthar according to the crew. We will arrive in Baldur's Gate within 2 days. In the mean time, Ser Caldus wants to speak with us. Shall we?

*You follow Leosin towards the Captain's Quarters as he knocks on the door. Ser Caldus greets you and lets you in and then gestures for you to sit down. He's half-armoured, surprisingly relaxed. He pulls out a bottle of clear liquid and pours himself a glass and downs it in one gulp before pouring himself another one. Leosin and Alia make their way inside after you and stand near the door.*

CALDUS: Ontharr didn't want to discuss this in front of the others at the war table. The 'fewer ears the better' he said. There's another reason why Ontharr wants you to go to Baldur's Gate.

*Caldus points up towards Alia who steps forward, setting a scroll down on the table. On the sheet, it depicts a diagram of what looks to be the hierarchy of the Cult of the Dragon.*

ALIA: The Cult operates on a specific hierarchy. Dragonclaws like Mellara lead cells to gather gold for the Cult. Each Dragonclaw operates under a Wyrmspeaker like Rezmir or your old friend Thaddak who became the White Wyrmspeaker. We can put two and two together and assume there are three others who haven't revealed themselves.

LEOSIN: If you all recall mon amie, Mellara's Herd emulated the Cult's hierarchy. The only difference is that unlike the Herd of the Dragon, the Cult's Wyrmspeakers are far more powerful.

ALIA: What's most pertinent to us is that Rezmir, the Black Wyrmspeaker, has or at least had five active Dragonclaws as of 1490. Mellara the Terror of the Sword Coast, Frulam Mondath, Langdedrosa Cyanwrath along with two others. 

CALDUS: Alia believes that she knows where our fourth Dragonclaw is hiding.

ALIA: Yes, I have it on good authority that one of her other Dragonclaws is in Baldur's Gate. My guess is that after Mellara and Frulam's deaths, Rezmir decided to switch tack and sow chaos another way.

CALDUS: Baldur's Gate is a central hub in the Sword Coast. It's no Waterdeep, but an unstable Gate means unfettered chaos for the west coast.

LEOSIN: Then forgive me for speaking out of turn, but surely if we know they are here our next plan of action is to strike them where they are, no?

ALIA: (Shaking her head) That's the thing. I have no idea who this Dragonclaw is. They could be a peasant or a Patriar for all we know.

CALDUS: A Patriar? Forgive me, I am not too familiar with the politics of the Gate.

LEOSIN: Baldur's Gate's nobility, monsieur. A lot of them live up in the Upper City. If the rest of you will recall, the First Talon is nobility from House Ravenshade, a Patriar family. The Dukes of Baldur's Gate are all Patriars.

ALIA: Finding a Cultist in Baldur's gate won't be hard, the city's history is full of blood. But how do we find a Tiamat Cultist that does not want to be found?

LEOSIN: Rezmir told you that her Dragonclaws would stop you, no? The Cult is nothing if not opportunistic. If we can find a way to attract enough attention to ourselves maybe they'll be stupid enough to strike.

ALIA: Or we could gather contacts in the city to help us locate them before they find us. Either way, we will be playing a waiting game in Baldur's Gate.

CALDUS: There's also the matter of discretion. Once we make port at the Gate, I will have to leave you there. We cannot travel together over such a great distance, it's not safe. That and Ontharr wants me and the Gauntlet in Waterdeep sooner rather than later.

LEOSIN: Alia and I will remain in Baldur's Gate until this Dragonclaw situation is resolved. After that, we'll find our own way to Waterdeep.

CALDUS: In the mean time, find one of the Gauntlet's contacts. Head up to [[Ackyn Selebon]] in the Outer City. He runs a stable called the [[Black Saddle]]. He can get your caravan outfitted for the journey north. 

LEOSIN: It may also do you some good to reach out to any other allies you can while we're here. I know that Lady [[Margaret Ravenshade]] is no Talon herself but she plans on running for the position of Duke in light of [[Belynne Stelmane]]'s declining health. I will gather what I can from our [[Harpers]] here in the city.

CALDUS: The specifics are up to you, ultimately. Securing [[Xaria Trevisa]]'s alliance and the safety of the city is paramount. Do whatever you need to in order to achieve your goal. Dismissed.

#### Part Two: Blade in the Dark
*That night, as the Piercing Light continues its journey down the Chionthar, you find yourselves settling into your cabins below decks. Glass runs back and forth experimenting with some new toy, while (Wyrmling) plays with some of the clueless crew members in the guise of a child. In the stillness of the cabin, you find yourself drifting off just a little before a loud scraping sound and knocking calls you deckside.*

*You walk up to the top of the ship where Alia, Leosin and Ser Caldus are staring at something ahead. The Piercing Light seems to have stopped, and while the surrounds are quite dark you can tell that you're nowhere near your destination. Looking for answers, you approach the three at the bow.*

LEOSIN: Something the matter, Ser Caldus?

CALDUS: Vermeillon's lighthouse is off and the gate is closed. Ship got scraped against the rocks cause we can't see. Bah, gods-damned Shadow Curse. The good news is that we're not gonna sink, but we're gonna have to take a look.

ALIA: At the ship or at the town?

CALDUS: Both. Kelemvor knows why the Mayor won't move his people out of the proximity of the Curse. You'd think that he'd consider the safety of the townsfolk.

*Alia adjusts her armour and satchel and ties her hair back before heading to the side of the ship. In one movement, she lights up her hand and creates dancing lights and sends it down as she looks at the side of the Piercing Light.*

ALIA: Say less. I'll go take a look to see what's happening. Lower the tender.

*A Gauntlet member nods and lowers the smaller boat into the water and Alia walks back a little from the portside. She checks something in her satchel before walking forward.*

CALDUS: Wait-

*Before Caldus can finish his sentence, Alia has already jumped and disembarked from the ship, landing rather quietly onto the skiff. As she gets up, she looks up at the ship and shoots Caldus a confident smile.*

CALDUS: Gods damn it. Well, the rest of you better go with her. None of us are going anywhere until that gate is opened. And don't give me that look. If you think that dragonslayers are above a little menial labour you'd be wrong.

LEOSIN: I shall assist Caldus's men in repairing the hull. Make sure you have light with you to keep the Shadow Curse at bay. Technically we're not in it just yet, but even at this distance you can't be too careful. If anything changes we'll reach out to you.

*The boat inches slowly across the Chionthar, getting closer and closer to the town of Vermeillon. In the distance, you can see the massive silhouette of Vermeillon Manor overlooking the rest of the town. Looking at it form here, there can't be more than a few dozen houses at most, and the majority of them seem to be centered on a well-paved and developed section.*

ALIA: I don't hear anyone.

*The boat finally brings you close enough to the docks that you can disembark. A thick fog covers the majority of the town, and combined with the darkness it makes it difficult to see what's ahead of you. As you all disembark, Alia begins walking up the jetty and towards the town proper.*

ALIA: I don't like this. Let's look around for any townsfolk. I'll take your lead.

*As you walk closer to the centre of town the first thing Alia points out to you is the trail of corpses in front of a massive, dead tree. The blood on the ground has darkened, so this was no recent atrocity, but it has yet to dry up. The Drow bends down to examine the corpses and turns back to you.*

ALIA: Murdered. Some sort of beast. Happened a few hours ago at most by the looks of things.

### 3.2 Arrival at Baldur's Gate
*The Piercing Light's journey comes to an end at the foot of a hill just in front of a massive structure. Before your eyes you behold the sight of Wyrm's Crossing, large and imposing bridge that crosses the River Chionthar. Differently shaped houses and watchtowers dot both the surface and legs of the bridge, each connected by a series of wooden walkways and platforms.

*In the middle of the bridge is a large island largely made up of what looks to be a rocky hill. A strong, well-fortified and dominating castle with granite walls sits atop it. The walls and turrets of the structure are squared off, and banners marked with the symbol of a closed blazing fist in a blue field flap against the breeze from the Sea of Swords. Caldus / Glass whispers the name 'Wyrm's Rock', and explains that the Flaming Fist use it as a sort of gatehouse that connects the north and south portion of the Crossing.*

CALDUS: Here we are, the city of Blood. Baldur's Gate.

*The toll of the distant bells rings throughout the valley. Just past the mountain behind Wyrm's Rock is the city proper but even from here the noise from the harbour is hard to miss.*

When the players arrive in [[Baldur's Gate]], they will dock in the Outer City just below [[Rivington]]. [[Caldus Merevain]] will say that the streets of the city are far too narrow to allow the [[Sylvira-Van]] to be allowed inside and they'll have to find somewhere for [[Glass]] to park the caravan. Alternatively, if the players travelled on land, [[Glass]] will say he will find somewhere to park in [[Rivington]]. Either will immediately recommend heading straight up the hill to [[Ackyn Selebon]] when they can.

CALDUS: This is as far as we I can take you. Rivington is just up the hill. If it were up to me, I'd get you all to head up to Ackyn as soon as possible. But knowing him he's probably busy with customers. Place like the Gate gets a lot of business from caravans entering and leaving the city and his is the most practical place for them to rest their wheels.

CALDUS: I'll be heading straight to [[Waterdeep]] from here. Hopefully I'll see you there in a month's time. Good luck on your mission. May [Ilmater](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ilmater) watch over us all, and may [Helm](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Helm) protect us in our journeys.

*Caldus tips a goodbye salute to your party and boards the Piercing Light once more. Glass is already disembarking the Caravan from the ship, and the crew all give him a wave as the van rolls onto the docks at Rivington. The ship will remain here for a while, but you will not be able to join him on his journey to Waterdeep without endangering him or yourselves.*

GLASS: I'll find the Black Saddle. You guys can go explore, I'll keep the Dragon safe with me.

*As Glass escorts the caravan up the hill, Leosin and Alia step off the Piercing Light and stand in front of you. Both have determined looks on their faces as they look up at Rivington.*

ALIA: We might be here a long while. A few days, maybe a week or two.

LEOSIN: There are a multitude of taverns in the Gate, but none quite as frequented as the [[Elfsong Tavern]]. If you want my advice, find room and board there once you make it into the city. That is where Alia and I will make our headquarters.

ALIA: If you learn anything of note, meet us there. Either as soon as you learn something or when you feel like you've gathered enough evidence.

LEOSIN: Not to be the bearer of bad news, but this may be the last time we all find ourselves in civilisation for a while. Make the most of it while you can, my friends. It's no Elturel, but trust me, once you find yourselves out there you'll miss the warmth of the city.

*You ascend up the hill and make your way through the narrow streets of Rivington. While you're not in the city proper just yet, it's evident you're not too far away. Unlike Elturel, the streets of the Outer City are far less pristine and more earthly. Urchins run around in droves, stray dogs bark at your party as you pass by and lone cats jump from between the low rooftops. Beggars are in far more abundance, even here in Rivington. As you look around you notice that funnily enough, you're not the only ragtag group of individuals here. It seems that adventurers of all sorts filter in and out of the city. It is after all, described as the city that's 'halfway to everywhere'.*

As they explore the Outer City, the party may be surprised to find that news of [[Greenest]] has yet to arrive here. On the day of their arrival, players will be able to purchase the most recent copy of [[Baldur's Mouth]] and will discover that the town is far more concerned with the rise of gruesome murders in the [[Lower City]]. 

#### From the Shadows
As the players get close to [[Wyrm's Rock]], they notice what looks to be a group of twenty [[Flaming Fist]] soldiers set up around in a protective perimeter around a horse-drawn chariot. This is [[Liara Portyr]], who has proclaimed herself to be the Grand Duke of [[Baldur's Gate]] in [[Ulder Ravengard]]'s absence. As the players approach, they overhear her speech.

LIARA: People of Rivington, hear my call. I am Blaze Portyr, Liara Portyr. In this crime-ridden city of blood we call home, too many of our 'leaders' have become complacent and greedy. They sit up in their mansions in the [[Upper City]], lording over us all like we're cattle.

*You get closer and notice that a small crowd has gathered around this woman. From this distance, you can get a better look at her now. She looks to be a human in her mid to late forties, sporting greying auburn hair. Her steel plate is covered with what looks to be a fine white tabard, with the symbol of the flaming fist over her chest. The soldiers around her stand ready, watching for any movement from the crowd.*

LIARA: Well, that will all end soon. With the help of the Flaming Fist, I promise to end the tyranny of the Patriars here in Baldur's Gate. All I ask is for your support.

Characters who succeed on a DC 15 Perception check or have a passive perception of 15 or higher will notice a colourful parrot. It squawks in the air before landing on a roof near what looks to be a hooded assassin aiming a crossbow at [[Liara Portyr]].

*You look up in an opportune moment just as Liara finishes her sentence. Inside Sharess's Caress is a hooded figuring aiming a crossbow straight at Blaze Portyr.*

If the players react, the Assassin will fire preemptively and shoot a nearby [[Flaming Fist]] soldier instead of [[Liara Portyr]]. If the players do not react, one of the [[Flaming Fist]] soldiers will instead hear the sound just in time to protect her from harm. The Assassin will then attempt to run inside to lose the party. Liara will be rushed off to safety by the [[Flaming Fist]]. A gnome woman named [[Devella Fountainhead]] will then ask the adventurers to pursue the attacker.

DEVELLA: You there. You look able, bring me that Assassin and I'll reward you!

On a DC 15 Acrobatics or Athletics check, they will be able to pursue the Assassin through Sharess's Caress. The following scene then happens.

*You push past the busy crowd, making your way through the dancers and workers of the oldest profession in pursuit of the Assassin. The hooded figure gives you a small look as he turns around and makes his way to the back of the pleasure house and finds himself running upstairs. You give chase, nearly bumping into several patrons on your way up but you keep your eyes trained on the prize. As you reach the edge of the building, there's nowhere left for the Assassin to go. You have them cornered.*

Trying to confront the Assassin will yield no response and a fight will ensue. Three more Assassins will join the fray, hidden by the Invisibility spell. The main Assassin will attempt to run away while the other three hold you in place. After the fight is over, players may want to assess the bodies. A DC 15 Investigation check will yield that the Assassins are far too well-armed to be simple thugs.

*You head back down to the street and speak to the gnome woman waiting for you. Her scaled armour is covered in what looks to be a similar tabard to what the rest of the Flaming Fist was wearing, but there is an air of authority about her. DEVELLA: Managed to catch the bastard?

[[Devella Fountainhead]] will reward the characters with 1000 GP regardless of whether or not the main Assassin was caught. She will then explicitly ask them to keep the origin of the attackers to themselves if it's discovered. She will lament that she's already knee-deep in another investigation and can't handle another group involving themselves in Baldur's Gate or more chaos being thrown into the city at such a precocious time.

DEVELLA: Liara has a lot of big ideas. Talks big about 'removing the corruption of the Patriars' while she's a Patriar's daughter herself. Plenty in Baldur's Gate would want her dead, but that's between you and me. Anyway, I've taken up enough of your time. Thank you again, strangers. You've made an ally with the [[Flaming Fist]] today. Well, at least with me.

*She gives you a respectful soldier's salute and slips her helmet back on. Her hands motion for two guards to go up the path you ascended and they begin to lock the area off. As the minutes pass, that part of Wyrm's Crossing seems to have been sealed off for the day, much to the dismay of its patrons.*
