### Side Quest - Il Met By Moonlight

Impyerno Lima continued on their journey with the Champion of the Goblin Village. That morning, Ryn felt a little sensitive to the sun. After an examination, Yan and Lioran quickly realised he was turning into a vampire. Hoping to find a cure and eager to press on, they continued to follow the Champion to hunt down the Vampire Florian once more. 

As they journeyed through the mountains, Ryn realised that the Champion was heading straight for the Temple of Selune he once lived in for years. However, upon their approach a feeling of overwhelming dread overcame Ryn as he looked upon the vibrant Temple he once called home, now empty and abandoned. After climbing over the fence, they were met with nothing but the sound of the wind passing through the trees and mountains.

Within the Temple grounds, the party saw the once peaceful garden was now overgrown. Yan could see the remnants of a battle painted across the walls with blood and steel. Deeper inside, they found nothing more than the remains of the other Selunites killed by the invaders, and the twisted remains of Ryn's former family and friends slaughtered and left to rot. Within the main sanctuary, Ryn came face-to-face with Florian once more who offered him a lifetime of happiness in his court. Ryn declined, and after a long battle the party emerged victorious and repelled Florian from Ryn's home.

Their troubles did not end there however, as Ryn came face-to-face with his former rival Tovan. The Orc demanded answers from him, believing Ryn to be responsible for sending the Sharrans that killed his friends and family. Ryn having no memory of this denied it, and eventually Tovan believed him. Tovan told Ryn of what happened that fateful night; how he watched his friends get slaughtered, how he was hung from the rafters after he attempted to escape, and how his hatred for the Sharrans and a deal with the Vecna brought him back from the dead as a force of vengeance.

The two parted ways once again, though not before Tovan revealed the truth of Ryn's background to him. His memories of the fire were not that of his village burning, but a raid by the Sharrans gone wrong. Tovan called him a pretender, not believing him to be a true Selunite. The two parted ways once more, though with far less hostility than the last time. Tovan warned Ryn that the people who killed their family were still out there somewhere, and to seek out the Sharrans in the Dessarin Valley if he really wanted to atone for his mistakes.

Back in Berdusk the party was able to retrieve the Inert Belt of Giant's Strength from the Champion and learned about its properties from Flint, the Minotaur Blacksmith. Unfortunately, they also learned that a second murder has also happened in Berdusk with the victim being a former guard captain, killed in the same manner as the previous victim. Eager to move on to Elturel, the party decided to board a ship and set sail to the Holy City where Lioran's family awaits.
