### 2.7 Whispers of the Brotherhood

The Arcane Brotherhood's ambush proved to be a difficult fight, with Assassins of the Brotherhood successfully managing to take down several party members. Working together as a unit, Impyerno Lima emerged victorious and defeated Thuran Yale, but not before the elf could fatally strike Yan with a Disintegrate and use Feeblemind on Ulena.

In the aftermath of their victory, they discovered that the Thuran they defeated was simply a simulacrum, one of dozens created to taunt Yan into coming after him. As the dust settled, Yan felt compelled to step into his real body, regaining some of his sensations in the process. 

While they survived the fight, they all learned some uncomfortable truths about each other as well as Yan's former mentor. Whatever Thuran had become, it's something far more sinister. That and the Elf seemed far too eager for Yan to reunite with his sister in the future. What dark plans Thuran has in store remain a mystery, but all roads seem to point to Andora Lawntill. Thankfully, unlike his former master, Yan is able to sense where she and his mother is. For now.

After the fight, the party retired to Lioran's house for some well-earned rest and relaxation. Yan enjoyed the comforts he had missed out on, raiding Lioran's house for food and drink. Meanwhile, Zil was able to understand her companions a little more, confronting Ryn about his insecurities while also pondering her own purpose in life.

The next morning, Lioran and the rest of Impyerno Lima returned to the Hall of the Triad to report to his stepfather and Chapter Leader Ontharr Frume, giving him some grave news about the Cult of the Dragon. Lioran and Zil found themselves quite put out as both Ontharr and the First Talon reprimanded them for their actions.
