### 2.8 Keeper of Secrets

Impyerno Lima spent the majority of their day resupplying for the adventures ahead. Lioran and Ulena stocked up on components, while Zil armoured herself at the local blacksmith.

Later on, they came upon Varia, Grace and Glass being spoken to by a mysterious old man whose gravitas seemed to carry an air of authority about him. He answered vaguely, speaking in riddles and did not allow the group to peer too closely into his identity.

In the afternoon, they went to go investigate the lead they found from the Arcane Brotherhood and headed towards the Grand Cemetary. There, they came upon a grim sight, a a murdered Talon alongside his grieving sister. As the party went to investigate it further, they found themselves beneath the streets of Elturel where a den of Necromancers had set up a ritual circle.

During the fight, Ulena almost found herself disintegrated and Yan lost one of his 3 lives. In the end, Impyerno Lima emerged victorious and all was right again. That is, until a skeletal figure appeared and spoke to them...
