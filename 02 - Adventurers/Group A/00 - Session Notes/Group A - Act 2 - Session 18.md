### 2.9 Assault on Fort Morninglord pt.2

After a long night of fighting, Impyerno Lima successfully fought back the Cult's forces at Fort Morninglord. They pursued the Cult where they hid, all the way up the mountains to a war camp.

After an interesting attempt at infiltrating the camp, the heroes eventually found themselves facing a familiar foe. After a long fight, they "slayed" the Dragonclaw known as Langdedrosa Cyanwrath and his Young Blue Dragon Azureth. As the battle ended, Lioran received a message from Mira, letting him know that she and her team survived as well thanks to their guidance.

Now aboard the Order's Warship the Piercing Light, they head for Baldur's Gate to pursue an alliance with the Talons of the Raven Queen. But even as they rest, it seems that there is no end to their troubles as they are visited in their dreams.
