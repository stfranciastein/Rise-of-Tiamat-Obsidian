### 3.1 Not-Quite-There

Aboard the Piercing Light, Impyerno Lima made their way towards Baldur's Gate. Later that night, they found themselves subjected to another vision. This time however, they were confronted by Rezmir in a more direct manner. She warned the party that following her would lead to their demise as she accelerated the curse once more.

The next day, they found themselves sailing down the Chionthar passing through an area close to the Shadow-Cursed Lands. As they traversed that section of the Chionthar, Zil told the party that she could feel the veil between the Shadowfell and the Material plane getting weaker.

In the evening, the Piercing Light found itself stuck. Caldus told the party that the lighthouse of Vermeillon seems to have been turned off. Eager to get a move on, Alia disembarked from the ship to investigate followed closely by Zil and Impyerno Lima. As they explored the town, things began moving in the shadows, hiding in the fog. Following a trail of blood, they began moving up the lighthouse in hopes of finding answers. As they reached the summit, they found themselves under attack by a large ghostly figure.
