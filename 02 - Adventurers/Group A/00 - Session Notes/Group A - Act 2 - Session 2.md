### 1.2 The Hunter Games

On the third day of the Festival, the Great Hunt began in earnest. Lioran and Ulena went off to purchase supplies around town and partake in some festival foods. They also found themselves at a magic shop where the pair bought a deck of cards with mysterious magical properties. Yan, Ryn and Glass had an awkward encounter with Zilliletha who was told to join them in the Great Hunt as their fifth teammate. After an address by Governor Nighthill, the teams set off to find their quarry. Aboard a rented carriage driven by their trusty Kobold Glass, the team rode out towards the Troll Mountains in search of a Hydra.

At the foot of the mountain they found a mysterious crater at the front of the treeline that blackened the earth at their feet. The crater was massive, spanning 60 ft. wide and 300 ft. long. The group investigated briefly, but found no evidence of man-made tampering. As they ascended up the mountain, however, they discovered what remained of the Dwarven contestants who appeared to have been stabbed before they could set up camp. Ulena spoke to the nearby tree learning that the Dwarves simply 'fell over' and died. Later that night as they made camp, Zil and Lioran bore witness to mysterious lights accompanied by disembodied voices. Thankfully nothing came of it and the group went off to continue their hunt.

As Impyerno Lima reached the top of one of the mountain peaks, they heard two voices screaming and shouting as they engaged in combat. Rushing to their aid, the adventurers were confronted by a dangerous foe; a minotaur holding a massive Greataxe who had just finished killing another group of hunters at the peak. Our heroes drew their weapons as they prepared themselves for a fight.
