### 3.5 When in Rivington

Continuing on with their first day in Baldur's Gate, Impyerno Lima found themselves in Whitkeep Hostel meeting an assortment of different folks. Zil, Yan and Ryn found themselves standing in front of the famed wizard Natasha 'Tasha' the Dark as she briefed them on their next quest for the Zhentarim. The task was simple; steal the Ruby Diadem of Erlyn Blessedore from Nixylanna Vidorant's vault. In return, she would provide the party a sum of 3000 gold as well as supply Jamna with enchanted gear for the Heist of Goldmane Manor.

As the three discussed with Tasha, Lioran and Ulena spoke to two gnomes named Prolle and Ardryn on the other side of the hostel. The two seemed to be arguing about the loss of Ardryn's schematics at the hands of the Flaming Fist. Ardryn explained that he had been developing something known as the 'Arcana-Net', a revolutionary enchanted mirror that people could use to speak and see anyone holding a similar item within a 2 mile radius. Determined to help them get it back, Lioran and Ulena promised to help the gnomes with their problem.

After running into all sorts of shady characters in the Outer City (and Yan being robbed by quite the skilled Thief), the group finally made their way through Basilisk Gate and settled into the Elfsong Tavern. However, as Lioran, Ulena and Yan attempted to return to the Elfsong for the night, they found themselves confronted by a ghastly sight; a serial killer in the midst of a profane ritual.
