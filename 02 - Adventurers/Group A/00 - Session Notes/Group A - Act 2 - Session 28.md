### 3.10 The Low Lantern

In Wyrm's Rock above the Chionthar River, Yan and Lioran found themselves in quite the sticky situation as they were imprisoned for arson. While Yan was being pressured into admitting his crimes by a corrupt set of guards, Lioran found himself being coerced into making a statement saying the Wizard acted alone. During the night, Yan was introduced to a mysterious figure who seemed grateful for their killing of Mortlock Vanthampur. This figure offered him a way out; simply stay awake during the night and there will be an escape for the two of them.

Ryn, Ulena and Zil returned to Lady Ravenshade in the Upper City and informed her of the dire situation facing their two companions. After giving Margaret everything they had on Mortlock, she agreed to rescue their friends from their situation. In exchange, they would promise to see her investigation into the Vanthampurs through to its final conclusion. Unbeknownst to them, Yan had freed himself and ran back to Raven's Arbour, desperate to accelerate Margaret's action. As he reunited with Impyerno Lima, Yan explained to his friends that in his pride and naivety Lioran did not choose to escape with him.

The next morning, as news of Lioran's arrest and imminent execution made it to Baldur's Mouth, the party scrambled towards Wyrm's Rock in order to try and save him. Fortunately, just in the nick of time, Lady Margaret arrived at the and made quite the dramatic display as she and members of the Talons dropped the bodies of the Dead Three Cultists down to the street for the crowd to see.

As Lioran rested up for the afternoon, Impyerno Lima met up with Klim Jhasso once more, though they quickly found out he had very little to give in terms of information. Unfortunately, Klim even managed to cost them 100 gold as he brought them to the expensive tavern known as the Helm & Cloak. What he could tell the party was that believed Amrik to be one of the voices he heard while he was in captivity, and told them where to find the man at the Low Lantern, a tavern in the Lower City.

Later that day, the party went shopping for trinkets and doodads. Zil and Yan visited a jewellers and bought their respective pieces. As the party headed off to Sorcerous Sundries where they procured some cantrips for Yan, Ulena found herself chasing down and threatening a child in her giant eagle form in response to having rocks thrown at them.

In the evening, after Lioran had recovered from his taxing day in prison, Impyerno Lima ventured towards the Low Lantern and pursued their lead into Amrik. There, Ulena met a fiery bard named Asha who seemed to take a liking to her and the two hit it off that night. As Zil explored the ship, she found nothing that could incriminate neither him nor the Vanthampurs. Left with no other choice, they were forced into a confrontation with the older brother. With a dash of luck, Zil and Ulena managed not to tip Amrik off as to their true motives. After passing a gruesome 'test' Amrik set which lead to the murder of a young man named Prynn Derringwhistle, they now find themselves unwillingly working for the seedy loan shark.
