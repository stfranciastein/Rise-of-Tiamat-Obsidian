### 3.11 A Dirty Job

Intending to go to the Upper City right away to see Aerith Beldan, the Impyerno Lima attempted to begin their task but found themselves stopped by the Constabulary in the Upper City. When they attempted to return to the Elfsong, they were inadvertently split up. 

Zil and Ryn pursued a group of children into a dilapidated house where Zil witnessed the brutal stabbing of a drunken seadog. Acting quickly, she managed to save the man but the perpetrators were able to get away. Ryn tried to give chase but only managed to retrieve one body - a reanimated young boy marked with the symbol of Orcus; the Demon Lord of Undeath.

Ulena, Lioran and Yan found themselves face-to-face once again with another Bhaalist assassin. Acting on instinct alone, Lioran and the others pursued as he cast Wind Walk on the three of them. Eventually, they tracked the killer down to a warehouse where they found another entrance to the sewers.

After reuniting with the party - with the exception of their rat companion Ratthew - Impyerno Lima trudged through the dirty sewers of Baldur's Gate. While there, Lioran lead them through the darkness and unwittingly into a confrontation with three gelatinous cubes. After the fight, they realised that the killer had retreated to the sewers below Vanthampur Villa. Ryn tried to continue their pursuit but found himself forced to stop by an unknown force.

In the Upper City, the party found Aerith Beldan's residence; a House of Healing dedicated to Ilmater. Zil and Lioran found themselves butting heads about how to approach Aerith. Ultimately, Zil conceded and told Lioran that he could talk to the Dwarf before they resorted to violence. After a night of convincing his friends to help, both him and Ulena met and treated with Aerith. The two soon realised was no 'dirty' man whatsoever, just a good man trying to make Baldur's Gate a better place.

Returning to the Elfsong with little information on Amrik, the party had another heated conversation as Zil realised the others had gained nothing from the Dwarf. As they tried to come up with another plan - one that involved them giving their own gold to Amrik to buy themselves more time - Zil soon realised that Amrik had already caught wind of their visit to Aerith. A tense conversation with Amrik followed soon after, with everything hinging on Lioran following Amrik's command to count the gold. In his pride, Lioran refused and a confrontation with Amrik followed soon after. Though the party was able to nearly kill him, he managed to escape.

Now, they find themselves in Raven's Arbour. Hosted by an annoyed noblewoman and the First Talon of the Raven Queen.
