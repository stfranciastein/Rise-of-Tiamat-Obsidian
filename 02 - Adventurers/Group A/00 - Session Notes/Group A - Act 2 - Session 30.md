### 3.12 Vanthampur Villa

After a night's rest and with the aid of the First Talon of the Raven Queen, Impyerno Lima set about on their mission to bring down the Vanthampurs. Infiltrating the Villa, they managed to sneak their way inside until they found themselves forced to deal with the Duke's hired thugs upstairs. At first the party tried to keep the men alive, but after being discovered they were forced to kill them all.

Upstairs in his bedroom, they met with the final Vanthampur brother Thurstwell. The older man seemed all too eager to help bring down his family, and even gave them an Infernal Puzzle Box that was entrusted to him by his mother. Surrounded by his cadre of imps, Thurstwell told the party of Thalamra's plans to take over Baldur's Gate along with her associate from the east along with her Infernal Patron. Eager to stop the Duke's plans, the party made their way to the basement of Vanthampur Villa and into the sewers where they found the Cult's lair - not of the Dead Three - the Cult of the First Lord Zariel. 

After fighting their way through the dungeons and freeing their prisoners, Impyerno Lima finally found Duke Vanthampur and confronted her in her lair. Zil, eager to end Thalamra's reign hastily attacked and their battle began in earnest. After a long and hard skirmish the party emerged victorious. However, in her dying breath, Thalamra laughed and promised she would see the party 'in hell'. After the fight, they retrieved a key atop a stone altar which seemed to unlock the Vaults. As the vault doors swung open, the party soon discovered that Thalamra's associate was still within. As the figure turned, the party recognised his face almost immediately.

An old, familiar face.
