### 3.14 Cold, Red-Blooded Murder

Tasked with finding the woman in Zil's visions, Impyerno Lima headed straight for the Blushing Mermaid, a dive bar in the Lower City. There, they learned more about Mavis Ephante, the so-called spirit detective working with the Flaming Fist. After some time, they found her in Bloomridge Park, hunched over the body of a woman floating in the pond.

It was cold, red-blooded murder, she said. The kind of killing that required both passion and hate. As Impyerno Lima investigated further, it became clear this was no ordinary killing, but the work of the Slaughter Demon they'd dealt with before. Following leads uncovered at Candulhallow's Funerary Arrangements and during Mavis's spirit session, the party found themselves in the abandoned town of Tumbledown, just past Cliffgate.

As they delved into the sordid tale of Tumbledown, Zil learned the full picture piece by miserable piece. Before the Reclamation of Berdusk, in a small, unassuming town in Sunset Valley, there lived a family of four: Konstantinos the father, Eleni the mother, and their two daughters, Kassandra and Alessandra. As strife and war swept across the valley, Konstantinos was slain by Red-Blood mercenaries. When the company disbanded, little did they know that in that moment, they hadn't just ended one life, but four.

Because, dear audience, it turns out that the person responsible for the Slaughter Demon was no heartless, evil summoner — it would have been far easier if she was. Instead, Impyerno Lima found a young woman named Alessandra, warped and damaged by time and trauma. Alessandra explained how her mother Eleni, unable to look past the trauma of losing her husband, had tried to kill both Alessandra and Kassandra. Alessandra caught her in the act, but was unable to stop Eleni from killing her sister — and then herself.

After the deaths of her sister and mother, Alessandra had turned to Orcus, the demon lord who promised her the power to bring her family back. As Zil tried to talk her down while the Slaughter Demon bore down on her allies, she soon realized the only way this sordid affair would end was with Alessandra's death. In one slice, Zil silenced her troubles and damned Alessandra's soul to her demonic patron.

Now, as Zil's tears dry and Impyerno Lima say their farewells to the young girl, all that's left is to return to Xaria Trevisa and finish her initiation.
