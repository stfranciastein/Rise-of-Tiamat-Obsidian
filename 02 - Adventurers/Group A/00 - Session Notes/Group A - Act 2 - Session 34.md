### 3.16 The Feast of the Moon

As the 20th of Uktar fell on Impyerno Lima, the festivities during the Feast of the Moon were overshadowed by the looming threat of the Serpent's imminent attack. As the favoured adventurers met back up with Leosin and Talis Kasterel, they found themselves ambushed by the Cult of the Dragon prior to the celebrations.

When the dust settled and the fight concluded they were left with two choices; pursue the Serpent in her lair or wait for her to attack at the Epic of Abdel Adrian. Deciding to help Talis at the Oasis Theatre, they briefly partook in the celebrations before heading inside. They watched the Tale of Abdel Adrian told by the actors and learned that the gods including Tiamat had contingencies for the Time of Troubles when Ao the Overgod made all of the gods mortal.

The play concluded, but as it ended the Serpent revealed herself to be Asha Ardeat, the Fire Genasi who had taken an interest in Ulena not a few days ago. The fight was extremely tough, with both Ryn and Ulena being taken out early during their skirmish as Asha set the theatre ablaze with her powerful infernal magics. When it came down to the wire, Lioran, Yan and Zil were able to finally defeat her as they picked their friends up and ran to safety.
