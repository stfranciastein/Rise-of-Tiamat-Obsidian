### 3.16 The Feast of the Moon

Impyerno Lima became a five-member squad once again as Lioran and Zil formally joined their ranks. As the 20th of Uktar fell on the party however, the festivities during the Feast of the Moon were overshadowed by the looming threat of the Serpent's imminent attack. As the favoured adventurers met back up with Leosin and Talis Kasterel, they found themselves ambushed by the Cult of the Dragon prior to the celebrations.

When the dust settled and the fight concluded they were left with two choices; pursue the Serpent in her lair or wait for her to attack at the Epic of Abdel Adrian. Deciding to help Talis at the Oasis Theatre, they briefly partook in the celebrations before heading inside. They watched the Tale of Abdel Adrian told by the actors and learned that the gods including Tiamat had contingencies for the Time of Troubles when Ao the Overgod made all of the gods mortal.

The play concluded, but as it ended the Serpent revealed herself to be Asha Ardeat, the Fire Genasi who had taken an interest in Ulena not a few days ago. The fight was extremely tough, with both Ryn and Ulena being taken out early during their skirmish as Asha set the theatre ablaze with her powerful infernal magics. When it came down to the wire, Lioran, Yan and Zil were able to finally defeat her as they picked their friends up and ran to safety.

### Cacafonix's Addendum pt.1

*As he finishes his tale and adjusts his lute, Cacafonix walks up to a bar and a human man stands next to him. The man is handsome, if a little scarred. He couldn't be older than his early twenties though the sun has done quite a number on him. His once pale skin is tanned, and his short brown hair is bleached from his days travelling the desert. He's dressed in the Calishite garb of an adventurer.*

"It was a good story you told just then, if a little paraphrased."

"Not a fan of my work? Take it up with my-"

"No, no, I'm not complaining. It's just... I met them, you know. During all of that. Yeah, the white Tiefling, Lioran. He told me to go home right after he gave me and my friend Jenna money to enjoy the festival. We tried to scam him, but he saw right through us..."

"I see."

"We spent that money on a room in the Elfsong. If he and the others weren't there, if they didn't stop the Serpent from raising hell in the Gate, I wouldn't be here today. They're real heroes, no matter what anyone says about them."

"That was years ago. You a big fan of my work?"

"Somewhat."

"Then you'll know that they've had to make some pretty hard sacrifices. Especially when they went on that job for the devil. Or well... you know. Hard to spin that into a happy tale..."
