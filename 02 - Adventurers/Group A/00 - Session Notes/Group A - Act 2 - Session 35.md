### 4.1 The Convoy Begins

After defeating the Serpent Asha Ardeat, Impyerno Lima found themselves in a race against time as they uncovered the Orbs of Devastation that the Cult of the Dragon had smuggled into the theatre. Acting quickly, they managed to prevent further disaster at the sacrifice of their bag of holding. All seemed well, but later that night they had another vision granted by the curse. This time, they watched as the Cult of the Dragon destroyed the town of Soubar and had their curse accelerated once more by Wyrmspeaker Rezmir.

Spending their last day in Baldur's Gate and Candlekeep, they discovered some grim realities. Firstly, they learned that Thaddak had been watching them this whole time with more changlings. Secondly, Sylvira gave them 3 months at best left before the Abishai curse overwhelmed them. Thirdly, Lioran discovered Samuel's true identity as Gargauth. That and that Elturel may be bound to fall into Avernus at Zariel's hands. Lastly - and perhaps more pressingly - was that the Cult of the Dragon had access to the Lich-God Vecna's artifacts; Arkhan with the Hand and Eye of Vecna and Rezmir with the Book of Vile Darkness.

Returning to Baldur's Gate, Impyerno Lima finished up their business in the city. At Lioran's behest, they visited the Hhune House in the Upper City in an attempt to deal with a group of greater Devils in the guise of humans. Unfortunately, while they nearly won the battle, Lutecia Hhune managed to Plane Shift to Avernus and escaped.

Now they head off north to Waterdeep alongisde a merchant convoy lead by Balthazaar Baradult, with a long, winding road separating them between the Bloody Gate and the City of Splendors.
