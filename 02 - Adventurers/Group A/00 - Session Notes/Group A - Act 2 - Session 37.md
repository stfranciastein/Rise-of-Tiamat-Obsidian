### 4.3 Twenty-Sided Trouble at the Big Bad Base

The long and winding road to Waterdeep continued on with more problems for Impyerno Lima. They were able to get to know Cannon and Alain more during a combat-filled two days where they encountered Dragon Cultists, Barghests, Bandits and Elementals.

However, they soon found themselves at a hard stop. Firstly, because during the fight with the Elementals, the caravan broke down and cost them precious hours. Secondly and perhaps more concerningly was due to the dice-shaped problem; a mutated brand of gelatinous goo that had infected their caravans and supplies. Determined to solve the problem, they decided to find the Big Bad Base at Cannon's suggestion and hesitantly agreed to the company of Vellin Farstride, an Emerald Enclave member and a man on a hunt for the 'bandits' that destroyed the forests below Rivington.

In exchange for Vellin's help, Yan and Ulena made an uneasy promise with him vowing to find the wrongdoers who killed his friends. After arriving at the Big Bad Base, they noticed a sore lack of Kobolds or Goblins but plenty of samples of the gelatinous goop. As Zil followed the sounds of a struggling creature the party came upon a beautiful room lit up with bioluminescent plants. Not all is at it seemed, however, as when Zil looked closer she was able to determine what had happened to the cavern's previous occupants.
