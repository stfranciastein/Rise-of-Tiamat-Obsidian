### 4.4 The Icosahedrooze

As they explored the Big Bad Base, Impyerno Lima came upon the undead remains of Tunathranx, an Adult Black Dragon. The battle was hard-fought and many of their party members nearly lost their lives in the encounter but the heroes emerged victorious in the face of evil. Upon their return to the camp, they partook in some well-deserve R&D as Alain and Melchia heated up a nearby pond for the children and adults to enjoy.

In the stillness of the campfire, Impyerno Lima were able to have some heartfelt and reflective conversations. The first was between Ulena and Vellin, both of whom pondered the price of vengeance on the heart and soul. The second was between Zil and Lioran who grew closer as they talked about their past lives. For a moment, Zil and Lioran were able to find peace just for a little while until they all retired and returned to the Caravan.

In the night, however, their hopeful demeanours were crushed as they saw a vision of the future. They were able to glimpse into the road ahead. First they saw the Castle in the Clouds, a castle of Cloud Giant architecture. Secondly, they discovered the massive hoard that Rezmir had collected for the Dragon Queen herself. As they beheld the millions of gold in front of them however, they found themselves facing Rezmir in her full might, a portent of what is to come.
