### 4.5 Destiny Awaits

Continuing on their journey up North, Impyerno Lima's adventures lead them to meeting two passer-by's at camp named Jokketh and Xixilathariazes. The travelling adventurers informed them of the troubles in the Alliance Fortress of Ironspine Keep, saying they were rejected at the gates by the Talons of the Raven Queen.

With no other choice but to press forward with the convoy, the party marched on as they faced all manner of beasts up ahead. Firstly they found themselves running into the Cult of the Dragon, then a into a patch of poisonous fungus that seemed to invoke doom and despair. Finally, as the snow began to fall the men of Impyerno Lima found themselves entranced by five extremely bodacious human women in red outfits.

As their ninth day on the road passed, the people of the convoy gathered round that night and listened to Kaghain and the Ranger debate the origin of 'heroes'. Kaghain's stories proported that heroes were predestined to do great things by the gods, while the Ranger's tales told of heroes performing great deeds aided by luck which lead to the gods taking notice. Whoever was in the right, who's to say? Perhaps it didn't even matter, for it was clear that the people of Balthazaar's convoy could see what the adventurers could not; they were Heroes of Faerun in the making.

When they slept, they found themselves in a conversation with the Harper Rhylrae who reached out to them for the second time since Elturel. Trapped in his Infernal Prison with Matreous Melwin, they bid party to continue their journey to Waterdeep where he implored them to seek out Old Nim who held the key to their salvation.
