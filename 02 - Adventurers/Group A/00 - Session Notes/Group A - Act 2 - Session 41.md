### 4.7 Hold Back the Dead

At Ironspine Keep, Impyerno Lima bravely stemmed the tide of undead alongside members of Balthazaar's Convoy. Yan's spells held the north wall with Nauk might. Zil and Alain fended off the eastern invaders with both spells and blades while Lioran, Vellin and Khasar stood guard to the south. Meanwhile, Ryn, the Ranger, Ulena, Kaghain and Cannon warded off the western forces.

They maintained the line for the first wave, but when the fight began to turn against them the Ranger called upon Mahariel - a Young Red Dragon - to descend on the battlefield.

During the climax of the battle, an old enemy entered the board; Venomfang, the Silent Stalker. Unfortunately, even with the combined might of Impyerno Lima and their unlikely companion, the green dragon almost sent them to their doom. Things seemed grim as they fell one-by-one, but eventually the party emerged victorious - albeit at the cost of Yan's last spare life.
