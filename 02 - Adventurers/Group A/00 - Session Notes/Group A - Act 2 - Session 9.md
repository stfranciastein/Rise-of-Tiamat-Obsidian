### 2.2 - The City of the First Folk

In the city of Berdusk, they say that it doesn't take long before your coinpurse is liberated from your person by the Zhentarim. It was only a matter of time until this happened to Impyerno Lima, who had otherwise managed to keep themselves safe from any pickpockets. The party was quite light on coin already, so when a thief stole the rest of it and ran off, of course the party gave chase to her.

After a series of blunders, Ryn and Zil discovered the Black Cat Tavern, the openly known headquarters of the Zhentarim. While Ryn was unable to retrieve their gold, Zil was able to take the party's gold from the thief; the Ardragon Jamna Gleamsilver. Jamna explained that her little stunt was simply a way to open up a dialogue with the party, having heard that they have an invitation to Lord Goldmane's Fundraising Gala in Waterdeep. She wanted a simple favour; get her into that party and help her with the heist and they walk away with 10,000 Gold and any money or items they find whilst inside.

Deciding to make a bit more coin, the party went off to the Sunset Mountains in order to retrieve a Belt of Giant's Strength for Flint the Blacksmith. However, before they left they made a quick stop at a bakery as Yan presented Zil with the first birthday cake she's had in a while. 

While out in the wilderness, their wyrmling hatched from an egg to reveal a strange copper/green wyrmling mix, a creature they later named as Varia. While the wyrmling turned out to be quite playful, Yan, Lioran and Ulena realised over the next few days that Varia had an innate anger to it that gave them flashbacks to Venomfang. Able to dissuade it from its destructive tendancies for now, the party moved on and kept roaming the wilds until they found a Goblin village on the foot of the mountains. Inside, they met with the Champion who agreed to give them the Belt in exchange for their help exterminating a group of 'bloodsuckers' from their den in the mountains.

As they ascended up, Impyerno Lima met a curious man named Florian who was posing as a 'devout of Selune the Moonmaiden'. He offered to tell Ryn more about his past, which the Tiefling found himself agreeing to. After Florian's spell, Ryn began getting flashes of his true past; of blood on his hands and the sounds of men and women dying all around him which was in conflict to what he thought he remembered. In this moment of confusion, Florian and his hidden vampiric gnolls attacked the group. While they were able to defeat them, Ryn nearly died in the process and before Florian could be killed he retreated back to his lair.
