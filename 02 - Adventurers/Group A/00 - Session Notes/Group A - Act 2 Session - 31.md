### 3.13 A New Duke

With the defeat of Duke Vanthampur, Impyerno Lima uncovered the identity of her mysterious guest; their old Hobgoblin foe Kevdor. Unconvinced of his supposed innocence, Ryn attacked Kevdor on sight and a fight ensued - if one could call it that. Ozereus, as he later revealed, made quick work of Ryn as he fatally wounded him with one shot. The Tiefling then demanded gold as a bribe in order to keep them alive. Wanting to keep his friends up, Yan paid the toll and the party lived to see another day.

Upon their return to Raven's Arbour, they found themselves at a gruesome sight. Blood painted the walls of the pristine manor and Constabulary guards surrounded the premises. Inside, where they dreaded to find the remains of the Ravenshades, they found both Margaret and Cormyc alive and well. They found out later on that this had been a set-up by the Serpent, not a formal attack per say but a 'thank you' message for getting rid of Thalamra and her Zarielites.

Later that day after spending some well-earned down time in the Gate and putting together more information on the Serpent with Talis, Lioran, Ulena and Yan escorted Glass to Gond's House of Wonders where the Kobold went to work with his latest invention. While there, the trio met with Enver Gortash, a man Yan prodded for answers to his future project; the Bank of Lawntill-Kiln. 

Meanwhile, Ryn, Varia and Zil stayed back at the Sylvira-Van to decompress. As they sat in the afternoon sun, they found themselves intervening in an altercation between two humans; one young man and an older lady named Sebra. After saving her life, Sebra offered Ryn with a reading of his future where he learned that his parents, wherever they may be are still alive and waiting for him to return.

The next day, Zil finally faced her trials and arrived at the Temple of the Raven Queen in order to begin her initiation. Xaria Trevisa guided her into the Hall and bid for her to drink from the Silver Chalice where the Raven Queen gave her a mission; find the Elven woman responsible for the Slaughter Demon and exact the Queen's judgement. Then, and only then will Zil know if she will stand with Xaria as a Talon or live out her days as an exile.
