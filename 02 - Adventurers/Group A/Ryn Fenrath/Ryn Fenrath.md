### **BACKSTORY**

<img src="Ryn Fenrath-1784470191105.webp" alt="Ryn Fenrath" width="300" style="float:left; margin: 0 16px 12px 0;">Ryn grew up in a small mountain village where he was never fully accepted. His Tiefling horns and glowing eyes made him an outcast, and the villagers whispered devil child whenever he passed. He learned to stay quiet and keep to the edges of a place that feared him. 

One night, the village was attacked without warning. Ryn tried to fight back, but the flames overwhelmed everything. When he woke, his left arm and leg were gone, taken by the fire and the faceless attackers who left only ruins behind. A wandering monk by the name of Master [[Kaelor Shadow-Strike]] found him in the ashes and saved his life. 

The monks at the [[Monastary of the Moonmaiden]] forged new metal limbs to replace what had been lost. Ryn then spent his recovery in that hidden monastery, a place dedicated to silence, discipline, and purpose.  Ryn trained with unshakable dedication, rising faster than anyone expected. Master [[Kaelor Shadow-Strike]] saw incredible potential in him and often chose him to demonstrate advanced techniques. To Ryn, this was gratitude and hard work. To [[Tovan]], a senior student who had spent years trying to earn [[Kaelor Shadow-Strike]]’s approval, it was an insult. 

[[Tovan]]’s resentment grew into open hostility. He pushed too hard during sparring, glared from across the training hall, and muttered that a Tiefling outsider didn’t belong in the order. Ryn tried to ignore him, but the tension only grew. 

Eventually, [[Tovan]] forced a confrontation. During one training session, he taunted Ryn relentlessly, mocking his metal limbs, his past, and the destruction of his village. When Ryn stayed silent, [[Tovan]] struck him with a blow meant to humiliate. Something inside Ryn broke. His discipline cracked, replaced by the deep, dark anger he had buried since the night his home burned. 

He fought back with frightening intensity. In the struggle, he twisted [[Tovan]]’s arm too far. A sharp crack echoed across the courtyard, and [[Tovan]] collapsed, clutching his shattered arm. The monks rushed in, but Ryn saw fear in their eyes the same fear he had seen all his life. He realized he had lost control, and worse, he sensed a shadow within himself that he didn’t understand. 

That night, he packed his things and left. Master [[Kaelor Shadow-Strike]] met him at the gates, not with anger, but with quiet sorrow. He alone understood the truth Ryn’s Tiefling blood carried a subtle pull toward darker thoughts and impulses, something Ryn himself had never truly recognized. [[Kaelor Shadow-Strike]] placed a hand on his metal forearm and spoke the words Ryn would carry for the rest of his life: 

`A true hero is not measured by what he conquers, but what he overcomes.` 

Ryn bowed deeply and walked into the night. 

Now he travels the world as a quiet protector, helping those who cannot help themselves. He moves unseen, shaped by the discipline of the monastery and haunted by memories of fire. But he also carries something else within him. Sometimes, in the quiet moments, darker thoughts whisper from deep inside a part of him shaped by his Tiefling nature, a part he does not understand. 

Ryn tries to do good. He wants to do good. But the shadow inside him thinks differently, and he doesn’t even know it’s there.

### ACTII: The Prodigal Monk

#### Coming Home
In [[Chapter 2.0 - Tale of Two Cities]] when the party arrives in Berdusk, Ryn will instantly recognise [[Tovan]] in the crowd. When the two make eye contact, the Orc runs off into the distance. If Ryn gives chase, [[Tovan]] will disappear into the crowd. Monks aligned with [Shar](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shar) will strike the party in the streets regardless of whether or not they choose to pursue him. After the fight, if Ryn chooses to focus in on the markings, he will receive a vision of his memory of the fire in his village.

Flint the Blacksmith may ask the party to retrieve the Inert [[Belt of the Giant]], in which case they will be directed to the Sunset Mountains in order to retrieve it from a Goblin village named [[Sunset Camp]]. Their Champion and Ryn (unbeknownst to either) are facing the same foe; a Vampire named Florian.

Florian is a handsome man with pale white skin, white hair and gleaming yellow eyes. He initially presents himself as a fellow Selunite, simply 'basking in the Moonmaiden's grace'. He will even make small talk with the adventurers, and nothing short of Divine Sense or Detect Good and Evil will reveal his true nature for he glamours himself quite well.

To make nice, Florian offers to restore a memory Ryn has lost and will cast Greater Restoration on him. Ryn will then get a flashback of his earliest memory of being in the burning village, only this time he is holding a dagger, both of his hands covered in blood. He will see a vision of [[Kaelor Shadow-Strike]] 'rescuing' him as he remembers, but he overhears one of the senior monks say that he should be put down.

While this is helpful, the reality is that this is a trap by Florian to lure him in as he is a Vampire. Florian and his spawn have been using the old, abandoned [[Ill met by Moonlight]] as a base of operations. His 2 Gnoll vampires and 2 Vampire Spawn will attack the party. Defeating Florian is no simple task, and he will run away should his lackeys be defeated. If he is killed during the day, Florian will crumble into ash. If killed during the night, his body teleports back to the Temple.

After learning about his past, Ryn gains the ability Reaching Darkness & Pull of the Abyss:

==_**Reaching Darkness**_. When you make an Unarmed Strike, your reach is 10 feet greater than normal, as dark energy extends from you.== 

==_**Pull of the Abyss.**_ Whenever you hit with your Unarmed Strike, you can cause it to deal Necrotic damage rather than its normal damage type. You can also force the target to make a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, you can move the target up to 5 feet towards you, as dark energy swirls around it.==

In the [[Ill met by Moonlight]], Ryn will run into [[Tovan]] one more time who demands he answer for what he has done to [[Kaelor Shadow-Strike]]. He will be beyond reason until Florian reappears and attacks the party again. After emerging victorious, [[Tovan]] will ask Ryn why he really came back here before telling him the grim reality:

TOVAN: So, you really don't know, do you?

*Tovan laughs and looks around at the ruins of his home. He looks at Ryn up and down and at the rest of Ryn's friends before he lets out a sigh. He pulls down his hood to reveal his shaved head.

TOVAN: Do you remember when you left the Temple? The healers tried their best to get me fixed up, but you fucked me up pretty badly. I didn't think I'd be able to walk again. For hours I was so angry, 'that little devilspawn got the best of me', I thought. I'd never be able to live that down. But I sat and stewed for days, and I tried to forget. Told myself it was my fault for messing with you.

*Tovan begins to unwrap his gloves as the moonlight glimmers down on his dull green body. His eye twitches as his fists tighten up, preparing himself to say what comes next.*

TOVAN: A week after you left, that's when they came. Shadows in the night, but not like us. Real shadows. Wraiths, [[Kaelor Shadow-Strike]] called them. They gutted everyone one-by-one till there were only five of us left. I wanted to fight with the others, but my back was broken, my arm was fucked. So [[Kaelor Shadow-Strike]] told me to stay put in the larder. 

I listened to them die, saw their faces after the Sharrans burst in to clean up what the Wraiths missed. I tried to run, like a coward, but they caught me and...

*Tovan undoes his scarf and reveals the scar across his neck.*

TOVAN: They hung me from the rafters and left me to die. I remember feeling nothing but rage as I watched them walk away. All I could see were the smiles on their faces and that stupid feckin tattoo on their arms. As I slipped away, that's when he whispered to me. Vecna. He offered to bring me back to life to get my revenge on those that wronged me. So I took his deal.

*Tovan reveals his other hand. The skin is completely flayed, and parts of his insides are now on the outside. It's clear as day to Ryn that just like Yan Tovan is now a [[Revenant]].*

TOVAN: I've been doing his bidding, biding my time till I get strong enough to face the Sharrans on my own. I can feel them, the five people that came to the Temple. I've hunted one down already, and this Bloodsucker here was the second.

*Tovan points at Ryn.*

TOVAN: I thought you'd be one of them, you know. I hoped it would be. But when I saw you in Berdusk I was blindsided, I've wanted nothing more but vengeance ever since I died, but when I saw you I felt nothing. I wanted to face you there and then but I couldn't, the Sharrans were on me, so I ran. 

TOVAN: [[Kaelor Shadow-Strike]] never told you, did he? Well I guess he's dead, so now is as good a time as any. You're not a Selunite, at least you weren't when the old man found you. You were one of them, a Sharran. I don't know from what Temple, and by the looks of things neither do you.

TOVAN: If you really want to avenge our family and find answers, let me leave. There's a group of Sharrans up north in the Dessarin valley. I've got Sharrans down in Amn to kill, but if you want answers they'll know more.

At this point, Ryn can either choose to kill or spare [[Tovan]]. If Ryn chooses to spare him, Tovan will make good on his word and venture to Amn to hunt down one of the other members of the Sharrans that slaughtered the monks in the [[Ill met by Moonlight]]. If Ryn chooses to kill him, Tovan will simply say this is a waste of time and he will only regenerate the next day. If [[Yan 'Keyreeonis' Lawntill]] asks, Tovan will say he gained the ability to regenerate his body after killing his second target. In either case, the party will leave the Temple and return to Berdusk.
