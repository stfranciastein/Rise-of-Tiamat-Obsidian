### **BACKSTORY**

<img src="Ulena Kiln-1784472168807.webp" alt="Ulena Kiln" width="300" style="float:left; margin: 0 16px 12px 0;">Ulena, a Genasi born of a human mother and a genie father, grew up in the diverse yet isolating region of Silvermoon, where arcane creatures were common but her elemental nature still set her apart. 

Her father [[Sossa Kiln]], a Dao, was distant, but not cruel. His emotional coldness hid a deep fear that his presence put Ulena in danger. Her mother [[Rynae Kiln]] loved her, but carried a secret that slowly consumed her. 




When Ulena overheard their argument, it wasn’t just marital tension, it was a final decision. Her father had been commanded to return to his own realm, or else risk bringing calamity to his family. His sad glance as he left revealed his helplessness. 

Soon after, [[Rynae Kiln]] brought Ulena into the mountains and abandoned her, not out of cruelty, but out of desperation. Whether she fled something hunting them, was cursed, or was compelled magically is still unknown. 

Ulena survived only through the unexpected companionship of a wounded dire wolf, under the protective light of Selûne, forming a tie between her druidic path, the moon goddess, and the wilds. 

Upon returning home, she found a crate where her town should have been, nothing left but a symbol in the center of the scene. Wandering from sacred grove to moonlit ruin, she studied the Old Faith, but never stayed long. She carries both longing and fear, longing for belonging, fear of losing another “home.”

### ACT II: Hero's Journey

After departing from Berdusk in [[Chapter 2.0 - Tale of Two Cities]], the party will reunite with [[Glass]] and [[Grace]] in the Reaching Woods. There, they will meet [[Mr. Greenie]] for the first time, a green-skinned Dao who claims he "made a promise to keep her safe, one that I intend to keep." He will also inform Ulena that there is a woman who wants to meet with her. If pressed he will tell Ulena that she should see who it is for herself. The woman is of course [[Rynae Kiln]].

*As you make your way up north in the Reaching Woods, pushing past the flora and parting the brush with every step, you find yourself in a clearing. The trees seem to sing with the wind and the birds, its melody feeling like a gentle lullaby to your ears. In the middle of the clearing there is a fallen stump, once the base of a mighty tree.*

*When you get closer, you realise it's not the wind that's singing. Perched softly on the wood, you just about see a feminine figure. Her armour is covered in vines, her cloak a mantle of leaves and flowers. She looks to be brushing her long dark hair which cascades down her back. It looks wild, but purposefully so. As she turns her head, you could swear that Ulena was sitting right there, but this woman does not have her red skin. She is human-toned, dark and beautiful, with fine lines and freckles dotting her skin. When she fully turns to face your party, there is no mistaking her identity, not to you, Ulena.*

*This is your mother, [[Rynae Kiln]].*

RYNAE: "Who... who are you?" 

*Rynae stands herself up from the stump and picks up her staff. She does not point it at your party, but you can see that there is caution in her eyes and fire in her gaze as she assesses the situation. It's the same type of readiness you all know well.*

RYNAE: "I'm not here to seek trouble, friends. But know this, the Moonmaiden's blessings can hurt as well as heal."

On a DC 25 persuasion check - 20 if Ulena is the one making the attempt - she can be talked down. If not, she will summon 7 Redcaps in order to protect herself and engage in combat. 

*Rynae's eyes gloss over with a purple haze as your words reach her ears. Her staff flares as she points it towards your party it begins to glow with radiant fire and her eyes go completely white.*

RYNAE: Stay back! These paltry tricks will not work on me!

*She takes her staff and slams it into the ground as a portal appears. The wood then glows with bright white light, morphing until it's almost blinding and a constellation of the Archer appears above her.*

RYNAE: Knights of Silverymoon, aid me!

RYNAE: There is a Druid's Grove in the Misty Forest called [[Garrán]]. An old friend of mine, Archdruid [[Eamir]] oversees the Circle, and he still owes me quite a few favours. I know you have your business here, but meet me there and perhaps we can petition him to lend us a hand. If there are any others you may know who could be of help, we'll need them as well.
