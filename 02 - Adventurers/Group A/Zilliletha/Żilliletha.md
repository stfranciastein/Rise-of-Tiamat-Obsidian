### **BACKSTORY**

<img src="Zilliletha-1784472823198.webp" alt="Żilliletha" width="300" style="float:left; margin: 0 16px 12px 0;">Żilliletha, Żil, is a Shadar-kai woman born and raised in the [[Shadowfell]]—a plane where memories are more precious than gold, and pain serves as a reminder that one is still alive. Children are uncommon in the [[Shadowfell]], and so Żil’s birth was quietly revered. Appreciated, not celebrated. Love does not form here as it does in the so-called civilised societies of the Material Plane. 

Żil had a roof over her head, and what could only barely be called food, present on the table each night. Her home was built of stone and silence. Along its walls were names carved by careful hands, generations etched into them. 

The earliest name read [[Kaelis]]—the first of her family, as far as she knows. Each name carried a subtle desperation, a silent plea that whispered:

`Let this be proof that I was here.`

She was raised with a single, inescapable truth; if your life leaves no mark, it may as well have never been lived. Żil came to resent the quiet desperation of carving one’s existence into stone, mistaking record for meaning. A name on a wall was not a life remembered. It was a life begging not to be forgotten. This, she learned, was the quiet cruelty of the [[Shadowfell]]. 

Love, as she understood it, was restrained and wordless. Her parents offered no emotional warmth, no gentle reassurances. Hardship was endured in silence. Affection was rare. A hug was almost unheard of. A hand placed on her shoulder before danger was sacred. It was not cruelty that shaped them, but discipline. The lack of sentimentality was simply survival. 

Her parents had been raised during a harsher age, one marked by rituals known as the Krwawy Krzyk—“The Bloody Cry”. These rites were not acts of punishment, but defiance. In a realm where emotion dulls and existence fades into numb inevitability, pain became a weapon against stagnation. The rituals reminded the body—and the soul—that life still pulsed beneath the shadow. 

Where death, memory, fate, and loss intertwine, the Bloody Cry served as a rejuvenation of spirit. Not to glorify suffering, but to resist erasure. And although now the Bloody Cry serves as a tale of old, Żil had it performed on her before she was old enough to understand. She may have survived, but she realised something unsettling with time. The ritual reminded her she was alive, yet the [[Shadowfell]] gave her nothing to live for. 

Sometimes she chuckles to herself at the irony—the ritual meant to anchor her to life left her with a scar across her lips and a mind plagued by twisted dreams and unwelcome visions. Faces she does not recognise. Voices that speak without sound. Moments that feel borrowed from lives she never lived. However, this did not mean she would tolerate pointless cruelty. She knew if meaning was defined by impact, the [[Shadowfell]] would rot the tale she believes she owes the family — a name. And that when she finally returns, it must be a name that does not beg to be remembered, but worthy of life.

Żil serves the [Raven Queen](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Raven_Queen) not as a zealot, nor as a fanatic, but as a custodian. Her job is simple, but also sacred and, at times, heavy. She is to guide the dead when they linger around too long. She ensures souls pass on undisturbed. And most importantly, she prevents any interference with death: necromancy, resurrection, unfinished bindings. To the [Raven Queen](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Raven_Queen), death must be clean. To Żil, it must be meaningful. And that is where the fracture begins.

Żil is haunted by the fear of spending her life preserving the meaning of other souls and never creating her own. She has seen souls who lived and passed peacefully — souls who lived hollow lives and clung to existence in terror — and then, souls who were remembered only because someone else bothered to remember them. She questions, “If I die now, what remains of me?” A question that never escapes. There was one soul she guided whose fear mirrored her own — and she did not stay long enough to hear its final words.

It’s important to note that Żil does not hate the [Raven Queen](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Raven_Queen). In all actuality, she reveres her. But the reverence does not come without doubt. The [Raven Queen](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Raven_Queen) teaches that memories are sacred and death must not be denied, and Żil, up until this point, has always obeyed. But now, with time, she begins to wonder. Is memory enough? Is preservation the same as purpose? She fears that the [Raven Queen](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Raven_Queen)’s domain risks becoming a mausoleum of unlived lives. What’s scarier is that the [Raven Queen](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Raven_Queen) hasn’t corrected her.

The Krwawy Krzyk taught her what it feels like to be alive. Her service taught her what it feels like to be necessary. Now she fears becoming something else entirely: useful, remembered, and empty.

The irony of it all is cruel. She preserves meaning for others while desperately searching for her own. That tension is what drives her into the Material Plane. Not to escape the [Raven Queen](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Raven_Queen) — but to test whether meaning can be forged in life, rather than curated in death. That is her goal.

### **ACTII: Raven's Shade**

#### A Chance Encounter
In [[Baldur's Gate]] during her initiation, Zil will run into an elderly Shadar Kai woman in the Wide (just outside the [[Temple of the Raven Queen]]) named [[Katarzyna]] who heavily resembles her mother in appearance. She is pushed over by a group of youths and falls to the ground.

*As you exit the Temple, commotion draws your attention. A gang of teenage humans run past you/your group, pushing down an old woman in the process. She stands up from the ground and dusts herself off, adjusting her shawl in the process. She begins to slowly step towards you. As you come face-to-face with her, you recognise her features immediately. She's a Shadar-kai.*

KATARZYNA: Ah, it's been quite a while since I've seen one of our ilk pass by. Not Shadar-kai, of course, but a Talon.

*You study her body for a second and this woman looks to be fragile and malnourished. There are subtle signs that you recognise that suggest she was strong, once. But standing here and now, it's plain as day that her days as a Talon of the Raven Queen are long gone. At least by a century.*

KATARZYNA: Judging by that look on your face, you've been tasked with your initiation, yes? The [[Talons of the Raven Queen]] think they're subtle.

[[Katarzyna]] will explain that she was once a member of the [[Talons of the Raven Queen]] in her 'youth' but she was given an order she could not complete. On a successful DC 10 Persuasion check, she will lament and say one of her comrades were resurrected by a necromancer and she could not bear to end his life. [[Xaria Trevisa]] then dismissed her from the [Raven Queen](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Raven_Queen)'s service. If asked, [[Katarzyna]] will say this was before [[Cormyc Ravenshade]] was made First Talon.

She will then politely ask if Zil could accompany her to Heapside so she can go pay a visit to an old friend of hers. Her usual companion seems to have run off to chase birds and her basket is quite heavy. Zil can refuse and [[Katarzyna]] will be disappointed but respectfully say goodbye. If Zil does go with her, [[Katarzyna]] will ask her about her own adventures and afterwards begins to tell her own story as they walk to Heapside.

KATARZYNA: After I was banished from the service I found myself wandering Toril for quite some time. My feet took me to all manner of different places, not just the Sword Coast. I was a thief, an adventurer and later even a wife to a dashing human man. But in the back of my mind, all of it felt like ash in my mouth without the [Raven Queen](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Raven_Queen)'s blessing.

*When Katarzyna speaks, there's an air of contempt in her voice like she despises what her life was like. But in it there's also a deep sadness and regret.*

KATARZYNA: My husband and I worked in [[Baldur's Gate]] for some time but he was human, you see. Before I met him I used to feel sorry for the younger races, to live lives so short... how could one experience life to the fullest? But he taught me that it is precisely that which makes their lives so enviable. To know one doesn't have much time left in the world makes one appreciate the time they have left. I only wish I realised it sooner.

*As she finishes her sentence, a tear falls from her eyes and she wipes it off her cheek before smiling. She leads you through the graveyard and towards a pair of headstones that read 'Mikael, loving husband and father', the other says 'Zilesta, beautiful daughter and brilliant mind'.*

KATARZYNA: Hello dear ones.

*Katarzyna kneels down and takes the basket off your hands and places a bouquet of Night Orchids on the headstone. In the stillness of the moment, there's a warm comforting breeze that passes momentarily as she utters a small prayer. A pair of ravens land on the headstones, and Katarzyna smiles.*

KATARZYNA: Zilesta wanted to be a Cleric of Ilmater. She wanted to 'help those in need'. I remember feeling such disappointment, I called her many cruel things a mother should never say to her daughter. I just wanted her to dream bigger, be someone of renown like I tried to be while she still had time.

*She shakes her head and laughs to herself. The old woman wipes another tear down her cheek as she looks towards a group of young men and women watching your group in the distance.*

KATARZYNA: But I was the fool in the end. You see them? They visit her grave more often than I, and I try to come every other day. I did not realise until the end that she had touched so many people with her kindness, that even if she did not become a hero like Jaheria.

#### A Choice Is Made
During Zil's initiation, she will be tasked with finding the source of the murders of [[Cold, Red-Blooded Murder]]. [[Xaria Trevisa]] will tell her that they suspect the source to be a powerful spellcaster or a devil. The Clerics under [[Xaria Trevisa]]'s directive have ascertained that there is necromancy involved with this case.

At the end of the questline when [[Pacted Slaughter Demon]] is revealed to be a greater demon puppeteering bodies through possession, Xaria will be impressed regardless of how Zil decides to end the questline and make her a full talon. Back in the [[Temple of the Raven Queen]], the scene happens.

*As you enter the Temple of the Raven Queen, you are brought by a Cleric towards the Ceremony Hall. The absence of the other Talons within alarms you for a brief moment. Then, you see her. Xaria Trevisa stands at the end of the hall, kneeling on the ground looking up at the stained glass window. The red light filtering through the panes makes her shadow look even larger than it should. As she stands up, Xaria's black armour glistens like obsidian in the light and her usually pale skin is basked in deep, dark, crimson.*

XARIA: I am impressed with your performance, young one. To not only root out but slay a demon of this calibre... I would be honoured to have you join us. 

*Xaria turns, facing you directly now. Her cloak billows almost dramatically as a chilling breeze passes through the length of the chamber.*

XARIA: But first, step forward child. The Queen would see the veil of your soul laid bare.

*As she finishes speaking, the conspiracy of ravens fly into the room, cawing and loudly flapping their wings. They make their way to the end of the Hall, roosting in the rafters up above. A cloud of loose feathers descends as they land, and Xaria picks one out of the air without breaking eye contact and hands it to you with a smirk. 

*Xaria steps to the side and before you stands a blackened mirror, its corners decorated with golden ravens. A single black candle stands atop a beautifully carved pedestal, though its light does not seem to reflect on the mirror's surface. Your breath catches in your lungs for a second as the gravity of the situation pulls you down from the adrenaline high.*

XARIA: Light the feather and speak into the mirror, let the Queen hear your judgement.

*You bring the feather above candle. As the feather catches ablaze, your heart pounds in your chest. You can feel a cold presence in the room, the same presence that has been watching you for some time. In this moment, standing in front of Xaria, you know for a fact that the Raven Queen is here.*

At the end of Zil's confession, the mirror clears and the room brightens once more. Xaria then begins the Oath of the Talons (written in [[Talons of the Raven Queen]]). Once Zil finishes her vows, she is now a full-fledged Talon.

XARIA: It seems the Queen has spoken, kneel before me.

*As you get closer, Xaria puts the blade on your shoulders gently. The lights in the room begin to turn purple, and the statue of the Raven Queen fades into darkness once more before all you see is Xaria's silhouette. When you look up, however, the Raven Queen looks down at you. Her porcelain mask hiding her true form. Her presence is somehow both terrifying but comforting at the same time.*

XARIA: Repeat these words.

*The Shadar-kai draws a blade from nowhere and places it on your shoulder, alternating between your left and right between every vow. When she's finished, she lifts the blade up into the air and it vanishes into the darkness. Her stern face twists into a slight smile as the darkness coalesces in her hand to present you a pin.*

She presents Zil with a pin that represents the [[Talons of the Raven Queen]]. She pins it onto Zil's clothes herself, and the pin briefly glows purple as the [Raven Queen](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Raven_Queen) acknowledges the presence of a new Talon.

XARIA: Welcome to our ranks, Zilliletha, I trust you won't disappoint. You are a true Talon now, sworn to serve the Raven Queen in life and in death. You will go far in our ranks, I think.

XARIA: Torbar has tasked most of your cell into pursuing the [[Cult of the Dragon]], as you already know. Your next mission will not surprise you. This spellcaster, this... Rezmir... there is something twisted about her nature that offends the Queen's balance. By order of the First Talon, you are tasked with putting an end to this sorcerer.
