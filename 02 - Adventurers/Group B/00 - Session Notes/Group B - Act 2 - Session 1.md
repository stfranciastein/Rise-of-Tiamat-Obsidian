### Chapter 1.0 - The Great Hunt

### 1.1-1.2 The Hunter Games 

Upon arriving in Greenest, the party received an enthusiastic greeting as a blue-skinned Tiefling named Azure  gushed over their presence in town. As new upcoming heroes of the Sword Coast, the other townsfolk were more than excited to witness them and the arrival of the other esteemed guests of the Great Hunt.  While there, the party reunited with some familiar faces such as Torbar and Leosin who were there for the festivities. even met some new ones. Most notably, the Androgynous Dragon-like person Soma and the Aasimar Paladin Aramir.

They attended the festivities on their second day in Greenest, going to the carnival on the other side of town. There the party participated in several events. An archery competition which sadly Caradil lost, a strength competition in which Aramir found himself bested by Lord Goldmane, a Pie Eating competition which Soma won, a Dunking Competition which bested them all, an Acrobatics course which Aramir claimed victory over and a cup-shuffling game that Soma lucked out on.

On the third day of Greenest, the Great Hunt began in earnest and the party set off to hunt their quarry; a dangerous Chimera that the Huntmaster Daelaan Winterhound proclaimed to be his 'white whale'. The party spent some time searching the woods for their target, running into the remains of several competitors and getting splattered with blood as some of them fell out of the sky.
