### 2.5 - The Holy City

Aboard the Tidecutter, the Bush Whackers ran into an assortment of characters. One of them included a Red Wizard of Thay named Azbara Jos, an Orc named Khasar and a noble Shield Dwarf by the name of Thogli Battlehammer. After being ambushed, Captain Saltbeard forced them off the ship and the party had to swim to shore with their new Orc and Dwarf companions.

Later, after crossing the River Chionthar with a 'hag's' help, they reunited with their friends only to find that Auron had gone missing along with Caradil's companion Rune. Determined to continue the search but with no real lead to pursue, they decided to head to Elturel with Khasar's suggestion in hopes of finding help there.

There, they finally met with Ontharr Frume and reunited with Matreous Melwin. In their meeting they discovered that the Cult has no real presence nearby, but Leosin fears that there may be a strike force in the making. Matreous also told the party of a lead concerning a group of Necromancers beneath Elturel in the Grand Cemetery.

As they made their way towards the graveyard, they heard a scream errupt from a nearby alley and found a woman holding the body of a dead man.
