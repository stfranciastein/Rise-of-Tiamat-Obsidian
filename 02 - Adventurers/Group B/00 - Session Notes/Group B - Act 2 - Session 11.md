### 2.6 - Keeper of Secrets

Inside the Nest of the Eldritch Eye, the Bush Whackers waded through sewage and ichor and found themselves confronting an evil cult once more. However, this was not the Cult of the Dragon but rather a group of Necromancers working for the Whispered One, Vecna.

As they thwarted the Cult's plans, Khasar disappeared once more to report back to the Third Talon. The rest of the Bush Whackers were awarded greatly by the High Observer for their bravery, and Thogli decided to stay on as a permanent party member. In the end, Thavius Kreeg thanked them for their service and the party was able to pass on a message to Azbara Jos from Brotherhood Inquisitor Yale.

Later that evening they left Grace at the Silver Fire Academy, and discovered that Cerulean had been thieving as of late though the dragon would not admit it. The following morning, the Bush Whackers made their way to the Bent Helm in order to deal with the shady individuals known as the Zhentarim.
