### 2.7 Masterpiece Imbrolgio

The Bush Whackers spent the majority of their time doing a job for the Zhentarim. During their test, they met the Otter Traxigor once more and warned him that Matreous and Orenius were on the lookout for him.

After completing their 'initiation', they were given a job by a mysterious client named Renard Belrose who was looking to hire help in retrieving the Constantori's Portrait. After they were acquainted with Belrose's 'guest', they set off to the Guildhouse of the Agile Hand to try and find the painting. 

While the Bush Whackers weren't as quiet as they could be, they were able to get through most of the Guildhouse unseen until Caradil tipped off the security. Thankfully they defeated the majority of the Agile Hand along with their Guildmaster, Dusk.

In the afternoon after a successful mission, they headed back to the Phontyr's Unicorn and decompressed, running into Stone again who was alive and well. The next morning, the party set forth to the town of Lerwickchester and began looking into the Hill Giant terrorizing their peaceful hamlet.
