### 2.8 Fee-Fi-Fo-Fum

After clearing out the Dragon Cultist camp, the Bush Whackers discovered that its Dragonclaw Ozereus the Ghost had been expecting their presence. A short conversation followed before the Tiefling introduced the real threat to the group; Venomfang the Silent Stalker.

The battle was nearly lost, but thanks to their combined efforts and an unknown Cultist Recruit coming in to help them out they nearly defeated the Green Dragon. Unfortunately, the party was not able to kill him and he and Ozereus were able to get away.

Returning to Lerwickchester, Aramir convinced Elder Rova and the townsfolk must rekindle their partnership with Lythe. Especially in light of the foreboding omen that was Thaddak's blight spreading upon the land once more.

Back in Elturel, the Bush Whackers resupplied and headed straight for the Hall of the Triad. During the war council, the party reunited with all of their previous companions and discussed their plans for the future. After the council, Ontharr tasked them with two things; investigating the attack on Fort Morninglord while another group attacked the Eastern camp, and the other being to secure the allegiance of the Talons of the Raven Queen.
