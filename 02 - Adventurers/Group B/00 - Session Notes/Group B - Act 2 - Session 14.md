### 2.9 Assault on Fort Morninglord pt.1

After a communal night of drinking with their companions, the Bush Whackers set off to combat. The plucky heroes rode straight towards Fort Morninglord accompanied by the Harper Leosin Erlanthar, their new Dwarven companion Thogli of Clan Battlehammer and their returning Elven Druid Tierbhal an Garran.

While they were able to repel the forces of the Cult, they soon realised that the battle was far from over. In an instant, they witnessed a resurrection ritual that turned their fallen foes and allies into zombified units. Determined to finish the fight, they followed the tide of undead to the north where Langdedrosa awaits.
