### 2.10 Assault on Fort Morninglord pt.2

Fighting through the Cult of the Dragon's Forces, the Bush Whackers did battle with all manner of creatures before reaching Langdedrosa's camp. When they found the half-dragon, Aramir challenged him to a duel which ended briefly as Langdedrosa overpowered him. The other Bush Whackers were forced to enter the fray, and chaos ensued as the Cult of the Dragon's forces descended upon them. 

As Langdedrosa got close to death, he called upon his Dragon companion Azureth activating their bond. The battle proved difficult, especially after a good deal of them were downed and the Young Dragon carried Aramir up into the air. Tierbhal pursued but was unable to stop Azureth from dropping him and ending his life. Thankfully, with the help of their companions the Bush Whackers survived and emerged victorious, slaying the Dragon in the end.

Though the battle had been won, at night they found themselves confronted by Rezmir and the red-robed figure once more who revealed himself to be Severin. In their dreams, the Bush Whackers were forced to experience the fate that awaited them as Rezmir gave them a taste of their impending transformations.

Now, they board the Piercing Light and the Bush Whackers head to the City of Blood; Baldur's Gate.
