### 3.1 - Not-Quite-There pt.1

As the Bush Whackers travelled down the Chionthar, they found themselves faced with another mission. In addition to securing the alliance with the Talons of the Raven Queen, they are to track down Rezmir's fourth Dragonclaw, a female bard known only as the 'Serpent'. Caldus and Talis, formerly known as Alia, informed them that the Serpent was more than likely headed towards Baldur's Gate.

Later that evening, as they decompressed from the fight with Langdedrosa, the Piercing Light was almost capsized as it scraped against the rocks while trying to maneuver away from the closed river gate in Vermeillon. Talis, wanting to get ahead of the problem, immediately jumped into a boat and went to investigate. 

The Bush Whackers all followed suit, with Caradil noticing something was wrong as the river seemed to be alive. In the town itself, the found no sign of life save for the shadows that still haunted the streets. In their investigations, they ran into a familiar foe; the Slaughter Demon sent to kill the Red-Bloods. While it was able to get away, they knew that it would return sooner rather than later.

Exploring Vermeillon manor, the party was subject to many apparitions within. Including but not limited to; Helmed Horrors, Flameskulls, Shadows, Ghosts of fallen loved ones and a disembodied voice. When they finally escaped, they headed for the catacombs only to find Jenna Maris seconds away from being executed.
