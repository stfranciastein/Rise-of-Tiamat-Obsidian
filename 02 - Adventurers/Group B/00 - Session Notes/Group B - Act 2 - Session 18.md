### 3.3 - Arrival in Baldur's Gate

Exploring the catacombs beneath Vermeillon, the Bush Whackers confronted the Caprathorn in its lair. With luck, they were able to paralyze it with Axerias's blood stake and extract the essence of Zariel from within its body. The party managed to escape just in time for it to regain control of its body and they sealed it back in the mines with a bit of dynamite and a dash of luck.

Back aboard the Piercing Light, the Bush Whackers found themselves enjoying a well-earned bit of rest as they descended down the river Chionthar. The crew reflected on their experiences in Vermeillon and pondered the road ahead. Caradil looked back on his days in the Renblades, Aramir discussed life growing up as a noble of House Plentyleaf, Soma spoke of their experiences living in the Swamps to the north. Tierbhal also talked about his father's animosity for life outside of the Grove, while Thogli mused about his days as a young man in Citadel Adbar. Later that night, Cerulean also spoke to Soma about the visions hes been getting of his hatch-siblings and how he wants to resist his blood.

In Baldur's Gate, the crew found themselves faced with several encounters; a reunion with their Devilishly handsome friend Lucien Shadowblade of Waterdeep, an attempted assassination on Blaze Portyr, and finally an invitation from the Zhentarim. As they headed deeper into the bloody gate, they knew that their stay here was going to be eventful. Especially with the laughter coming from the bridge below.
