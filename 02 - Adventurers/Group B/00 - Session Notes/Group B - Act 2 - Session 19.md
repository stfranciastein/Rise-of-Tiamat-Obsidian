### 3.4 The Lower City

As the Bush Whackers explored the bloody city of Baldur's Gate, they found themselves dealing with all sorts of trouble once more. Firstly, they ran into a man eager to cut down a house in front of the Chionthar. Upon reporting it to the owner, she went back home and almost fell into the river though thankfully Aramir saved her life. Unfortunately, in revenge she murdered her would-be killer and was promptly arrested. Further along in Whitkeep, Caradil met two gnomes who seemed to be in distress about their missing schematics. He promised to aid them, and in exchange he would create copies of his invention for them, Arcana-Net; a magical item that is capable of granting visual and verbal communication between long distances.

After looking around Little Calimshan, the party was warned of a killer on the loose on the streets and were told of the 8pm curfew. They then checked into the Elfsong for two tendays, settling in for a while before heading off to go shopping for supplies and selling their well-earned loot. In their time in the city, Soma sent a message to their father at Sorcerous Sundries and Aramir spoke to the Githzerai skull whose origins have eluded him for so long. While Caradil attempted to learn more about the Guild and the feared Duke Vanthampur, he found himself largely stonewalled by the majority of the Baldurians he spoke to.

To end the day, they found themselves gravitating to Raven's Arbour. Lady Margaret Ravenshade spoke to them about her investigation into the murders in Baldur's Gate, and sent them on a quest to a nearby bathhouse to look into a lead her men found. In return, they would gain a powerful ally amongst the Patriars and the Lord's Alliance. 

The next morning, they confronted Gardak Brighthorn and Soma negotiated the return of Ardryn's schematics. As Caradil the papers to its rightful owner, Ardryn, Glass and Ackyn set off on upgrading the Sylvra-Van for the road ahead.

At Whitkeep, they also managed to speak with a famous wizard named Natasha 'Tasha' the Dark and were given another job for the Zhentarim; help Natasha reclaim her lost Diadem from Nixylanna Vidorant in Vidorant's Vault in Seatower and she would happily supply the party for their heist on Goldmane Manor. 

During that time, Soma split off from the group and found themselves back in the Upper City, looking for a quick tarot reading from Sebra; the dear old woman they saved from a brutish noble the day before. As Sebra told her fortune, Soma found themselves having a vision of the killer's motives; a tie to the unholy trinity of the Dead Three, Myrkul, Bane and Bhaal.
