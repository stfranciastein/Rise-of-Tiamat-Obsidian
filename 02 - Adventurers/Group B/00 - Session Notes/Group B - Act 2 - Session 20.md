### 3.5 Den of the Dead Three

As the Bush Whackers continued their preparations and resupplied, they found themselves reuniting with their Goliath friend Stone in the Blushing Mermaid tavern. While there, they also ran into the Wizard Traxigor who was accompanied by a Tiefling Knight named Valor. The Tiefling and Otter asked if the adventurers could investigate a rogue book merchant in their place to which the Bush Whackers accepted.

The party resumed their investigations into the murders in Baldur's Gate. This lead them to Mortlock Vanthampur's bathhouse whereupon they later discovered that the noble had been harbouring Dead Three cultists in the sewers. While inside the Den of the Dead Three, they also freed a nobleman named Klim Jhasso who seemed eager to escape his captivity. After clearing out most of the cultists inside, the party finally confronted Mortlock at the heart of his lair and did battle with him. When the dust settled and Mortlock finally fell, he promised that this would not be the last time they see him.

When the Bush Whackers attempted to leave the bath house they found themselves surrounded by corrupt Flaming Fist soldiers who attempted to arrest them. Acting on instinct, Soma and Tierbhal forced the soldiers to flee and the party made their escape. For now.
