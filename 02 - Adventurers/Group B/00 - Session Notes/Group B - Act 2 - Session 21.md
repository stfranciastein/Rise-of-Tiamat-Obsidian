### 3.6 Mazfroth's Mighty Digressions

As the Bush Whackers escaped from their would-be captivity at the hands of the Flaming Fist, they returned to the Elfsong Tavern to recuperate. At night, Caradil found himself visited by a strange crow who thanked him and the party for their killing of Mortlock Vanthampur. The next morning however, it seemed that the city was not quite as thrilled with his death as the Fist seemed to be on the lookout for potential suspects.

A day of inquiries and two awkward confrontations with both the Talons and Lady Ravenshade later, the party found themselves approached by another aide, this time by an unnamed imp who refused to elaborate on who or what sent him. The imp proved difficult to talk to but seemed eager to help, promising them aid with their investigation into the Vanthampurs.

Later, as the Bush Whackers continued on their mission to help the Archwizard Traxigor, they were able to uncover a scam by the Amberdune Books stall. As it turns out, Korvala had been selling gingwatzims in order to save up enough money to hire a powerful spellcaster. After confronting her in her home, Soma and Tierbhal dashed off with the evidence Traxigor needed. 

Their victory seemed to be cut quite short, however, as they descended from their rooms and discovered that even more people had been killed as they slept.
