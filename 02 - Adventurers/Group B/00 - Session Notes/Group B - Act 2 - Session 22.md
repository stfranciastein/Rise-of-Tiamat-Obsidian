### 3.7 Low Lantern

After a long night at the Elfsong Tavern, the Bush Whackers went about their day to continue their investigation into the Vanthampurs and to get some more errands done. While Soma and Caradil went shopping, Aramir pursued a band of Knights from House Greenwood and learned some grave news; his father's imminent death and his supposed poisoning, and his younger brother's involvement with Amrik Vanthampur.

Back in Baldur's Gate, after some consideration the Bush Whackers then decided to head to the Low Lantern with a plan to find Aramir's brother. While there, Aramir tried to flirt with Lyra to learn more about the Bard and learned that Amrik was down below the decks. In their confrontation, Amrik revealed that he had Aramir's brother hidden somewhere. In his rage, Aramir attacked and a fight followed soon after. Before they could kill him, Amrik managed to escape and promised that they would see each other again soon.

In their attempt to leave the Low Lantern, the Bush Whackers ran into the Cult of the Dragon. Not wanting to force a confrontation, they ran and found themselves splitting up in an attempt to lose the heat on their persons. Soma found shelter outside of Sorcerous Sundries while Caradil and Aramir ran into some suspect individuals. While Caradil was able to prevent danger, Aramir fought with a killer on the Heaps and nearly lost his life for good. Thankfully, Tierbhal and Stone managed to get to him on time and they were able to reunite with Caradil.

Now, they descend into the sewers of Baldur's Gate and in a sticky situation.
