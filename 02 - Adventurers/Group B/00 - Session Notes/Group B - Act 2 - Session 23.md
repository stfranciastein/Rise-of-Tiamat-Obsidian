### 3.8 Vanthampur Villa

After a night of trouble, the Bush Whackers found themselves in the 'haunted' town of Tumbledown. While there, Caradil witnessed a strange sight as several individuals seemed to congregate on the Cliffside Funerary Home. The next morning, they attempted to investigate the graveyard but they didn't find anything of note save for the disturbed body of Gilrain Szarr.

One failed stakeout later, they decided to make their way towards Raven's Arbour and met with Lady Margaret Ravenshade and her son Cormyc. While Margaret was disappointed by the events at the Low Lantern, she found herself compelled by Aramir's conviction. She agreed to support their assault on Vanthampur Villa, requesting only that the party spare Thalamra and Thurstwell if they are found innocent.

Accepting Cormyc's aid in getting them into Vanthampur Villa, they found themselves fighting their way through the Duke's forces, meeting all sorts of people such as Grand Mistress Slobberchops, and Thurstwell Vanthampur. During their conversation, Thurstwell presented the Infernal Puzzlebox to Soma, which both him and his patron Lucien were unable to open. He also seemed interested and quite eager to have the Bush Whackers murder his mother and brother, alleging that Thalamra is working with a 'friend from the east' to take over Baldur's Gate.

Piece by piece, they slowly started to get an idea of their foe. Opening the door to the basement, the Bush Whackers made their way beneath the Villa, spiralling inevitably to the centre of this infernal conspiracy.
