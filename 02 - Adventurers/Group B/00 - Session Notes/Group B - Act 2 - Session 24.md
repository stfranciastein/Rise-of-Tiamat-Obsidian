### 3.9 A New Duke

The Bush Whackers confronted the rest of Duke Vanthampur's forces, they found more prisoners held within including Satiir Thione-Hhune and a Candlekeep Tabaxi Wizard. Through speaking to them, they learned that Thalamra had become transformed into a Greater Devil known as an Erinyes in service to the First Lord of Avernus; Zariel. 

Fighting through the ranks of the Cultists, they eventually found themselves face-to-face with Thalamra herself. During their confrontation, the party found Aramir's brother Augustus but arrived too late as Thalamra quickly slayed him. Aramir attacked in anger, beginning a fight to the death. The battle was taxing, and many of them nearly lost their lives but they managed to emerge victorious. 

After their combat Soma later discovered another Essence of Zariel and consumed it, gaining another Blessing from the First Lord. Deeper in the tunnels beneath the villa, the party also found the Vanthampur Vault, and inside they met a familiar face - Ozereus the Swift. The Tiefling taunted them and left after a brief exchange, letting the Bush Whackers pillage the coffers. Within the loot, they met the Solar Samuel, an agent of Torm who agreed to aid them in their campaign in exchange for his freedom. 

Upon their return to the surface and Raven's Arbour they were met with a grizzly sight, a 'thank you' from the Serpent and a warning that she would strike at Margaret soon. Now, they ponder their next move.
