### 3.10 Exit Bear, Stage Right

After emerging victorious from their battle with the Vanthampurs, the Bush Whackers attempted to return the Shield of the Celestial to the Thione-Hhune family only to find out that Lutecia Hhune was a Horned Devil in disguise. As they escaped from the Hhune family home, they dashed off to rescue Danthelon's supplies outside the city limits.

Back in Willowvale, they found themselves talking to familiar faces such as the wizard Feldir Whitefyre and Pyrax an Garrán of Tierbhal's home grove. Feldir told the Bush Whackers about the new dangers in the Cloak Wood, namely the Suneater Owlbear that was making regular raids on the Coast Way.

One failed negotiation later, they fought the Suneater Owlbear in its lair. The party emerged bloodied but victorious, finding out upon their return to Willowvale that 2 days had passed since their departure. But, it was not all bad news, for they were also about to receive a blessing that they didn't know they would get. Meeting me.
