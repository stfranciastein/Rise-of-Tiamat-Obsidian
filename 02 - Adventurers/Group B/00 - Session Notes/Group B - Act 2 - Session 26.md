### 3.11 Reflections and Dragons

After making quick work of the Devastation Orb, the Bush Whackers pressed on toward Shade Rest. Tierbhal remained behind in Willowvale to tend to his fallen friend Pyrax, while the rest of the group set out for Candlekeep, Larissa rejoining their ranks along the way. The journey there wasn't uneventful they were ambushed by more devils, as well as a vengeful bandit and his forty friends who nursed a grudge against the party.

Arriving in Candlekeep, the party set to work and began researching all manner of things ranging from Devil Pacts to research on Chromatic and Metallic Dragons. With Sylvira's help they were also able to determine that the 'solar' inside the Shield of the Celestial is actually a fallen Archdevil named Gargauth. Acting quickly, Sylvira banished the shield into a pocket dimension before it could do more harm. Aramir, meanwhile, met with the Great Reader and learned that his family's founding member, Cassios, had been a Hellrider over 200 years ago, having once confronted the Cult of the First Lord near Waterdeep. Soma's research turned up its own revelations: first, the Harmonic Convergence, a celestial event said to exert a powerful influence over the Weave; and second, that the mysterious presence watching over Cerulean was likely an agent of the Court of Metal a group of Ancient Metallic dragons long entwined with history's turning points.

As their day in Candlekeep drew to a close, grimmer news awaited that night; they had at most 2-3 months left to live and the clearer their visions of Rezmir grew, the closer they came to losing their bodies entirely. Rezmir, who they also learned had been warped by magic and had abilities far beyond an ordinary spellcaster. 

Now, just two days away from the Feast of the Moon, they've returned to Baldur's Gate - party member richer and one shield poorer.
