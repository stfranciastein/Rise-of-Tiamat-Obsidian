### 3.12 Cold, Red-Blooded Murder

It was cold, red-blooded murder, plain as day. That's what Mavis described it as. When they found that girl by the pool she was soaked through and through with blood, sweat and tears. The broad puffed on her pipe as they told her about the killer. A demon with almost as many arms as it had victims. When the Inspector looked closer, there was no doubt they had nowhere to go but to tumble on down to Tumbledown.

Deep in the catacombs they began to piece the sorry tale together. A young girl named Alessandra made slick deal with the Demon Lord Orcus in order to exact her vengeance on the Red-Blood Mercenaries. Her plans were almost done, but the Bush Whackers gave her a good one-to and ended her tale. All seemed well, until they got back to the Temple.
