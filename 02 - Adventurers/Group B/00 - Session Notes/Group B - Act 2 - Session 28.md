### 3.13 A Favour for Natasha

After a long day working with the Talons of the Raven Queen, the Bush Whackers caught up with their companions at the Elfsong Tavern and pondered their future journeys to the North. The next morning, they performed their last side quest in the Bloody Gate; the Favour for Natasha at Vidorant's Vault. As they snuck about, quiet as a mouse, they were able to make it deep into Nixylanna's lair and extracted the Ruby Diadem with only a few traps triggered. 

Unfortunately, they did not disarm the pressure plate and were spotted by the Master Thief. Vidorant seem amiable enough and even offered them a deal; impressed by their skills as a group. The Bush Whackers returned to Whitkeep Hostel without the diadem, but were still rewarded by Jives as a gift for the attempt.
