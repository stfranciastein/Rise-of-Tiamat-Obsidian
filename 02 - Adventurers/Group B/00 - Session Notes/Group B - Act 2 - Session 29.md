### 3.14 The Feast of the Moon

With one task left ahead of them, the Bush Whackers tried to get a head start of the Serpent Asha Ardeat. Unfortunately, they quickly found themselves at a loss as Leosin and Tallis were both missing from the Elfsong. As they searched for the Harper, they were lead to his last known location by Klim Jhasso. Caradil filled him in on their plans, but unbeknownst to them Leosin had been enthralled by Asha and Klim was non the wiser.

After dealing with the ambush set before them by the Cult of the Dragon, they pursued a lead to go where Leosin and the Harpers had last seen Asha. However, upon exploring the premises, they soon realised that it was just a decoy. Finding themselves now two steps behind, they ran to Little Calimshan and arrived at the Feast proper. There, the party were forced to keep up appearances and partake in the festivities. During the Epic of Abdel Adrian, Asha taunted them one last time provoking Caradil into running from his seat and confronting her. At the climax of their fight, all seemed lost until Soma and Tallis brought their party back from the brink and vanquished the fourth Dragonclaw.

That night during their well-earned sleep, the party found themselves being jostled into another vision - this time of Rezmir and Severin at the ruins of Soubar. As the Wyrmspeaker taunted them once more and Rezmir revealed her artifact, the party came to the grim realisation that the Cult were in possession of far more than just the Dragon Masks. And while for Caradil and Aramir it didn't seem like anything more than just the same threats, Rezmir's challenge to Soma seemed far more personal.
