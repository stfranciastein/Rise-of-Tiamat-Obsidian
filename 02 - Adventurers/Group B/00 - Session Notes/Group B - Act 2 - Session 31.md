### 4.2 - Twenty-Sided Trouble at the Big Bad Base

Pressing on with their journey, the Bush Whackers were able to get to know their fellow travellers more during their combat-filled two days. Unfortunately, the found themselves at a hard stop after failing to secure the caravan against an Earth Elemental. While Glass was able to partially fix it, this setback delayed their journey for several more hours. Their troubles didn't stop there, however. 

That very night before they slept, the party came across another sample of the mutated brand of gelatinous goo. The same kind that had infected their caravans and supplies. Determined to solve the problem, they decided to find the Big Bad Base at Cannon's suggestion, as the Goblin believed its Kobold and Goblin inhabitants to be the source of this ooze. 

As the party explored the area they didn't find anyone within until they reached the inner chamber. As Caradil watched an Ogre fall into the acid, they soon found themselves face-to-face with a geometric enemy.
