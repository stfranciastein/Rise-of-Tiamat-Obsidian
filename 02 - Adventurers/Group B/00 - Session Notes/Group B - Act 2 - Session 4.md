###  1.5 On The Road

The Bush Whackers encountered Grace once more, saving her this time from a pack of Gnolls. In the aftermath of the fight, Aramir noticed that Grace was not truly 'human' in the way she appeared, but a Fey like Tierbhal and Soma. The party then simply sent her on her way once more, with the gift of 10 Gold.

Later the party arrived in the village of Windpeak after being  directed there by Lord Ravenshade. Things seemed tense and uneasy as the villagers didn't seem to want to speak to them. Fear and paranoia was high in the air as they searched for Ravenshade's men. They found what remained of the Talons stuffed into a crate inside the warehouse, but quickly discovered that the Cult still had a presence here.

After a lengthy battle with a small group of Cultists, Caradil found a door on the far side of the chamber that lead to the final room. Inside, the Dragonborn Paladin Zeraxes was speaking to the new Wyrmspeaker, Thaddak Ice-Veins. Thaddak spoke briefly to Zeraxes about the Cult's progress before turning to face Caradil and ordering Zeraxes to attack.

In the aftermath of the battle, Caradil discovered the location of the Dragon Hatchery. Additionally, he chose to keep for himself and Soma what remained of the wealth of Windpeak that was taken by the Cult. Tierbhal, Aramir and Stone did not agree with this and abstained from the gold.

Auron, the Cleric of Chauntea, joined them as the Bush Whackers talked him down from the edge, both figuratively and literally. He took up the mantle of Bahamut once more, leaving the village of Windpeak to do battle with the Cult of the Dragon.
