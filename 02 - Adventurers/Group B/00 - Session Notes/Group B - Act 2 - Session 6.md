## Chapter 2.0 - Tale of Two Cities

### 2.1 - Berdusk, the City of Pariahs

The Bush Whackers arrived in the city of Berdusk, now under control by the tribal Orcs of the Sunset Vale. They met with the High Harper Jaheria who gave them a grim view of their future; find the person responsible for their curse or perish and turn into monsterous Abishai.

During their time in Berdusk, they also ran into a familiar face; the Gnomish Zhentarim agent Jamna Gleamsilver. The woman offered them a deal; bring her with them to the Greenest Gala and in exchange they'll get paid 10,000 Gold and get to keep anything they find in Goldmane Manor. The party agreed and signed the contract, sealing their agreement with ink.
