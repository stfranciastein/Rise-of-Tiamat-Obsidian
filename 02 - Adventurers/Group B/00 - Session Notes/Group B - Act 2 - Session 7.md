### 2.2 - Ill Met By Moonlight

In Berdusk, the Bush Whackers found themselves wandering the city streets, looking for the Wizard of Candlekeep that was rumoured to have been spotted there. 

Through a series of shennanigans related to their investigation, they found themselves in the company of a mysterious alcholic otter. After giving it several bottles of booze, the otter managed to throw it up and reveal that it was in fact Archwizard Traxigor.

Later on, as they prepared to leave the city, Caradil and Aramir found themselves tailing a man who attempted to run away from them. Upon confrontation however, they soon discovered that it was no man at all, but some sort of strange disfigured creature.

Outside the city walls, they were able to locate the village below the Sunset Mountains, meeting its Goblin residents lead by the Champion known as Jaflin. The party was told by Jaflin that Vampires have been descending from the mountain tops and killing his people, and the Bush Whackers agreed to help him in exchange for the Belt of Giant's Strength.

Up near the peaks of the Sunset Mountains, they came upon a ruined Temple to Selune which seems to have been overrun by Sharrans and undead creatures. The party did battle with the initial occupants, making quick work of them but not before one of the creatures could bite Tierbhal and take some of his vitality. Now, they prepare to explore the Temple further.
