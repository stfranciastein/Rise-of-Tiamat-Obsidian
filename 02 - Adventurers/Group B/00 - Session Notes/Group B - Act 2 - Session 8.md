### 2.3 - Belt of Giant's Strength

In the Temple of the Moonmaiden, the Bush Whackers fought off the Sharran Monks and undead creatures that had made the ruins their home. The fighting was tough and a few members nearly lost their lives in the process, but with radiant magic in their repertoire they were able to stem the tide. Afterwards, Tierbhal reconsecrated the Temple to Selune once more, ensuring that no undead will be able to thrive there in the future.

On their journey back, the Blue Dragon egg hatched and out came a Blue Wyrmling with specs of Bronze on its body emerged. The Bush Whackers decided to name the Wyrmling Cerulean, the Calming Sea.

Their troubles did not seem to be over in Berdusk. Upon their return, Tierbhal and Caradil had a heated confrontation. Tierbhal expressed his disappointment that Caradil did not return the money of Windpeak and his anger had reached a boiling point. In the end, Tierbhal was unable to reconcile with Caradil's lack of empathy and left the party, with Stone joining him out of loyalty.

Later that night, the party met Thelvin Kaervath back in the Patriarch's Rest. Thelvin told Caradil that he'd been looking for their old companions in the Renblades. The grizzled ranger has been on a quest to bring justice to Audric's name, but the Greypools declined his offer. Caradil was also told that that Amory -- another former Renblade -- had been involved with some 'shady' dealings with Xanathar's Thieves Guild and warned him that he may be hunting for him as well.

The next morning, as the Bush Whackers prepared to spend another few days in Berdusk, they came upon a grizzly scene; a murder most foul, commited by gods-know-who or what. According to the handsome Devil in their ear, a demon is behind this, but where it may be is for the party to find out. That, and they must find their lost companions Auron and Glass, who seem to have reappeared all of the sudden after being gone for several days.
