### BACKSTORY

<img src="Aramir Void-Blade-1784473822865.webp" alt="Aramir Void-Blade" width="300" style="float:left; margin: 0 16px 12px 0;">Aramir was raised as the youngest of three sons and one daughter in the wealthy [[House Plentyleaf]]. The eldest child, a daughter, treated him more like her own son than a younger brother. She often scolded him for getting into trouble and comforted him whenever he was sick or hurt.

Unknown to Aramir, this eldest “sister” is actually his biological mother. As a young woman she fell in love with a wandering knight of no noble blood—an affair that would have brought disgrace upon the family. To hide the scandal, her parents (Aramir’s grandparents) pretended the boy was their own child and raised him as the youngest son of the Plentyleaf line. Aramir still believes the story he was told. 

His true father, the knight, is still alive and wanders the Sword Coast. He knows of Aramir’s existence but has never met his son and has chosen to keep the secret.

Aramir’s aasimar lineage awakened when he was fourteen. An orc raiding party descended on [[House Plentyleaf]]'s estate—located halfway between [[Neverwinter]] and the Neverwinter Wood—in a senseless act of violence. The estate was rich in both pipeweed fields and gold but was only lightly defended, as many of the household guards had been called to the city to deal with the open chasm. In the massacre, only Aramir and his father (the man he believes is his father) survived. Aramir became the sole heir to the Plentyleaf lands.

Soon afterward, his father remarried a woman from Neverwinter who openly despised Aramir. When she later gave birth to twin boys, the new stepmother quickly turned the old lord’s favor toward her own sons. Despite being the rightful heir, Aramir found himself pushed aside in his own home.

Since the raid, Aramir has burned with hatred for those who prey on the innocent for material gain, personal pleasure, or the advancement of evil causes. He swore the Oath of Zeal and dedicated himself to protecting the weak. His father eventually sent him south to the town of [[Greenest]] “to bring honor to the family name.” By then Aramir had already spent years serving in the court of Lord [[Dagult Neverember]], where he learned the intricacies of noble life from a young age. He later distinguished himself helping turn back the tides of undead that plagued Neverwinter.

It was during one of the many desperate defenses of the city that he earned the moniker “Void-Blade.” When he unleashed his paladin smite, radiant silver light and swirling void-black energy erupted from his blade—an unmistakable sign of his awakened Aasimar heritage.

### ACT II - Prodigal Son

#### A Conspiracy
In the city of [[Baldur's Gate]] in [[Chapter 3.0 - The Bloody Gate]], Knights from [[House Greenwood]] will be rumoured to have been spotted looking for a nobleman from [[Neverwinter]]. These are [[Eddard Greenwood]] and [[Jon Greenwood]], along with [[Karl Melwin]] of [[Waterdeep]]. When the party meets them, they learn that they are looking for [[Augustus Plentyleaf]] and Aramir respectively. According to Eddard, [[Nedes Plentyleaf]] has fallen ill and likely doesn't have much time left to live. 

EDDARD: My condolences my Lord, we were ordered by our patron to seek out the remaining sons of Lord [[Nedes Plentyleaf]] in order to inform them of this news.

[[Eddard Greenwood]] will also remark that [[Augustus Plentyleaf]] was last seen talking to [[Amrik Vanthampur]] at the [[Low Lantern]] tavern. When the Knights attempted to get more information, the Baldurians all warned them against making any dealings with [[House Vanthampur]]. Instead, the Knights have left word at Augustus's last known location at the [[Helm and Cloak]] and are currently planning on going to [[Ironspine Keep]] to help [[Lorgan Brandis]].

If the party attempts to question [[Amrik Vanthampur]] on the whereabouts of [[Augustus Plentyleaf]], he will remain cagey and vague. If Aramir decides to pursue an aggressive route, he will immediately attack and fight until he's blooded. If he is able to escape, the party will not be able to kill him unless they pursue the quest [[Den of the Dead Three]] and [[A New Duke]].

In [[Vanthampur Villa]], Duke [[Thalamra Vanthampur]] will be holding [[Augustus Plentyleaf]] hostage inside of her hidden temple to [[Zariel]]. She will be caught ready to sacrifice him to the First Lord and will have the following to say about Augustus:

THALAMRA: Your brother here miscalculated. Trying to take me and my son for a fool, thinking he could outsmart us. And when he gets caught, he tried flaunting his money about. At me, of all people. He will learn, and you will learn that nobody plays with me or my family. 

AUGUSTUS: Aramir, please help! Stop her!

THALAMRA: Quiet now, rat. The adults are talking.

*One of the cultists stab Augustus in the gut, making him whimper in pain. Thalamra turns to you as she manifests a set of plate armour and a large sword. In her other hand appears to be a whip, with devils carved into the grip and handle.* 

THALAMRA: Do you know how your brother came into his current predicament? Your family promised the First Lord many souls in exchange for wealth and power. Your father has enjoyed the benefits for too long without paying the price. My Lady has been patient, but she grows tired and seeks to collect. Those Orcs didn't finish the job, but it doesn't matter. Fate as seen to it that this little rat here found himself in the wrong place at the wrong time.

*With a thought and slices Augustus's torso clean open, almost bisecting him completely. As she turns towards your party you see a massive set of feathery wings emerge from her back. Thalamra, it seems, is no longer an ordinary Warlock of the First Lord but an Erinyes.*

THALAMRA: The Descent is almost upon us and my Lady needs as many souls as possible for the Blood War. Your brother will not be the first nor last to serve her needs. You shall be next. So come, Paladin. Let us see who has their god's favour.

[[Thalamra Vanthampur]] will kill [[Augustus Plentyleaf]] off-initiative unless prevented. If not, she will first swing at him making him immediately die due to his lack of hit points. In the event that the party somehow stops both attempts, the cultists with the Duke will ensure that he bleeds out. The only way to save him is through the Revivify spell as the ritual will send his soul straight to [[Avernus]] and [[Zariel]].

Upon the Duke's defeat and her uttering her final 'goodbye' to the party, [[Thalamra Vanthampur]] will remark that [[House Plentyleaf]] is doomed to fall. She will then taunt Aramir by saying that whoever sold [[House Plentyleaf]] to [[Zariel]] still intends on upholding their end of the bargain and that he won't have long before the end is upon him.

THALAMRA: The forgotten child... you shall be the one to see your House burn. See you in hell.

After [[Thalamra Vanthampur]] is slain and is forced to reconstitute in [[Avernus]], what remains of [[Augustus Plentyleaf]] can be investigated. On his body is a record of all of his notes on attempting to track down the person responsible for the downfall of [[House Plentyleaf]].

The handwriting is hardly anything to gawk at, but it's clear that Augustus was meticulous in tracking down whoever this evil was. According to his notes, he believes that someone close to the family must've made a deal with [[Zariel]] to secure [[House Plentyleaf]]'s prosperity. He was heading to [[Candlekeep]] in order to find more about his family history when he was caught by [[House Vanthampur]]. [[Amrik Vanthampur]] immediately recognised him as [[Zariel]] marked him as a debtor to the First Lord.

#### Danger in Daggerford
In [[Candlekeep]], Aramir can spend a few hours in the Great Library to learn that one of his ancestors, Cassios of Elturgard. Cassios was once one of the original [[Hellriders]] from [[Elturel]]. However, after the events of the Charge of the Hellriders he found himself leaving Elturgard and moving north to the city of [[Waterdeep]].

*Cross-referencing the dates with archived papers from Waterdeep Watch, you start to get a much clearer and detailed picture. According to several editions of the Watch, Cassios was allegedly involved with dealing with the 'Cult of the First Lord' in the outskirts of Waterdeep. As you continue to flick through several papers, the coverage changes to celebration. According to the Watch, Cassios and his adventuring party put the entire plot down. After that, Cassios and his party were recognised as heroes and given titles.*

Cassios's adventuring party consisted of:
- Cassios of Elturel, a male human Paladin of Lathander.
- Stefan Halvachar, a male gnome Cleric of Selune.
- Eamonn an Garrán, a male wood elf Druid of Eldath.
- Lady Prior Heavyhand, a dwarf female Rogue.

Further investigation will yield Lady Prior returned to [[Mithral Hall]] over one hundred years ago. Asking [[Tierbhal an Garrán]] or anyone in [[Garrán]] for the location of [[Eamonn an Garrán]] will result in the party learning that Eamonn died of sickness years ago. Stefan of [[House Halvachar]] is the only remaining member of the adventuring party and has retired to [[Daggerford]].

Asking either [[Sylvira Savikas]] or one of the other Avowed in the Great Library will result in the party learning that Infernal Pacts do not need the consent of the victims, only requiring the signee to be willing to make that sacrifice. If asked for more insight, Sylvira will say she believes that there's something else that happened with Cassios and suspects he made a deal with the Zarielites that his descendants are now paying the price for. She will also mention that there is likely 'his' copy of the Pact stored wherever he may be buried.

There are no records of where Cassios was interred, but asking [[Stefan Halvachar]] will yield in him revealing that he was given something by Cassios prior to his death; an infernal puzzle box. This box is almost as ancient and sealed as the one that houses [[Thavius Kreeg]]'s pact, but can be solved either by [[Lucien]] or [[Sylvira Savikas]]. Inside, the following pact is written;

(write pact about making sure the family stays wealthy as long as the leader of House Plentyleaf continues to sacrifice souls to Zariel).
