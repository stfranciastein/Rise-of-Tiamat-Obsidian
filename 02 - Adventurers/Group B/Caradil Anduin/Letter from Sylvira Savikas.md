*Caradil,

*Your letter is appreciated and all is well here in Candlekeep. I heard about what happened in Greenest, grateful that you and the rest of your company are keeping well.*

*While unfortunately I cannot support you in any direct way from all the way here, I can give you some much needed help. My husband Orenius is destined for Elturel and should be there in a few days time. One of our comrades, a firebrand of a Wizard by the name of Traxigor has been on the move for far too long and hopes that he can intercept the old man in one of his usual haunts.* 

*Meet with him there if you can, he will provide you with what you need.*

*Sylvira Savikas
Archwizard of Conjuration
Great Reader of Candlekeep
Utmost Expert of the Hells*
