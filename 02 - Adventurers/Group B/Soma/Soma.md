### BACKSTORY

<img src="Soma-1784475185736.webp" alt="Soma" width="300" style="float:left; margin: 0 16px 12px 0;">Soma is the result of a deal between two vicious creatures who sought to further their agendas through the union. While the hag (mother) sought to secure her legacy and hold in the region, the dragon (father) wished for something far greater - a soldier who'd be their eyes and ears, and who'd exercise his will indiscriminately. Born from magic, Soma has faced expectations that they could never truly jump. 

Torn between two beings who both tried to distance the child from the other one, they would learn the methods of each of them. The hag taught him magic and deception, and the dragon would teach they about fear, and how to use terror as a weapon. Despite it all, Soma had love for both of their parents - they were never abused, but they continue to be blind to the fact that they were trained to be used from birth.

Come their teenage years, however, when they rummaged through an adventurer's abandoned bag, they found a book. It was the most stereotypical book about a hero and their adventures, and yet it captivated Soma's mind and heart. The story of the good deeds, and the world described within, captured their imagination, and lit the candle of curiosity within their chest - what's it like to be good? To DO good? It was against everything that they had heard and knew before, but it gave them a goal to achieve that was actually theirs, and not their parents. To be a hero.

The opportunity arose when their mother captured a band of adventurers who later broke free, killing her in the process. The spell that bound Soma to the old cove's location faltered as her corpse hit the ground, and for the first time in their life, Soma was free. Saddened by the death, and not wanting to turn to their father after the two had a smaller altercation, Soma took what he deemed necessary and headed out to see the world for the first time in their life, and to try to become a hero themselves.

Suffice to say, his father is not very happy about this development, and is now trying to find ways to get his kid "back".

### OTHER

- Their "Mother's" (Hag's) name was [[Maria Wormspeaker]], dragon father's name is [[Nerkurik]] (roughly translates to spear slaughter in draconic - comes from his VERY sharp, spear like tail that he uses to stab and impale adventurers on. There is always at least one rotting corpse decorating that tail).
- They hear the whispers of dragons long-gone occasionally - let them be good, or bad. This has been with them since their birth - however, above all these whispers roars their father's voice which is also present if the dragon wishes to communicate with his child. Nowadays it’s mostly in the veins of “get back here or I will get you back myself and you don’t want that” kind of things.
- They have a giant snail friend (think of a giant african snail for size) who they just affectionately call [[Slime]]. It has been their companion since they were a child - a creature who never spoke, never obeyed commands, and yet somehow would still keep showing up wherever Soma went - at a speed that would not be possible for a snail. The snail's shell always has something inscribed onto it, but the meaning of it is obscure to Soma, they could never "read it". These symbols / letters / whatever they might be shift - each time Soma sees Slime after being apart, the shell shows something different.
- Will do everything and anything for a book that peaks their interest - ones listing forbidden or forgotten magic are usually in this category. When I say "everything and anything", I mean it.
- Major daddy issues, so they have a thing for dragonborns and half-dragons. As such, they are willing to do things for them that they would not do for individuals of other races.
- Since they were raised far away from civilization, they cannot recognize social cues, and do not know the norms. For example, in a tavern they would try to take everything they need - even from other tables, or if someone offered them a handshake, they would have no idea what to do with it.
- Views other hags as if they were completely normal grandmas. Loves talking and cooking with them.

### ACT II: Hagborn

When the party leaves [[Berdusk]] after the events of [[Chapter 2.0 - Tale of Two Cities]], Soma will be given a vision of a hag preying upon children in a forest where the river splits the woods into two. A DC 10 History check will reveal that this is likely the Reaching Woods just North East of the Chionthar. The party will reunite with [[Glass]] and [[Grace]] alongside [[Mr. Greenie]] who will tell them about the druid in the woods that they need to go help.

They will run into [[Rynae Kiln]] who Soma and [[Tierbhal an Garrán]] will immediately recognise as being charmed by a hag. [[Rynae Kiln]] will not trust Soma whatsoever, despite what the rest of the party may say and immediately engage in combat.

*As you make your way through the woods you find yourself in a clearing. It's peaceful and serene here, but something immediately catches your eye as you exit the treeline. A woman with dark skin and long curly black hair sits atop a tree stump, staff to the side. She wears a long green dress, her leather armour covered in leaves and vines. She picks up on your party's arrival immediately and stands up, staff in hand.*

RYNAE: Who are... you. Stay back!

*The woman points her staff towards Soma and it begins to glow a bright white, its the type of pale radiant energy you know all too well from adventuring with your own druid friend Tierbhal. She is preparing to engage in combat.*

RYNAE: I do not fear you, Hagspawn!

*She slams her staff into the ground as vines erupt from the ground and shoot into the air forming a large circle. A portal appears, and four strange looking Gnomish figures step out into the light. Their red hats stand straight and flop down to their backs, and their pointed ears and long eyebrows give them a fierce but deadly looking visage. That, and their rather large and sharp sickles.*

If the party follows her after the fight, the events of [[Grim Tales for Bratty Children]] will occur, but Soma will be the subject of most of the hag's magic. At the end of the adventure, instead of disappearing [[Marianne]] will attempt to bargain with Soma into not destroying her shade because it would be "too annoying" for her to rebuild her strength. Instead she offers a piece of her hair which will give a party member a +2 bonus to the attribute of their choice to a maximum of 20, but it will only work if [[Marianne]]'s shade is still alive and if its consumed immediately after the deal is made.
