<img src="Cacafonix-1784487663757.webp" alt="Cacafonix" width="300" style="float:left; margin: 0 16px 12px 0;">Cacafonix is a High-Elf Bard that journeys the Sword Coast making a living off of telling heroic tales.

On his introduction, he will tell the party that in order to 'win the hearts and minds' of the commoners of the Sword Coast, they require his talents in building their saga. Cacafonix does not need gold for his services, but is happy to take any donations or tips. 

As a whole, he is brutally honest about the people's perception of the party, though he dresses it in compliments. Or at the very least will try his best to.
