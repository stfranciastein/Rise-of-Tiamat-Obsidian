#### **BACKSTORY**

<img src="Cannon-1788962596614.webp" alt="Tierbhal an Garrán" width="300" style="float:left; margin: 0 16px 12px 0;">Canon is a 14-year-old Goblin inventor and rogue born in the Fields of the Dead. She is a shrewd and witty would-be artificer protogee.

Having recently finished studying in [[Candlekeep]] under the mentorship of [[Kheril Hammerbind]], she wishes to travel the Sword Coast and hone her craft by visiting every major city in the [[Lords' Alliance]]. Her journey began in [[Baldur's Gate]] at [[Gond's House of Wonders]] but it is her wish to go to the [[Host Tower of the Arcane]] in [[Luskan]].
