Glass is a 7-year-old, 2 and a half feet tall adult Kobold Artificer introduced at the beginning of [[Chapter 6.0 - The Mentor]] when the players visit the hideout of [[Mellara, the Terror of the Sword Coast]]. 

He is an extremely skilled yet humble mechanic that seeks to create powerful weapons and machines. His namesake is that of the glass monocle that he wears which he fashioned as a hatchling. In battle he prefers the use of his repeating crossbow, which he infuses with magic he learned from the wisewomen of his tribe and [[Candlekeep]].

____
### ACT I - Kobold Tinkerer

If attacked, Glass will run away and will appear in [[Candlekeep]]. Regardless of the outcome, he becomes employed under the services of the adventurers after the events of [[Chapter 7.0 - Deathstruck]] as the driver of the [[Sylvira-Van]].

During [[Act 1 - The Curse of Willowvale]], Glass will be able to create makeshift traps for the party in order to help them with the fight against the [[Herd of the Dragon]]. Additionally, he can also be taken to the leadup fight with [[Mellara, the Terror of the Sword Coast]] but will opt to stay with the Caravan prior to entering the final boss arena.

____
### ACT II - Mechanic

With a little more experience under his belt, Glass will be able to perform upgrades for the [[Sylvira-Van]] based on the upgrades on that page. Additionally, he will begin work on three prototypes; the defensive bracer, the piercing bracer and the glider cloak.

While in [[Baldur's Gate]], he will also ask the players for a contact that knows how to get 500 GP worth of Copper wire and the Essence of a Lightning Elemental. This contact can be any member of the [Guild](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/The_Guild) or the [[Zhentarim]]. If the players do not accept his request, he will need to be rescued from a thief encounter in the [[Lower City]].
