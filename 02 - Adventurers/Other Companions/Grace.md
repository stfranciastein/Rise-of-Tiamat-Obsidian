<img src="Grace-1784488921933.webp" alt="Grace" width="300" style="float:left; margin: 0 16px 12px 0;">Grace is the illegitimate 8-year-old half-elf child of [[King Issalren]] of the [[Summer Court]].  She can be first encountered in [[Act 1 - The Curse of Willowvale]], [[Chapter 1.0 - The Woods]] being attacked by a pack of wolves. After the attack, she asks the players for gold, claiming to be travelling to [[Elturel]] in order to become a famous wizard.

She is secretly under the protection of a powerful Djinn named [[Mr. Greenie]]. Unbeknownst to her, the Djinn was sent by her father [[King Issalren]] in order to make sure Grace grew up safe and happy on the material plane.

____
### ACT I - Saving Grace

After the adventurers free Grace in [[Act 1 - The Curse of Willowvale]], she can be encountered again in [[Chapter 4.0 - Veins of Ice]] as a potential victim of [[Thaddak Ice-Veins]]. If she is fatally wounded, she will rise again in [[Act 2 - Hoard of the Dragon Queen]] during [[Chapter 1.0 - The Great Hunt]] through [[Mr. Greenie]]'s magic.
