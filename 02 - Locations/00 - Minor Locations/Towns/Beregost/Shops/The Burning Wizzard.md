The Burning Wizzard in [[Beregost]] is owned by an eldery wizard named Mageary 'Maggie' Ember. Maggie is quite ecclectic and speaks as though she were a hag. Fortunately, she is simply mad in the head.

If asked about any interesting figures in town, she will proclaim to have seen a handsome wizard in a red coat, [[Matreous Melwin]]. The players can either egg her on or tell her that he's married, depending on whether or not they have learned this information.
