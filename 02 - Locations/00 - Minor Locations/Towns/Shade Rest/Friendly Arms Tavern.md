Formerly built atop an old fort, the original Friendly Arms Inn was destroyed during a Goblin raid. Years later when [[Shade Rest]] was founded, [[Britha Torin]] and her wife [[Darine Torin]] rebuilt the Inn as a tavern with Shade's Rest right next door run by [[Janys]].

**Inventory:**
As a small tavern in a largely small town, the tavern only has:
- Ale: 1 sp.
- Wine: 2 sp.
- Mystery Drink: 1cp (Actually Fish Oil from [[Waterdeep]]).
