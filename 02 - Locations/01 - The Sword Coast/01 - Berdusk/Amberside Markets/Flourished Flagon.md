**Tags:** #Berdusk #AmbersideMarkets #Taverns 

A tavern on the west side of the [[Amberside Markets]] in [[Berdusk]] run by a Gnome woman named Vittoria. Vittoria is about a few decades away from retirement and has a flat nose and dark skin. She distrusts the Goblinoids, Orcs and Goliaths, believing them to be savages.
