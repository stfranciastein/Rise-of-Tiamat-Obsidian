The Reclamation was an event that occurred in 1490 Dr, prior to the beginning of the Tyranny of Dragons. In response to the oppressive rule of the humans and elves, the Orcs of the Sunset Valley

On the 13 of Uktar, the city of [[Berdusk]] was attacked by [[Kurtog Chain-Smasher]] along with the [[Free Riders]]; a collection of tribes and herds of Goblinoids, Goliaths and Orcs. During the Reclamation, many families that made up the [[First Folk]] were killed. From the ashes of the conflict, a new government was formed which comprised of the remaining members of the [[First Folk]] and the [[Free Riders]].

A memorial for those who fell was erected in front of the [[Evensong Towers]], depicting both human and orc soldiers alike. In the aftermath of the conflict, many humans and elves in Berdusk find themselves in constant social conflict.
