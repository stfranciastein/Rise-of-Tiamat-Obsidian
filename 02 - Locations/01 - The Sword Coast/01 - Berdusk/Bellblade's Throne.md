A tavern in [[Berdusk]] owned by the [[Zhentarim]]. 

Behind the bar is a large Orc male named Lortar. He will only be able to sell medium-tier alcohol, nothing imported or expensive.

A massive banner of the Zhentarim's logo flaps steadily against one wall from the draft coming in.

[[Jamna Gleamsilver]] will return here upon attempting to steal from the characters at the beginning of [[Chapter 2.0 - Tale of Two Cities]].
