**Tags:** #Berdusk #Innerchamber #Temples 

A temple to the god Helm. It is currently presided over by a male halfling cleric named Crimpar. The temple provides many services, including a way to reverse the effects of lycanthropy or vampirism prior to becoming a full spawn. 

It is possible to exorcise the demon from Jol here during the quest [[Demonic Vengeance]], though this will lead to Crimpar's death.
