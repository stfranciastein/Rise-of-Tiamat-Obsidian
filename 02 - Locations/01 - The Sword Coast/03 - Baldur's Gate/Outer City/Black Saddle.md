The Black Saddle is owned by [[Ackyn Selebon]], a former member of the [[Order of the Gauntlet]]. It is an equipage in the [[Outer City]] of [[Baldur's Gate]].

Ackyn opened up the Black Saddle in 1482 DR after being grievously injured in the aftermath of a mission for the Order. He left [[Elturel]] and returned to [[Baldur's Gate]] where he and his husband settled in the farmlands.
