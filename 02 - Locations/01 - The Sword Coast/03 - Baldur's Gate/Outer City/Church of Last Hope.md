The Church of Last Hope is an unaffiliated temple in [[Baldur's Gate]] under the care of a human woman named [[Sister Éabha]]. The Church often feeds the poor and destitute, and as such the [[Temple of the Open Hand]] acknowledges it as its sister location on the opposite side of the Chionthar River.

Upon the completion of [[Exit bear, stage right]], the patrons of the Church will be significantly happier due to the better supplies.
