Rivington is a small town on the south side of the Chionthar River. It is part of the [[Outer City]] of [[Baldur's Gate]].

- It is home to the Rivington Rats, a gang lead by an elven woman called Zenovia Dawg. She stands at 5ft 10 inches tall, has short blonde hair shaved at the sides and tied into a bun.
- There are many beggars asking for alms in this part of town.
- Primarily made up of farmers and commoners.
