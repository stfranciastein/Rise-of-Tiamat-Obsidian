A[[Baldur's Gate]] prison located on Wyrm's Crossing. It is also the headquarters of the [[Flaming Fist]] and is only used as a tollhouse and not at all whatsoever a place for coronating anyone.

Wyrm's Rock has five upper floors and several sublevels.
- The sublevels represent the prisons of Wyrm's Rock.
- The middle floor is on the bridge level and contains the armory/quartermaster.
- The second floor is the temple to Helm, dining hall and a celebration chamber.
- The third floor is the commisary/kitchen.
- The fourth floor is the barracks.
- The fifth floor contains Duke Ravenguard's chambers and office.
