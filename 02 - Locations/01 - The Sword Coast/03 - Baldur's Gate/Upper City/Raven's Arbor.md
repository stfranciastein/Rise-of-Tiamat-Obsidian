A large mansion in the [[Upper City]] of [[Baldur's Gate]], and the home of [[House Ravenshade]]. The premises are secured by [[Lorcan Ravenshade]]'s magic as well as the presence of four guards:
- Tessa Corvain: human woman with tan skin, wavy black hair, brown eyes.
- Uruk: half-orc man with green skin, buzzed hair, thick beard.
- Fenrik: female half-elf with tan skin, white hair and purple eyes.
- Ikaras: female tabaxi, black fur, black leathers, golden eyes.

If the players have no home and their rank with the [[Lords' Alliance]] is a 15 or higher, they can sleep in Raven's Arbor for the duration of their stay in [[Baldur's Gate]].
