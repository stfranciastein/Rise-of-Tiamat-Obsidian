Dameon Lawntill was a human male fisherman living in Tuern with his mother-in-law [[Martha Lawntill]]. He was the father of [[Yan 'Keyreeonis' Lawntill]]. In 1488 DR, Dameon was murdered by the [[Arcane Brotherhood]] Inquisitor [[Thuran Yale]] in order to facilitate [[Yan 'Keyreeonis' Lawntill]]'s training.
