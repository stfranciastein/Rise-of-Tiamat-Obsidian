Elira Thorne is a tiefling commoner who resides in [[Elturel]]. Elira is a florist who lives ner the western side of the docks. She lived with her husband [[Ontharr Frume]] and [[Lioran Thorne]] prior to them assuming their responsibilities during the Tyranny of Dragons.

She currently takes care of the house in their absence along with [[Pearl]], a pregnant white cat.
