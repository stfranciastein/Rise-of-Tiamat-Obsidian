Kaelor Shadow-Strike is a human monk from the [[Monestary of the Moonmaiden]]. He was responsible for overseeing the training of the monks there, including the adventurer [[Ryn Fenrath]]. In 1490 DR, Kaelor along with several other monks were slaughtered by Dark Justiciars from the Church of Shar.
