Maria Wormspeaker is the name of a hag that lived in the [[Mere of Dead Men]]. During her lifetime, Maria sired a child with the Adult Black Dragon [[Nerkurik]]. This union resulted in the birth of the Hexblood [[Soma]]. 

During [[Soma]]'s lifetime, Maria was able to impart her knowledge onto the young child. When Maria was slain by adventurers, Soma escaped from the confines of the marsh and made their way to [[Greenest]].
