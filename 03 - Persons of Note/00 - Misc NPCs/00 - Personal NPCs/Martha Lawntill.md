Martha Lawntill was a high-elf fighter from the island of [[Tuern]]. At a young age, Martha was quite the well-travelled adventurer. She often favoured the crossbow and greatsword. In her old age, Martha became paralysed and was unable to take care of herself. This meant that [[Dameon Lawntill]] and his son [[Yan 'Keyreeonis' Lawntill]] had to take care of her.

In 1488 DR, [[Thuran Yale]] of the [[Arcane Brotherhood]] murdered both [[Dameon Lawntill]] and Martha in order to begin [[Yan 'Keyreeonis' Lawntill]]'s training. According to Thuran, she begged for him to stop as he slowly took their lives.
