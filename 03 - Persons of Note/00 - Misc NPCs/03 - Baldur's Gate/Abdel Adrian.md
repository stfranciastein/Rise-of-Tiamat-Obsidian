Grand Duke Abdel Adrian was a member of the [[Council of Four]] and one of Bhaal's [[Bhaalspawn]]. During the Iron Crisis, he defeated his fellow Bhaalspawn; Sareevok Anchev and became crowned Grand Duke of [[Baldur's Gate]]. He was later assassinated by a Slayer.
