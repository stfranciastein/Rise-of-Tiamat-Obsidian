[[Aerith Beldan]], a Shield Dwarf Cleric of Ilmater who runs Beldan's House of Healing in the [[Upper City]] and a former [[Order of the Gauntlet]] member. Aerith is 4ft 8, more stout than the average dwarf and has a buzzed haircut and a long beard that rests just above his belly. He has two children named Gulrik and Anise Beldan who help him run the House of Healing. His wife, a Shield Dwarf named Sarta Beldan is a former Paladin for the [[Order of the Gauntlet]].

He is currently in debt to [[Amrik Vanthampur]] for 20,000 GP. He admits to having paid Amrik already but Amrik keeps expecting a 'protection fee' of 10,000 GP from him on a monthly basis and he has stopped his payments as of late, telling Amrik that he can 'shove his threats up his arse.'

If Aerith is killed either by [[Amrik Vanthampur]] or by the party, his position as head Cleric of the House of Healing is replaced by Anise who is a Cleric of Bahamut.
