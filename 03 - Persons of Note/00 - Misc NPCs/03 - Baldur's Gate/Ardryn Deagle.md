Ardryn Deagle is a Rock Gnome Artificer that can be found in [[Baldur's Gate]] inside of [[Whitkeep Hostel]]. Ardryn is 3ft tall with a shock of white hair and a styled, pointed beard. He wears brass armour with a blue undertunic. 

He is the primary quest giver for [[Welcome to the Arcana-Net]] and is the partner of [[Pernilla 'Prole' Cabrenock]]. Ardryn is able to provide the adventurers with copies of 'Arcana-Glass', the first in many devices that will begin the 'Arcana-Net'. At the conclusion of his quest, he will also be able to upgrade the [[Sylvira-Van]] with the Sending Station.
