Katarzyna or 'Kasha' is an old Shadar-Kai woman living in the [[Outer City]] of [[Baldur's Gate]]. In her prime, Kasha was a prized member of the [[Talons of the Raven Queen]] until she refused to slay one of her comrades. In response, [[Xaria Trevisa]] ordered her exile.
