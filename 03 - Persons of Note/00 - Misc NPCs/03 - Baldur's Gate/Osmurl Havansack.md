Osmurl Havansack is a shield dwarf and the High Constable and Master of Walls in the city of [[Baldur's Gate]]. Osmurl is about 5ft tall, which is quite tall for a dwarf. He has a long grey beard divided into five sections, and brushed back grey hair.

He is responsible for ensuring salaries of the [[Constabulary]] are paid, that [[Stormkeep]] is well-stocked, and the upkeep of the city's walls is completed in an orderly manner. He also communicates regularly with the [[Flaming Fist]] to make sure that the walls outside of the [[Upper City]] is well-maintained, as well as their fortress [[Wyrm's Rock]].
