An Earth Genasi in his 30s from the city of [[Waterdeep]] in the [[Sea Ward]], next to Mystra's House of Wonder. He used to frequently visit the House of Heroes during his time in [[The Watch (Waterdeep)]]. Balthazaar is 6ft 5, has short black curly hair shaved at the sides, a thick black beard with golden beads in his braid. He wears earth-toned leathers with a blue cloak fastened to it with a pin depicting the banner of [[Waterdeep]].
