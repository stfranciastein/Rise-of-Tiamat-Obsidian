<img src="Kagain Brawnanvil-1788915580993.webp" alt="Kagain Brawnanvil" width="300" style="float:left; margin: 0 16px 12px 0;">Kagain is a Gold Dwarf and a partner of [[Balthazaar Baradult]]. He is a not well-spoken man but like most dwarves he enjoys a good night of food and drinks while sharing stories around the campfire. He is a member of Clan [[Brawnavil]], a well-known royal family with links to the [[Battlehammer]] Clan.

He is technically of royal blood, but due to the amount of cousins and siblings he has from his uncle Connerad [[Brawnavil]]'s line, the responsibility of rule is far from his mind.
