### BACKSTORY

Born in the city of [[Suzail]], Auron was quite the adventurous type as a young man. Unfortunately, his family could not afford for him to leave the city, so when Auron came of age he did the next best thing to becoming an adventurer. He joined the city watch and was a guard for quite some time. While there, he met a human Cleric of [Bahamut](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Bahamut) named [[Joric]]. The two formed a fast friendship which eventually developed into something more. 

When [[Joric]] was forced to move away from [[Suzail]] to the village of [[Nashkel]], it put the nail in the coffin of their relationship.
