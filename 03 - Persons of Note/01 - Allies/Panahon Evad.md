### BACKSTORY

Panahon 'Pan' Evad was born as the youngest child in a family of six. His mother Primavera noticed he had an affinity with nature and would often call on him to help tend their garden. She could see what his father Verano could not; the child's affinity for nature and a way of communicating with the branches below their feet. As a boy, Pan would often wander the woods, leaping from tree to tree and warding away any who would bring them harm. In return for his protection and company, the trees would 'help' Pan traverse the woods, protecting him with their magic.

At the age of 15, a fire broke out in his village. Pan was away in the woods, and by the time he returned it had taken the lives of his entire family, leaving him orphaned. While Pan was in the orphanage, he developed a rage that terrified his peers and any who got too close. As he grew older he eventually learned to harness that rage into controlled bursts, a skill that came in handy when he joined the Army of Summer. During his days as a soldier, he served his time with dignity and honour. His peers respected his quiet, reserved nature and he made many friends in both the service and in the city. 

While Pan was fiercely loyal, he became disillusioned in his last campaign. During this difficult period, he found himself doing things he didn't want to do. The final straw was when Pan was ordered to attack a family of innocent civilians. In that moment he made a decision that would change his life forever. He turned on his fellow soldiers, killing them and letting the family escape. Knowing he had done the right thing, he returned to the city with a clear conscience.

When word eventually got back to his officers of what he'd done, he was immediately sentenced to death for treason. By a stroke of luck or perhaps fate, the jailor took pity on him and released him the night before his execution and told him to run. Pan ran towards the forest and found himself kneeling before a great tree, its branches reaching so high that it seemed to touch the sky itself. In his silent plea for help, the base of the tree opened, forming a portal to Toril. Pan walked through the opened portal and vowed to never return to the Feywild.

In Toril, he met a minotaur man named Maximus and the two travelled the Sword Coast for some time. Eventually, after a few decades the two married and retired to a life in the countryside in a small farm raising sheep. They weren't rich, but with the money they made as adventurers they were comfortable and life was good for a while. Unfortunately, his past caught up to him and Eladrin assassins razed his farm. He and Maximus defended themselves for a time, but the assassins were far too skilled and their tenacity was unmatched. Pan survived the attack, but in the aftermath of the battle he realised Maximus had succumbed to his wounds.

As he emerged from the ashes of their home, Pan swore to never again lay down his roots, to never again become too complacent in his place. For when he allowed himself to do so, fate had taken everything from him. Vowing to never make the same mistake again, Pan wanders the Sword Coast as a solitary adventurer and guardian of nature.
