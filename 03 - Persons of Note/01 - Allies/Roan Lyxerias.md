Roan Lyxerias is a 7ft tall, thick, muscular tabaxi man in his forties. Originally from Calimshan, he is currently a spy for the Wizards of [[Candlekeep]]. He speaks Common, Abyssal and Infernal and wears a white tunic with long coattails and has pale white fur. He has heterochromia, and one of his eyes is blue while the other one is gold.

As a young man, Roan was an urchin on the streets of [[Calimport]] in the [[Khanduq Ward]], specifically [[Taanith's Khaduq]]. There, he met an elven Calishite Wizard named Tanaka Lyxerias who took him in and taught him the ways of the Bladesinger. 

While he was in the care of Tanaka, the elf treated him like a son. When Roan came of age, he ventured to through the Lands of Intrigue, Vilhon Reach and the Heartlands before coming to the Sword Coast. He joined the wizards at [[Candlekeep]] in order to expand his magical knowledge.

As the years went by he - along with [[Traxigor]] - both grew tired of being confined to the fortress and decided to get back into field work. Unfortunately, Roan was captured by [[Amrik Vanthampur]] several weeks back while he was on a mission from [[Candlekeep]] to the North.
