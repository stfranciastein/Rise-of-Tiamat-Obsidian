### BACKSTORY

![[Stone-1784475645908.webp|300]]Stone was born in a small Goliath village called Kiln, tucked inside of a valley in the [Star Mounts](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Star_Mounts). His people lived quiet lives, working the land in tandem with the nature spirits of the [High Forest](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/High_Forest).

When Stone reached adulthood, he took on the duty his father held before him; guarding the lands of the High Forest. When poachers, loggers or wandering monsters would threaten the treeline or Kiln, Stone would meet them with his blade. In return for his devotion, the trees of the High Forest granted him a primal power. One that he could harness to better serve as a guardian for both the forest and his people. 

That life ended the day he grew careless. A vindictive and powerful group of poachers hunted him down and captured him. They bound him and tortured him for days, cutting out his tongue in an attempt to break his spirit. When Stone finally broke free he slaughtered his captors with ease, but it was too late. Kiln was razed to the ground, and the leaders far beyond his reach. 

To this day, Stone wanders the Sword Coast in search of the butchers who murdered his kin. And when he finds them, the wrath of the forest will be with him.
