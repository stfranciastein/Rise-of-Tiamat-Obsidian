#### **BACKSTORY**

<img src="Tierbhal an Garrán-1784762057280.webp" alt="Roan Lyxerias" width="300" style="float:left; margin: 0 16px 12px 0;">Tierbhal was born and raised in the [[Misty Forest]] in 1414. As the second oldest child, his human mother [[Adaya Smithwake]] would often lean on him and his oldest sister [[Anya an Garrán]] to help keep an eye on his two younger sisters, Eileen and Eideen. 

Raised in the Druid's Grove of [[Garrán]], Tierbhal didn't know much about the outside world growing up. His Elven father, Archdruid [[Eamir an Garrán]], was also quite overprotective of him and his family and sequestered them within the bounds of the grove. As a result, he spent most of his time in worship to Eilistraee, their Patron God. 

It wasn't until his teens when his mind began to wander, as what happened to most boys his age. Curiosity lead him to wandering outside of the forest whereupon he befriended a group of humans from the nearby town of Julkoun. He grew close with them as time went on, meeting with them for years in secret and watching them grow much faster than him and his siblings. 

When he came of age, Tierbhal decided to leave Garrán in order to see more of the world, much to his father's disapproval. He now walks the Sword Coast with little on him but the clothes on his back.

### ACT I - Fortune-Seeker

During the events of [[Act 1 - The Curse of Willowvale]], Tierbhal will start to display strange behaviour. During long rests, he will begin scribbling constellations on a piece of paper that can only be read using see invisibility. If asked to describe or explain what he's drawing, he will not be able to give them a solid answer.
