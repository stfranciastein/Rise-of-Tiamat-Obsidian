A derelict monastary atop the Sunset Mountains that was formerly devoted to Selune the Moonmaiden. The human monk known as [[Kaelor Shadow-Strike]] was its leader, and he trained many monks here under his careful watch. 

The monastery was active for many years before being attacked by Sharrans 1490 DR. This lead to the death of the majority of its occupants, though some managed to escape through the secret entrances in the mountains.
