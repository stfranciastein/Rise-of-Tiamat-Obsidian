[[Shade Rest]] is a small village located just South East of the [[Cloak Wood]] Forest. 

**Status:**
- [[Impyerno Lima]]: Safe and secured by the Flaming Fist as the result of [[Chapter 2.3 - Saving Shade Rest]].
- [[The Bush Whackers]]: Destroyed by [[Batuu Wolf-Blood]] after the events of [[Chapter 2.3 - Saving Shade Rest]] was abandoned in favour of [[Chapter 3.0 - Candlekeep]].

The village square itself is walled off from the north and south, while the east and west is open farmland. Most citizens live in huts and cottages in the surrounding countryside, but there are a few buildings within the central square.

**History**
The village of Shade Rest was founded only 50 years ago, after [[Old Shade Rest]] was attacked by an Adult Green Dragon named [[Venomfang]]. The founders of Shade Rest consisted of the farmers, labourers and the remaining guards. The old sage [[Marianne]] and [[Janys]], the Innkeeper, are all who remain from the original populace.

**Governance**
Shade Rest is lead by a democratically appointed Elder, as is the norm with most small settlements across the Sword Coast. The current leader of Shade Rest is Elder [[Cormil Bonham]], a retired Paladin of Chauntea who was also once a member of the [[Order of the Gauntlet]]. 

**Notable Locations**
As a small, but well-serviced settlement, Shade Rest has more amenities than most. It is the home of the [[Friendly Arms Tavern]], a smithy run by [[Darine Torin]], a Temple to Chauntea where [[Larissa Bonham]] can be found, the Shade's Rest run by [[Janys]], [[Marianne]]'s Fortune Teller tent and a magic shop run by [[Tamae Tideon]].
