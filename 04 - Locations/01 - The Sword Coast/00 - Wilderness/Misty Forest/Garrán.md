### BACKGROUND

Glasgarrán or Garrán is a Druid's Grove located in the heart of the [[Misty Forest]].  It is the home of a large temple to Silvanus, and is lead by the Wood Elf Archdruid [[Eamir an Garrán]]. Garrán is also a member of the [[Emerald Enclave]]. 

It is often said in the village of [[Julkoun]] that Garrán exists in its own demi-plane. According to local rumour, in order to find Garrán you must be of pure heart and soul. The truth of the matter is far more simple. Erected around the forest are powerful runic enchantments placed by the druids which prevent wanderers into stumbling upon their grove. 

There are precisely 40 people that live in Garrán, most of whom are druids, wild barbarians or rangers. The few that are not are children or family members who simply help maintain the grove. When a person comes of age in Garrán, their faces are marked with druidic tattoos which attune them to the ancient Fey-Crossing Tree. The tree then begins to bears fruit, signifying their life. This fruit only falls when they perish. When someone in Garrán dies, their bodies are laid to rest in the [[Misty Forest]], and a tree is planted above their grave.

### FAITH

As members of the [[Emerald Enclave]], Glassgarrán worship a variety of gods including Chauntea, Mielikki, Silvanus and Eldath. There are also several worshippers of Eilistraee in Garrán, due to the influx of converts coming in from the [[Sharnlands]] in the Underdark.

____
### GOVERNANCE

Garrán is lead by Archdruid [[Eamir an Garrán]], who has a close inner circle who help him oversee the affairs of the grove. These are [[Anya an Garrán]], [[Thalia an Garrán]] and [[Eamonn an Garrán]]. Eamir son, [[Tierbhal an Garrán]], has been estranged from the grove and his father but is still considered to have valuable input by [[Anya an Garrán]].
