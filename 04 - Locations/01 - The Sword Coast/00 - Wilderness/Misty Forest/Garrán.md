Glasgarrán or Garrán is a Druid's Grove located in the heart of the [[Misty Forest]].  It is the home of a large temple to Eilistraee, and is lead by the Wood Elf Archdruid [[Eamir an Garrán]]. Garrán is also a member of the [[Emerald Enclave]]. 

It is said that in order to find Garrán, one must be of pure heart and soul. In truth, there are powerful enchantments placed by the druids that prevent wanderers into stumbling upon their grove. 

There are precisely 30 people that live in Garrán, most of whom are druids themselves. The few that are not are druids-to-be or their families who help maintain the grove.
When an druid comes of age in Garrán, their faces are marked with druidic tattoos which attune them to the ancient Fey-Crossing Tree. The tree grows fruit
