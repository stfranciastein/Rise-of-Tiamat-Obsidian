A blacksmith in [[Berdusk]] owned by a pair of Minotaur husbands by the name of Flint and Steel.

Flint is somewhat short for a Minotaur but that does not say much. He grew up in a remote herd near the Troll Mountains and moved to [[Berdusk]] after meeting his husband Steel.

Flint is the main proprietor of the [[Silver Sword]] and the primary questgiver for the [[Ill met by Moonlight]] side quest, and a witness to [[Cold, Red-Blooded Murder]].

Flint is able to make +1 weapons as well as eventually make an identical copy of an inert [[Belt of the Giant]] at the start of [[Act 3 - Rise of Tiamat]]. This will require 10,000GP to make.
