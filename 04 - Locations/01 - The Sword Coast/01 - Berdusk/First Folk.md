The First Folk of [[Berdusk]] are the group of representatives that govern the city. They reside in the [[Evensong Towers]], where dignified evening parties were once held twice every tenday. Originally, these were reserved for the members of the First Folk, but since the [[Reclamation]], [[Kurtog Chain-Smasher]] has opened up the celebration to the public.

There are currently nine families that make up the First Folk. Half of the clans of the [[Free Riders]] are represented in the government. These are; Clan [[Chain-Smasher]], Clan [[Shackle-Breaker]], Clan [[Wolf-Blood]], Clan [[Storm-Crown]] and Clan [[Flame-Arrow]]. The other families are House Calanthal, Myrmast, Hardomeir, Quarlagast.
