*You approach Evensong Towers and make note of its magnificant craftmanship and architecture. The high walls, bridges and pointed roofs make it seem like it was taken directly from a children's storybook.

The interior is equally as overwhelming to look at. There are several antechambers which hosts a variety of spells, scrolls, ballads and other orations during revels.

Originally Evensong Towers was a temple dedicated to the god [Milil](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Milil), Evensong Tower has been converted by the [[Free Riders]] and the newly-incarnated [[First Folk]] into the seat of [[Berdusk]]'s government.
