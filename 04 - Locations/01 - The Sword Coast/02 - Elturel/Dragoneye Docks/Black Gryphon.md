The Black Gryphon is an alchemy shop in the [[Dragoneye Docks]] district of [[Elturel]]. It is run by a Drow woman named Nenle T'var. Nenle moved from [[Menzoberranzan]] in order to get away from the worshippers of Lolth. Nenle despises the Companion, but it keeps any potential 'spies' away which keeps her happy.

7 Greater Healings
2 Superior Healing Potions
