A clothing shop in [[Elturel]] that specialises in robes and frocks. It is run by a white-furred Minotaur named Thalan. He is able to create magical pieces of clothing, enchanting robes with the following upgrades:

1. +1 to any ability check, 500 GP per stat to a maximum of 2.
2. 1 charge of a spell, 500 GP per spell level to a maximum of 3rd level.
