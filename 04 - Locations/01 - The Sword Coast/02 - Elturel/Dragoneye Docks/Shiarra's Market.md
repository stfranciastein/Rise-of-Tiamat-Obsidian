Shiarra's Market is an open market on the [[Dragoneye Docks]] of [[Elturel]]. It is often frequented by the few members of the [[Zhentarim]] that are present in the city, though they do not tend to pickpocket here much due to the fear of incurring the wrath of the [[Order of the Companion]].
