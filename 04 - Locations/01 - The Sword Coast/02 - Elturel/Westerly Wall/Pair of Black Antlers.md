The Pair of Black Antlers is a tavern in the [[Westerly Wall]] district of [[Elturel]]. It is the de-facto headquarters of [[Ontharr Frume]], second only to the [[Hall of the Triad]]. Frume specifically asked for there to be an entrance to the Pair of Black Antlers from the [[Hall of the Triad]].

Its namesake is due to the fact that there is a pair of black antlers spanning 2 meters tall per antler mounted on the wall. The Black Antler has been visited by Volothamp Geddarm and confirmed for its authenticity.
