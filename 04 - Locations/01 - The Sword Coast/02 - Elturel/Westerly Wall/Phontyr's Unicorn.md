Phontyr's Unicorn is a upper-class tavern in the [[Westerly Wall]] district of [[Elturel]]. It is run by a rather well-endowed tiefling woman named Karyssa. Karyssa originally hailed from [[Waterdeep]] but moved to [[Elturel]] in order to get away from her family.

The [[Agile Hand]] often make themselves busy at the Unicorn.
