The Silver Fire Academy is a magic school in [[Elturel]] located on the [[Westerly Wall]] district. It is exclusively reserved for women, though unlike most magic colleges who only allow entry for Wizards, the Silver Fire Academy also accepts Sorcerers and Warlocks.

Its namesake comes from the goddess of magic [Mystra](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Mystra). It was originally formed in order to help train wizards and sorceresses deal with the [[Cult of the Dragon]], which at the time was lead by [[Sammaster]].
