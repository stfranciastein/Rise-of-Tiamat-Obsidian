The Sunwake Oven is a bakery on the [[Westerly Wall]] district of [[Elturel]]. They specialise in pierogis and other small pastries.

It is run by two dwarves named Jespar and Barzan Sunwake. Their son, Yurie, was once a worker there but tragically died when he was young. [[Lioran Thorne]] often frequents the Sunwake Oven.
