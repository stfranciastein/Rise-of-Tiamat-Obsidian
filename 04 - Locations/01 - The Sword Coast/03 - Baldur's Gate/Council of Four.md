The Council of Four are the ruling Dukes of [[Baldur's Gate]]. As of 1481 DR, the current members of the Council of Four are as follows:
- Grand Duke [[Ulder Ravengard]].
- Duke [[Thalamra Vanthampur]].
- Duke [[Belynne Stelmane]].
- Duke [[Dillard Portyr]].

The former Grand Duke was of course [Abdel Adrian](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Abdel_Adrian), a famous adventurer and Bhaalspawn.

In order for things to be submitted to the Council's review, it must first go through the Parliament of Peers - an advisory council comprised of fifty Baldurians - in the [[Observer's Watch]]. In addition, the Parliament is also responsible for electing new Dukes to the council who hold the most authority within the city.

Currently, both [[Margaret Ravenshade]] and [[Enver Gortash]] are running for office in hopes of replacing [[Belynne Stelmane]] who is currently in very poor health.
