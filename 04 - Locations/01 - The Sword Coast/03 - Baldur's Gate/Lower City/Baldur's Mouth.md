Baldur's Mouth is a news publication in [[Baldur's Gate]] run by Ettvard Needle, the human male editor-in-chief of the newsprint. Ettevard is the son of a minor noble.

Its building is on the Eastway district of the [[Lower City]]. The Mouth prints out daily broadsheets that are often sensationalised.

`A Gazette a day keeps ignorance away!`

It is the only publication available for the city's residents and guests to keep up with the latest events. It is a powerful tool for swaying the minds of Baldurians, depending on who is able to bribe them.
