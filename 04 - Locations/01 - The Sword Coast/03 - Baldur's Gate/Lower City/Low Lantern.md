The Low Lantern is a three-masted merchant ship that is permanently docked in the [[Lower City]] of [[Baldur's Gate]], on the far east side of the harbour. It is not seaworthy whatsoever and was converted into a tavern many years ago by a middle-aged water genasi named Laraelra, whom the locals have taken to calling the 'Captain' instead.
