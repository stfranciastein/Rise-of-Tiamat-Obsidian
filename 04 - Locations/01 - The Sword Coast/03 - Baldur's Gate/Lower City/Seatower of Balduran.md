*As you pass by the Seatower district, you notice the large fortification situated on a small makeshift island. The locals seem to call it the Seatower of Balduran. Its high walls appear similar to Wyrm's Rock, though more warships surround this area of the Gate. Flaming Fist banners hang from its walls, while Gondian trebuchets lay slack on the upper surface.*

Seatower is a [[Baldur's Gate]] prison located in the [[Lower City]]. It is also one the headquarters of the [[Flaming Fist]] and primarily holds their naval forces. The marshal and most of the officers responsible for the day-to-day running of [[Flaming Fist]] operations resides here. Thus, [[Liara Portyr]] has made it her office.

There is a large chain that can only be activated from Seatower. The last time it was used was to entrap pirates from [[Calimport]] while the wizards of [[Razimuth's Tower]] singed their ships along with the [[Flaming Fist]].
