Sourcerous Sundries is a famous magic shop owned by the Archwizard [[Lorroakan]]. It is located near the docks of the [[Lower City]] and services all walks of life. 

There are two attendants in Sorcerous Sundries.
1. The first is a Satyr wizard named Lucrecia Bonafacia. She is about 5'5, has short mousy brown hair and horns that curve upwards and down like a goat. She prefers to show off her calves as she says it is quite the workout walking from her home in [[Little Calimshan]] to the [[Lower City]].
2. The second is a tall, Red Dragonborn bard named Ember. She only arrived in [[Baldur's Gate]] about a year or so ago but found peace in working for a magic shop, despite how busy it is.
