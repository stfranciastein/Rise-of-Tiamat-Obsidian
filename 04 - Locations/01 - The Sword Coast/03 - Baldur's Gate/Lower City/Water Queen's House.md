The Water Queen's House is a temple to the sea god Umberlee in [[Baldur's Gate]]. It is headed by a human woman named Allandra Grey, a Waveservant of Umberlee. Allandra is a human woman in her 40s with white hair, a beautifully painted face. Her hair is long and is inlaid with gems and metalwork.

Otyn, a druid of the Circle of the Sea of the [[Emerald Enclave]] is visiting the Water Queen's House.
