- A shop run by a dwarf named Entharl Danthelon. Entharl is a blonde, beardless dwarf that stands at about 4 and a half feet tall. This is where the quest [[Exit bear, stage right]] can begin.
- Danthelon will offer the following items for sale:

1. Robe of the Land and Sky: 2000 Gold; grants bonus damage to unarmed strikes equal to your Wisdom modifier.
2. Harmonic Dueller: 1500 Gold; grants bonus damage equal to your Wisdom modifier.
3. Potion of Fire Giant's Strength 750 GP.
4. Pipe of the Sewers 1000 GP.
5. Potion of Radiant Resistance (3) 300 GP.
6. Potion of Superior Healing 450 GP.
7. Potion of Healing (15) 50 GP.

- At the end of [[Exit bear, stage right]], these items will be discounted. He regenerates his stock of potions every 7 days.
