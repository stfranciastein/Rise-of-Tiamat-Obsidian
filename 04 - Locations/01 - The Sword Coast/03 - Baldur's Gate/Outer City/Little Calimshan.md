Little Calimshan is a small, enclosed district in the [[Outer City]] of [[Baldur's Gate]]. The scent of cinnamon and the sounds of exotic, reeded instruments slip over its walls and draws several passerbyes into having a look.

The houses in Little Calimshan are divided into druadachs, or neighborhoods. Typically, each druadach's inhabitants are members of an extended family.

[[Margaret Ravenshade]] is often said to send money to Little Calimshan, as it reminds her of home.
