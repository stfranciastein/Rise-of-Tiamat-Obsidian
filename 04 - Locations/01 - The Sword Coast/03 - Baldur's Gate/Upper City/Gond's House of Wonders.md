Gond's House of Wonders is a building in the [[Upper City]] of [[Baldur's Gate]] that serves as a vast workshop for many of the crafters and inventors that the temple houses. 

To enter the building, a person must pass bronze locks that appear to float in mid air. The entrance to the House of Wonders has never been closed, though in the event that they need to be the workers can activate the traps and close every window and door. 

Each day, the House of Wonders rings with the clamour of hammers and saws. Acolytes craft knicknacks that are sold in the museum; the Hall of Wonders.

Master Craftsmen from all over the Sword Coast and beyond come to the House of Wonders to 
lecture and teach apprentices and novices. There is a wing for nearly every discipline, work or scholarly pursuit in the House of Wonders. There are at least a hundred priests and acolytes living in the House of Wonders.

- The main receptionist of the House of Wonders is a Gnome inventor named Kellan Deagle. Kellan is a few hundred years old and is quite friendly towards any who wish to explore.

- Enver Gortash frequents the House of Wonders, working with a few gnomes on building something called a Steel Watcher.

- The Hall of Wonders (Museum): has many models of ships and inventions such as the shell of a Gondian Runepower Bomb, the ship of Balduran, a mechanical model of [[Candlekeep]] and replicas of famous weapons such as Blackrazor. Entrance costs 5 SP.
