- The Helm and Cloak is located in the heart of the [[Upper City]] of [[Baldur's Gate]]. It serves the upper crust and has the prices to match.

- A stay in the Helm and Cloak is 50 GP for a basic room and 100 GP for the suites. Each stay comes with a night in the spa and free dinner & drinks.

- Run by a Cormyrean husband and wife team. Vendren and Halesta used to be adventurers but once their party fractured they came to [[Baldur's Gate]] to share a less dangerous life together.

- The barkeep is an air genasi named Claudia, a woman with light blue skin, airy dark blue hair and glowing white eyes.

- The unicorn statue is touched by visitors for good luck. According to rumour, the Knights of the Unicorn (a group of young adventurers) use the Helm as their informal headquarters. Vendren and Halestra are former members of the Knights.
