The Council of Waterdeep consists of the Open Lord of Waterdeep ([[Laeral Silverhand]]), and several Masked Lords. As of the Tyranny of Dragons, an additional council has been called. This includes High Harper [[Jaheria]], [[Ontharr Frume]], [[Cormyc Ravenshade]], [[Anya an Garrán]], [[Dagult Neverember]] and [[Laeral Silverhand]].
