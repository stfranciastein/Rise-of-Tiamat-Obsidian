### BACKGROUND

The Emerald Grove is a druid's grove located alongside the Chionthar River between [[Elturel]] and [[Baldur's Gate]]. It is precisely in the middle of the two, right on the northern riverbank. It is lead by a wood elf Archdruid named [[Halsin]]. It is also a headquarters of the [[Emerald Enclave]].
