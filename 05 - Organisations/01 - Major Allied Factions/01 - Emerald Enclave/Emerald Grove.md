### BACKGROUND

The Emerald Grove is a druid's grove located alongside the Chionthar River between [[Elturel]] and [[Baldur's Gate]]. It is precisely in the middle of the two, right on the northern riverbank. It is lead by a wood elf Archdruid named [[Halsin Silverbough]]. It is also a headquarters of the [[Emerald Enclave]].

Several decades ago, [[Jaheria]] and the [[Harpers]] worked with the [[Emerald Enclave]] in order to defeat the Sharran General Ketheric Thorm. As a result of their battle with him, the lands near Reithwin were afflicted with the [[Shadow Curse]].
