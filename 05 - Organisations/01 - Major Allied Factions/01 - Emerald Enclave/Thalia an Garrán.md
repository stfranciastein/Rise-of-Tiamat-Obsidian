### BACKSTORY

A Wood Elf ranger of the [[Emerald Enclave]] hailing from the grove [[Garrán]] in the [[Misty Forest]]. Thalia was sent by [[Eamonn an Garrán]] in order to ensure that the northern Circles are represented in [[Chapter 1.0 - The Great Hunt]]. During the events of the Hunt, Thalia can be seen fighting two Enraged Minotaurs. If she is saved, she will return to [[Garrán]] provided she survives the [[Cult of the Dragon]]'s attack on [[Greenest]].
