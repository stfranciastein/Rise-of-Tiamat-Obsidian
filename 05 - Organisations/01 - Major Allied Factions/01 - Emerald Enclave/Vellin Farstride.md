### BACKSTORY

<img src="Vellin Farstride-1788910311856.webp" alt="Vellin Farstride" width="300" style="float:left; margin: 0 16px 12px 0;"> Vellin Farstride is a Hunter Ranger for the [[Emerald Enclave]], originally from the [[High Forest]]. He is a plane-jumper that regularly hunts fey and fiends. He wanders the coast with his Dire Wolf Companion Akela who het met as a child. As a young man, Vellin and Akela would often patrol their village. He quickly fell in love with the vast and beautiful wilds of the Sword Coast and decided to dedicate his life to protecting nature from threats from within and beyond.

During the Tyranny of Dragons, Vellin is attempting to find ways to join the fight against Tiamat and the [[Cult of the Dragon]]. He tells travellers he is simply a wandering ranger, but in reality he hopes to journey to [[Waterdeep]] and join the campaign against the Dragon Queen.
