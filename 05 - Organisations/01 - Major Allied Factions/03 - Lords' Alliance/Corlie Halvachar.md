**Bio:** A female Halfling Fighter (Champion)
**Age:** 44
**Status:** Alive

____
### BACKSTORY

Corlie was born and raised in the city of [[Waterdeep]]. As the youngest child of [[House Halvachar]], she had no ambitions about becoming the heir of her family fortune. From a young age, she set out to distance herself from her family's legacy but embraces the knowledge that she grew up a much more privileged life than most. 

She joined the [[City Guard (Waterdeep)]] and was a former Guard Captain before being transferred  currently a representative for the [[Lords' Alliance]] currently stationed in [[04 - Locations/01 - The Sword Coast/03 - Baldur's Gate/Outer City/Ironspine Keep]].
