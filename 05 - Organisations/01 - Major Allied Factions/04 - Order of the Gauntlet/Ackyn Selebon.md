**Bio:** A male Human Fighter (Battlemaster)
**Age**: 55

____
### BACKSTORY 

Ackyn Selebon was once a revered member of the [[Order of the Gauntlet]] but retired when he lost his leg during a skirmish with the [[Cult of Vecna]]. He moved to [[Baldur's Gate]] where he works his business in the Black Saddle, an equipage that sells materials for long-stance freight hauling: wagons, rope, netting, grease, chains, wheels and so on.

_____
### IN-GAME

After their initial meeting, Ackyn can be commissioned by the adventurers to fit the [[Sylvira-Van]] with a sending station for 2,000GP but will ask the adventurers to seek out [[Traxigor]]. He will not be able to be bargained with and will simply walk off if they try to do so.
