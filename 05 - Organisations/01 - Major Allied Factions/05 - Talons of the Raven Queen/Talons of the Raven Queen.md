### BACKGROUND

The Talons of the Raven Queen are a devout sect that worship the [Raven Queen](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Raven_Queen), a powerful deity that resides on the [[Shadowfell]]. The Talons are tasked with preserving the natural cycle of life, and helping lost or bound souls find their way to their rightful resting place, wherever that may be.

One of the greatest secrets of the Talons of the Raven Queen is their ability to use the [[Shadowfell]] to traverse the Material Plane. Powerful spellcasters who are attuned deeply to the [Raven Queen](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Raven_Queen) can enter and exit the [[Shadowfell]] at will. While the [[Shadowfell]] is a dark 'echo' of the Material Plane, Talons can see through these distortions and navigate the [[Shadowfell]] without losing their place in the Material Plane. Thus, the Talons are likely to have outposts there.

The Talons abide by the following tenets.
1. Death has its hour. De do not hasten an innocent's end, nor allow it to be stolen by others.
2. Vengeance serves balance. Retribution is not rage, it is course correction. To punish is to restore balance, not indulge in cruelty.
3. Souls are not tools. Using the spirits of the dead for convenience is prohibited, even if 'well-intended.' Resurrection is tolerated only if freely chosen by the soul.
4. Talons strike clean. Do not terrorize, torture or revel in fear. Kill cleanly when killing is required.

### RANKS

#### Watcher: 
Well trusted Talon sympathisers, not actual members but informants who are considered allies.

#### Pinion:
Recruits who have been scouted by the Third Talons.

#### Talon:
Full-fledged field operatives who have undergone the Veil Rite. 
Works directly under a Third Talon.

#### Third Talon:
The leader of a 'cell', in charge of assigning missions to Talons.
Works directly under the Second Talon.

#### Second Talon:
Oversees a region or a theatre and commands multiple cells.
Works independently but reports to the First Talon when necessary.

#### First Talon:
Directly represents the Raven Queen's will. Sets targets, greenlights operations from the Second Talon and is rarely deployed in the field. Must be a Chosen of the Raven Queen.

### INITIATION

#### Weighing of the Soul
To join the Talons and formally become a full-fledged member, you must swear service to the Raven Queen at a Temple of the Raven Queen. This initiation also includes a trial overseen by the Second Talon or an appointed Cleric. 

A Pinion is sent on a mission known as the Weighing of the Soul:
1. The Pinion is given a target to 'judge', a soul. It could be a necromancer, a resurrected soul, or any other force that keeps alive what the Queen sees as not belonging in this world.
2. No order is made, only a name is given. The Pinion must determine what must happen with the soul, weather it be banishment or atonement.
3. This trial proves whether you can follow the Raven Queen's will without being ordered. *"A Talon who kills blindly is no true Talon."*

#### The Veil Rite
Upon their return, they are brought to the Grand Chamber with the Second Talon or a Cleric. 

The initiate kneels before the Veil — a tall mirror of polished obsidian whose surface has been ritually blackened until it reflects nothing but shadow. They are presented with a single raven feather, still warm from the Queen’s blessing. A solitary black candle is lit before the mirror.

The initiate holds the feather above the flame until it catches fire, then releases it. As the feather burns to ash, they must speak their reasoning aloud — every observation, every doubt, every certainty. No justification. No plea. Only the cold, honest truth of what they have seen and decided.

The mirror then reveals the Queen’s verdict. If the surface remains dark and impenetrable, swallowing all reflection, the initiate has failed. Their judgment does not yet align with the Raven Queen’s will, and they are turned away from the order. If the mirror clears, becoming bright and silvery as still water beneath moonlight, their reasoning has been accepted. Their sight is true.

The custodian then reads out the Oath of the Talons.

`I stand before the Raven Queen,` 
`Lady of Death and Fate.` 
`With her anointed as my witness,` 
`I swear to uphold the Oath of the Talon.`

`I vow to carry the dead when they linger,` 
`to guide their souls across the veil with mercy and without delay.`
`I vow to free the dead from every bond,` 
`to ensure they do not fester among the living.`
`I vow to keep the cycle clean and unbroken,` 
`that no mortal hand shall twist what the Queen has finished.`
`I vow to place my duty above my own needs.
`Should by own soul one day return to the cycle, I vow to serve her even in death.`

`With this Oath I swear, I pledge myself to the Raven Queen. 
`From this day until my last, I am and will always be, a Talon.`
