### BACKSTORY

<img src="Nauk Flame-Arrow-1788911234856.webp" alt="Nauk Flame-Arrow" width="300" style="float:left; margin: 0 16px 12px 0;">Nauk is a half-orc warrior and a former senior member of the [[Free Riders]] in [[Berdusk]]. He is also publicly known as one of the Sword Coast's most famous mercenaries. He dresses in full battle gear, even when just sitting down for a discussion with a possible client or before bed. This is because what he wears and wields show clients what he brings to the table. He wears a set of Adamantine Armour and carries a large enchanted warhammer.

In reality, Nauk is an arms dealer who moves cargo by pretending to be a mercenary and joining convoys to smuggle goods. He is a well respected member of the [[05 - Organisations/01 - Major Allied Factions/06 - Zhentarim/Zhentarim]], regularly helping them on jobs in either [[Baldur's Gate]], [[Waterdeep]] or [[Neverwinter]].
