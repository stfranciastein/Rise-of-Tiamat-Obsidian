### BACKSTORY

The Court of Metal is less an organisation and more a set of Ancient Metallic Dragons who have remained in the shadows, waiting for the Dragon Queen Tiamat to arise. Lead by the Gold Dragon [[Protanther the Gold]], the Court of Metal are sequestered away in the Nether Mountains.

They were also responsible for the creation of the Vanguards of Bahamut, an ancient order founded with the assistance of the elder draconic god of metallic dragons. Legends tell of how a single Vanguard was capable of turning the tide of an entire battle or breaking the siege of a city. But the Vanguards of Bahamut were short-lived, lasting only a few decades before being exterminated by their enemies. That was a thousand years ago.

The last surviving Vanguard, a Half-Elf named Suldil, wishes to bring back the ancient Order, though [[Protanther the Gold]] believes that their age had passed, [[Tazmikella 'Zalla Temik' the Copper]] and [[Nymmurh the Bronze]] openly supported Suldil's efforts in rebuilding them. Thus, the Vanguard was reborn and silently patrols the world in their stead.

____
### RANKS

There are no other 'ranks' within the Court of Metal, as all of the Ancient Dragons and newly established Vanguards defer to [[Protanther the Gold]] as their leader. However, when dealing with the outside world, they dispatch the Vanguards to do their bidding, often blessed with boons to aid in their battle.

____
### INITIATION

To become a member of the Court of Metal, a person must perform a sacred rite known as the Oath of the Platinum Dragon. This is a sacred rite that bonds a mortal and a dragon together. In order for the sacred rite to work, it must be blessed by Bahamut. Thus, the first task of the fledgling knight is to gain Bahamut's favor. After that, metallic dragon eggs must be found. They cannot be stolen from their parents. Only eggs that have not been stolen and whose parents are dead are considered viable. The aspirant must then journey back to [[Protanther the Gold]]'s court and perform the ritual there.
