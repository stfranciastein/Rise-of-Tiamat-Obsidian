### BACKSTORY

Zalla Temik, or Tazmikella is an Ancient Copper Dragon and a member of [[Protanther the Gold]]'s [[Court of Metal]]. When she is in her disguise as 'Zalla', Tazmikella pretends to be an elven woman from [[Neverwinter]]. Zalla has coppery-tan skin, golden hair, bright green eyes and wears a set of elven armour that evokes draconic motifs.

As a dragon, Tazmikella is considered to be 'friendly' by her peers though she is very inexperienced in the customs of the younger races.
