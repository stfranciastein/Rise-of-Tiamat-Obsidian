#### **BACKSTORY**

Born in the City of [[Waterdeep]], Matreous of House Melwin is a very accomplished Transmutation Wizard, able to cast at least 6th level spells. Although he is a very learned individual, Matreous is quite adventurous often likes to spend most of his time out in the field. Matreous was once a mercenary himself. During his time as an adventurer, he made quite a handful of friends who went on to become prominent individuals themselves such as [[Rhylrae]] of the [[Harpers]], the Cleric [[Frulam Mondath]] and [[Torbar Dragonsbane]].

Matreous found himself going back to [[Waterdeep]] at the end of his time as an adventurer. During his retirement, Matreous met a Sun Elf woman who he eventually married ([[Elizabeth Melwin]]). Although she and Matreous have always wanted a child, Elizabeth's condition prevents her from ever having a successful pregnancy, leading to a constant strain in their marriage, and a rift that grows with each passing year. As a result, Matreous sees his assistant, [[Lyam Bentos]], as the son he never had. Matreous believes that Lyam would one day assume the position of Archwizard and earn a permanent spot in [[Candlekeep]], often praising the lad for his skills. 

Sometime in 1490, Matreous began overhearing rumours about the [[Cult of the Dragon]]'s return. Initial reports were sparse and unreliable. He reached out to his contacts in the [[Harpers]], and [[Rhylrae]] agreed to help him on his quest. The two began a slow but thorough investigation into this new branch of the Cult, and through careful work discovered the existence of the [[Herd of the Dragon]], then lead by [[Jaflin Stormcrown]]. Matreous agreed to send Lyam out into the field as his eyes and ears after the lad found out about his investigation into the [[Herd of the Dragon]]. For his 23rd birthday, Matreous had a special set of robes created for him that would aid in his field work, the [[Robes of the Wraith]].

Matreous's magic allowed him to scan the Sword Coast for various sites of powerful magic. As a result of this, he discovered two key locations near [[Candlekeep]]; an underground temple in the [[Cloak Wood]] (the location of [[Chapter 4.0 - Veins of Ice]]) and another in the Cloud Peak mountains (the location of [[Chapter 8.0 - Herd of the Dragon]]). During his investigation, a wizard named [[Feldir Whitefyre]] reached out to him - having heard of Matreous's previous exploits - in hopes of finding a cure to the curse that had befallen [[Willowvale]].

### IN-GAME

Matreous is the one that initially suspects that the 'curse' on[[ Willowvale]] is the result of a Shadow Druid's magic ritual in the [[Cloak Wood]] forest, specifically [[Thaddak Ice-Veins]]. Little did he know that [[Ysra Deathstrike]]'s changeling agents in [[Candlekeep]] had been watching his every move and had replaced [[Mattias Greenwood]]. One day, after taking the form of [[Sylvira Savikas]], the changeling presented Matreous with a book entitled '[[The Joy of Extradimensional Spaces (Book)]]'. Matreous was then forced inside the tampered [[Fistandia's Mansion]], where he was trapped in the Master Bedroom and time passed at an accelerated rate.

Matreous's role is decided by a choice in [[Chapter 2.0 - Shade Rest]].
1. If the adventurers decide to pursue the main quest, Matreous will be alive when they enter [[Fistandia's Mansion]].
2. If the adventurers decide to pursue the rescue portion of [[Chapter 2.0 - Shade Rest]] instead, Matreous will have been killed by the Spine Devil. His role is then given to [[Sylvira Savikas]] in finding the changeling in [[Candlekeep]] and [[Rhylrae]] assumes his role in [[Chapter 6.0 - The Mentor]] as the party's informant on the [[Cult of the Dragon]]'s activities.
