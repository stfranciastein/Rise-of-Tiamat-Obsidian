### BACKSTORY

Thuran is the type of person to say horrific things in a kind tone. He is ruthless, authoritative, and as [[Yan 'Keyreeonis' Lawntill]] would describe him, 'bitchy' in tone.

Raised in the slums of [[Luskan]] as an orphan, Thuran had no way of rising above his station in life. He would often beg for scraps from strangers who would only sometimes enough pity for him to actually provide him with food or coin. A particularly heated encounter with a cruel man saw him accidentally discover his knack for magic, when he made the man cower in fear and drop his coin.

Thuran continued to hone his magic on unsuspecting noblemen, attracting the attention of the [[Arcane Brotherhood]] who had just begun their efforts of re-establishing themselves. He has been a loyal member of the [[Host Tower of the Arcane]] ever since, quickly rising up in the ranks over the following years. When [[Amicia Lawntill]] became the leader of the [[Arcane Brotherhood]], he was taked with finding and training [[Yan 'Keyreeonis' Lawntill]].

During his mission, he murdered [[Dameon Lawntill]] and [[Martha Lawntill]] before beginning [[Yan 'Keyreeonis' Lawntill]]'s training. Later, he sent the young half-elf to the Sword Coast in order to retrieve a book from [[Candlekeep]] Hall.
