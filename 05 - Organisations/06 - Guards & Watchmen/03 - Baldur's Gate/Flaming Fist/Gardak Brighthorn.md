Gardak Brighthorn is a Dwarven Wizard, smuggler and a Gauntlet of the [[Flaming Fist]] in [[Baldur's Gate]]. He currently makes his home in [[Wyrm's Rock]], living in the former commissary.

He has medium-length auburn hair and a goatee that does nothing to compliment his thick neck and light eyebrows. He carries a staff and wears robes in the colour of the [[Flaming Fist]].

During the [[Welcome to the Arcana-Net]] quest, [[Ardryn Deagle]] states that Gardak stole his schematics. His partner, [[Pernilla 'Prole' Cabrenock]] describes him as a 'fuckfaced fucker'.
