The highly trained military organisation of fully-armoured soldiers responsible for watching over [[Waterdeep]]'s walls. In addition to their duties within the city, they would also provide their protection to the outer areas of the county.

A special aquatic division of the City Guard exists to provide protection against aquatic threats, as well as recovering bodies, salvaging lost cargo and patrolling a short distance outside the harbour. They specifically were placed within the vicinity of [[Deepwater Beacon]], an underwater lighthouse which served as a guiding light and rally point.
