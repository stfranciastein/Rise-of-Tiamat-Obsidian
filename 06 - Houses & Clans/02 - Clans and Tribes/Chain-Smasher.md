Clan Chain-Smasher is the Clan of [[Kurtog Chain-Smasher]]. Prior to his takeover of Berdusk, the Chain-Smashers were once an unorganised but strong, honourable collection of Orcs that lived in the Sunset Valley. Under Kurtog's leadership, he was able to unite them in order to form the vanguard for the [[Reclamation]] of [[Berdusk]].
