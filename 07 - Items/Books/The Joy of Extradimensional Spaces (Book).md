A book given to [[Fistandia]] by [Mystra](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Mystra). The book originally served as a permanently enchanted tome that could open a portal [[Fistandia's Mansion]], a unique version of Mordenkainen's Magnificent Mansion. After [[Fistandia]]'s disappearance in the Astral Sea, it was donated to [[Candlekeep]].
