**Tags:** #MagicItems #LettersAndNotes #CourtOfMetal 

This book written in Draconic details a thesis on the possibility of merging Chromatic and Metallic Dragons through an alchemical process created by a Cultist of Asgorath. Reading through this text, it's obvious that the author, Protander, must be a dragon as he often refers to Metallic Dragons using 'we' as a pronoun.

What stands out the most in relation to the mutated dragon is that they are said to be able to reach the status of 'young' in as short span of time as one year. As a side effect of this accelerated growth, Protander theorises that these mutant dragons will likely only live to the age of 50.
