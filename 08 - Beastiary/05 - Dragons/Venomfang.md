An Adult Green Dragon living in the tower of Old Shade Rest. While unaffiliated with any organisations in [[Act 1 - The Curse of Willowvale]], Venomfang becomes a member of the [[Cult of the Dragon]] after [[Thaddak Ice-Veins]] persuades him with the [[White Dragon Mask]].
