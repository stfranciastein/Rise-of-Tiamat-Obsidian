### A. Room & Board

#### Main Cities
- 5-10gp for a room in a luxurious establishment.
- 1-5gp for a room in a normal establishment.
- 5sp for a room in a poor establishment.
#### Small Towns
- 1gp for a room in a normal establishment.
- 5sp for a room in a poor establishment.
