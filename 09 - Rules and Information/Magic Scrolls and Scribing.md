### Scribing and Copying

***Copying a Scroll into a Spellbook.*** A Wizard spell on a Spell Scroll can be copied into a Spellbook. When a spell is copied in this way, the copier must succeed on an Intelligence (Arcana) check with a DC equal to 1O plus the spell's level. On a successful check, the spell is copied. Whether the check succeeds or fails, the Spell Scroll is destroyed. 

It takes 2 hours per level of the spell to copy one to your book. i.e. to copy Fireball you need to roll a 13 or higher on your Arcana check and spend 6 hours scribing it. If you have Ravenshade's Reading Glasses, you can get your clone to do this for you at night.

***Scribing A Scroll.*** *Any* spellcaster can transfer a spell to a scroll and create a Spell Scroll. Scribing a scroll takes an amount of time and money based on the level of the spell, as shown in the Spell Scroll Costs table. For each day of inscription, you must work for 8 hours. If a scroll requires multiple days, those days needn't be consecutive. 

To scribe a scroll, you must have proficiency in the Arcana skill or with Calligrapher's Supplies and have the spell prepared on each day of the inscription. You must also have at hand any Material components required by the spell; if the spell consumes its Material components, they are consumed only when you complete the scroll. The scroll's spell uses your spell save DC and spell attack bonus ***from the time you created it.*** If your spell save DC when you finish the scroll is 17, that is the DC of the scroll.

### Purchasing Scrolls

According to the DMG, the cost to buy a scroll is equal to double the amount it costs to scribe one. To do it completely flat would eradicate the need for some spell components, so I've added a 'service' tax to scrolls. Thus, the actual cost for scrolls is:

- Cantrip: 50GP
- 1st level: 100GP
- 2nd level: 300GP
- 3rd level: 500GP
- 4th level: 2000GP
- 5th level: 3000GP
- 6th level: 20,000GP
- 7th level: 25,000GP
- 8th level: 30,000GP
- 9th level: 100,000GP
