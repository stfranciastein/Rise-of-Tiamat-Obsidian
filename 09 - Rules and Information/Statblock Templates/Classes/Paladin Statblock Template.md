# Paladin

### Traits
`**_Aura of Protection._** Creatures in a 10-foot [rules]Emanation[/rules] originating from the Paladin have a +3 bonus to saving throws. The Talon can designate creatures to be unaffected by the aura.`

## Bonus Actions
`_**Spellcasting.**_ The Paladin is a 2nd-level spellcaster that uses Charisma as its spellcasting ability (spell save DC 16, +9 to hit with spell attacks). It has the following spells prepared from the Paladin spell list:`

`1st level (4/day)**:** [spells]Command[/spells], [spells]Cure Wounds[/spells].`

`2nd level (3/day): [spells]Wrathful Smite[/spells], [spells]Shining Smite[/spells].`
