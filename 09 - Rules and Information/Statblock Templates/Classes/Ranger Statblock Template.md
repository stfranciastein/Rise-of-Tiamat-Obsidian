# Ranger
## Traits
`_**Sharpshooter.**_ When making a ranged weapon attack, the creature ignores three quarters cover and half cover. Being within 5 feet of an enemy doesn’t impose [rules]disadvantage[/rules] on its attack rolls with Ranged weapons. Additionally, it does not suffer [rules]disadvantage[/rules] from long-range shots.`
