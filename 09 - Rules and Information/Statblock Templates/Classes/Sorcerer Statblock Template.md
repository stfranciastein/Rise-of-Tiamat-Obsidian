# Sorcerer

## Traits
﻿`_**Spell Sniper.**_ ﻿When the creature makes a ranged spell attack with at least 10 feet and requires it to make an attack roll, the range is increased by 60 feet. Additionally, the creature ignores three quarters cover and half cover. Being within 5 feet of an enemy doesn’t impose [rules]disadvantage[/rules] on its attack rolls with spells.`
