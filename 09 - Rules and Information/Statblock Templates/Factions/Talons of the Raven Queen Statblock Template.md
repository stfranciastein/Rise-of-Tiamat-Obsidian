## Bonus Actions
`_**Flight of the Corvid (5/day).**_ The Talon's Cloak propels it, giving it fly speed equal to its walk speed for the duration of its turn.`

## Reactions
`_**Corvid's Call (2/day).**_ When the Talon is attacked by an enemy within 30 feet of it that it can see, it can use its reaction to impose disadvantage on the attack roll, causing an invisible strike disrupts the enemy's thoughts before it hits or misses. Constructs are immune to this feature.`

`_**Feather Fall (1/day).**_ The Talon takes no fall damage as it casts [spells]Feather Fall[/spells] once per day.`
