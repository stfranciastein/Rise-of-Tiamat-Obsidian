# Zhentarim

**Tags:** #Templates #Statblocks #LordsAlliance 
## Traits
`_**Colossus Slayer.**_ When the Enclave Agent hits a creature with a weapon attack, the creature takes an extra 1d8 damage if it’s below its hit point maximum. It can deal this extra damage only once per turn.`

`_**Tag Team.**_ When the Enclave Agent takes the Help action, it can switch places with a willing ally within 5 feet of itself as part of that same action. This movement doesn’t provoke Opportunity Attacks.`

## Bonus Actions
`_**Standard Bearer.**_ The Alliance agent chooses up to three creatures within 60 ft. that can see it. Each target with a speed above 0 can immediately take a Reaction to right itself and end the prone condition. Additionally, it extends its Alliance Agent's Focus to those three creatures for 1 minute or until it is [condition]incapacitated[/condition].`

## Reactions
`_**Cunning Interference (2/day).**_ When a creature within 30 feet of the Zhent that it can see makes a saving throw, the Zhent can use its Reaction before the creature succeeds or fails to impose disadvantage on that roll.`

`_**Retaliate.**_ Immediately after a creature within 5 feet of the Zhent hits it with a melee attack, it can make an Opportunity Attack against that creature.`
