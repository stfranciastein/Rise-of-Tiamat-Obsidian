### 2.4 - Trouble on the Road 

Once upon a time, in a land known as Silverymoon there lived a little girl named Ulena. Her mother Rynae and her father Sossa Kiln loved her so very much. They lived a quiet, peaceful life in the bustling city of the Moonmaiden and all was well and good.

But one day, a deep and dreary cold began to creep down from the mountains. Sossa dismissed it at first as a change in the climate, but his wife was not so certain. Rynae of course, was correct. You see the Winter King had grown greedy and ambitious. Too ambitious for a King of the Winter Court. So when the time came for her to protect her daughter, the only thing Rynae could do was hide her away until the fighting was over. Little did the druid know that it would be 20 years before she would be reunited with her daughter again, after a chance meeting with a strange girl and her Djinn protector. 

Impyerno Lima worked to free Rynae from her curse, but Marianne the hag placed them under a geas and sent them into the Feywild. While there, there they found themselves subject to the stories inside the Grim Tales for Bratty Children and had to fight their way to through the stories to free themselves. While inside, Lioran discovered his friend Mira and her companions had been trapped by the hag as well. She explained that it had been two weeks since Impyerno Lima disappeared into the Feywild. When they emerged, the Cataclysm's reactivation confirmed the truth of her claim. 

Now, they journey to Elturel with Mira to finally continue their mission against the Cult of the Dragon.
