### 2.6 The Mysterious Client

Lioran spent the majority of first day back in Elturel settling home and trying to go back to normal. He lead a sermon during the Oathtaking ceremony, reunited with his mother and paid a visit to an old friend before meeting a new one in Pearl. In the mean time, rest of Impyerno Lima worked with the Zhentarim to steal back the Constantori's Portrait, a sentient painting. 

After the heist was done, they followed Lioran to the Order of the Gauntlet, only to find that Lioran was not himself and he had lead them to an ambush orchestrated by the Arcane Brotherhood. Thuran Yale taunted Yan with a puppeteered body of himself, his true self, and ordered his minions to attack the group.
