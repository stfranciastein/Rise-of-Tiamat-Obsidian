### 2.7 Whispers of the Brotherhood

The Arcane Brotherhood's ambush proved to be a difficult fight, with the Agents of the Brotherhood successfully managing to take down several party members. Working together as a unit, Impyerno Lima emerged victorious and defeated them in the end along with Thuran Yale, but not before the elf could fatally strike Yan with a Disintegrate spell, as well use Feeblemind on Ulena to reduce her to a simple creature.

In the aftermath of their victory, they discovered the Thuran they defeated was simply a simulacrum, created to taunt Yan into coming after him. As the dust of Yan's former body settled, he was able to take control of his real body, regaining some of his sensations in the process. And while they survived the fight, they all learned some uncomfortable truths about each other as well as Yan's former mentor. 

Whatever Thuran had become, it's something sinister, and the Elf seemed far too eager for Yan to reunite with his sister in the future. What dark plans Thuran has in store for the future are a mystery, but all roads seem to point to Andora Lawntill. Thankfully, unlike his former master, Yan is able to sense where she and his mother is. For now.

After the fight, the party retired to Lioran's house for some well-earned rest and relaxation. Yan enjoyed the comforts he had missed out on, raiding Lioran's house for food and drink. Meanwhile, Zil was able to understand her companions a little more, confronting Ryn about his insecurities while also pondering her own. 

The next morning, Lioran and the rest of Impyerno Lima returned to the Hall of the Triad to report to his stepfather and Chapter Leader Ontharr Frume, giving him some grave news about the Cult of the Dragon.
