### 2.9 Assault on Fort Morninglord pt.1

Emerging from the catacombs below Elturel, Impyerno Lima reported their findings to Thavius Kreeg who thanked them for their service with a hefty reward. Later that evening they reunited with Ontharr Frume and the Paladin gave them their mission; meet Cyanwrath in battle as he assaults Fort Morninglord. Another group lead by Mira Fenwillow was then ordered to pursue a lead to the East. That night, Lioran exchanged a tearful goodbye with Mira who seemed deeply worried about the future. 

As they slumbered, they found themselves dreamwalking in a strange prison somewhere in the hells. There, they reunited with the Harper agent Rhylrae and the Wizard Matreous Melwin. The pair warned them about another danger that walks Faerun, a monsterous individual with a stolen artifact from the Arch-Lich Vecna himself. Lucien also made an appearance, sensing the party's presence in the Hells. He told the group that he would be able to help them sneak into the Hells, but only after they finished with their prior obligations with him.

In the morning, they said their fond farewells to Grace and Lioran's family before heading out to meet the Cult of the Dragon in combat. As they arrived in Fort Morninglord, they found that the keep was already under assault by a small Kobold army, accompanied by a familiar Green Dragon.
