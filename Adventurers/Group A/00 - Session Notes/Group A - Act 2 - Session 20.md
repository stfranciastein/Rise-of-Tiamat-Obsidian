### 3.2 Not-Quite-There pt.2

After slaying the ghosts atop the lighthouse and reigniting the flames, Alia suggested the group go forward towards the dockhouse as she observed signs of life.

Inside, the party found two young humans named Becca and Daniel who were quite terrified and described the mysterious creatures in the water. The group healed them and told them to find safe harbour at the Piercing Light. Unfortunately, as the boat made its way towards the ship they immediately drowned as undead creatures dragged them down to the bottom of the Chionthar.

Following a message from the Raven Queen, the party continued to investigate the catastrophe and found a house with a trail of blood. As Lioran investigated the bodies inside and out, he realised they seemed to have died of other causes. Inside, they found the spirit of a young girl who was possessed by a demon. Zil and Yan both determined to put her spirit to rest decided to head towards the mansion atop the hill.

Inside the manor, the party found themselves trapped by an otherworldly force. Within the confines of the house, Impyerno Lima was subjected to several hauntings including Animated Armour, Helmed Horrors, Flameskulls and ghosts. Alia or 'Talis' warned them that another Warlock was closeby and Lioran found himself having visions of Fero's death. As Impyerno Lima continued exploring the mansion, they were confronted by a familiar face staring at them outside the window.
