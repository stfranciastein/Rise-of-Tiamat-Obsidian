### 3.3 Not-Quite-There pt.3

After finding a way out of the mansion by following a series of notes, they pursued their former companion Fero into the Vermeillon catacombs. Inside, they found the remaining survivors of the town including their former companion Fero and an elven woman named Jenna Maris who seemed to be marked for death. As they spoke to their old friend, they were promptly ambushed by the Slaughter Demon sent to kill her and the party managed to slay it just in time and saved Jenna's life. 

The party found themselves exhausted after the fight and were forced to ponder their choices; abandon their quest and return to the ship or continue down below to face whatever horror awaited them. After a lot of time and discussion, Impyerno Lima  decided to employ the help of Lord Lucien Shadowblade (the fetching and handsome Devil from the plane of Phlegethos) to get their strength back, with Zil offering her services to the Devil in exchange for some respite.

As they explored the catacombs Impyerno Lima learned the truth of the sordid affair. How Axerias the Elven Warlock miscalculated and inadvertently summoned something far too powerful; a Caprathorn from the Abyss who consumed the Essence of Zariel beneath Vermeillon and began its murderous rampage. 

In Axerias's study, they found what looked like a metal stake that Axerias theorised could paralyze the demon long enough for the Essence to be extracted from its body. Deeper in the catacombs, Fero unlocked the Demon's prison and was able to enter after nearly defeating the party. As they explored the old mines, they found that even with his newfound strength Fero was not able to defeat the Caprathorn in its hunting grounds. Deeper in the mine they also found a singular survivor, briefly saving the life of a human man named John only for him to immediately fall victim to the Caprathorn as soon as they turned their backs.

Impyerno Lima attempted to fight the creature, only to find that it was far too powerful for them to slay. Thankfully, with Axerias's stake, Zil was able to paralyze the creature and extract the Essence. In the wake of their victory, the town of Vermeillon began to recover and the souls of the restless dead were freed from their torment. They returned aboard the Piercing Light leaving Fero and the town behind, though in the back of their heads they knew that this was not the last time they'd see him.
