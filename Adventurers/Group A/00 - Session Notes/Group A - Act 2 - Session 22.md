### 3.4 Arrival at Baldur's Gate

As Impyerno Lima continued their journey down the Chionthar, they found themselves attacked once more by their old companion Fero. Unfortunately, Fero's power proved far too great to defeat and they were forced to give back the Essence to him and Tharizdun. 

Upon arriving in Baldur's Gate, Ser Caldus bid them farewell while Alia, Leosin and Glass ventured off to give the party some time to explore. During their brief period in the Blood Gate they met all sorts of different characters. In Wyrm's Crossing, they were able to prevent an assassination attempt on Blaze Portyr, the current head of the Flaming Fist. 

Later, Zil learned of Danthelon's missing shipments and the troubles of the Temples in the Outer City while Ulena browsed the 'goods' in Sharess's Caress. Now, they ponder their choices of what to tackle first.
