### 3.6 Exit Bear, Stage Right pt.1

As Ryn and Zil relaxed at the Elfsong Tavern, Lioran, Ulena and Yan faced down a masked killer and defeated him in combat. When they returned to their companions they were unable to discern the origin of the ritual they saw, though Zil had some inkling as to where to look for more information.

While the night winded down, Zil performed a good deed by giving her room up to two unfortunate souls who were sleeping rough on the streets of Brampton. In the mean time, Yan lost more gold to the pirate Tarina who escaped after a bar brawl broke out in the Elfsong. 

The next day, Ulena and Lioran successfuly recovered Ardryn's blueprints for Arcana-Net and Yan was able to persuade the Gnome to provide them with an extra 3 devices. Zil made good on her promise to help Sister Eabha at the Church of Last Hope, where she got a better idea of how desperate they were for supplies.

Later, Impyerno Lima reunited and decided to journey south past Willowvale to the northern part of the Cloakwood where they found a familiar face. After a lengthy battle with Fey creatures, the group and Tamae began an investigation into the troubles that were haunting the Coast Way once more.
