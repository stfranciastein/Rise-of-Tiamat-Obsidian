### 3.7 Exit Bear, Stage Right pt.2

Looking for clues regarding the weakened barrier between the Material Plane and the Feywild, Impyerno Lima ventured into the Cloakwood. As they explored old territory, they ran into Thaddak Ice-Veins once more. After a lengthy battle where they defeated another one of the bugbear's simulacrums, they found themselves empty handed save for the treasures that were left behind by the mine's previous occupants.

Returning to Shade Rest, they joined Tamae in continuing to investigate Danthelon's missing supplies. In the northern part of the Cloakwood, they found a fey-touched grove guarded by Redcaps. Zil and Yan convinced the Redcaps to let them see their business partner; a fey-touched creature known as a Suneater Owlbear.

After defeating the creature, the party retrieved the missing shipments and made their way back to Shade Rest. As per Tamae's invitation, they spent the night there for old time's sake and celebrated their victory. During their night of merriment, they met the Elven bard Cacafonix who sang their praises both literally and figuratively. Now they rest under familiar stars as they ponder their next move.
