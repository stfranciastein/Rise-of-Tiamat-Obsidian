### 3.8 The Upper City

After a night of celebrations, the plucky heroes known as Impyerno Lima took advantage of the quietness of Shade Rest. Ulena visited a few old friends and in the process of catching up, helped the people of Shade Rest once more by enriching their lands. Meanwhile, Zil and Lioran had a lengthy discussion about their future with Impyerno Lima after being formally invited to join their group.

On their way back to Baldur's Gate, the party found themselves confronting a deadly group of cultists lead by a man armed similarly to the serial killer they defeated in the city. After saving their sacrificial lamb, the party soon found out that the cultists were worshippers of Bhaal; the Lord of Murder. They quickly drove back to the city, informing Lady Margaret Ravenshade of their findings in hopes of aiding her investigation. After a tense but fruitful conversation, Impyerno Lima learned that Lady Ravenshade strongly believes these murders to be another union between the Dead Three; Bane the Tyrant, Bhaal the Assassin and Myrkul the Necromancer.

Later that day, Zil began the process of the preparing for her initiation into the Talons when she ran into a familiar face. Familiar not because she knew of her, but of how much this old Shadar-Kai woman reminded her so much of her own mother. Katarzyna and Zil spoke as she visited her family in the graveyard and offered Zil sage advice.
