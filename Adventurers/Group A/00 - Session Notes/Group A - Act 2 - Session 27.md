### 3.9 Den of the Dead Three

As Impyerno Lima continued their investigation into the Cult of the Dead Three, they found themselves visiting the bathhouse that Lady Ravenshade described. During the day, it appeared to be nothing more than a well-maintained facility, but careful questioning lead them to believe that something far more illicit was happening there at night.

After a brief pizza break they went back to their mission. Upon their return they attempted to question the groundskeeper, a man named Jabaz, into telling them about potential Cultist activity. However, they were soon set upon by Bhaalist assassins and a Paladin of Bane and a fight ensued. During their scuffle, Yan set the roof of the bathhouse on fire but they were quickly able to put it out and continue onwards.

Down in the dungeon of the Dead Three, Impyerno Lima found all manner of gruesome sights. From murder victims to a man being tortured by Cultists of Bane. They were able to save him in the end, and the man introduced himself as Klim Jhasso. Ryn ensured he got back out to the surface, but failed to persuade him that they did not cause the fire. In his fear, he ran away and the group continued their delve. In their explorations, they also met a very helpful and hungry rat named Ratthew who lead them through the dungeons. 

As they found themselves at the heart of the complex, they confronted Mortlock Vanthampur. In their fight with him, Mortlock proved to be a very powerful barbarian, able to down several members of Impyerno Lima before finally being slain by Yan. In his dying breath, Mortlock grinned and told Lioran than soon enough Elturel and its people will burn.

In Mortlock's possessions, Impyerno Lima found a hoard stolen from the Cult of the Dragon. As they plundered the chests and left, they soon realised that the Flaming Fist had surrounded the bathhouse. Eager to escape, Zil, Ryn and Ulena all scattered off leaving Yan and Lioran to be cuffed and imprisoned by the Flaming Fist.
