### 1.4 The Cult Strikes Back

Impyerno Lima defended the town of Greenest to the best of their ability, taking on dozens of Dragon Cultists in the process. They managed to save most of the townsfolk while working in tandem with the other heroes at Greenest. As they fought their way to Nighthill Manor to secure the location, the Half Dragon known as Rezmir made her appearance atop an Adult Black Dragon, disintegrating a child they'd hoped to save. The party survived as Rezmir flew off, and they continued to rescue as many civilians as possible before heading up to the manor. They, they discovered that Nighthill's guards had been murdered and the reward for the Hunt had been stolen.

As the dragons circled overhead, the group returned to the centre of town to find a massive Half Blue Dragon. The unnamed fighter taunted them into a duel in exchange for the lives of the hostages. As the party debated their choice, he began to execute their hostages one by one. Not wanting to see more people get hurt, Lioran stepped forward to face the Dragonclaw. Unfortunately, the champion proved too strong for Lioran and he was slain. Ulena rushed toward his fallen body as the Cult pulled back and brought Lioran back to life, using his own diamond to revivify him.

Governor Nighthill, Cormyc and Torbar rejoined the group and told them of the damage done to the town. The Emerald Enclave Harpers took heavy losses but survived, as did the Talons and Harpers. Lioran's two Order of the Gauntlet comrades unfortunately were disintegrated by Lennithon during his initial assault on the keep. Additionally, it seems that Leosin has been taken prisoner by the Cult. The party ponders their next move as they try to gather themselves for the journey ahead.
