### 1.6 The Hatchery

As their journey continued, the group found themselves at the Cult of the Dragon's Raider Camp, atop a hill overlooking the thousands of Dragon Cultist soldiers in a valley below. Overwhelmed by their extreme numbers, the Impyerno Lima argued over their next plan of action but ultimately decided to disguise themselves and sneak into the Raider Camp.

The party successfully infiltrated the camp and entered the Dragon Hatchery without harm, save for Yan's dignity when he was forced to dance in front of a Blue Dragonborn and made himself kiss his foot.

Inside the Hatchery, they found Frulam Mondath's chambers, who they discovered was now working for the Cult of the Dragon. Hidden in her notes they also found records about the Arcane Brotherhood's involvement with the Cult of the Dragon, and an upcoming partnership with the Red Wizards of Thay.

Unfortunately, Yan triggered a trap and caused Ulena and Lioran to fall down a hole where they were confronted by Frulam herself. Unable to avoid a fight, the party decided to engage with her in combat. The battle was hard fought, they managed to slay her in the end. Yet in the wake of her defeat, they found themselves exposed to her ritual.

After a brief encounter with death, they managed to escape with their lives. Well, that and a Green Dragon Egg.
