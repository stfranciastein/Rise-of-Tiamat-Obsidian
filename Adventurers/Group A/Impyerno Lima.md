A band of adventurers who were tasked by [[Feldir Whitefyre]] of [[Willowvale]] to seek out the wizard [[Matreous Melwin]] in [[Candlekeep]]. This party originally consisted of [[Thavion Brightfell]], [[Ulena Kiln]], [[Yan 'Keyreeonis' Lawntill]], [[Fero Mörktträ]] and [[Ryn Fenrath]]. 

After the events of [[Act 1 - The Curse of Willowvale]], [[Fero Mörktträ]] fell in battle to [[Mellara, the Terror of the Sword Coast]]. [[Thavion Brightfell]] retired once more and left the party. He ventured to the Wood of Sharp Teeth where he began to rebuild [[Fero Mörktträ]]'s home. 

In their place, [[Lioran Thorne]] and [[Zilliletha]] joined the party in [[Act 2 - Hoard of the Dragon Queen]]. 

____
### EXTRA INFORMATION

The two horses they purchased to pull the [[Sylvira-Van]] are named Fero and Darrieth, named after their former companion and one of the Readers at [[Candlekeep]].

### PROGRESS

#### ACT I - The Curse of Willowvale
- Released the [[Essence of Zariel]].
- Saved [[Shade Rest]].
- Found [[Matreous Melwin]] dead.
- Killed [[Thaddak Ice-Veins]]'s simulacrum but did not free the prisoners from [[Willowvale]].
- Killed [[Batuu Wolf-Blood]] and freed the prisoners from [[Nashkel]].
- Met [[Rhylrae]] and did not kill [[Harandiir]] or [[Zeraxes]]. [[Yan 'Keyreeonis' Lawntill]] lost his life and became a [[Revenant]] at the end of their imprisonment.
- [[Ysra Deathstrike]] assassinated [[Fero Mörktträ]] and stole the [[Essence of Zariel]].
- Signed a deal with [[Lucien]] in order to get the location of the hoard.
- Did not stick around to get robbed by [[Jamna Gleamsilver]].
- Took [[Leosin Erlanthar]] with them to fight [[Mellara, the Terror of the Sword Coast]].
- Gave the hoard to [[Venomfang]].
- [[Fero Mörktträ]] died in [[Chapter 8.0 - Herd of the Dragon]].

#### ACT II - The Hoard of the Dragon Queen
- Did not take Windpeak's riches in [[Chapter 1.0 - The Great Hunt]]'s prologue chapter.
- Hunted the Enraged Hydra.
- Discovered Lord [[Theodore Goldmane]]'s cheating.
- Won the Great Hunt.
- Did not impress [[Langdedrosa Cyanwrath]].
- Saved [[Grace]] and took her with them.
- Rescued [[Leosin Erlanthar]] in time.
- Kept the egg containing a [[Mutated Dragon]] (Green).
- Retrieved the [[Belt of the Giant]] for [[Flint]].
- Did not resolve the murders in [[Berdusk]].
- Attempted to kill [[Azbara Jos]].
