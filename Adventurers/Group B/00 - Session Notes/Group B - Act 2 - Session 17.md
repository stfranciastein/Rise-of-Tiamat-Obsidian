### 3.2 - Not-Quite-There pt. 2

As the Bush Whackers did battle with the Slaughter Demon, they were unfortunately unable to save the life of Jenna Maris and several commoners. The fight was quite deadly, but they emerged victorious once more.

Delving deeper into the catacombs they were able to find the truth of the deaths in Vermeillon; an Elven Warlock named Axerias attempted to open a golden catacomb containing the Essence of Zariel with the help of a demon. In calling for one's aid, he accidentally unleashed something called a 'Caprathorn'; a powerful fiend that neither he or his companions could kill or contain.

Now, they pursue a powerful Warlock down the abandoned Vermeillon mines, where an Ancient Evil may await them.
