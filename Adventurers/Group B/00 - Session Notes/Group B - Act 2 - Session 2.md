### 1.3 The Great Hunt

As they entered its lair, the Bush Whackers found themselves in danger as an Enraged Chimera beared down upon them in a surprise attack. The battle was hard-fought but the heroes emerged victorious. Caradil lost his life for the first time, though thankfully their plucky druid Tierbhal managed to save him from the grasp of death.

Journeying back to Greenest, things were on the up and up for the party as they returned with their kill. After a few moments of bonding, the party attended the Feast at the end of the Hunt. However, right before they could be given their accolades, a flash of lightning streaked across the sky and thunder quaked in the distance. And with it, death by the name of Lennithon approached the town of Greenest.
