### 1.4 Cult Strikes Back

After a difficult encounter full of fire and blood, the Bush Whackers survived the night, though not without trouble. The Cult of the Dragon's raid on the town was successful, as the town was no match for the three dragons or the Cult's overwhelming numbers.

During the night, the Dragonclaw Langdendrosa Cyanwrath challenged Aramir to a duel. While the fight was quick, Cyanwrath found himself impressed with the Aasimar's strength and promised to meet Aramir again in the battlefield, leaving him alive as the Cult pulled its forces back.

The following morning, they met with Governor Nighthill's council as they discussed the next steps. After some deliberation, they decided to follow Lord Ravenshade's lead in the small village known as Windpeak.

On their way there, however, Tierbhal and Caradil found themselves running into a familiar face in the form of a girl stuck in a tree.
