### 1.6 The Raider Camp and the Dragon Hatchery

The Bush Whackers did battle with the Cult once more and discovered the existence of the monsterous Abishai, draconic fiends seemingly made from human sacrifices. 

As they approached the Raider Camp, they met the fey-touched girl Grace once more. Not trusting her to keep herself safe, they invited her into the caravan and left Auron and Glass to watch over her.

 They then snuck into the camp dressed cultists, smooth-talking their way past the Cultists and avoiding Cyanwrath in the process. Caradil discovered a tent with 3 seemingly high-ranking Cult members consisting of a Blue Tortle, A Red Dragonborn, and a massive Minotaur.

Soma was able to get them even further into the camp straight into the Hatchery, as they pretended to be a Cultist Spellcaster with some willing sacrifices to give to the ritual of ascension. Within, they discovered Frulam Mondath's treachery.

After a chance encounter with the statue and freeing Leosin, they discovered a Blue Dragon egg in the Hatchery. Before they could run back to the caravan, Lennithon descended down and prevented them from going further.

Now they head east as they go with Leosin to meet with the High Harper, with new claws in place of their hands.
