### 2.4 The Murders in Berdusk

After several failed attempts at getting good information from three kobolds in a trenchcoat, the Bush Whackers were finally able to track down the location of Avdul. Unfortunately it seemed that the Orcish man had taken his life, and they found themselves at a dead end. 

Determined to keep moving, they decided to find the human farmer named Joss. After a series of unfortunate events, they were able to kill the Slaughter Demon that possessed Joss, but not before the Entity could devour Joss and the Cleric's souls. Thankfully, with Aramir's divine magic on their side they were able to dispatch of the demon quite quickly.

At night, the entire party had a strange dream. They found themselves in the middle of a purple-hued room, with several doors blocked off by magical barriers. Inside, a metallic dragon spoke to them and told the party to find the 'Wizard'.

The following morning, Amory was the next person on their list, and the group rushed over to the Sanguine Dawn Inn in order to find him. Several locked doors and traps later, they found Amory's inner sanctum.  There, they confronted the Elven Ranger and his Assassins. The fight proved to be quite difficult, and they nearly lost their lives in the process but they emerged victorious.

Back in Berdusk, the party resupplied for the road ahead, and boarded the Tidecutter with the intent of getting off near Scornubel to reunite with their children.
