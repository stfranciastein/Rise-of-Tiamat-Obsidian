### **BACKSTORY**

<img src="Ambrosius-1784473063574.webp" alt="Ambrosius" width="300" style="float:left; margin: 0 16px 12px 0;">Ambrosius was orphaned at a very young age, he doesn't even remember his parents name or faces, only that his mother had emerald green hair and his father had a long muddy coloured beard flecked with streaks of bright flaming orange He was orphaned on the road from Baldur's gate when goblins attacked his parents camp one night. They were traveling back to their village that he does not know the name of after taking Ambrosius to see a doctor as he was suffering from a fever that the village clerics couldn't seem to shift. 

It was a clear bright moonlit night, his father was tending the fire whilst his mother was rocking Ambrosius to sleep swaddled in his fathers Red Gnome hat, his mother was singing a classic gnomish nursery rhyme to him in a voice that seemed capture the attention of all that heard it. Little did this know that this singing would lead to their end, it caught the attention of some passing goblins who wanted to capture this siren and have her sing for them forever back at their camp. 

Ambrosius' father was staring lovingly at his wife and fairly new born son when suddenly his chest, shoulder, and stomach exploded with three crude arrows poorly aimed. He fell face first into the fire without a noise His mother went to scream but her mouth was quickly covered from behind by another goblin. Ambrosius was dropped and rolled close to the fire, unable to roll over he was forced to watch his father slowly begin to catch fire. The acrid stench of burning hair and boiling flesh filled the air. His mother struggled against the sickly green hand gripping her face. she bit down as hard as she could which cause the goblin to scream out in pain and let her go. She immediately picked Ambrosius up chucked him gently into their tent full of soft furs and pillows. She then grabbed a burning log from the fire and started swinging wildly at the goblins that had her surrounded screaming at the top of her lungs all the while But she was outnumbered, 10 to 1 there was no hope. Realising that this woman would not go quietly the goblins decided she was too much trouble and she was stabbed in the side with a crude black iron spear. The goblins then piled onto her showering her with blows from every angle. The goblins continued untill a bolt from a crossbow all but exploded one of their heads. They were quickly set upon by a group of heavily armoured men wielding maces. 

Ambrosius forced to watch from the tent was crying out in horror not understanding what was going on. The camp was filled with the screams of goblins, flashes of divine light and the heroic shouts of a group of Roaming Paladins who heard the screams coming from the road. Eventually the fighting subsided and the Paladins heard a cough coming from the smouldering Gnome by the fire. The fire had completely engulfed Ambrosius' father burning all his hair off and leaving his skin bubbled and and sloughing off in chunks. He managed to utter out a word from almost fused lips. "Ambrosius" he struggled whilst lifting what was left of his arm and pointing towards the tent. A Dark skinned Gnome was ordered to check the tent where he found Ambrosius swaddled in a large red gnome's hat. 

The remaining men tried their best heal his parents with all the magic they could. Sadly Ambrosius's mothers wounds were clearly fatal, her head caved in from repeated clubbing there was nothing left of her to save. Ambrosius's father saw the men's failed attempts to revive her and knew his wounds were also to far gone. He looked at the Paladin tending to his immolated flesh and managed to say "Ambrosius" one more time before giving the Paladin that was trying his best to bring back his form using healing magic a knowing look. The Paladin immediately understood his gaze and stopped using magic and drew out a dagger from his belt and pierced his chest in one smooth motion. Ambrosius let out a sigh of relief and passed instantly The following years of Ambrosius' life were hard. Taken in by these saviour paladins who quickly became his family. His main mentor was Pip Four-Stones. A Stout Deep Gnome who got his name from his shockingly outlandish bravado and bullishness. Despite being a Paladin he was almost arrogant and was fond of women often offering them the chance to inspect his "four-stones" themselves. 

Pip taught Ambrosius everything he knew about Gnome life and culture, but being a deep gnome himself he lacked the knowledge of the nuances of Forest Gnome culture leading Ambrosius to have a gap in his knowledge of nature and the woodlands he was so fond of. Ambrosius was far from the perfect student, often giving into boredom and childish whims, often day dreaming whilst studying texts. He Excelled in all physical matters, honing his combat and his body all life to the point where he was comically muscular for a paladin, let alone a gnome. Combat and Conversation came to him with ease, despite his silver tongue he often found himself in trouble with the locals whether it be womanising or sabotaging the local industry. This often led to him and his paladins fleeing whatever village or settlement they were camped from in the night. Being chased out more than once. Despite his outward confidence he has some deep rooted insecurities and has attachment issues, falling deeply in love at first sight and struggling to let go of new found friends. He is plagued by nightmares of that fateful night the goblins murdered his parents and of all his previous and possible future mistakes.
