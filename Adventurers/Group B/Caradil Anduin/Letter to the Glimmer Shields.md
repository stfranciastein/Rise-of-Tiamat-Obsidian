
Paladins,

I unfortunately do not know any of your names and you most likely do not know me but my name is Caradil. Some months ago I met a member of your order, Ambrosius, while travelling down along the Sword Coast and we became fast friends while smashing awakened shrubs. Before long we found ourselves hired by a mage in Willowvale to help discover the source of the plague that had been diminishing their crops and this lead us on a winding path to fight the cult of the dragon, a path I still walk but no longer with Ambrosius.

You may have already heard what happened but you are owed the story from someone who was there. On our quest to hunt these villains, we tracked them to an abandoned temple of Bahamut in the Cloud Peaks where some of their leaders were performing a ritual. We fought and defeated them but not before Ambrosius was struck down. He sought no glory and never cared about riches. He fought to defend those that could not defend themselves and every loss weighed heavy on his kind heart. 

Ambrosius was steadfast in his beliefs and his dedication to Silvanus was unwavering. It may sound odd but there was a warmth that came from Ambrosius and I always felt safe knowing his shield was guarding my back. I've felt a little colder since he fell.

When we weren't smiting our foes together, he was always merry. We would drink, dance and share stories of our journey's, even bickering from time to time when my sticky fingers got the best of me but he was always joyous and I always trusted him. He told me once of his parents deaths and it seemed to give him strength, something I admired and envied him for. Even now I wish I could be half as strong as he was. 

We were unable to retrieve his remains or anything he carried with him at the time but I have kept everything else he left at our camp in hopes of returning them to you. I've left everything as well as this letter with Ontharr for when you return. I only hope he is sober enough to remember. The Sword Coast forever owes Ambrosius a debt that cannot be repaid and I will always remember him.
May Silvanus guide you.

Caradil of Waterdeep
Bush Whacker
Ranger of the Renblades
