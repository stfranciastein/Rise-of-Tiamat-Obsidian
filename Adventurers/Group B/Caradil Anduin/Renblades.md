The Renblades were founded roughly 180-200 years ago but [[Caradil Anduin]] does not remember much  about the first High Captain. The second High Captain, Meesandara Greypool served for 40 years until her natural death followed by Isen Tenderstem who chose to retire, naming [[Audric Anduin]] as his successor. He served for 8 years until his execution by [[dagult Neverember]]'s orders. The High Captain was awarded a manor located in the Sea Ward known as the House of Ren. [[Audric Anduin]] spent very little time there, preferring to remain at the central barracks in the Castle Ward. 

The hierarchy of the Renblades was divided between several of the city's wards, with captains acting as leaders in each of them. The High Captain acted as captain of the Castle Ward as well as overall leader of the Renblades. They would liaison with the other captains, the other forms of city security and the city's council. Under the captains were Patrol Lieutenants who were given control of smaller patrol groups, usually made up of 5-15 members. Patrol Lieutenants were given some agency in regard to their patrol groups hierarchy and numbers but were ultimately subject to the rulings of their captains.

Despite the population of Waterdeep being mostly humans, Caradil's group was made up of a diverse cast of misfits and outcasts. Almost all of them were recruited by Audric personally and whether this was due to his caring nature or his aim to improve the quality of the city's defenders was never known. When they were off duty, Caradil and his group could usually be found at Azuth's Mug tavern where they would.


Isen Tenderstem
- Elf male around 500 years old. He has long ginger hair that is intricately braided. He served as the third High Captain until he chose to retire to the nearby forests.

Audric Anduin
- Human male with mostly grey hair tied into a neat top knot and a grey pointed beard and brown eyes. He was the 4th and final High Captain of the Renblades. He was captain of the Dock Ward when Caradil met him.

Ssississass Vrakrakas
- Female red dragonborn standing at around 6'6". She is tanky but surprisingly nimble, her horns split into three points like a trident. She and Caradil were recruited around the same time and formed a close bond. She lets Caradil call her Sisi but hates when anyone else calls her that. She served as Patrol Lieutenant for Caradil's unit.

Brack
- Male drow, around 400 years old. He has straight, shoulder length hair and slender pointed features, standing at 5'6". Other Renblades jokingly nicknamed him "Brizzt" though his feelings on the name were unclear as he was quiet, stoic and professional.

Ilara Greypool
- Human female with long pale blonde hair tied into a tight braid, standing at 5'10". She is a member of the minor noble house Greypool. All members of the family serve in the Renblades for at least 5 years in honour of the founder of their house, Meesandara, who was the second High Captain of the Renblades. She took her service seriously, hoping to rise to High Captain like her ancestor.

Jory Greypool
- Human Male with short pale blonde hair, standing at 5'8". The black sheep of the family and Ilara's younger brother, Jory preferred drinking and gambling to patrols and paper work. He begrudgingly did his service and intended on leaving once he had served his 5 years.

Thelvin Kaervath
- Human male with dark skin. He has tightly shaved hair and a long forked beard. He is large and intimidating, especially for a human but always joyous and well tempered.

Gorzeen
- Half orc male with black hair shaved on the sides. He is covered in orcish tattoos. He is an ex convict who was given a second chance much like Caradil. He follows the letter of the law in spite of his criminal past
