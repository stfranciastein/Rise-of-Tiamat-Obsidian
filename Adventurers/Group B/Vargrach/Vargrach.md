#### **BACKSTORY**

<img src="Pasted image 20260719164354.png" alt="Vargrach" width="300" style="float:left; margin: 0 16px 12px 0;">A kobold who has seen his fair share of life and actually lived to tell the tale, Vargrach is very distinguished in the way he presents himself, and has an “old veteran” aura to him which makes him look a lot less approachable than he actually is. He is significantly better mannered than most of his kin, but he still has some Kobold quirks, for example he is curious to a fault and sometimes willingly walks into dangerous situations just because he needs to check out something. 

He also tends to find joy in the smallest of things - give him a small rock you found and he will cherish it like it’s the most precious piece of jewelry ever created. He finds himself getting easily attached to the less fortunate creatures of the wild, or in cities, and he is really attentive to others and their mood. Can be a bit of an overbearing grandparent type when he is taking care of hurt creatures or companions. He is not particularly ashamed of being a dhampir - he often jokes that the transformation gave him a “killer smile”, not just headaches, BUT he will hide himself from others whenever he has to drink blood, being afraid that others would abandon him if they saw him feast. He is not the cleanest with bloodsucking either, he tends to have dried blood on him or his attire, but he usually does not care enough to clean it off. Or anything really, he is dirty as hell.

Born in Icewind Dale, he and his small tribe were one of the hardy kobold clans who could survive in the harsh, icy landscape. They were not hostile, per say, but they were not docile either - they would often mingle among the other people living in the Dale if they struggled, but otherwise they’d keep to themselves.

One day, Vargrach, along with the few remaining kobolds of the tribe who survived a particularly harsh season decided to venture into one of the Ten Towns to try and get some resources - instead what they found before they could arrive was a pair of vampires, who were driven by hunger, and attacked them. All, but Vargrach died that day, and Varg himself could barely get away. The bite he had suffered was the one that ended with him turning into a dhampir.

Alone, and suffering from an illness that he did not know was vampirism, Vargrach decided to head south, where he hoped the land would be less cruel. He wandered for a really long time, very close to meeting his end until a dragonborn named Smokewhisper, member of the Emerald Enclave, found him. He took pity on Varg, and nursed him back to health, and also informing him that he was a dhampir, and things would be different now for him. Smoke introduced him to other druids who after some time warmed up to Vargrach, and began to teach him of the ways of the green.

Varg spent a LOT of years among the druids, finding a new family for himself who he adored and would do anything for. But all good things must come to an end, and when Smokewhisper passed away, Vargrach took it upon himself to finish what Smoke had started - a lengthy encyclopedia detailing all living animals and monsters of Faerun, and how their properties can be used in druidic rituals and healing. 

And such, began the era of his life where he would find himself wandering, recording and helping whoever needed and accepted their aid, just like Smokewhisper did before him. Upon hearing the recent troubles in Willowvale, he decided to head there to investigate, and to, of course, offer his help.
