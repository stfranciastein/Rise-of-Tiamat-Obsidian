### BACKGROUND

Baldur's Gate is a sprawling trade city on the River Chionthar, described by locals as being "halfway to everywhere." Known as the City of Blood for its long history of violence and intrigue, it is one of the largest and most influential settlements on the Sword Coast, rivalled only by Waterdeep.

The city is divided into three main sections: the [[Outer City]] (including the town of [[Rivington]] and the fortress of [[Wyrm's Rock]]), the [[Lower City]], and the [[Upper City]].  Wyrm's Crossing is the great bridge spanning the Chionthar that connects the northern and southern halves of the city and is watched over by [[Wyrm's Rock]], a fortified gatehouse garrisoned by the [[Flaming Fist]].

Political power in the city rests with the Patriars, noble families who alone may hold the title of Duke. The current Grand Duke, [[Ulder Ravengard]], has been absent from the city, leaving [[Liara Portyr]] to proclaim herself Grand Duke in his stead and campaign publicly against Patriar corruption, much to the ire of the nobility she was born into.

### FAITH

Baldur's Gate hosts a variety of faiths rather than one unifying creed, and the [[Flaming Fist]] tolerate nearly every shrine within the city walls, even ones devoted to darker powers like [Bane](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Bane) and [Bhaal](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Bhaal).

#### Predominant Faiths
Three deities predominate among ordinary Baldurians. [Umberlee](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Umberlee), the Bitch Queen, is venerated by sailors and dockworkers for favour and safety at sea. [Tymora](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Tymora), Lady Luck, is invoked for wealth, trade and gambling. [Gond](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Gond) the Wonderbringer is honoured widely for the city's engineering and craft, his following extending well beyond his own temple.

#### The Temple District
Most of the city's established temples, staffed by full clergy and open for daily worship, are clustered in the Temple District of the [[Upper City]] (commoners are not permitted to remain in the Upper City after dark).

- Water Queen's House: The [[Temple of Umberlee]], among the oldest temples in the city.
- The Lady's Hall: A temple to [Tymora](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Tymora), the second-oldest in Baldur's Gate.
- High House of Wonders: The grand temple-workshop of [Gond](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Gond), adjoining the Hall of Wonders museum.
- The Watchful Shield: A shrine to [Helm](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Helm).
- The Rose Portal: A shrine to [Lathander](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Lathander).
- The Unrolling Scroll: A shrine to [Oghma](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Oghma).
- Shrine of the Suffering: A shrine to [Ilmater](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ilmater).

#### Other Faiths
Outside the Temple District, the [[Temple of the Open Hand]] in Rivington serves [Ilmater](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ilmater)'s followers in the Outer City under Father [[Lorgan Brandis]], while the [[Temple of the Raven Queen]] in the Upper City is home to the [[Talons of the Raven Queen]] under Second Talon [[Xaria Trevisa]].

### ORGANISATIONS

A number of organisations hold sway in Baldur's Gate.

#### Flaming Fist
The [[Flaming Fist]], marked by their sigil of a closed, blazing fist on a blue field, serve as the city's standing military and law enforcement. They are garrisoned at [[Wyrm's Rock]] and throughout the city.

#### Patriars
The Patriars are the ruling noble families of Baldur's Gate, from whose ranks the city's Dukes are always drawn. [[House Ravenshade]] is one such family; [[Margaret Ravenshade]] is currently campaigning for the vacant Dukeship following [[Belynne Stelmane]]'s decline.

#### Harpers
The [[Harpers]] maintain informal contacts throughout the city, including [[Roisin Ravenshade]] at the [[Elfsong Tavern]].

#### Zhentarim
The [[Zhentarim]] have a foothold in Baldur's Gate through agents such as [[Jamna Gleamsilver]].

#### Talons of the Raven Queen
The [[Talons of the Raven Queen]] operate out of the [[Temple of the Raven Queen]] in the Upper City under Second Talon [[Xaria Trevisa]].

#### Order of the Gauntlet
The [[Order of the Gauntlet]] is represented in the city by [[Ackyn Selebon]], who runs the [[Black Saddle]] stable in the Outer City.

7. [[Sharess's Caress]]: A pleasure house in Rivington.
8. [[Elfsong Tavern]]: The most frequented tavern in the [[Lower City]], run by [[Traxigor]].
9. [[Blushing Mermaid]]: A tavern in the Heapside district of the [[Lower City]], owned by Captain Grisly and tended by Bosun Gannet.
10. [[Temple of the Raven Queen]]: A temple in the [[Upper City]] overseen by Second Talon [[Xaria Trevisa]].
11. [[Raven's Arbor]]: The manor of [[House Ravenshade]] in the [[Upper City]].
12. [[Temple of Umberlee]] (Water Queen's House): One of the oldest temples in the city, dedicated to the sea goddess [Umberlee](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Umberlee).

14. The Lady's Hall: A temple to [Tymora](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Tymora), patron of luck and trade.
