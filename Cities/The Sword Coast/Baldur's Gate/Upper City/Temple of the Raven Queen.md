A temple to the Raven Queen and the headquarters of the [[Talons of the Raven Queen]] in [[Baldur's Gate]]. Currently run by Second Talon [[Xaria Trevisa]].

- Sells discounted Thieves Tools 15 GP.
- Many Drow/Shadar-Kai make their home there.
- Inside of the Temple is always dimly lit with purple light.
- The windows are tinted with magic and divination magic cannot pierce it.
- Draped with purple banners.
- A murder of crows and a conspiracy of ravens constantly fly in and out.
- There are only about 10 Talons here at any given time.
