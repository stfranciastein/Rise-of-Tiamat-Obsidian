**Tags:** #BaldursGate #UpperCity #Manorborn #Vanthampur

The ancestral villa of [[House Vanthampur]] in the Manorborn district of [[Baldur's Gate]]'s [[Upper City]]. Duke [[Thalamra Vanthampur]] lives here along with her sons [[Thurstwell Vanthampur]], [[Amrik Vanthampur]] and [[Mortlock Vanthampur]].

If [[Thalamra Vanthampur]] is deposed at the end of [[Chapter 3.0 - The Bloody Gate]], the control of the manor will either go to [[Thurstwell Vanthampur]] or [[Enver Gortash]].
