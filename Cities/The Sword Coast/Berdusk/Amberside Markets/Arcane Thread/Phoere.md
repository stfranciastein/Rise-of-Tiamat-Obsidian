### BACKSTORY

Nobody knows when Phoere came to Toril or why. All the people of [[Berdusk]] know is that one day, one of the shopfronts in the [[Amberside Markets]] was suddenly occupied by a strange shop. The [[Zhentarim]] once tried to ask Phoere for 'protection money', but the agents they sent were suddenly lost. Phoere went out into the woods that day and released two rabbits into the wilds during hunting season. To this day, she continues to be the propietor of [[Arcane Thread]].
