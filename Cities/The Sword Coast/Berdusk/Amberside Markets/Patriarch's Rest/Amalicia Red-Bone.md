**Bio:** A female Orc
**Age:** 50

_____
### BACKSTORY

Amalicia is a rugged woman that has owned the [[Patriarch's Rest]] for many years. Inherited straight from her mother and her mother before her, Amalicia is fiercely protective of her place of business and has often found herself kicking out rabble-rousing drunks that would damage her property.

_____
### IN-GAME

[[Patriarch's Rest]] is one of the two taverns in [[Berdusk]] that the players can stay in after their first night in [[Twilight Hall]]. Amalicia will be more welcoming of the 'wild' races such as Goliaths, Orcs, etc. She will lightly tease the 'softer' races by asking if they're not sure they'd rather go to the [[Flourished Flagon]].

Amalicia can also let players know about the [[Il met by Moonlight]] quest, as [[Flint]] is a frequent customer of hers and was asked by the Minotaur to let any adventurers know about the job. She will also be available to help with identifying [[Avdul]]'s last known whereabouts.
