**Bio:** A male Minotaur
**Age:** 33

_____
### BACKSTORY

Flint is somewhat short for a Minotaur but that does not say much. He grew up in a remote herd near the Troll Mountains and moved to [[Berdusk]] after meeting his husband [[Steel]].

____
### IN-GAME

Flint is the proprietor of the [[Silver Sword]]. questgiver for the [[Ill met by Moonlight]] side quest, and a witness to [[Demonic Vengeance]].
