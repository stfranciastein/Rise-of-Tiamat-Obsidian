### BACKSTORY

Sylvira is married to [[Orenius Savikas]].

Savikas created the [[Sylvira-Van]] after the events of [[Chapter 3.0 - Candlekeep]] and reverse-engineering [[The Joy of Extradimensional Spaces (Book)]]. She gives it to the adventurers as a gift for helping solve the case of [[Matreous Melwin]]'s disappearance and provides them the services of [[Glass]]. She also gives the Kobold a list of upgrades they can do to the [[Sylvira-Van]].

In [[Act 2 - Hoard of the Dragon Queen]], she will make another appearance in [[Baldur's Gate]] looking for her friend [[Traxigor]]. She will also ask them to look into finding the book known as Mazfroth's Mighty Digressions, as it had gone missing from [[Candlekeep]] a month ago.
