**Bio:** A male Human Wizard of [[Deneir]].
**Age**: 80
**Status:** Alive
____
### BACKSTORY

Darrieth from [[House Brightflame]] is one of many Darrieths. Born as the only child, Darrieth had many expectations set upon him since birth, all of which he was able to rise above from. He has many children, though his favourite is [[Darron Brightflame]]. Darrieth has a very slow way of talking, to the point where it seems he does it on purpose. He is also quite fond of pastries and offers them regularly to any passerbies. He currently works as one of the five Readers at the gates.
