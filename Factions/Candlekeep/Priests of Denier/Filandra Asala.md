**Bio:** A female Green Dragonborn Wizard of [[Deneir]].
**Age**: 50.

____
### BACKSTORY

Filandra was born in the city of [[Scornubel]] where she worked as the Court Wizard to the Merchant King Gorvan. She moved to [[Candlekeep]] at the age of 36 to become a more learned individual but grew attached to the presence of so many other fellow intellectuals. She now works as one of the five Readers at the gates.
