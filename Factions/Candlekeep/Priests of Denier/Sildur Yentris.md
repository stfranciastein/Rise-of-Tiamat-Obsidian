**Bio:** A male High Elf Wizard of [Deneir](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Deneir).
**Age**: 344.

____
### BACKSTORY

Sildur was born in the city of [[Silverymoon]] but moved to [[Candlekeep]] at a young age of 120. He has been a devout Wizard of [Deneir](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Deneir) ever since, and works as one of the five Readers at the gates.
