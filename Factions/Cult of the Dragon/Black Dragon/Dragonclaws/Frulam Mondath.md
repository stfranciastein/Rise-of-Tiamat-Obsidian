A female Human Cleric (War Domain) of [[Tiamat]].

**Age:** 56

**Status:**
Neither party has met this NPC.
****
**Backstory:**
Frulam Mondath was from the village of [[Greenest]]. She adventured the Sword Coast for a while alongside [[Matreous Melwin]], [[Rhylrae]] and [[Torbar Dragonsbane]], serving as a Cleric of Bahamut at their side. After a long journey with her companions, she eventually retired from her life on the road. She moved back to [[Greenest]] and stayed there along with her husband, Maerwin Mondath. 

One day, Cultists of Zariel attacked them on the road back from [[Baldur's Gate]], leading to the death of Maerwin. The cleric wanted revenge for her husband's death, but when she reached out to her old companions, none of them were able to help for they too had found peace. She reached out to the [[Harpers]], the [[Order of the Gauntlet]], to anyone. But none of them answered. So Frulam cried out into the void, looking for something, anything that would help her. One night, as she wept and wept at her husband's memory, she found the [[Cult of the Dragon]]. [[Tiamat]] gave her the strength and resources she needed to exact revenge on the [[Cult of Zariel]] and any who didn't help her in her time of need.
