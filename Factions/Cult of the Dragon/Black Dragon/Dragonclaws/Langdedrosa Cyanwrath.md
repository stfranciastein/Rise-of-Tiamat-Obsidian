**Bio:** A male Blue Half-Dragon Paladin (Oath of Conquest) of [[Tiamat]].
**Age:** 45
**Status:** Alive

____
### BACKSTORY

Not much is known about Langdedrosa other than his role in the [[Cult of the Dragon]] as a member of [[Wyrmspeaker Rezmir]]'s [[Black Dragonhands]]

____
### PERSONALITY

- Prideful, loves a good fight.
- As an enemy, Langdedrosa will often stay back to challenge the players alongside his own men.
- As a companion, he retains his prideful nature and will often suggest the more brutal, pragmatic approach rather than the 'good' choices.

____
### IN-GAME

Depending on the party's decisions (or ability to defeat him prior to the end of [[Act 2 - Hoard of the Dragon Queen]]), he can either;
1. Die at the hands of the adventurers (at any point during Act 2 but most likely at the final confrontation in TBD)
2. Join them as a permanent companion. Langdedrosa will the fight against the [[Cult of the Dragon]] either directly or by becoming a contact with information on the Cult's movements. If the party has [[Cormyc Ravenshade]], they will not be able to recruit Cyanwrath as [[Torbar Dragonsbane]] will immediately execute him upon his surrender.
Defeating Cyanwrath will level the players up to level 8.
