### BACKSTORY

Born in Calimshan in the city of [[Calimport]], Severin grew up with a gift for magic. His innate talent allowed him to quickly become an accomplished wizard and adventurer, often praised by his patrons for his precision and expertise. His ambition pushed him to even greater heights, and he went on to be the court wizard to the Syl-Pasha of [[Calimport]]. While working for the Syl-Pasha, Severin met [[Paetra Silrajin]] who he would eventually marry and have three children with.

Severin made many enemies during his time as an adventurer, though most did not live long enough to exact their revenge upon him. Severin was methodical in eliminating his enemies, always making sure to scorch what remained so that they could not return. However, a chance encounter saw Severin give mercy to a fallen foe, and he allowed one of his enemies to live. A year later, when Severin was pre-occupied with an event held by the Vizars of [[Calimport]], he returned home to his family and found nothing but ashes where his home once stood.

He left his life behind in [[Calimport]] that day and disappeared into the desert, vowing to avenge the ones he lost. When he found the man that killed his wife and kids, he destroyed the man's entire village in a single night. The pain in his heart grew to an all-encompassing darkness. In his lowest moment, a hand reached out to him, and Severin found himself joining the [[Cult of the Dragon]].

As a new but respected member of the [[Cult of the Dragon]], Severin heard whispers from an unknown entity nudging him to carefully reread the texts translated by [[Sammaster]]. He discovered that it was living dragons, not dracoliches that should be the targets of their devotion. Later, Severin journeyed to [[Mintarn]] to seek out the Ancient Red Dragon [[Hoondarhh]]. The Wyrm was cruel and efficient, much like what Severin had grown into during his exile. Yet despite his cruelty, [[Hoondarhh]] found himself becoming fast friends with Severin. When he returned to Calimshan to the other members of the [[Cult of the Dragon]], they were impressed. That night, [[Tiamat]] revealed herself to Severin in a dream and gave him knowledge of the five [[Dragon Masks]].

 A person who is erudite in draconic lore becomes a wyrmspeaker while wearing the mask, which allows the wearer to think like a dragon, gain favor among dragons, and subtly influence their behavior. When all five are brought together, they magically merge into a single Mask of the Dragon Queen. With the assembled mask, the cult can release Tiamat from her prison in the Nine Hells.

Severin was able to find three of the five [[Dragon Masks]] within a few years time, but the others evade his grasp. That is, until [[Thaddak Ice-Veins]] learns the location of the [[White Dragon Mask]] during the events of [[Act 1 - The Curse of Willowvale]].

### IN-GAME

Severin's existence is not fully revealed until [[Chapter 8.0 - Castle in the Clouds]], but it can be implied several times in the story. He is first referred to by [[Matreous Melwin]] in [[Act 1 - The Curse of Willowvale]], but only as the Red Wyrmspeaker. In [[Chapter 1.0 - The Great Hunt]], it is possible for players to hear his first name in a conversation between [[Zeraxes]] and [[Thaddak Ice-Veins]] or by [[Frulam Mondath]] at the Raider Camp. During the visions given by the [[Cataclysm]], the adventurers will also see him shrouded in darkness, with only his silhouette visible to them.

#### Act I
In [[Act 1 - The Curse of Willowvale]], Severin is responsible for ordering [[Wyrmspeaker Rezmir]] in scouring the Coast Way and the Uldoon Trail for any and all of its treasures. When [[Thaddak Ice-Veins]] retrieves the [[White Dragon Mask]], Severin is the one who promotes him to the position of the White Wyrmspeaker instead of [[Alia Sanem 'Talis Kasterel']].

#### Act II
In [[Act 2 - Hoard of the Dragon Queen]], Severin oversees [[Wyrmspeaker Rezmir]]'s hoard collection, never joining the fray but always watching from a distance. After contracting the effects of the [[Cataclysm]], Severin will speak to and taunt the adventurers in their visions of [[Scornubel]] and [[Triel]] being decimated during the events of [[Chapter 2.0 - Tale of Two Cities]]. Once the [[Cult of the Dragon]] has made its way to Dragonspear Castle in [[Chapter 4.0 - Up and Down Under]], Severin withdraws from the field and returns to the [[Well of Dragons]]. When [[Wyrmspeaker Rezmir]] dies at the end of [[Chapter 8.0 - Castle in the Clouds]], Severin will take note and mark the party for death.

#### Act III
In [[Act 3 - Rise of Tiamat]], Severin will make many recurring appearances as the Red Wyrmspeaker. Most notably, in the beginning of [[Placeholder]] he will send assassins to attack the players. The point of this is to trick them into thinking the [[Blue Dragon Mask]] is in [[Mintarn]]. When the players arrive, they will be attacked by [[Lennithon]] who will fight them to the death. During this time, he will send [[Hoondarhh]], [[Voaraghamanthar]] and [[Venomfang]] to attack [[Waterdeep]]. 

If the [[Goldmane Manor]] heist was completed and [[Blue Dragon Mask]] was given to [[Jamna Gleamsilver]], Severin will know that it has been moved to [[Baldur's Gate]]. He will use the attack on [[Waterdeep]] as a distraction to send a group of soldiers lead by [[Arkhan the Cruel]] to kill the [[Zhentarim]] leadership and take back the mask. 

If the [[Goldmane Manor]] heist was completed and the [[Blue Dragon Mask]] was given to [[Laeral Silverhand]], [[Arkhan the Cruel]] will attempt to kill her which results in the death of [[Torbar Dragonsbane]] who protects her long enough for [[Cormyc Ravenshade]]'s forces to arrive in [[Waterdeep]] but still takes the [[Blue Dragon Mask]]. 

Upon the retrieval of the [[Blue Dragon Mask]], Severin will then reach out to [[Neronvain]] and attempt to get him to return to the [[Well of Dragons]]. [[Alustriel Silverhand]] will tell the party that they do not have long before [[Neronvain]] heeds the call. The next time they enter the [[Sylvira-Van]], however, they will be thrust into [[Avernus]] after Severin sabotages their spelljammer.

During this time, Severin will retrieve the remaining masks to create the [[Mask of the Dragon Queen]] and begin the summoning ritual. He will do everything in his power to stop the adventurers from disrupting the ritual, though [[Tiamat]] devours him as soon as she comes out of the portal. Once she is defeated in the Material Plane, Severin will taunt the players and say she will return soon enough. As he dies, he will drop the [[Mask of the Dragon Queen]] which will crumble into pieces along with any of the remaining [[Dragon Masks]].
