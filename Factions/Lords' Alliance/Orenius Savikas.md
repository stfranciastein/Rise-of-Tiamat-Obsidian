**Bio:** A male Human Monk (Warrior of the Elements)
**Age**: 40
**Status:** Alive

____
### BACKSTORY

Orenius is a consummate academic. He has an interesting collection of interests and rarely stays in the same place, always moving around. He is quite reserved and is often considered quite rude by most, the exception being his wife [[Sylvira Savikas]] who finds him to be quite charming in his brooding way and finds their differences to be of interest rather than a turn-off.

Born to a minor noble family in [[Waterdeep]], Orenius was a studious child who loved reading. He was quite aimless at first, but due to his love of reading he found himself drawn to the life of adventure. During his travels, he came upon a temple of monks dedicated to [[Mystra]] and joined them, finally believing to have found his purpose in life.

He became quite skilled as a monk, becoming proficient in wielding magic as well as his fists. During his travels, he came upon a grizzly sight, a Tiefling woman under attack from a group of Hobgoblins. Her caravan was ransacked, her wells of magic tapped, and from what Orenius could see she was backed into a corner. He jumped into the fray, deftly avoiding any attacks from the creatures and making short work of them in a flash. [[Sylvira Savikas]] found herself impressed, and Orenius escorted her to [[Candlekeep]].

In his travels, Orenius would often write to [[Sylvira Savikas]] and kept in close contact with her over the following months. One day, when he returned to the Sword Cost and visited [[Candlekeep]], Sylvira invited him to her dormitory and things developed from there. Years later, the two married and have been together ever since.

### IN-GAME

Orenius makes an appearance in [[Act 1 - The Curse of Willowvale]] in [[Chapter 3.0 - Candlekeep]]. He will arrive in [[Candlekeep]] by foot, flashing the symbol of [[Deneir]] in front of [[Darron Brightflame]] who lets him in immediately bypassing the requirements to enter. He can then be encountered again speaking to [[Sylvira Savikas]], urging her to leave [[Candlekeep]] with him so he can protect her. She will refuse, and he will walk out of the tower in anger.

In [[Act 2 - Hoard of the Dragon Queen]], Orenius can be encountered again in the City of [[Elturel]]. He will be meeting with [[Ontharr Frume]] and the [[Order of the Gauntlet]], letting them know about the danger coming to the city in a few days time. The party can meet him afterwards and he will reveal that he is a member of the [[Moonstars]], an off-shoot organisation with ties to the [[Harpers]].
