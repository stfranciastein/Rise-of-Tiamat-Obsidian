#### **BACKSTORY**

Born in the city of [[Baldur's Gate]] to the [[Noble Houses/House Ravenshade/Ravenshade]] family, Cormyc and his twin sister Niamh lived a comfortable life in the Upper City. As Patriars, the Ravenshades had access to the best academic and martial education money could buy. The twins were able to enjoy a privileged life that most could only dream of. Their family's money helped in more ways than one, enabling them to learn from the best of the best in Baldur's Gate. 

Unfortunately, money can't buy everything. One night, on a walk home from the Lower city, Cormyc and his sister were ambushed by a group of Assassins, fatally wounding both as they tried to defend themselves. As he laid there on the ground watching the life drain from his sister's eyes, that's when he first heard it; a woman's voice, cold and eerie but with a touch of comfort. 

It began with a whisper at first, then a booming presence as he felt himself sinking into the cobble. A flock of Ravens swirled above Cormyc as fiery vengeance stirred in his heart. He didn't know how he recognised her, but he did. Like he'd known her all his life. As the Matron's hand reached down, Cormyc rose from the ground, reborn as an instrument of her will.
