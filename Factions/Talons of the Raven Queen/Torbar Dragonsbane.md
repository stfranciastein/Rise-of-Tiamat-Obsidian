**Bio:** A male Black Half-Dragon Barbarian.
**Age:** 58
**Status:** Alive

****
#### **BACKSTORY** 

Torbar is the Half-Dragon spawn of a Minotaur mother and an Adult Black Dragon named [[Voaraghamanthar]]. He was initially unaware of his heritage until he joined the adventuring party of [[Matreous Melwin]], [[Rhylrae]] and [[Frulam Mondath]]. The four ventured the Sword Coast for a while, slaying many mighty beasts where Torbar's impressive strength came in handy. In incredible size of 8ft made it difficult for him to stay hidden, but his skill with the blade more than made up for it.

Never one to settle down in one place for too long, Torbar has yet to retire from his life as an adventurer. He is currently travelling the Sword Coast as the Third Talon of the [[Talons of the Raven Queen]]. He does this alongside his husband [[Cormyc Ravenshade]], his First Talon. Torbar is fiercely protective of the man and will risk his own life to save him.

****
#### **IN-GAME ROLE**

#### ACT I
During the events of [[Act 1 - The Curse of Willowvale]], Torbar is largely in the background along with [[Cormyc Ravenshade]]. He will wait at the tavern in [[Candlekeep]] for his partner, while the Aasimar speaks with [[Lorcan Ravenshade]]. Adventurers can learn more about him and the [[Heroes of Phandalin]], but he won't divulge much information about the [[Talons of the Raven Queen]].

### ACT II
Torbar, [[Cormyc Ravenshade]] and [[Xaria Trevisa]] will be meeting in [[Greenest]] momentarily for the Great Hunt. When the Hunt begins in earnest, [[Xaria Trevisa]] leaves the town and Torbar and his husband stay for the celebrations. When [[Lennithon]] and the [[Cult of the Dragon]] attack, he will defend the townsfolk from danger alongside the other heroes.

Later, Torbar will be found in the Mere of Dead Men during [[Chapter 6.0 - Castle Naerytar]], trying to hunt down [[Wyrmspeaker Rezmir]] and her dragon.
