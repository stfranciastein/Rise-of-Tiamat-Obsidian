After defeating [[Mellara, the Terror of the Sword Coast]], she will drop her Greatsword amidst her ashes. Nothing short of a Cleric using Divination will tell players that this weapon is cursed.

#### Curse Effects
_**Draconic Hunger.**_ This weapon is cursed. While attuned to it, you cannot voluntarily end the attunement, and the weapon cannot be removed from your possession unless the curse is broken. If the sword remains 5ft away from you for longer than 1 round, you take 2d10 Psychic damage and have [rules]disadvantage[/rules] on attack rolls and ability checks until you retrieve it. If a creature other than you attempts to wield or attune to the sword, it lashes out uncontrollably, dealing 2d10 lightning damage to the would-be wielder at the start of each of their turns. 

While under the effects of this curse, you gain access to the following properties:
- You cannot be surprised. 
- You gain the ability to summon this weapon to your hand as a bonus action. 
- You can cause the weapon to deal Lightning damage in place of its normal damage type. 
Additionally, you must reduce a creature with a challenge rating of 1/8 or higher to 0 hit points at least once every 5 days, or gain one level of exhaustion.

If the weapon is destroyed, anyone attuned is immediately disintegrated along with it. If you die while attuned to this weapon, after 1 minute your body disintegrates into fine gray ash and your soul is claimed by Tiamat in Avernus. Once claimed, you cannot be returned to life by any means short of a wish spell or divine intervention, and even then only after your soul has been freed. 

The curse can only be broken by a [spell]Remove Curse[/spell] spell cast by a Cleric of Bahamut in a ritual using a freshly harvested heart of an Adult or older Chromatic Dragon. When the curse is removed, the weapon turns into a mundane greatsword and loses all other properties.
