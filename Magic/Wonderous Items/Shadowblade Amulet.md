### BACKGROUND

Created by [[Belial]] of [[Phlegethos]], the Shadowblade Amulet is a powerful magical artifact capable of extending one's spellcasting abilities to inordinate amounts.

_____
#### Description
*This beautifully crafted golden amulet was carefully moulded into shape by expert hands. The centerpiece is dotted with dozens of rubies, with one large one in the middle that glows with crimson light. Even touching it with your hands makes you feel powerful.*

While attuned to this amulet, it provides the wearer with a +5 to Arcana and History. Additionally, they gain access to the following effects.

***Steady Mind.*** You gain Advantage to Constitution saving throws when maintaining concentration.

***Steady Hands.*** You have a +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls with magic attacks.

This item has 9 charges and regains 1d10+2 charges daily at dawn.

***Untapped Potential (X Charges).*** As a bonus action, you can restore spell slots equal to the amount of charges used. The combined levels of the spell slots you regain cannot exceed the number of charges you expended (you choose which slots to regain).

***Interplanar Travel (3 Charges).*** You can cast the Plane Shift spell without any components.

***Channel Infernal Magic (5 Charges).*** As a free action, you tap into the power of the amulet and make the next spell you cast cost no spell slots.

***Archdevil's Focus (7 Charges).*** As a bonus action, you focus your will and channel your magic through your amulet. For next concentration spell that you cast, you can choose to indefinitely prolong its duration and do not need to make Constitution saving throws to maintain concentration when you take damage.

At the end of each day, make an Arcana check (DC = 8 + the spell slot used to cast the spell). On a success, the effect persists. On a failure, you lose concentration and are mentally fatigued and have disadvantage on all Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma saving throws. Additionally, you cannot use Archdevil's Focus again for 1d8+2 days.

### Curse
This item is cursed. While attuned, fiends have advantage on Charisma (Deception, Intimidation and Persuasion) checks made agfainst you, and you have Disadvantage on saving throws against their spells.
