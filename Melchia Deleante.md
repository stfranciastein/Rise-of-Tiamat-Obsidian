Melchia is a bubbly, rock gnome originally from the town of [[Beregost]]. She was a very well-read Avowed in [[Candlekeep]] but decided to go to [[Blackstaff Academy]] in order to get a formal education in magic. She has a crush on [[Alain Gilfont]].
