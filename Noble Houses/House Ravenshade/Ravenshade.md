A patriar family in [[Baldur's Gate]] that dealt in the gem and jewelry trade, as well as the dye and ink trade. As trade specialists, their manor is located in the central-west area of Manorborn.
****
**Notable Members**
- [[Lorcan Ravenshade]]: Patriarch of the House and the High Acolyte of [[Candlekeep]].
- [[Margaret Ravenshade]]: A famous patriar in the [[Baldur's Gate]] courts, often praised for her contributions to the [[Lower City]]. Effective leader of House Ravenshade while Lorcan is away at [[Candlekeep]].
- [[Cormyc Ravenshade]]: A recently awakened Aasimar Paladin of the Raven Queen. Cormyc has largely abandoned his roots as nobility and spends most of his time on the road with the [[Talons of the Raven Queen]].
- [[Niamh Ravenshade]]: Cormyc's twin sister. A fighter who died at the hands of House [[House Vanthampur]] assassins disguised as [[Lower City]] thugs.
- [[Roisin Ravenshade]]: A Bard that spends most of her time at the [[Elfsong Tavern]] in the [[Lower City]]. Roisin serves alongside the [[Harpers]] under direct orders from [[Jaheria]] herself.
