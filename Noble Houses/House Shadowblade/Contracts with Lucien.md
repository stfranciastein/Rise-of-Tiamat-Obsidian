### Impyerno Lima

#### Context
[[Impyerno Lima]] signed the pact with [[Lucien]] in [[Act 1 - The Curse of Willowvale]], [[Chapter 7.0 - Deathstruck]] after [[Yan 'Keyreeonis' Lawntill]] promised [[Venomfang]] the horde of gold collected by the [[Herd of the Dragon]].

#### Wording
This contract binds [[Yan 'Keyreeonis' Lawntill]], [[Thavion Brightfell]], [[Ulena Kiln]], [[Fero Mörktträ]] and [[Ryn Fenrath]] into a deal with one Lord [[Lucien]] Shadowblade of [[Waterdeep]]. Upon agreeing and signing this contract, the recipient is bound to their agreed obligations and parameters within the pact, as is the scribe. Upon completion of the agreed obligations and parameters on both sides of the pact, the contract is fulfilled and concluded. 

This contract as a whole remains until the predetermined period of eternity, allowing the recipient, should they feel inclined, to invoke and trigger the pact any time thereafter through verbal acceptance in relative proximity of this contract. Any violation of this contract on the part of the scribe immediately holds this contract null and void, requiring a penalty payment of 15,000 gold pieces to the recipient to be paid immediately. Any violation of this contract on the part of the recipient immediately invokes a punishment as will be decided by the scribe. 

The scribe, Lord [[Lucien]] Shadowblade of Waterdeep, agrees to provide the location of the Hoard collected by the Golliath known as [[Mellara, the Terror of the Sword Coast]]. The recipient, agrees to be the instrument of the scribe's will in retrieving the [[Shadowblade Amulet]] when called upon at their own discretion, as was verbally agreed upon by the recipients. Upon invocation of the scribe’s favour, the recipients must make reasonable and continual effort toward fulfilment. Failure to do so within a timeframe deemed reasonable by infernal custom constitutes violation.

#### Amendments
Since [[Fero Mörktträ]] died at the end of [[Act 1 - The Curse of Willowvale]], he is technically freed from service to [[Lucien]]. However, since he was revived by [[Tharizdun]]'s magic he feels compelled to follow the Pact.

### The Bush Whackers

### Zil

This contract binds [[Zilliletha]] into a deal with one Lord [[Lucien]] Shadowblade of [[Waterdeep]]. Upon agreeing and signing this contract, the recipient is bound to their agreed obligations and parameters within the pact, as is the scribe. Upon completion of the agreed obligations and parameters on both sides of the pact, the contract is fulfilled and concluded.

The pact remains dormant but valid until its terms are completed. Once fulfilled, the Recipient may invoke and trigger the agreement at any future time by verbally affirming their acceptance while in relative proximity to this physical contract.

Any violation of this contract on the part of the scribe immediately holds this contract null and void, requiring a penalty payment of 5 gold pieces to the recipient to be paid immediately. Any violation of this contract on the part of the recipient immediately invokes a fitting punishment as will be decided by the scribe.

The scribe, Lord Lucien Shadowblade agrees to supply all current living members of Impyerno Lima with Potions of Restful Slumber. He further commits to personally watching over their bodies during the 10 minutes of slumber, guaranteeing their complete safety.

The recipient, [[Zilliletha]] agrees to serve as the instrument of the Scribe’s will by retrieving the grimoire of [[Wyrmspeaker Rezmir]] of the [[Cult of the Dragon]] promptly upon her death. Neither the recipient or any members of Impyerno Lima, or any third party aside from the scribe must not open the grimoire at any time and shall deliver it to the Scribe at the earliest available opportunity. 

Failure to do so within a timeframe considered reasonable by infernal custom shall be deemed a violation of this pact.
