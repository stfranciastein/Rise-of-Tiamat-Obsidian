Tadgh and his twin brother [[Daithi Serelim]] are woodcarvers and carpenters by trade. They are trying to connect to their roots and visit the [[High Forest]]. They both have long brown hair, pale skin and tribal tattoos on their faces. They are hauling cargo from Cormyr.

Despite not being combat-oriented, Tadgh knows how to hold a bow.
