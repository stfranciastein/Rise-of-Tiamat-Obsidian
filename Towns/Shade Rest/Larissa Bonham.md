A female Human Cleric of Chauntea.

**Age:** 23

**Status:**
- [[Impyerno Lima]]: Alive (survived the events of [[Chapter 2.3 - Saving Shade Rest]])
- [[The Bush Whackers]]: Dead (turned into a fungal zombie cleric by [[Thaddak Ice-Veins]])
****
She is the daughter of Elder [[Cormil Bonham]], and currently the head Cleric at the [[Temple of Chauntea (Shade Rest)]]. Larissa has been in [[Shade Rest]] for all of her life, taking over her mother's duties at the Temple after she passed. She is close friends with [[Tamae Tideon]] and is fiercely loyal and protective of her, though she understands that the Genasi will more than likely move on in the near future due to the trouble with the [[Herd of the Dragon]].
