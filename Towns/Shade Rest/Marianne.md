Marianne appears to be a fragile old woman, and claims that she used to be one of the founding members of [[Shade Rest]] when the town originally migrated outside of the [[Cloak Wood]] after [[Venomfang]] destroyed the original village.

In reality she is a powerful old Sea Hag named Zokoy. The hag killed the original Marianne not long after [[Shade Rest]] was founded. Normally,  Marianne offers her services to adventurers as a fortune teller, using her tarot cards as a medium. These readings are always false. Whenever Marianne wants to give people's real fortunes, she gently asks for a story to be told to her in exchange. In return she will show their future but take away the memory of the story given to her.
