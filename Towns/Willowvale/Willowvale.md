Willowvale is a small, idyllic town with a population of 100 people. It is located between the city of Baldur's Gate and the village of Shade Rest. The townsfolk are primarily made up of farmers and labourers. There are two Flaming Fist patrolmen that often check in on Willowvale on rotation.

The main food source comes from the ample farmland. There is one inn & tavern in Willowvale called the Hefty Pint run by [[Alessan]], and a simple general goods store called Bella's Goods run by [[Isabella Dorin]].

Almost exactly 1 year ago, a curse befell Willowvale, stripping the land of its vitality. [[Feldir]], the town's mage, believes this to be a curse that has befallen the land.

The westernmost side of the town borders the Coast Way.
