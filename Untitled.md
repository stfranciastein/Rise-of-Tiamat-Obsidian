Homebrew item patch notes.

- _**Amulet of Mind Shielding.**_ Now called the Amulet of Serenity. Previously did most of the things a Ring of Mind-Shielding does. Now it only prevents others from communication attempts and explictly only makes you immune to 'Scrying'. Otherwise it supersedes the need for the Ring of Mind-Shielding which requires attunement and the amulet doesn't.
- _**Arcana Glass.**_ Wasn't properly displaying charges. It is now.
- _**Belt of Inert Giant's Strength.**_ Now called the Belt of Evolving Giant's Strength. Formatting to make it look more like my other homebrews. It also now allows the attuner to speak Giant similar to the Belt of Dwarvenkind.
- _**Blinkback Belt.**_ No longer requires attunement, but your weapons only return at the start of your next turn.
- _**Garb of the Earth and Sky.**_ Now called the Garb of the Earth and Air. Changed the bonus to be a flat +2 to unarmed strike damage and lets you push creatures back 5 ft.
- ***Both of Glass's Bracers.*** Both of these items require a free hand to activate.
- ***Greatsword of the Storm.*** Now named Vaelrix (named after the soul inside the blade). Changed it rarity to Very Rare. Since the jig is up, the item's full stats are now there as well. Have fun!
- ***Matreous's Satchel of Holding.*** Now named Lyam's Satchel of Holding. Formatting fixes.
- ***Moonlight Bolter.*** Has the 'Radiant Ammunition' property which lets it deal Radiant damage. Instead of being a situational 2d4 radiant, it now does a flat 2d4 Radiant extra damage. The damage increases by 1d4 if the target is an undead or fiend, similar to Divine Smite. Requires attunement by a Cleric, Druid or Paladin of Selune.
- ***Harmonic Dueller.*** Now called the Rapier of Harmony. Instead of giving a flat damage bonus based on your Wisdom modifier, now it's activatable as a bonus action and lasts for a minute to bring it down to 'Rare'.
- ***Ring of the Forest.*** The curse reactivates every time you attune to it instead of being gone for good.
- ***Night Owl's Glasses***. Now called Ravenshade's Reading Glasses. Clerics can now attune to it. Added Detect Magic as one of the spells (1 charge) and changed See Invisibility to cost 2 charges.
- ***Robe of the Dragon.*** Extremely minor text changes.
- ***Truthseeker.*** The advantage of Inquisitor's Shot also now includes Fiends. Additionally, the speed bonus is now 10 ft. but only applies if you're holding Truthseeker at the start of combat. Reworded Repentence so that lower rolls are considered failures instead of the higher end.
- ***Wand of the Binder.*** Now called Axerias's Old Reliable. Sorcerer, Warlock and Wizard Cantrips deal an extra +1 damage.
- ***Wand of the Brightblood.*** I have no idea who still has this, but it explicitly now only lets you twinned spell an attack roll spell of no higher than 2nd level.
