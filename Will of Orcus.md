### PART I - Scouting Ahead

*Khasar shuffles through a duffle bag and brings out a rudimentary map of what appears to be a temple. Marked on the bottom is obliterated script written in what you can only guess is an ancient language. Judging by age, it looks at least to be a few hundred years old but the map itself likely a traced copy of the original.*

KHASAR: This map here was given to us by a couple from the Western Heartlands who did battle with Cultists of Orcus over three decades ago.

*Khasar spreads the map out fully and pins each end with four heavy marked stones in order to keep it open. His eyes gleam as the stones activate and projects what looks to be a floating hologram of the temple.*

KHASAR: I'll get to the point. The Second Talon sent me up north to investigate Ironspine Keep and stop the undead, though we couldn't have anticipated we'd be dealing with a Zulkir of this strength.

ALAIN: Zulkir or not, no common Necromancer can raise dead at this level. More than likely this ancient Temple still carries some residual energy from when the Cultists were operating in the area. If the Zulkir is anywhere, they're likely deep within.

KHASAR: Exactly. I propose we visit this Temple and see what we're dealing with. If [[Venomfang]] was operating here, his phylactery is likely nearby.

ALAIN: A Dragon is one thing, but a Dracolich is another problem entirely. He can't be allowed to continue his unnatural lifespan.

KHASAR: If the Necromancer is anywhere, it'll be at the heart of the temple here. This'll also give us some insight as to why the [[Red Wizards of Thay]] are involved with the [[Cult of the Dragon]].
